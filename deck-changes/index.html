What has changed in Ordpar's Anki decks, newest first. AnkiWeb doesn't tell you when a deck you downloaded is updated, so this list does. Each entry says which deck it reached:

  • 6000 Most Frequent Norwegian Words, in two parts on AnkiWeb (Part 1, Part 2).

How to update is on each deck's AnkiWeb page. Changes to the dictionary itself have their own list: Changes to the dictionary.

2026-10-25

6000 Most Frequent Norwegian Words, both parts:

  • Every card rebuilt on Ordpar. The back of each card is now the word's article from Ordpar, with every example in Norwegian and English, and a small article for each linked word.
  • A reverse card for every word, English to Norwegian, after all the forward cards.
  • The AI-written explanations and sentences are gone, and all the audio with them, and so are the fields that held them.
  • The word list corrected: 205 words added, 109 retired, and the frequency ranks renumbered.

2026-07-25

6000 Most Frequent Norwegian Words:

  • Dictionary entries for the first 2,500 words.

2026-06-20

6000 Most Frequent Norwegian Words:

  • Every dictionary entry updated. The example sentences have a button that shows their English, so you can use them to practise.

2024-01-11

6000 Most Frequent Norwegian Words:

  • Dictionary entries for the first 1,500 words.
