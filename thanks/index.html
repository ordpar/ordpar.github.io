Readers help us in two ways: by telling us about mistakes in the articles, and with ideas for making the dictionary or the Anki deck better. Here we list the mistakes we have corrected and the ideas we have taken up or plan to. We name a reader only if they asked us to.

Ideas from our readers

  • A note on words with more than one card: a card's front now says when its spelling also has a card as another word class, such as “Also in this deck as: adverb, interjection”, after a reader found such cards hard to tell apart. In the decks since their update of 11 November. Reported by Moomoo
  • A second hint on the Anki cards: after the Hint shows an example sentence, a second button shows the whole sentence in the other language, with the word you are practising left blank, so you can see what the other words in the example mean. In the decks since their update of 11 November. Suggested by Moomoo
