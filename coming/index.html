Coming to Ordpar

What we have decided to build, and what each is waiting for. There are no dates: each comes when what it waits for is in place.

  • Norwegian you can listen to. Audio of the headwords and the example sentences. Waiting for: funding.
  • Ordpar in Norwegian. The menus, the settings and the articles' labels in Norwegian, as a choice. Waiting for: the site's first full version (1.0), when its building stage ends.
  • More languages. Bokmålsordboka's articles in other languages than English. Waiting for: the English to be complete.
  • More example sentences. New examples for the articles and senses that have none yet, each one checked by hand before it is added. Waiting for: time to check them.
  • Better English equivalents. Every English equivalent under a headword reviewed, and each linked to the sense of the article it translates; pointing at one, or pressing it, will show its senses in the article. Waiting for: the revision of the lexicographical principles our work builds on, then time to do the work.
