About Ordpar

Ordpar is a Norwegian–English dictionary on the web. It is built on Bokmålsordboka, the Bokmål dictionary from Språkrådet and the University of Bergen, and adds an English translation to each article: the definitions, the examples and the set phrases. Every article has a page; those we have not translated yet are shown in Norwegian.

Ordpar's aim is to help people learn Norwegian. Translating Bokmålsordboka into English, and later into other languages, is how we work towards that: a learner can read a Norwegian word's whole dictionary entry in a language they already know. Ordpar is free to use, with no advertisements and no account, and its English is free to reuse (see The English, below).

How far it has come

Bokmålsordboka has about 94,200 articles, and 21,873 of them have been translated so far: 10,132 complete, with the short summary at the top, and the rest with every definition and example in English, their summary still to come. The translated articles are mostly the common, longer ones, so they hold a larger share of the dictionary's text: about 36 % of it, counted in running words across all its definitions, examples and etymologies. We intend to translate the whole of Bokmålsordboka into English, and then to add other languages.

The English

The English is written with AI (Claude, by Anthropic) under Robin Fauskanger's direction and review. You may reuse it on the same terms as the Norwegian, CC BY 4.0: credit the English to Ordpar (ordpar.no), and the Norwegian to Bokmålsordboka.

Our changes to the Norwegian

We show Bokmålsordboka's articles in a layout close to ordbokene.no's, with these changes of ours, as of its data of 25 September 2026:

  • Cross-references that pointed to the wrong sense, homograph or article, corrected: 82 in 80 articles
  • Typos, punctuation and formatting corrected: 99 in 97 articles
  • The more common spelling of the headword put first, where Bokmålsordboka lists it second: 4 articles
  • Other corrections (a word form, a list of compounds, a misplaced passage, a duplicated set phrase and a duplicated link target): 5 in 5 articles
  • Example sentences of our own, added where a sense had no example: 450 in 271 articles. Each is marked with a small raised "+", so it is never taken for Bokmålsordboka's.

Each corrected article says so at its foot, with a link to Bokmålsordboka's article on ordbokene.no. Any mistakes our changes introduce are ours. Every page credits Bokmålsordboka in its footer, with a link to ordbokene.no.
