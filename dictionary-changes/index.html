What has changed in the dictionary's content, newest first: newly translated articles, our corrections of Bokmålsordboka's text, and each newer copy of Bokmålsordboka's data, the work of Språkrådet and the University of Bergen. With a newer copy, the articles not yet translated follow it at once, with our corrections kept wherever the text they correct is unchanged; the translated ones keep the Norwegian their English was written for until we revise them. Today's totals are on the About page.

2026-11-27

  • New summary glosses. Every summary gloss, the English equivalents at the top of an article, has been written again under new guidelines, and 7,357 of them now read differently. 6,124 articles that had none have them now, and so do 1,025 of the newly translated articles.
  • Bokmålsordboka's data of 27 November 2026 (Språkrådet and the University of Bergen).
  • Citations checked: 10,154 citations, the English given for a word where an article links to it, have been checked, 8,377 of them for the first time, and 1,911 of them revised.
  • We translated 402 articles again in full under the new guidelines.
  • Words mentioned without a link are now linked to their own article, as odel in the etymology of adel: in 26 articles so far.
  • Bokmålsordboka has corrected some of the cross-references we had corrected, in its own way, so fewer corrections of ours remain.
  • Totals: 23,417 translated (+1,208), 17,412 of them complete (+7,144); our corrections of cross-references 18 in 18 articles (was 25 in 25); words mentioned without a link, now linked, 26 in 26 articles (new); of typos, punctuation and formatting 328 in 321 articles (was 329 in 322); other corrections 13 in 13 articles (was 12 in 12).

2026-11-23

  • Bokmålsordboka's data of 21 November 2026 (Språkrådet and the University of Bergen).
  • We revised the English of over 250 articles, among them the short summary at the top of 124.
  • A word used only in set phrases no longer has a summary of its own, such as avhenge, used in avhenge av: its English is on the set phrase. Two such words, åle and prakke, are shown in Norwegian again; their set phrases åle seg and prakke på keep their English, and the two cross-references that led to the words now lead to the set phrases.
  • Eight of our corrections apply again. Bokmålsordboka's data of 18 November changed the text of eight mistakes we had corrected but left a mistake in each; our corrections now fit the text as it stands.
  • CEFR levels: 812 words and set phrases have a new level, most of them after the words of the Anki deck were judged a second time and, where the two judgings differed, a third; komme trekkende med has a level now.
  • Totals: 22,209 translated (−2), 10,268 of them complete (−58); our corrections of cross-references 25 in 25 articles (was 23 in 23); of typos, punctuation and formatting 329 in 322 articles (was 322 in 315); other corrections 12 in 12 articles (was 11 in 11).
