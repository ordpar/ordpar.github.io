What has changed in the dictionary's content, newest first: newly translated articles, our corrections of Bokmålsordboka's text, and each newer copy of Bokmålsordboka's data, the work of Språkrådet and the University of Bergen. With a newer copy, the articles not yet translated follow it at once; the translated ones keep the Norwegian their English was written for until we revise them. Today's totals are on the About page.

2026-10-23 — the starting point

The totals when this list began, with Bokmålsordboka's data of 25 September 2026. No changes are listed: later entries count from here.

  • Articles: 94,197, of which 21,873 have been translated into English (23.2 %): 10,132 complete, with the short summary at the top, and 11,741 with every definition and example in English, their summary still to come.
  • Share of the text translated: 36.3 % of the dictionary's running words (495,400 of 1,365,856), across its definitions, examples and etymologies.
  • Our corrections of Bokmålsordboka's text: 190 in 185 articles (82 cross-references in 80 articles; 99 typos, punctuation and formatting errors in 97; the more common spelling of the headword put first in 4; 5 other corrections in 5). Each corrected article says so at its foot.
  • Example sentences of our own, added where a sense had no example: 450 in 271 articles, each marked with a small raised "+".
