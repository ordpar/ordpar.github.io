innrømme

verb

to admit · to grant

Inflection

Etymology

av tysk einräumen; av rømme (II)to escape · to run away · to flee, opprinnelig ‘overlate noen et rom ved å rømme det’from German einräumen; from rømme (II)to escape · to run away · to flee, originally ‘to give someone a room by vacating it’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. erkjenne (2)to acknowledge, to recognise, vedgå (1)to admit, to confessto acknowledge, to recogniseerkjenne (2), to admit, to confessvedgå (1)
    • innrømme sine feilto admit one's mistakes
    • du må da innrømme at gutten er flinkyou have to admit the boy is clever
  2. la få;
    gi (1)to give    to let have;
    to givegi (1)
    • innrømme kunden 10 % rabattto give the customer a 10% discount
    • innrømme de undertrykte større frihetto grant the oppressed greater freedom
