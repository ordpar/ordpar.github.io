“bar” is an inflected form of:

  • bære I homograph 1 verbverb (past)(preteritum) to carry · to wear · to bear holde noe oppe (og bevege seg framover med det)
  • bare II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) Norwegian only
