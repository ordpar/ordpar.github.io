inngå

verbverb

to form part of · to enter into

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. være en del av;
    høre medto go with it     to be part of;
    to go with ithøre med
    • alt dette inngår i avtalen all this is included in the agreement
    • inngå i det vanlige opplegget to form part of the usual arrangement
  2. gå inn i et gjensidig forpliktende forhold to enter into a mutually binding relationship
    • inngå en kontrakt to enter into a contract
    • inngå ekteskap to enter into marriage
