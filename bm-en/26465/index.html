inngå

verb

to form part of · to enter into

Inflection

Etymology

av inn (II)in · into · inwards og gåto walk · to go · to run, to work (of a machine) · to turn outfrom inn (II)in · into · inwards and gåto walk · to go · to run, to work (of a machine) · to turn out

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. være en del av;
    høre medto go with it    to be part of;
    to go with ithøre med

    • inngå i det vanlige oppleggetto form part of the usual arrangement
  2. gå inn i et gjensidig forpliktende forholdto enter into a mutually binding relationship
    • inngå en kontraktto enter into a contract
    • inngå ekteskapto enter into marriage
