allerede

adverb

already · as early as

Etymology

fra lavtysk egentlig ‘helt rede’from Low German, properly ‘quite ready’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. alt (III)already · right, all the way · however much, no matter howalreadyalt (III)
    • allerede på den tidenalready at that time
    • allerede i 15-årsalderenas early as the age of 15
    • jeg er allerede ferdig med halvpartenI have already finished half of it
    • de er allerede kommetthey have already arrived
  2. bare (III, 1)justjustbare (III, 1)
    • allerede dette er nok til å overbevise megthis alone is enough to convince me
