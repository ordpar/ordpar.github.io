reise seg

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. rette seg opp;
    stå opp    to straighten up;
    to stand up     Eng. phraseto rise to one's feet
    • hun reiste seg for en eldre dame på trikkenshe gave up her seat for an elderly lady on the tram
    • vi reiste oss fra bordetwe got up from the table
  2. gjøre motstand;
    gjøre opprør    to resist;
    to rebel     Eng. phraseto rise up
    • bøndene reiste seg mot de høye skattenethe farmers rose up against the high taxes
  3. komme tilbake etter et nederlagto come back after a defeat Eng. phraseto get back on one's feet
    • bedriften har reist seg etter konkursenthe company has got back on its feet after the bankruptcy
  4. stige opp;
    kneise    to rise;
    to tower
    • fjella reiste seg i horisontenthe mountains rose on the horizon
