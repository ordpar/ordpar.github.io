privatisere

verb
Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre privat (II, 1)private;
    overføre offentlig eiendom eller drift til private    to make privateprivat (II, 1);
    to transfer public property or operations into private hands
    • partiet vil privatisere den nasjonaliserte industrienthe party wants to privatise the nationalised industry
  2. framstille som en privat (II, 2)private eller personlig sak;
    framheve personlige, ikke-allmenne forhold    to present as a privateprivat (II, 2) or personal matter;
    to emphasise personal rather than general concerns
    • privatisere det religiøse livto make religious life a private matter
    • en må ikke privatisere alvorlige samfunnsproblemerserious social problems must not be turned into private matters
