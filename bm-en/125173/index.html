politi og røver

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. barnelek der den eller de som er politi, skal fange røvernechildren's game in which whoever is the police has to catch the robbers Eng. phrasecops and robbers
    • barna lekte politi og røverthe children were playing cops and robbers
  2. ordensmakt og lovbryterthe forces of law and order and the lawbreaker
    • etterforskningen var et kappløp mellom politi og røverthe investigation was a race between the police and the criminal
