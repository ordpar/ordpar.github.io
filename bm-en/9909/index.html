desto

adverb

all the (more, greater, …) · so much the

Etymology

gjennom tysk; fra gammelhøytysk des diu, kasusformer av das ‘det’through German; from Old High German des diu, case forms of das 'that'

Senses and Example Sentences

dess (II)all the (more, softer, …) · the (in the paired 'the … the …')before a comparativekomparativ (I): so much;
