ta

verbverb

to take · to catch · to grab

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt taka Old Norse taka

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gripe med hendene eller redskap (og holde fast eller flytte) to grasp with the hands or a tool (and hold fast or move)
    • ta tauet to take the rope
    • ta fatet og sett det på bordet take the dish and put it on the table
    • hun tok hardt i armen min she gripped my arm hard
    • ta fram koppene to get out the cups
    • han har tatt opp lekene fra gulvet he has picked up the toys from the floor
    • han tar boka ut av bokhylla he takes the book out of the bookcase
    • hun tok duken av bordet she took the tablecloth off the table
  2. fange;
    få tak i     to catch;
    to get hold of
    • ta laksen i garn to catch the salmon in a net
    • en målmann som tar alle baller a goalkeeper who catches every ball
    • tyvene ble tatt the thieves were caught
    • ta noen på fersk gjerning to catch someone red-handed
  3. tilegne seg;
    skaffe, hente;
    kreve;
    stjele     to acquire;
    to obtain, to fetch;
    to charge;
    to steal
    • ta det en kan få to take what one can get
    • ta lærdom av noe to learn a lesson from something
    • ta hjem varer to bring home goods
    • hvor skal jeg ta penger fra? where am I to get money from?
    • sitatet er tatt fra Bjørnson the quotation is taken from Bjørnson
    • de tok inn kr 15000 på loppemarkedet they took in 15,000 kroner at the flea market
    • banken tok høy rente the bank charged a high rate of interest
    • ta kr 500 for turen to charge 500 kroner for the trip
    • ta lommeboka fra noen to take the wallet from someone
    • forskere som tar andres ideer researchers who take others' ideas
  4. få kontroll over;
    tiltvinge seg;
    erobre     to gain control of;
    to seize by force;
    to conquer
    • ta en by to take a city
    • ta over styre og stell to take over the running of things
    • de tok festningen they took the fortress
    • de har tatt makten i landet they have seized power in the country
    • ta kommandoen to take command
    • ta noe i besittelse to take possession of something
    • hun tar roret she takes the helm
    • faen ta deg! damn you!
    • han tok henne med vold he took her by force
  5. banke opp;
    straffe;
    drepe;
    slakte     to beat up;
    to punish;
    to kill;
    to slaughter
    • bare vent, jeg skal nok ta deg! just you wait, I'll get you!
    • reven tok hønene the fox killed the hens
    • vi skal ta grisen i morgen we're going to slaughter the pig tomorrow
  6. få til å forsvinne;
    fjerne     to make disappear;
    to remove
    • nabohuset tok all utsikten the neighbouring house took away the whole view
    • ta luven fra konkurrentene to steal a march on the competitors
    • ta blindtarmen to remove the appendix
    • ta stingene to take out the stitches
    • ta noe vekk to take something away
  7. føre inn i en ny tilstand;
    overmanne, gripe     to bring into a new state;
    to overcome, to seize
    • sinnet tok henne anger seized her
    • gråten tar ham he is overcome by tears
  8. komme borti;
    berøre, nå     to come into contact with;
    to touch, to reach
    • ikke ta på maleriet! don't touch the painting!
    • ta seg i håret to touch one's hair
    • hun tok seg for pannen she put her hand to her forehead
    • hun har aldri tatt i et ratt she has never held a steering wheel
    • flyet tok bakken the plane touched the ground
    • de tar land lenger mot nord they make land further north
  9. virke på en overflate;
    kjennes, merkes;
    slite     to act on a surface;
    to be felt, to make itself felt;
    to wear, to be taxing
    • høvelen tar dårlig the plane cuts poorly
    • de nye skoene tar over vristen the new shoes pinch over the instep
    • sola tok godt the sun was strong
    • sykdommen hadde tatt på henne the illness had taken its toll on her
    • oppstigningen tar på kreftene the climb is taxing on one's strength
  10. føre til et bestemt resultat to lead to a particular result
    • epidemien tok livet av mange the epidemic took the lives of many
    • arbeidet tar nesten knekken på ham the work is almost the death of him
  11. utføre en oppgave;
    lage, gjøre;
    fotografere     to carry out a task;
    to make, to do;
    to photograph
    • ta oppvasken to do the washing-up
    • han tok en kopi he made a copy
    • de tok mange bilder i ferien they took many photos on holiday
  12. ha evne til å klare;
    mestre     to have the ability to manage;
    to master
    • hun tar alt med en gang she grasps everything at once
    • sjekta tar sjøen best the boat handles the sea best
  13. vinne, oppnå to win, to gain
    • ta sølvmedalje i roing to win a silver medal in rowing
    • ta knekten med esset to take the jack with the ace
    • ta stikk to win a trick
  14. gjøre bruk av to make use of
    • ta noe som mønster to take something as a model
    • ta tiden til hjelp to enlist the help of time
    • jeg vil ikke ta det ordet i min munn I won't let that word cross my lips
  15. velge blant flere muligheter;
    plukke ut     to choose among several options;
    to pick out
    • hvilken lue skulle han ta? which cap should he take?
    • ta parti for noen to take someone's side
    • ta noen i lære hos seg to take someone on as an apprentice
    • ta noen som vitne to take someone as a witness
    • du tar vel en sjekk? you'll take a cheque, won't you?
    • hver tar sin, så tar jeg min each takes their own, then I'll take mine
  16. få i seg mat, drikke, medisin eller lignende to take in food, drink, medicine or the like
    • ta tran to take cod liver oil
    • ta jernpiller to take iron pills
    • de tok en matbit før de dro they had a bite to eat before they left
    • jeg har tatt meg en dram I've had a dram
  17. måle (1)to measure to measuremåle (1)
    • ta mål to take measurements
    • ta temperaturen to take the temperature
    • ta pulsen to take the pulse
  18. ha en viss utstrekning i tid eller rom to have a certain extent in time or space
    • reisen tok lang tid the journey took a long time
    • turen tar en uke the trip takes a week
    • sofaen tar stor plass the sofa takes up a lot of space
  19. ha plass til;
    romme     to have room for;
    to hold, to accommodate
    • flasken tar en liter the bottle holds a litre
    • salen kan ta 450 personer the hall can hold 450 people
  20. bevege seg eller føre i en bestemt retning, på en bestemt måte eller med et bestemt framkomstmiddel to move or steer in a particular direction, in a particular way or by a particular means of transport
    • ta til skogs to head for the woods
    • ta til venstre to turn left
    • ta veien over heia to take the road over the moor
    • ta skipet inn fjorden to steer the ship into the fjord
    • ta los om bord to take a pilot on board
    • de tok trappa i to sprang they took the stairs in two bounds
    • ta toget to take the train
    • hun tok sykkelen til butikken she took the bicycle to the shop
  21. reagere eller oppfatte på en bestemt måte;
    forstå, tolke     to react or perceive in a particular way;
    to understand, to interpret
    • ta det med ro to take it easy
    • han tar alt med et smil he takes everything with a smile
    • de tok det tungt they took it hard
    • ta feil to be mistaken
    • han tok det med fatning he took it calmly
    • hun tok det de sa bokstavelig she took what they said literally
    • ta noe på den rette måten to take something the right way
    • ta noen med det gode to win someone over by gentle means
    • de tok henne for en annen they took her for someone else
  22. brukt i uttrykk for å mene noe eller gjøre noe for å endre en tilstand, utvikling eller lignende;
    utføre en bestemt handling     used in expressions for holding an opinion or doing something to change a state, development or the like;
    to carry out a particular action
    • ta stilling til noe to take a stand on something
    • ta avstand fra noe to distance oneself from something
    • ta ordet to take the floor
    • ta følgene to take the consequences
    • hun tok mot til seg she plucked up courage
    • ta til gjenmæle to reply, to respond (to criticism)
    • ta i ed to swear in
    • ta sjansen to take the chance
    • ta hevn to take revenge
    • ta avskjed to take one's leave
    • ta eksamen to take an exam
    • ta seg en tur to go for a walk
    • ta til våpen to take up arms
  23. brukt i uttrykk for at noe endrer seg på en bestemt måte;
    utvikle     used in expressions for something changing in a particular way;
    to develop
    • ta form to take shape
    • ta fyr to catch fire
    • treet tok skade the tree was damaged
  24. brukt i en konstruksjon med ‘og’ og et annet verb: gjøre, utføre (det som det andre verbet angir) used in a construction with og 'and' and another verb: to do, to carry out (what the other verb indicates)
    • jeg tar og går ut I'll just go out
  25. i grammatikk: følges av et bestemt ledd;
    kreve en viss form av et tilknyttet ord     in grammar: to be followed by a particular element;
    to require a certain form of an associated word
    • et verb som tar objekt a verb that takes an object
    • preposisjonen ‘til’ tar genitiv i norrønt the preposition til 'to' takes the genitive in Old Norse

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

ta av
  1. fjerne (fra) to remove (from) Eng. phraseto take off
    • ta av lokket to take off the lid
    • han tok av lua he took off his cap
    • han ble tatt av saken he was taken off the case
  2. gi ly to give shelter
    • skogen tar av for vinden the forest gives shelter from the wind
  3. spakne to abate Eng. phraseto die down
    • vinden tar av the wind is dying down
  4. gå ned i vekt to lose weight
    • ta av tre kilo to lose three kilos
    • han har tatt av litt i det siste he has lost a little weight lately
  5. begynne å stige for alvor;
    nå de store høyder     to begin to rise in earnest;
    to reach great heights     Eng. phraseto take off
    • det var i fjor at salget virkelig tok av it was last year that sales really took off
  6. legge til side;
    holde av;
    reservere     to set aside;
    to keep back;
    to reserve
    • ta av en vare til noen en kjenner to set aside an item for someone one knows
    • de har tatt av noen plasser på første benk they have reserved some seats in the front row

    • flyet tok av the plane took off
