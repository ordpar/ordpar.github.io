renne II homograph 2

verbverb

to run · to flow · to rise (of the sun)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt renna Old Norse renna

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. om væske eller masse: strømme, flyte, sige of a liquid or a mass: to stream, to flow, to seep
    • tårene renner the tears are flowing
    • elva rant stri the river ran fast
    • en strøm av gjørme har rent ned fra vulkanen a stream of mud has flowed down from the volcano
    • smeltevannet renner ut i havet the meltwater runs out into the sea
    • melet rant ut av posen the flour poured out of the bag
    brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
    • rennende vann running water
  2. gi fra seg eller bli fylt av væske to give off liquid or to become filled with it
    • vannkranen rant the tap was running
    • nedbørsmåleren rant full the rain gauge filled up
    brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
    • rennende nese a runny nose
    • ha rennende øyne to have watery eyes
  3. fare av sted;
    gli     to rush along;
    to slide
    • steinblokkene renner ned fjellsiden the boulders are sliding down the mountainside
    • sinnet rant bort the anger drained away
  4. komme opp, stige opp;
    jamfør solrenningsunrise     to come up, to rise;
    compare solrenningsunrise
    • nå fjelltoppen idet sola renner to reach the summit just as the sun rises
    • det var ikke lenge til dagen rant it was not long before day broke
  5. spire, gro to sprout, to grow
    • åkeren vil snart renne the field will soon be sprouting
