tilknytte

verb

to connect · to affiliate

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

knytte til;
sette i forbindelse medto attach, to connect;
to affiliate with
  • bygget skal tilknyttes fjernvarmethe building is to be connected to district heating
  • huset er tilknyttet offentlig kloakkthe house is connected to the public sewer
  • klubben står tilknyttet Norges Fotballforbundthe club is affiliated to the Norwegian Football Federation
