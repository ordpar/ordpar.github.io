fostre

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt fóstra

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sørge for barn under oppveksten;
    fø opp;
    oppdra
    • landet har fostret mange store sønner
  2. skape (4)to bring about;
    to produce    , vekke (II, 2)figuratively: to make someone more aware of or enthusiastic about something;
    to give rise to;
    to evoke
    • fostre nye tanker
