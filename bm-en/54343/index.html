sleip

adjective
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt sleiprOld Norse sleipr

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som har en fuktig overflate det er vanskelig å få feste på;
    glatt (2)slippery, hålslippery    having a damp surface that is hard to get a grip on;
    slippery – compare glatt (2)slippery and hålslippery
    • bakken var sleip etter regnetthe ground was slippery after the rain
  2. slimeteslimyslimyslimete
    • fisken var sleip og glapp ut av hendenethe fish was slimy and slipped out of my hands
  3. som er slu og utspekulert;
    lur (IV, 1)sly, utkrøpenwily    who is cunning and calculating;
    slylur (IV, 1), wilyutkrøpen
    • han var en sleip fyr en ikke kunne stole påhe was a slippery character who could not be trusted
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • det var sleipt gjortthat was a sly thing to do
