tilgi

verb

to forgive · to excuse

Inflection

Etymology

etter lavtysk togevenafter Low German togeven

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. la krenkelse eller urett være glemtto let an offence or a wrong be forgotten
    • jeg tilgir det vonde du har gjort mot megI forgive the harm you have done me
    • de tilgav ham aldrithey never forgave him
  2. ikke ta ille opp;
    bere over med    not to take amiss;
    to bear with
    • tilgi at jeg går ut et øyeblikkexcuse me for stepping out for a moment
