hel II homograph 2, heil

adjective

whole, entire · intact, unbroken · completely, entirely (adverb helt)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt heillOld Norse heill

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ikke i stykker;
    uskadd, i stand    not in pieces, unbroken;
    undamaged, intact
    • koppen er helthe cup is intact
    • buksa er like helthe trousers are still in one piece
    • strømpene er helethe socks have no holes
    • det var ikke et helt egg igjen i kartongenthere wasn't one unbroken egg left in the carton
  2. av samme materiale;
    ublandet    of one and the same material;
    unmixed, pure
    • en genser i hel ulla sweater of pure wool
  3. i sin fulle størrelse;
    udelt, uredusert, fullstendig    at its full size;
    undivided, unreduced, complete
    • et helt brøda whole loaf
    • en hel timea whole hour
    • utslette en hel byto wipe out an entire city
    • steke fisken helto fry the fish whole
    • fortelle den hele og fulle sannhetto tell the whole and complete truth
    brukt som substantiv:used as a noun:
    • to halve er en heltwo halves make a whole
  4. svær, stor, dugeliggreat, big, considerable
    • det er en hel evighet sidenit's been a whole eternity since then
    • en hel hærskarea whole host
  5. ikke mindre ennno fewer than, as many as
    • det var hele 40 stykker av demthere were no fewer than 40 of them
  6. brukt som adverb: fullt ut, fullstendig, aldelesused as an adverb: fully, completely, entirely
    • helt sikkertabsolutely certain
    • begynne helt forfrato start again from the very beginning
    • bilen er helt nythe car is brand new
    • jeg er helt utkjørtI'm completely worn out
    • vente seg noe helt annetto expect something completely different
    • temperaturen var helt nede i –30 °Cthe temperature was all the way down to –30 °C
