glitre

verb

to glitter, to sparkle, to glisten · to shine, to excel, to distinguish oneself

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt glitraOld Norse glitra

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. skinne sterkt og blinkendeto shine brightly and flashingly, to glitter, to sparkle
    • det glitret i sjøenthe sea was glittering
    • juletreet glitrerthe Christmas tree is sparkling
  2. utmerke seg;
    glimre (2)to shine    to distinguish oneself;
