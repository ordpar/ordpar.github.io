sommertid

noun feminine or masculine

summertime, the summer season · daylight saving time, summer time · reduced working hours in summer

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. tid på året da det er sommerthe time of year when it is summer
    • den rolige sommertidenthe quiet summer season
    • kafeen setter bord og stoler ut for servering i sommertidenin the summer the café puts tables and chairs outside to serve customers
  2. det at tiden er skjøvet en time fram i sommerhalvåret for å utnytte sol- og dagslys bedre;
    motsatt vintertid (2)standard time    the practice of putting the clock forward an hour during the summer half of the year to make better use of the sun and the daylight;
    opposite of standard timevintertid (2)
    • stille klokka til sommertidto put the clock forward to summer time
  3. avtalefestet kortere arbeidstid om sommerencontractually agreed shorter working hours during the summer
