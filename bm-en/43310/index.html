organisere

verb

to organise

Inflection

Etymology

fra fransk, av organe ‘organ’from French, from organe ‘organ’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bygge opp eller innrette hensiktsmessig;
    legge til rette for;
    sette i system;
    ordne    to build up or arrange in a practical way;
    to make arrangements for;
    to put into a system;
    to sort out
    • de organiserte hjelpearbeidetthey organised the relief work
    • hun organiserer en fest på lørdagshe is organising a party on Saturday
    • motstandsbevegelsen er godt organisertthe resistance movement is well organised
  2. gjøre til eller bli medlem av en organisasjon, for eksempel en fagforening;
    gå sammen for å fremme felles interesser    to make into, or become, a member of an organisation, for example a trade union;
    to join together to advance common interests
    • de fleste er organisert i LOmost people are members of the LO
    • arbeiderne har organisert segthe workers have unionised
