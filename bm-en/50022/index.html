samspill, samspell

noun neuter

interplay · interaction · teamwork

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det at flere (musikere, skuespillere, idrettsfolk eller lignende) spiller sammen på en bestemt måtethe fact of several people (musicians, actors, sportspeople or the like) performing together in a particular way
    • samspillet mellom skuespillerne var godtthe interplay between the actors was good
  2. det at flere faktorer virker sammenthe fact of several factors working together
    • samspillet i naturen må ikke forstyrresthe balance of nature must not be disturbed
