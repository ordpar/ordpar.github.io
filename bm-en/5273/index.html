a proposal from the floor, a motion from the floor

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

særlig i politikk: forslag som blir satt fram av en representant (opprinnelig fra en representantbenk) under forhandlingene, uten at saken har blitt forberedt (av et styre eller en komité) especially in politics: a proposal put forward by a member (originally from the members' benches) during the debate, without the matter having been prepared beforehand (by a board or a committee)
