rekke IV homograph 4

verb

to hand, to pass · to hold out, to stick out

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt rekjaOld Norse rekja

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. strekke kroppsdel ut (i full lengde)to stretch out a part of the body (to its full length)
    • rekke opp håndento put one's hand up
    • hun hadde rakt tunge til demshe had stuck her tongue out at them
  2. gi noen noe med utstrakt armto pass someone something with an outstretched arm
    • rekk meg fatet!pass me the dish!
    • hun rakte boka til lærerenshe handed the book to the teacher
