nordover

prepositionpreposisjon

northwards · north (in a northerly direction)

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. i retning mot nord in the direction of the north
    • veien går nordover sletta the road runs northwards across the plain
  2. brukt som adverb: mot nord used as an adverb: northwards
    • vi skal nordover i sommer we are going north this summer
