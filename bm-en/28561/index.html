kanskje

adverb

perhaps · maybe

Etymology

av kunnecan, to be able to · to know (a skill or language) · may, might og skje (II)to happen · to take place · to occurfrom kunnecan, to be able to · to know (a skill or language) · may, might and skje (II)to happen · to take place · to occur

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. brukt for å uttrykke en mulighet;
    kan hendeperhaps, maybe    used to express a possibility;
    maybekan hende
    • de kommer kanskje i morgenperhaps they'll come tomorrow
  2. brukt for å bekrefte et utsagnused to confirm a statement
    • fikk jeg ikke rett, kanskje?wasn't I right, then?
