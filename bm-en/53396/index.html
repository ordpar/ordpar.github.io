skoleår

noun neuter

school year

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. del av året da skoler har undervisningthe part of the year when schools are in session
    • skoleåret varer fra ca. 20. august til ca. 20. junithe school year lasts from about 20 August to about 20 June
  2. år da en går på skoleyear in which one is at school
    • skoleårene var en sorgløs tidthe school years were a carefree time
