velte II homograph 2

verb

to overturn · to knock over · to come pouring

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt veltaOld Norse velta

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. falle eller rulle over ende;
    kantreto capsize    to fall or roll over;
    to capsizekantre
    • traktoren veltet i skråningenthe tractor overturned on the slope
    • steinene veltet nedover fjellsidenthe rocks tumbled down the mountainside
    • bølgene kom veltende inn mot landthe waves came rolling in towards the shore
  2. komme fram i stor mengde;
    strømme (I, 2)to stream, velle (1)to gush    to come forth in great quantity;
    to streamstrømme (I, 2), to gushvelle (1)
    • røyken veltet framthe smoke came billowing out
    • snøen bare veltet nedthe snow just came pouring down
    • en vond følelse veltet over mega bad feeling swept over me
    • sorgen veltet opp i hamgrief welled up in him
    • stanken veltet ut i rommetthe stench poured out into the room
  3. få til å rulle over ende;
    dytte (I, 1)to push, tippe (III, 2)to tip over, vippe (III, 4)to knock over    to make something roll over;
    to pushdytte (I, 1), to tip overtippe (III, 2), to knock overvippe (III, 4)
    • velte stein ut av åkerento roll stones out of the field

  4. skyve over påto shift onto

    spolereto ruin    to cause to fail;
    to ruinspolere

    • det dårlige været veltet alle planene for morgendagenthe bad weather wrecked all the plans for the next day
