løsning I homograph 1, løysing

noun masculine or feminine

a solution, an answer · an arrangement, a way of doing something · the loosening or releasing of something

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt leysing, av løysa ‘løse, frigi’; jamfør løseto solve · to loosen · to dissolveOld Norse leysing, from løysa 'to untie, to set free'; compare løseto solve · to loosen · to dissolve
