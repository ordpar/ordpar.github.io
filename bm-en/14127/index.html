feile

verb

to err, to fail · to do wrong · to be the matter with, to ail

Inflection

Etymology

gjennom lavtysk, fra fransk faillir; samme opprinnelse som fallereto fail, to go wrong · to decline, to go downhill · to go bankruptthrough Low German, from French faillir; same origin as fallereto fail, to go wrong · to decline, to go downhill · to go bankrupt

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ta feil;
    mislykkesto fail · to go wrong, to turn out badly    to be mistaken;
    to failmislykkes
    • han har prøvd og feilet mange gangerhe has tried and failed many times
    • å feile er menneskeligto err is human
  2. begå et feiltrinn (2)a transgression;
    handle uriktig    to commit a transgressionfeiltrinn (2);
    to act wrongly
    • han feilet, men har sonet straffenhe did wrong, but he has served his sentence
  3. være i veien medto be the matter with, to ail
    • hva er det som feiler deg?what is the matter with you?
    • det er alltid noe som feiler hennethere is always something wrong with her
