innskrenke

verb

to restrict, to curtail, to cut back

Inflection

Etymology

etter tysk einschränken; beslektet med skrankecounter, barrierafter German einschränken; related to skrankecounter, barrier

Senses and Example Sentences

gjøre mindre;
redusere (1)to reduceto make smaller;
to reduceredusere (1)
  • fabrikken måtte innskrenkethe factory had to cut back
  • innskrenke presidentens maktto curtail the president's power
