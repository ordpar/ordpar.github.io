fersk

adjective

fresh · new

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk versch, norrønt ferskr; samme opprinnelse som frisk (II)healthy, well · fresh · brisk, livelyfrom Low German versch, Old Norse ferskr; same origin as frisk (II)healthy, well · fresh · brisk, lively

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som ikke har begynt å tape seg, tørke, visne eller lignende;
    nylig hendt, laget eller tilberedt;
    ny (II, 1)new, frisk (II, 1)fresh, unspoiled    which has not begun to deteriorate, dry out, wither or the like;
    recently happened, made or prepared;
    newny (II, 1), fresh, unspoiledfrisk (II, 1)
    • fersk malingwet paint
    • ferske bollerfreshly baked buns
    • ferske sporfresh tracks
    • ferske nyheterthe latest news
  2. som ikke er salt eller saltetwhich is not salt or salted
    • fersk sildfresh herring
    • ferskt vannfresh water
  3. uerfaren, ny;
    nybakt (2)newly minted    inexperienced, untried;
    newly mintednybakt (2)
    • ferske fjesfresh faces
    • han var fersk i fagethe was new to the trade

Set phrases

på fersk gjerning mens en gjør noe galt;
på ferskenin the actwhile one is doing something wrong;
in the actpå fersken Eng. phrasered-handed
  • butikktyven ble tatt på fersk gjerningthe shoplifter was caught red-handed
