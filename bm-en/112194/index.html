felle ut

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: felleto fell, to bring down · to shed · to hand down (a verdict)
  1. skille ut to separate out Eng. phraseto precipitate out
    • oppløsningen i væsken ble felt ut the dissolved matter in the liquid was precipitated out
  2. dra ut to pull out Eng. phraseto fold out
    • felle ut bladet på lommekniven to fold out the blade of the pocket knife
