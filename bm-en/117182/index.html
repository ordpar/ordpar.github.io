henge sammen

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: hengeto hang · to hang around, sammentogether
  1. ha alle deler festet sammen til en helhet to have all the parts fastened together into a whole Eng. phraseto hold together
    • skoene hang så vidt sammen the shoes were barely holding together
  2. holde sammen to keep together Eng. phraseto stick together
    • søstrene hang alltid sammen the sisters always stuck together
  3. stå i forbindelse med;
    høre sammen     to be connected;
    to belong together     Eng. phraseto hang together
    • hvordan kan dette henge sammen? how does this all fit together?
