granske

verb

to scrutinise, to examine closely · to investigate (officially)

Inflection

Etymology

av grann (II)thin med betydning ‘tydelig’from grann (II)thin in the sense ‘clear’

Senses and Example Sentences

undersøke, saumfare, gå grundig over, igjennom, undersøke vitenskapeligto examine, to scrutinise, to go over thoroughly, to study scientifically
  • legen gransket røntgenbildene nøyethe doctor examined the X-rays closely
  • hun gransket temperaturmålerneshe scrutinised the temperature gauges
  • bli gransket fra topp til tåto be scrutinised from head to toe
  • myndighetene gransker episodenthe authorities are investigating the incident
