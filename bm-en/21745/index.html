gående

adjective

walking · on foot · a pedestrian

Inflection

Etymology

presens partisipp av gåto walk · to go · to run, to work (of a machine) · to turn outpresent participle of gåto walk · to go · to run, to work (of a machine) · to turn out

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som gårwalking, on foot
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som nærgåendecoming too close; intrusive, oppegåendeswitched-on, sharp · up and about, ambulatory, pågåendeongoing · pushy
  2. brukt som substantiv: fotgjengera pedestrianused as a noun: a pedestrianfotgjenger
    • gående og syklendepedestrians and cyclists

Set phrases

holde gående holde i drift; drive på medto keep in operation; to carry on with Eng. phraseto keep something going
  • de holdt det gåande i mange årthey kept it going for many years
  • holde driften gåendeto keep the business running
