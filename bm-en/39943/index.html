mål I homograph 1

noun neuter

goal, aim · measurement, dimension · target · finish line

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt málOld Norse mál

Senses and Example Sentences

    • skredderne tok mål til den nye dressenthe tailors took measurements for the new suit

    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som kubikkmåla cubic measure, a unit of volume · cubic capacity, volume in cubic units, kryddermåla spice measure (unit of volume, 1 ml or 1/5 teaspoon) · a spice measuring spoon
