vidunder

noun neuter
Inflection

Etymology

av ved (II)by, next to · at (a place, institution or time) · by means of, through og under (I)miracle · wonderfrom ved (II)by, next to · at (a place, institution or time) · by means of, through and under (I)miracle · wonder

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. noe uvanlig vakkert, dyktig, fullkomment eller lignende;
    under (I)miracle · wonder    something unusually beautiful, skilful, perfect or the like;
    a miracleunder (I)
    • naturens mange vidunderethe many wonders of nature
    • været var et vidunder hele helgenthe weather was a marvel all weekend
  2. enestående og fantastisk person, gjenstand eller hendingan outstanding and amazing person, thing or event
    • regnemaskinen var et teknisk vidunderthe calculating machine was a technical marvel
    • han er et vidunder til å arbeidehe is a marvel when it comes to work
    • hun er et rent vidunder på fotballbanenshe is an absolute wonder on the football pitch
    • et lite vidunder av en platea little marvel of a record
