preke II homograph 2

verb

to preach · to chat

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt prédika, fra latin praedicare ‘utrope, utsi, forkynne’; av prediketo preachOld Norse prédika, from Latin praedicare ‘to proclaim, to declare, to preach’; from prediketo preach

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. holde preken (1)a sermon;
    forkynne    to deliver a sermonpreken (1);
    to preach
    • presten prekte over søndagens tekstthe priest preached on Sunday's text
  2. formane til;
    propagandere for;
    agitere    to exhort to;
    to propagandise for;
    to agitate
    • preke avholdssakto preach temperance
    • preke noen ørene fulle av noeto talk someone's ear off about something
  3. prate, snakke sammento chat, to talk together
