nedover

preposition

down · downwards

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. langs, over og i retning ned;
    nedetterdown    along, over and in a downward direction;
    downnedetter
    • gå nedover gatato walk down the street
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • steinen ruller nedoverthe stone rolls downwards
  2. brukt som adverb: søroverused as an adverb: southwards
    • nedover i Europadown south in Europe
  3. i overført betydning: dårligere (med helse, økonomi, popularitet eller lignende)figuratively: worse (in health, finances, popularity or the like)
    • det går nedover med demthings are going downhill for them
