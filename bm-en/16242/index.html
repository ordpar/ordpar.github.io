forflytte

verbverb

to move (something) from one place to another · to transfer (someone to another post)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

flytte fra et sted til et annet to move from one place to another
