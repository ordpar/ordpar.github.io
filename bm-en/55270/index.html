snyte I homograph 1

verbverb

to cheat, to swindle, to con · to blow one's nose

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt snýta, sammenheng med snute (I)a snout Old Norse snýta, connected with snute (I)a snout

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. rense nesa for slim ved å blåse kraftig ut to clear mucus from the nose by blowing hard
    • trekke fram et lommetørkle for å snyte seg to pull out a handkerchief to blow one's nose
  2. lure til seg penger eller andre goder som en ikke har rett til;
    svindleto swindle, lure (I, 5)to fool;
    jamfør snytt (1)cheated     to get money or other benefits one has no right to by trickery;
    to swindlesvindle, to foollure (I, 5);
    compare snytt (1)cheated
    • snyte på skatten to cheat on one's taxes
    • gateselgeren snøt oss skikkelig the street vendor well and truly conned us
    • jeg følte meg snytt og bedratt I felt cheated and deceived
    • Norge ble snytt for seieren Norway was robbed of the victory
