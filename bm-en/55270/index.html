snyte I homograph 1

verb

to cheat, to swindle, to con · to blow one's nose

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt snýta, sammenheng med snute (I)a snoutOld Norse snýta, connected with snute (I)a snout

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. rense nesa for slim ved å blåse kraftig utto clear mucus from the nose by blowing hard
    • trekke fram et lommetørkle for å snyte segto pull out a handkerchief to blow one's nose
  2. lure til seg penger eller andre goder som en ikke har rett til;
    svindleto swindle, lure (I, 5)to fool;
    jamfør snytt (1)cheated    to get money or other benefits one has no right to by trickery;
    to swindlesvindle, to foollure (I, 5);
    compare snytt (1)cheated
    • snyte på skattento cheat on one's taxes
    • gateselgeren snøt oss skikkeligthe street vendor well and truly conned us
    • jeg følte meg snytt og bedrattI felt cheated and deceived
    • Norge ble snytt for seierenNorway was robbed of the victory
