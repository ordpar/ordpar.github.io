vanke

verb

to hang out · to frequent · to be on offer

Inflection

Etymology

trolig fra lavtysk; samme opprinnelse som norrønt vakka ‘reke, drive omkring’probably from Low German; same origin as Old Norse vakka ‘to drift, to wander about’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ha sin gang;
    stadig komme innom et sted;
    ferdes    to have one's regular haunts;
    to keep dropping in at a place;
    to move about
    • jeg vanket mye på kafeerI spent a lot of time in cafés
    • de vanker i et kunstnermiljøthey move in artistic circles
    • han har vanket her i huset i årevishe has been coming to this house for years
    • de vanker sammenthey hang out together
  2. være å få;
    bli å få    to be on offer;
    to be coming one's way
    • det vanket brus og bollerthere were fizzy drinks and buns
    • det vanker julingthere's a beating coming
