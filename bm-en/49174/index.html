russ I homograph 1

noun masculine

a school leaver (in the Norwegian spring graduation celebration)

Inflection

Etymology

trolig fra latin depositurus ‘som skal avlegge (hornene)’, av deponereprobably from Latin depositurus ‘who is to shed (the horns)’, from deponere

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. avgangselev ved videregående skole (som feirer i vårmånedene)final-year pupil at an upper-secondary school (who celebrates in the spring months)
    • russ som fester høylyttgraduating pupils partying loudly
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som blårussbusiness-stream school leaver (blue cap), rødrussgeneral-studies school leaver (red cap)
  2. i bestemt form entall: alle russ i et kull (i et område, på en skole)in the definite singular: all the leavers in one year group (in an area, at a school)
    • russen har oppført seg fint i årthe leavers have behaved well this year
  3. om eldre forhold: avgangselev ved gymnas (1)an upper-secondary academic school, handelsgymnasa commercial upper-secondary school eller realskolea lower-secondary schoolhistorical: a final-year pupil at an upper-secondary academic schoolgymnas (1), a commercial upper-secondary schoolhandelsgymnas or a lower-secondary schoolrealskole
  4. om eldre forhold: student i første året etter studenteksamenthe school-leaving examinationhistorical: a student in the first year after the school-leaving examinationstudenteksamen
