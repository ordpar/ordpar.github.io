østover, austover

preposition

eastwards, east

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i retning mot østin a direction towards the east
    • de seilte østover fjordenthey sailed eastwards up the fjord
  2. brukt som adverb: i østlig retning, mot østused as an adverb: in an easterly direction, towards the east
    • dra østoverto head east
