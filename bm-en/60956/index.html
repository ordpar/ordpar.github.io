tilstrømming

noun feminine or masculine

tilstrømning

noun masculine or feminine

an influx, an inflow · a turnout

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør strømme (I)to stream, to pour · to flockcompare strømme (I)to stream, to pour · to flock

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det at noe strømmer til;
    strøm av væske, varer, kapital eller annet (til et sted)    the fact of something flowing in;
    a flow of liquid, goods, capital or the like (to a place)
    • tilstrømming av en væskethe inflow of a liquid
    • tilstrømming av utenlandsk kapitalan influx of foreign capital
  2. det at mange strømmer til;
    stort frammøteturnout    an influx of people;
    a large turnoutframmøte
    • tilstrømmingen var så stor at mange måtte ståthe turnout was so large that many had to stand
