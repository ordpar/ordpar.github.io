mening, meining

noun feminine or masculine

meaning, sense · opinion, view · point, purpose

Inflection

Etymology

av meneto think, to be of the opinion · to mean · to intendfrom meneto think, to be of the opinion · to mean · to intend

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. tanke, innhold, betydning (i et ord, en ytring, en handling eller lignende);
    sammenheng, plan, hensikt    the thought, content or significance (of a word, an utterance, an action or the like);
    sense, plan, purpose
    • skjønne meningen i det som blir sagtto grasp the meaning of what is being said
    • er det meningen at det skal være slik?is it meant to be like this?
    • det som gir livet meningwhat gives life meaning
    • ta alt i beste meningto take everything in the best possible spirit
    • det er vel en mening med detthere must be some point to it
    • det var ikke meningen å skremme degI didn't mean to frighten you
    • det er meningen å rive denne veggenthe plan is to tear down this wall
  2. oppfatning, overbevisning;
    syn (I, 7)view    opinion, conviction;
    viewsyn (I, 7)
    • skifte meningto change one's mind
    • etter min meningin my opinion
    • det er min oppriktige meningthat is my sincere opinion
    • gjøre seg opp en mening om noeto form an opinion about something
    • det er bare én mening om den sakenthere is only one opinion on that matter
