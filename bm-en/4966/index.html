begripe

verb

to understand, to grasp, to comprehend

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk ‘gripe om, fatte’from Low German ‘to grasp, to comprehend’

Senses and Example Sentences

forstå betydning eller sammenheng;
skjønneto understand · to realise · to get, fatteto grasp, to understand · to take, to form (a decision, hope) · to catch fireto take in a meaning or a connection;
