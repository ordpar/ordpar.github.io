grunn I homograph 1

noun masculine

reason, grounds · ground, land · foundation, basis

Inflection

Etymology

sammenfall av norrønt grunnr maskulinum ‘bunn, grunn, grunt sted i vannet’ og grund femininum ‘mark, slette’a merging of Old Norse grunnr masculine 'bottom, ground, a shallow spot in the water' and grund feminine 'field, plain'
