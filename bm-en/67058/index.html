vedgå

verb

to admit, to acknowledge · to confess to · to concern (someone)

Inflection

Etymology

av ved (II)by, next to · at (a place, institution or time) · by means of, through og gåto walk · to go · to run, to work (of a machine) · to turn outfrom ved (II)by, next to · at (a place, institution or time) · by means of, through and gåto walk · to go · to run, to work (of a machine) · to turn out

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gå med på (at noe er sant);
    innrømme, tilstå    to agree (that something is true);
    to admit, to confess
    • vedgå at en har gjort noeto admit that one has done something
    • jeg må vedgå at jeg er reddI must admit that I'm afraid
    • vedgå et drapto confess to a killing
  2. vedkommeto concernto concernvedkomme
    • dette vedgår ikke megthis doesn't concern me
