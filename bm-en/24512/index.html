humre

verb
Inflection

Etymology

beslektet med humle (I)a bumblebeerelated to humle (I)a bumblebee

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. om hest: knegge dempet med lukket munnof a horse: to nicker, to whinny softly with the mouth closed
    • hestene humret mot oss da vi kom i stalldørathe horses nickered at us when we appeared in the stable doorway
  2. om folk: le dempetof people: to laugh quietly, to chuckle
    • karene humrer og lerthe men are chuckling and laughing
