smidig

adjective

supple · flexible · smooth

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk smidich, opprinnelig ‘som er lett å smi’from Low German smidich, originally 'easy to forge'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som lett lar seg bearbeidethat is easily worked or shaped
    • smidig jernmalleable iron
  2. myk, spenstigsupple, lithe
    • være smidig som en kattto be as lithe as a cat
  3. som lett tilpasser seg eller kan tilpassesthat adapts easily or can easily be adapted
    • en smidig politikeran accommodating politician
    • et smidig systema flexible system
  4. lett, eleganteasy, elegant
    • smidig og god språkføringsmooth and effective use of language
