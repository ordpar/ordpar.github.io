bygdelag

noun neuter

a rural district, a group of neighbouring villages · a home-district society

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. krets, samling av bygder som hører sammen;
    jamfør lag (4)local circle of people    a district, a cluster of rural communities that belong together;
    compare lag (4)local circle of people
    • han var kjent rundt om i mange bygdelaghe was known all around in many rural districts
  2. forening i en større by av folk fra samme bygd eller landsdel;
    jamfør lag (6)association    a society in a larger town for people from the same rural community or region;
    compare lag (6)association
    • bygdelagene i Oslothe regional home-district societies in Oslo
