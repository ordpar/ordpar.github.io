fornye

verb

to renew · to replace

Inflection

Etymology

av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess og ny (II)new · another, further · fresh, recentfrom for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess and ny (II)new · another, further · fresh, recent

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre ny igjen, bytte utto make new again, to replace
    • fornye møblementetto get new furniture
    • fornye maskineneto replace the machinery
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • etter fornyet overveielseafter renewed consideration
    • med fornyet kraft la de i veithey set off with renewed strength
    • se på noen med fornyet interesseto look at someone with renewed interest
  2. etablere på nytt;
    gjøre gyldig på nytt;
    forlenge (1)to extend    to establish anew;
    to make valid again;
    to extendforlenge (1)
    • fornye et vennskapto renew a friendship
    • fornye en kontraktto renew a contract
    • passet må fornyes hvert tiende årthe passport must be renewed every ten years
