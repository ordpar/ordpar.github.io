stappe II homograph 2

verb

to stuff, to cram · to mash

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt stappa; samme opprinnelse som stampe (II)to tamp downOld Norse stappa; same origin as stampe (II)to tamp down

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. pakke hardt sammen og fylle eller tette noeto pack tightly together and fill or plug something
    • de stappet mose i veggsprekkenethey stuffed moss into the cracks in the wall
    • stappe klærne i ryggsekkento cram the clothes into the rucksack
  2. fylle med noe en pakker hardt sammen;
    stoppe (I, 1)to stuff    to fill with something one packs in tightly;
    to stuffstoppe (I, 1)
    • stappe pipato pack a pipe
  3. putte eller stikke gjennom en åpningto put or push through an opening
    • han stapper hendene i lommahe stuffs his hands in his pockets
  4. røre sammen;
    knuse (1)to crush, mose (III)to mash · to purée, to crush to a pulp    to mix together;
    to crushknuse (1), to mashmose (III)
    • stappe potetene i sausento mash the potatoes into the sauce
