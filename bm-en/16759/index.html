forråde

verb

to betray · to give away, to reveal (unintentionally)

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk ‘råde falskt’, jamfør norrønt forráða; av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess og rå (V)to prevail, to reign · to be in charge · to advisefrom Low German ‘to advise falsely’, compare Old Norse forráða; from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess and rå (V)to prevail, to reign · to be in charge · to advise

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. opptre troløst overfor;
    sviketo betray    to act treacherously towards;
    to betraysvike

    • bli forrådt av sin egen brorto be betrayed by one's own brother
  2. gjøre kjent;
    røpeto give away · to reveal · to betray    to make known;
    to give awayrøpe
    • øynene forrådte hennes raseriher eyes betrayed her fury
    • ikke en mine forrådte hva han tenktenot a flicker of expression betrayed what he was thinking
