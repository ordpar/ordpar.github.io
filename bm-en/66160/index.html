vakker

adjective

beautiful · lovely (of weather, a day, a view) · fine, noble (of a deed or words)

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk, samme opprinnelse som norrønt vakr; jamfør vak (II)awake · alert, vigilant, attentivefrom Low German, same origin as Old Norse vakr; see vak (II)awake · alert, vigilant, attentive
