unnta

verb

to except, to leave out of account · to exempt, to withhold

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør norrønt taka undan ‘ta bort’compare Old Norse taka undan ‘to take away’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. se bort fra;
    ikke la telle med;
    jamfør unntattexcept · apart from    to disregard;
    not to count something in;
    compare unntattexcept · apart from
    • når en unntar et par kommafeil, var avskriften korrektleaving aside a couple of comma errors, the transcript was correct
  2. holde unnato withhold
    • unnta opplysninger om en søkerto withhold information about an applicant
    • dokumentene var unntatt offentlighetthe documents were exempt from public disclosure
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • unntatte opplysningerwithheld information
