synke

verbverb

to sink · to fall, to drop · to slump, to collapse

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

samme opprinnelse som søkke (II)to sink same origin as søkke (II)to sink

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. sige eller gli nedover;
    søkke (II, 1)to sink     to settle or slide downwards;
    to sinksøkke (II, 1)
    • hjulene sank ned i søla the wheels sank into the mud
    • grumset synker til bunns the sediment settles at the bottom
    • synke dypt ned i sofaen to sink deep into the sofa
    • båten sprang lekk og sank the boat sprang a leak and went down
  2. bli lavere to get lower
    • priser og lønninger sank prices and wages fell
    • nivået har sunket betraktelig the level has dropped considerably
    • temperaturen synker the temperature is falling
    • sangen steg og sank the singing rose and fell
    brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
    • synkende temperaturer falling temperatures
  3. sige sammen;
    falle (II, 1)to fall, segneto collapse     to slump down;
    to fallfalle (II, 1), to collapsesegne
    • synke sammen to slump over
    • synke i kne to sink to one's knees
    • byggverket sank i grus the building collapsed into rubble
  4. gli inn eller trenge seg dypt inn to slide in or force its way deep in
    • kniven sank inn i ryggen hans the knife sank into his back
  5. fortape seg to lose oneself
    • synke i drømmer to drift off into dreams
  6. fordøye (1)to digest to digestfordøye (1)
    • synke maten to digest one's food
