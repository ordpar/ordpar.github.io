synke

verb

to sink · to fall, to drop · to slump, to collapse

Inflection

Etymology

samme opprinnelse som søkke (II)to sinksame origin as søkke (II)to sink

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sige eller gli nedover;
    søkke (II, 1)to sink    to settle or slide downwards;
    to sinksøkke (II, 1)
    • hjulene sank ned i sølathe wheels sank into the mud
    • grumset synker til bunnsthe sediment settles at the bottom
    • synke dypt ned i sofaento sink deep into the sofa
    • båten sprang lekk og sankthe boat sprang a leak and went down
  2. bli lavereto get lower
    • priser og lønninger sankprices and wages fell
    • nivået har sunket betrakteligthe level has dropped considerably
    • temperaturen synkerthe temperature is falling
    • sangen steg og sankthe singing rose and fell
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • synkende temperaturerfalling temperatures
  3. sige sammen;
    falle (II, 1)to fall, segneto collapse    to slump down;
    to fallfalle (II, 1), to collapsesegne
    • synke sammento slump over
    • synke i kneto sink to one's knees
    • byggverket sank i grusthe building collapsed into rubble
  4. gli inn eller trenge seg dypt innto slide in or force its way deep in
    • kniven sank inn i ryggen hansthe knife sank into his back
  5. fortape segto lose oneself
    • synke i drømmerto drift off into dreams
  6. fordøye (1)to digestto digestfordøye (1)
    • synke matento digest one's food
