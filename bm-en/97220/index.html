herpesere

verb
Inflection

Etymology

trolig av harpe (II)riddle · screen, coarse sieve

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ødeleggeto ruin · to break · to destroy, skamfere
    • de herpeserte bilen
  2. herje (2)to make a racket, to bang about;
    to treat carelessly;
    to dominate
    • herpesere med noen
