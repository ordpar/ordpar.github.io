beholde

verb

to keep, to retain

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

fortsette å ha;
ikke gi slipp påto go on having, to keep;
not to let go of
  • beholde jobbento keep one's job
  • beholde maktento stay in power
  • beholde kontrollento keep control
  • beholde livetto survive
  • han fikk lov til å beholde gårdenhe was allowed to keep the farm
  • et skjørt som beholder fasongena skirt that keeps its shape
  • du kan bare beholde støvlene påyou can just keep your boots on
  • jeg beholdt mistanken for meg selvI kept my suspicion to myself
