pine III homograph 3

verbverb

to torment, to torture · to force, to squeeze (oneself)

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. plage, mishandle, torturere to torment, to maltreat, to torture
    • pine og plage noen to torment and harass someone
  2. tvinge, presse to force, to squeeze
    • pine seg inn i kjolen to squeeze oneself into the dress
