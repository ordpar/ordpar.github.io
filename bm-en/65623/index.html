utpressing

noun feminine or masculine

utpresning

noun masculine or feminine

blackmail, extortion · pressing out, extraction (of juice)

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å presse penger av noen ved trusler eller lignendethe act of extorting money from someone by means of threats or the like, blackmail
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som pengeutpressingextortion of money
  2. det å presse ut, for eksempel saft av frukt og bærthe act of pressing out, for example juice from fruit and berries
