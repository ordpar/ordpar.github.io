renne I homograph 1

noun masculine or feminine

a channel · a chute · a gully

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt rennaOld Norse renna

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. lang, smal fordypning som samler og leder bort vann eller annen væske;
    grøft, avløp    a long, narrow hollow that collects and carries away water or other liquid;
    a ditch, a drain
    • vannet ble ledet i rennerthe water was led away in channels
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som gjødselrennea dung channel, stikkrenneculvert, takrennegutter
  2. lang, smal fordypning i jordoverflaten (undersjøisk eller på land)a long, narrow depression in the earth's surface (undersea or on land)
    • en renne i fjelleta gully in the mountainside
  3. lei for båta fairway for boats
    • båtene følger en renne inn til kaiathe boats follow a channel in to the quay
  4. råk (I)an open channel in the icean open channel in the iceråk (I)
    • en renne i isena channel in the ice
