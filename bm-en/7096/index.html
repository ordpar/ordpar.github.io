borti

preposition

against · in contact with · over in

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i kontakt med, i berøring medin contact with, touching
    • han har det med å dumpe borti tinghe has a habit of bumping into things
    • hun har vært borti litt av hvertshe has come across all sorts of things
    • komme borti noento brush against someone
    som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • det verste jeg har vært bortithe worst thing I have ever come across
    • sneie så vidt bortito graze it ever so slightly
  2. borte iover in, off in
    • langt borti skogenfar off in the woods

Set phrases

legge seg bort i/borti blande seg (utidig) inn ito meddle (unduly) in, to interfere in Eng. phraseto stick one's nose in
  • de hadde vett til å ikke legge seg borti andres sakerthey had the sense not to meddle in other people's affairs
  • hun la seg alltid bort i andres problemshe was always interfering in other people's problems
