stampe II homograph 2

verb
Inflection

Etymology

samme opprinnelse som stappe (II)to stuff, to cram · to mashthe same origin as stappe (II)to stuff, to cram · to mash

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. arbeide noe tett og fast sammen med tramping, støting eller bankingto work something close and firm together by treading, pounding or beating
    • jordveggene var stampetthe earth walls were rammed
    • han stamper jorden godt nedhe tamps the soil down well
    • stampe grøtto pound porridge
  2. behandle ulltøy for å gjøre det tettere;
    valketo full (woollen cloth)    to treat woollen cloth to make it denser;
    to full (woollen cloth)valke
    • stampe vadmelto full homespun woollen cloth
  3. tråkke, trampe (II, 1)to stamp;
    støte foten hardt mot underlaget    to tread, to stamptrampe (II, 1);
    to thrust the foot hard against the ground
    • stampe i golvetto stamp on the floor
    • gå og stampe i høy snøto trudge through deep snow
  4. om fartøy: hogge (3)to pitch med framstavnen i sjøen uten å komme seg framoverof a vessel: to pitchhogge (3) with the bow in the sea without making headway
    • båten stampet i sjøenthe boat pitched in the sea
  5. i bridge: melde stampemeldinga sacrifice bidin bridge: to make a sacrifice bidstampemelding
