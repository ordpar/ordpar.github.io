verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

samme opprinnelse som stappe (II)to stuff, to cram · to mash the same origin as stappe (II)to stuff, to cram · to mash

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. arbeide noe tett og fast sammen med tramping, støting eller banking to work something close and firm together by treading, pounding or beating
    • jordveggene var stampet the earth walls were rammed
    • han stamper jorden godt ned he tamps the soil down well
    • stampe grøt to pound porridge
  2. behandle ulltøy for å gjøre det tettere;
    valketo full (woollen cloth)     to treat woollen cloth to make it denser;
    to full (woollen cloth)valke
    • stampe vadmel to full homespun woollen cloth
  3. tråkke, trampe (II, 1)to stamp;
    støte foten hardt mot underlaget     to tread, to stamptrampe (II, 1);
    to thrust the foot hard against the ground
    • stampe i golvet to stamp on the floor
    • gå og stampe i høy snø to trudge through deep snow
  4. om fartøy: hogge (3)to pitch med framstavnen i sjøen uten å komme seg framover of a vessel: to pitchhogge (3) with the bow in the sea without making headway
    • båten stampet i sjøen the boat pitched in the sea
  5. i bridge: melde stampemeldinga sacrifice bid in bridge: to make a sacrifice bidstampemelding
