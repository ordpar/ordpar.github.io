lik III homograph 3

adjectiveadjektiv

like, similar to · equal (to)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt líkr, opprinnelig ‘som har samme form’ Old Norse líkr, originally ‘having the same form’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. som minner om eller ligner på noen andre eller noe annet;
    ensartetuniform, homogeneous, of the same kind     which resembles or is similar to someone or something else;
    uniformensartet
    • like barn leker best birds of a feather flock together
    • hun er lik faren sin she is like her father
    • han er svært lik seg på bildet he looks very much like himself in the photo
  2. som stemmer helt overens;
    identisk med     which corresponds exactly;
    identical to
    • en nautisk mil er lik 1852 m a nautical mile equals 1852 m
    • formuen min er lik null my fortune amounts to nothing
    • alle er like for loven everyone is equal before the law
    • lik lønn for likt arbeid equal pay for equal work
    brukt som adverb used as an adverb
    • dele likt to share equally
  3. særlig i komparativ og superlativ: bra (1)good, dugende, god especially in the comparative and superlative: goodbra (1), capable, fine
    • hun tok den likeste hun fant she took the best one she found
    • kameraten var ikke stort likere his friend was not much better

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

holde ved like
  1. holde i god stand ved å reparere skader, utbedre slitasje og lignende;
    hindre forfall     to keep in good condition by repairing damage, making good wear and tear and the like;
    to prevent deterioration     Eng. phraseto keep in good repair
    • holde boligen ved like to maintain one's home
  2. sikre at noe fortsatt eksisterer;
    opprettholde     to ensure that something continues to exist;
    to maintain     Eng. phraseto keep up
    • holde kontakten ved like to keep in touch
    • de holdt tradisjonen ved like they kept the tradition alive
    • jeg prøver å holde franskkunnskapene ved like I try to keep my French from getting rusty
