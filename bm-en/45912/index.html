prate

verb

to talk, to chat

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. snakke gemyttlig sammen;
    snakke om;
    passiareto chat, drøfte    to talk together in a genial way;
    to talk about;
    to chatpassiare, to discuss
    • vi pratet om gamle dagerwe talked about the old days
    • prate politikkto talk politics
    • hva prater du om nå?what are you talking about now?
    • jeg har ikke pratet med banken ennåI haven't spoken to the bank yet
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som koseprateto have a cosy chat, småprateto make small talk
  2. snakke uten særlig sammenheng med virkeligheten;
    tøve, vrøvle, skryte    to say things with little connection to reality;
    to talk nonsense, to drivel, to boast
    • å, du prater!oh, you're talking rubbish!
