prate

verbverb

to talk, to chat

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. snakke gemyttlig sammen;
    snakke om;
    passiareto chat, drøfte     to talk together in a genial way;
    to talk about;
    to chatpassiare, to discuss
    • vi pratet om gamle dager we talked about the old days
    • prate politikk to talk politics
    • hva prater du om nå? what are you talking about now?
    • jeg har ikke pratet med banken ennå I haven't spoken to the bank yet
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som koseprateto have a cosy chat, småprateto make small talk
  2. snakke uten særlig sammenheng med virkeligheten;
    tøve, vrøvle, skryte     to say things with little connection to reality;
    to talk nonsense, to drivel, to boast
    • å, du prater! oh, you're talking rubbish!
