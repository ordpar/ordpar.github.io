lønne

verb

to pay · to reward

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt launa; av lønn (I)pay, salary, wages · reward · just desertsOld Norse launa; from lønn (I)pay, salary, wages · reward · just deserts

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi i vederlagto give in return
    • lønne noen for noeto reward someone for something
  2. gi lønn;
    betale for arbeid;
    jamfør lønnetpaid    to pay a wage;
    to pay for work;
    compare lønnetpaid
    • lønne noen godtto pay someone well
    • bedriften lønnet dem dårligthe company paid them badly

Set phrases

lønne seg
  1. gi (god) fortjeneste;
    gi rimelig vederlag    to yield a (good) profit;
    to give a reasonable return     Eng. phraseto pay off
    • driften lønner seg ikkethe business is not profitable
  2. være klokt;
    være umaken verdt    to be wise;
    to be worth the trouble     Eng. phraseit pays to
    • det lønner seg ikke å bli sintthere is nothing to be gained by getting angry
