spann II homograph 2

noun neuter

team (of draught animals)

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk, av spannen ‘spenne’from Low German, from spannen 'to harness'

Senses and Example Sentences

to eller flere trekkdyr spent for vogn, slede eller redskaptwo or more draught animals harnessed to a carriage, sleigh or implement
used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som firspanna team of four draught animals, hundespanna dog team, tospanna carriage drawn by two draught animals

Set phrases

gå i spann
  1. trekke sammento pull together
  2. arbeide i lag;
    samarbeide    to work as a team;
    to cooperate     Eng. phraseto work in tandem
