si III homograph 3

verb

to say · to tell

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt segjaOld Norse segja

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. uttrykke med ord eller tale;
    ytre (III)to say, uttale (II, 1)to utter    to express in words or speech;
    to sayytre (III), to utteruttale (II, 1)
    • si noe morsomtto say something funny
    • hun sier at det regnershe says it's raining
    • sa du noe?did you say something?
    • han sa god natt til osshe said good night to us
    • de har knapt sagt et ord i hele dagthey've barely said a word all day
  2. fortelle;
    meddele, varsle    to tell;
    to inform, to notify
    • folk sier han drikkerpeople say he drinks
    • ikke si det til noendon't tell anyone
    • det blir sagt at det spøker herit's said that this place is haunted
  3. uttrykke (2)to express;
    bety    to expressuttrykke (2);
    to mean
    • hva vil kunstneren si med dette kunstverket?what does the artist mean by this artwork?
    • navnet sier meg ingentingthe name means nothing to me
  4. framføre en personlig vurdering;
    erklære, hevde, mene    to offer a personal assessment;
    to declare, to claim, to think
    • jeg kan ikke si jeg liker detI can't say I like it
    • hva sier du til dette?what do you say to this?
    • hva vil folk si?what will people say?
  5. avtale (I, 1)to agree on, fastsetteto agree onavtale (I, 1), to settle
    • la oss si i morgen klokka nilet's say tomorrow at nine
    • da sier vi det sånnthen that's settled
  6. gi lyd;
    sende ut en lyd    to make a sound;
    to emit a sound
    • pang, sa pistolenbang, went the pistol
    • kua sier møthe cow goes moo
  7. spå;
    varsle    to predict;
    to foretell
    • det gikk som hun sait turned out just as she predicted
    • var det ikke det jeg sadidn't I say so
    • det er ikke godt å si hva hun forstodit's hard to say what she understood
