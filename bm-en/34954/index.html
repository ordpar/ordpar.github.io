lette II homograph 2

verbverb

to ease · to lighten · to lift

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt létta; jamfør letteasy · light (in weight) · slight, slightly Old Norse létta; compare letteasy · light (in weight) · slight, slightly

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gjøre lettere;
    gjøre mindre vanskelig     to make lighter;
    to make less difficult
    • lette en byrde to lighten a burden
    • det vil lette arbeidet that will make the work easier
    • lette adgangen til høyere utdanning to ease access to higher education
  2. frigjøre (fra plager og bekymringer);
    gjøre mindre trykkende, lindre, mildne     to free (from troubles and worries);
    to make less oppressive, to relieve, to soothe
    • lette trykket i beholderen to release the pressure in the container
    • jeg skulle ønske jeg kunne lette sorgen for deg I wish I could ease your grief
    • kjenne seg lettet over noe to feel relieved about something
  3. raneto rob to robrane
    • de lettet ham for både kort og kontanter they relieved him of both his cards and his cash
  4. løfte litt;
    heve (seg)     to lift slightly;
    to raise (oneself)
    • lette på skapet for å kikke under to tilt the cupboard a little to look underneath
    • lette på lokket to lift the lid
    • fuglene lettet the birds took off
    • det er på tide at vi letter på oss og går hjem it is time we got ourselves up and went home
  5. letne (2)to let up, klarne (1)to clear to let upletne (2), to clearklarne (1)
    • tåka lettet the fog lifted
    • det ser ut til at det letter opp it looks as though it is brightening up
