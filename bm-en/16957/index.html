forsterkning

noun masculine or feminine

reinforcement, strengthening · reinforcements (extra troops or help)

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  2. i flertall: støttetroppa support platoon, hjelphelp · aidin the plural: a support platoonstøttetropp, helphjelp
