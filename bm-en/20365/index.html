gnageB2, level B2

verbverb

to gnaw (at) · to chafe, to rub

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt gnaga Old Norse gnaga

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. bruke tennene på noe hardt eller seigt;
    skave biter av med tennene     to use one's teeth on something hard or tough;
    to scrape bits off with the teeth
    • gnage på et kjøttbein to gnaw on a meat bone
    • musa hadde gnagd hull i veggen the mouse had gnawed a hole in the wall
    • gnage bark av trærne to gnaw bark off the trees
  2. gni mot noe så det gjør vondt eller blir en skade;
    gnisse;
    slite (3)to wear (down), tære     to rub against something so that it hurts or causes an injury;
    to chafe;
    to wear (down)slite (3), to erode
    • gnage hull på sokken to rub a hole in one's sock
    • skoen gnager the shoe chafes
  3. volde lidelse;
    pine, plage;
    nageto gnaw (at), to nag (at)     to cause suffering;
    to torment, to trouble;
    to nag (at)nage
    • sulten gnager i tarmene hunger gnaws at one's guts
    • samvittigheten gnager one's conscience gnaws
    • tvilen gnagde henne doubt gnawed at her
    brukt som adjektiv used as an adjective
    • en gnagende uro a gnawing unease
  4. gnåleto nag, to grumble · to harp on, to keep repeating, mase (II, 2)to nag to harp ongnåle, to nagmase (II, 2)
    • gnage på det samme to keep harping on the same thing
