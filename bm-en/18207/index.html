frostfri

adjective

frost-free · non-freezing, frost-resistant

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør fri (II)free · off, on leave (from work) · clear of, rid ofcompare fri (II)free · off, on leave (from work) · clear of, rid of

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. trygg for frostsafe from frost
    • frostfri kjellera frost-free cellar
  2. som tåler sterk kulde uten å frysewhich withstands severe cold without freezing
    • frostfri kjølevæskenon-freezing coolant
  3. uten frostwithout frost
    • frostfri natta night without frost
