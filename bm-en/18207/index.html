frostfri

adjectiveadjektiv

frost-free · non-freezing, frost-resistant

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

jamfør fri (II)free · off, on leave (from work) · clear of, rid of compare fri (II)free · off, on leave (from work) · clear of, rid of

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. trygg for frost safe from frost
    • frostfri kjeller a frost-free cellar
  2. som tåler sterk kulde uten å fryse which withstands severe cold without freezing
    • frostfri kjølevæske non-freezing coolant
  3. uten frost without frost
    • frostfri natt a night without frost
