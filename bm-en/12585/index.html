enda II homograph 2

adverb

even (before a comparative) · another, one more · still, yet

Etymology

norrønt en þáOld Norse en þá

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. brukt som tidsadverb: fremdeles på det tidspunktet;
    ennåstill · yet    used as an adverb of time: still at that point in time;
    yetennå
    • de er enda ikke kommetthey have not arrived yet
    • da bodde de enda i Bergenat that time they were still living in Bergen
    • da var han enda unghe was still young then
    • vi har enda ikke sett noe til demwe still haven't seen anything of them
  2. i tillegg til det som er nevnt eller er vanlig;
    ytterligere    in addition to what is mentioned or is usual;
    further, additional
    • enda en gangone more time
    • enda et eksempelanother example
    • de gikk enda tre kilometer innover i skogenthey walked another three kilometres into the forest
    • fortsette enda noen år i bedriftento stay on a few more years at the company
  3. brukt foran komparativ (I)the comparative for å uttrykke høyere gradused before a comparativekomparativ (I) to express a higher degree
    • enda størreeven bigger
    • enda bedreeven better
    • enda penereeven prettier
    • enda flere barneven more children
    • enda lenger unnaeven further away
    • skape enda mer begeistringto create even more excitement
    • tilstanden har blitt enda verrethe condition has become even worse
  4. likevelnevertheless · after all · anyway, tross altafter allnonethelesslikevel, after alltross alt
    • fattig, men enda tilfredspoor, but content nonetheless
    • det kan jeg enda gå med på, men …I can still go along with that, but …
  5. sanneligcertainly · really · indeedcertainlysannelig
    • det var da enda godt han hadde alarmit certainly was a good thing he had an alarm
  6. brukt i uttrykk for ønske som ikke kan oppfylles;
    bare    used in expressions of a wish that cannot be fulfilled;
    if only
    • om det enda var så enkeltif only it were that simple
    • hadde hun enda sagt noeif only she had said something
    • kunne han enda følt fortvilelseif only he could have felt despair
