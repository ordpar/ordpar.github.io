skilles

verb

to part · to get divorced · to be separated

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gå fra hverandre;
    avslutte forbindelse    to part, to go separate ways;
    to end a relationship
    • skilles som gode vennerto part as good friends
    • de to kan ikke skilles atthe two cannot be separated
    • så skiltes våre veierand so our paths parted
  2. oppløse ekteskap;
    skille seg (2)to divorce    to dissolve a marriage;
    to divorceskille seg (2)
    • de skiltes etter ti års ekteskapthey divorced after ten years of marriage
  3. bli delt;
    skille fra hverandre    to be divided;
    to be separated from one another
    • punktene skilles med blanke linjerthe items are separated by blank lines
    • kornet skilles fra agnetthe wheat is separated from the chaff
