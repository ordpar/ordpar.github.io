slå ned

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. slå en person så hardt at hen går over ende og blir skaddto hit a person so hard that they fall over and are injured Eng. phraseto knock someone down
    • han ble slått ned på et utestedhe was knocked down at a nightspot
  2. nedkjempe;
    knuse (2)to crush    to defeat;
    to crushknuse (2)
    • opprøret ble slått nedthe uprising was put down
  3. om lyn: treffeof lightning: to strike
    • lynet slo ned i hyttathe lightning struck the cabin
  4. om tanke, følelse eller hendelse: treffe brått og uventet med stor kraftof a thought, feeling, or event: to strike suddenly and unexpectedly with great force
    • tanken slo ned i megthe thought struck me
