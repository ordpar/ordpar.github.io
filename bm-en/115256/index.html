få igjen

to get back, to get in return · to get one's comeuppance, to get what one deserves

Senses and Example Sentences

bli gitt noe som en følge av noe en har gjortto be given something as a result of something one has done
  • dette krever nok litt større innsats enn det jeg får igjenthis probably takes a bit more effort than I get back out of it
  • bare vent, du skal nok få igjen for at du ødela kvelden for alle!just wait, you'll get what's coming to you for ruining the evening for everyone!
