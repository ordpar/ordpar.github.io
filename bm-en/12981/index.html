erfaring

noun feminine or masculinesubstantiv hunkjønn eller hankjønn

experience · an experience (one that teaches something)

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. ferdighet, innsikt eller viten som er oppnådd gjennom egen opplevelse eller øvelse skill, insight or knowledge acquired through one's own experience or practice
    • mangle erfaring to lack experience
    • hun er en kvinne med erfaring she is a woman of experience
    • han har stor erfaring på dette feltet he has a great deal of experience in this field
  2. hendelse, observasjon eller opplevelse som gir innsikt an event, observation or experience that yields insight
    • gjøre sine erfaringer to gain experiences of one's own
    • ha gode erfaringer med noe to have had good experiences with something
    • hun ble en erfaring rikere she was the wiser for the experience

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

av erfaring basert på egen opplevelse based on what one has been through oneself Eng. phrasefrom experience
  • tale av erfaring to speak from experience
  • vite noe av bitter erfaring to know something from bitter experience
