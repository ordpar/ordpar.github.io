orkester

noun neuter

an orchestra, a band · an orchestra pit · a seat in the front stalls

Inflection

Etymology

fra gresk, opprinnelig ‘korets danseplass mellom scenen og tilskuerne i et teater’from Greek, originally ‘the chorus’s dancing space between the stage and the audience in a theatre’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. større gruppe av musikere som spiller sammena larger group of musicians who play together
    • en konsert for kor og orkestera concert for choir and orchestra
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som janitsjarorkestera wind and percussion band, symfoniorkestera symphony orchestra
  2. orkestergravan orchestra pitan orchestra pitorkestergrav
  3. orkesterplassa seat in the front stallsa seat in the front stallsorkesterplass
