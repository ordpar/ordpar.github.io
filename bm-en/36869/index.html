mangle I homograph 1

verb

to lack · to be missing

Inflection

Etymology

av tysk mangeln; jamfør mangela lack · a shortage · a defectfrom German mangeln; compare mangela lack · a shortage · a defect

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ikke ha, være uten, savne, trengenot to have, to be without, to lack, to need
    • mangle matto be short of food
    • mangle erfaringto lack experience
    • mangle retningssansto have no sense of direction
    • hun manglet to poeng på å komme innshe was two points short of getting in
    • vertinnen sørget for at gjestene ikke manglet noethe hostess made sure the guests wanted for nothing
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • det skyldes manglende viljeit is due to a lack of willingness
  2. være borte, ikke finnes, skorteto be missing, not to be there, to be in short supply
    • det mangler tre personerthree people are missing
    • det mangler en skrue i motorenthere is a screw missing in the engine
    • det manglet ikke på advarslerthere was no shortage of warnings
  3. være i veien med;
    feile (3)to ail    to be the matter with;
    to ailfeile (3)
    • det mangler henne ingentingthere is nothing wrong with her

Set phrases

det skulle bare mangle brukt for å uttrykke at noe er sagt eller gjort med glede eller som en selvfølgeused to express that something is said or done gladly or as a matter of course Eng. phraseof course
