mangle I homograph 1

verbverb

to lack · to be missing

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av tysk mangeln; jamfør mangela lack · a shortage · a defect from German mangeln; compare mangela lack · a shortage · a defect

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. ikke ha, være uten, savne, trenge not to have, to be without, to lack, to need
    • mangle mat to be short of food
    • mangle erfaring to lack experience
    • mangle retningssans to have no sense of direction
    • hun manglet to poeng på å komme inn she was two points short of getting in
    • vertinnen sørget for at gjestene ikke manglet noe the hostess made sure the guests wanted for nothing
    brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
    • det skyldes manglende vilje it is due to a lack of willingness
  2. være borte, ikke finnes, skorte to be missing, not to be there, to be in short supply
    • det mangler tre personer three people are missing
    • det mangler en skrue i motoren there is a screw missing in the engine
    • det manglet ikke på advarsler there was no shortage of warnings
  3. være i veien med;
    feile (3)to ail     to be the matter with;
    to ailfeile (3)
    • det mangler henne ingenting there is nothing wrong with her

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

det skulle bare mangle brukt for å uttrykke at noe er sagt eller gjort med glede eller som en selvfølge used to express that something is said or done gladly or as a matter of course Eng. phraseof course
