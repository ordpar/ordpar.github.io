ryke

verb

to snap · to break · to go (be lost) · to give off smoke

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt rjúkaOld Norse rjúka

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sende ut røyk eller damp;
    dampe (1)to steam    to send out smoke or steam;
    to steamdampe (1)
    • det ryker fra pipasmoke is coming from the chimney
    • det ryker fra tekoppensteam is rising from the teacup
    • hesten er så varm at det ryker av denthe horse is so hot that it is steaming
  2. bevege seg raskt;
    fyke (2)to dash, fare (II, 1)to travel;
    falle (II, 1)to fall    to move quickly;
    to dashfyke (2), to travelfare (II, 1);
    to fallfalle (II, 1)
    • prisene røk i væretprices shot up
    • ryke i gulvetto go crashing to the floor
    • han røk over endehe toppled over
  3. gå i stykker;
    briste (1)to snap, slitneto wear through    to break, to fall apart;
    to snapbriste (1), to wear throughslitne
    • koppen røk i stykkerthe cup broke to pieces
    • det vennskapet vil rykethat friendship will fall apart
    • korsbåndet har røketthe cruciate ligament has ruptured
  4. tape (II, 1)to lose, miste;
    forsvinneto disappear, to vanish · to go missing;
    gi tapt    to losetape (II, 1), to be deprived of;
    to disappearforsvinne;
    to give in
    • der røk den sjansenthere goes that chance
    • ryke 3–5 i en fotballkampto lose 3–5 in a football match
    • alle pengene ryker i regningerall the money goes on bills
