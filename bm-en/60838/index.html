tillegge

verb

to attribute, to ascribe · to attach (importance, weight) · to add

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. legge til;
    tilføye    to add;
    to append
    • jeg har ikke noe å tilleggeI have nothing to add
  2. tilskrive noe eller noen en bestemt egenskap eller funksjonto ascribe a particular quality or function to something or someone
    • tillegge en sak stor vektto attach great importance to a matter
  3. tilskrive noen en bestemt mening, holdning eller lignendeto attribute a particular opinion, attitude or the like to someone
    • du tillegger meg meninger jeg ikke haryou're attributing opinions to me that I don't hold
