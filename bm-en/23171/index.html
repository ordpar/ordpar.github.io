hende III homograph 3

verb

to happen, to occur

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt henda; beslektet med håndhand, opprinnelig ‘berøre med hånden’Old Norse henda; related to håndhand, originally ‘to touch with the hand’

Senses and Example Sentences

    ramme (III)to hit · to affect badly · to strike    to happen by chance or unexpectedly;
    to strike, to befallramme (III)

    • jeg håper det ikke har hendt dem noeI hope nothing has happened to them
