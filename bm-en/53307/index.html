skole I homograph 1

noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

school

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt skóli, gjennom lavtysk; fra gresk skhole ‘fritid brukt til lærd virksomhet’ Old Norse skóli, through Low German; from Greek skhole 'leisure used for scholarly activity'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. institusjon for planmessig undervisning og opplæring an institution for systematic teaching and instruction
    • skolen begynner 20. august school starts on 20 August
    • han arbeider i skolen he works in the school system
    • skulke skolen to skip school
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som danseskolea dance school, grunnskolecompulsory school (primary and lower secondary combined) · basic education, høyskolea university college, internatskolea boarding school, rideskolea riding school
  2. bygning som er innrettet til å drive undervisning i;
    skolebygninga school building     a building designed for teaching in;
    school buildingskolebygning
    • bygge ny skole to build a new school
    • bo like ved skolen to live right by the school
  3. elever og lærere ved en skole (I, 2)a school the pupils and teachers at a schoolskole (I, 2)
    • hele skolen hadde fri i går the whole school had the day off yesterday
  4. vitenskapelig eller kunstnerisk retning eller krets a scientific or artistic movement or circle
    • de utviklet en ny skole innenfor landskapsmaleriet they developed a new school of landscape painting
  5. livserfaringer som utvikler og former en person life experiences that develop and shape a person
    • oppveksten i bygda var en viktig skole growing up in the village was an important formative experience
    • de har gått i en hard skole they have been through a hard school
