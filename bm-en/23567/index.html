hilse

verbverb

to greet · to say hello · to send regards

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt heilsa, beslektet med hel (II)whole, entire · intact, unbroken · completely, entirely (adverb {helt}), heil (II)hail; hell (I)luck, good fortune · a stroke of luck, a lucky coincidence og helsehealth Old Norse heilsa, related to hel (II)whole, entire · intact, unbroken · completely, entirely (adverb {helt}), heil (II)hail; hell (I)luck, good fortune · a stroke of luck, a lucky coincidence and helsehealth

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. ønske vel møtt eller farvel med ord eller bevegelse to wish welcome or farewell with words or a gesture
    • hilse med hånden to greet with a wave of the hand
    • hilse i hånden to greet with a handshake
    • de hilste hverandre med et kyss på kinnet they greeted each other with a kiss on the cheek
    • den fremmede hilste ‘god kveld’ da han kom inn the stranger said 'good evening' as he came in
    • hilse velkommen til møtet to welcome someone to the meeting
  2. overbringe en hilsen to pass on a greeting
    • hils alle kjente say hello to everyone we know
    • jeg skal hilse fra foreldrene mine my parents send their regards
    • jeg skulle hilse og si takk for lånet I was to pass on their thanks for the loan
  3. i militæret: gjøre honnør (2)a salute in the military: to give a salutehonnør (2)
    • soldaten hilste med hånden til lua the soldier saluted with his hand to his cap
  4. besøke (1)to visit to visitbesøke (1)
    • du må stikke innom og hilse på oss en dag you must drop by and see us one day
    • i morgen skal jeg hilse på bestemor på sykehuset tomorrow I'm going to visit grandma in hospital
  5. mottato receive · to accept · to welcome to receivemotta
    • bli hilst med sterkt bifall to be greeted with loud applause
    • mange hilste forslaget med glede many welcomed the proposal
