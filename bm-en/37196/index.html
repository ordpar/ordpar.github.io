noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

martyr

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

gjennom latin; fra gresk martys ‘vitne’ via Latin; from Greek martys 'witness'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. person som ofrer livet for sin tro;
    blodvitnemartyr     person who sacrifices their life for their faith;
    a martyrblodvitne
    • Stefanus var den første kristne martyren Stephen was the first Christian martyr
  2. person som må lide for sin overbevisning person who has to suffer for their convictions
    • frihetskampens martyrer the martyrs of the struggle for freedom
  3. uskyldig offer innocent victim
    • like å spille martyr to like playing the martyr
