vrake

verb

to reject, to discard, to turn down · to grade, to sort · to wreck, to write off

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk wraken eller av vrak (I)a wreck · flotsam, driftwoodfrom Low German wraken or from vrak (I)a wreck · flotsam, driftwood

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. forkaste som ubrukelig;
    forsmå (1)to spurn, vise bort;
    kaste (II, 4)to discard    to reject as useless;
    to spurnforsmå (1), to turn away;
    to discardkaste (II, 4)
    • vrake et forslagto reject a proposal
    • jenta vraket guttenthe girl turned the boy down
  2. om vrakergraders: undersøke varekvalitet og skille ut det som ikke holder målof gradersvraker: to inspect the quality of goods and sort out what does not measure up
    • vrake fiskto grade fish
    • vrake tømmerto grade timber
  3. gjøre til vrak;
    ødelegge    to reduce to a wreck;
    to destroy
    • noen melder bilen stjålet etter å ha vraket den selvsome people report the car stolen after wrecking it themselves
