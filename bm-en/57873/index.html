stue I homograph 1

noun feminine or masculine

a living room, a sitting room · a cottage · a lodge

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt stofaOld Norse stofa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. lite, enkelt hus;
    stovea cottage    a small, simple house;
    a cottagestove
    • de bodde i en liten stuethey lived in a little cottage
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som husmannsstuea crofter's cottage, årestuea cottage with a central open hearth
  2. hytte brukt til overnatting eller serveringa cabin used for lodging or for serving food
    • skiforeningens stuer melder om godt besøkthe ski association's lodges report good numbers of visitors
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som fjellstuea mountain lodge, vaktstuea guardroom
  3. oppholdsrom i bolighusa sitting room in a dwelling
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som bestestuethe best parlour, dagligstuea living room, forstue (I)an entrance hall, peisestuea room with an open fireplace, spisestuea dining room
  4. (større) rom eller lokalea (larger) room or premises
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som rådstuea town hall, sykestuean infirmary, skolestuea schoolroom, systuea sewing room, tingstuean assembly house
