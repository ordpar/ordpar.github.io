duo

noun masculine

duo, pair · duet

Inflection

Etymology

fra italiensk; fra latin ‘to’from Italian; from Latin 'two'

    duett (1)a duet    piece of music composed for or performed by two musicians;
    a duetduett (1)

  2. gruppe av to sangere eller musikeregroup of two singers or musicians

  3. to personer som opererer sammen;
    par (I)a pair · a couple · a couple of, a few    two people who operate together;
    a pairpar (I)
