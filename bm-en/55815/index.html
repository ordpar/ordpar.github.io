spare

verbverb

to save · to spare · to economise

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt spara Old Norse spara

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. legge til side;
    ikke bruke     to set aside;
    not to use
    • spare 500 kr i måneden to save 500 kroner a month
    • spare den nye kjolen til jul to save the new dress for Christmas
  2. bruke mindre to use less
    • du sparer penger på å handle der you save money by shopping there
    • spare tid to save time
    • spare plass to save space
  3. la være å gjøre to refrain from doing
    • spar deg det bryderiet spare yourself the trouble
  4. bruke minst mulig to use as little as possible
    • spinke og spare to scrimp and save
    • spare på kreftene to conserve one's strength
  5. fritato exempt, to release, to excuse, skåneto spare, to shield · to protect from damage, to be gentle with to exempt, to release, to excusefrita, to spare, to shieldskåne
    • spare noen for noe to spare someone something
    • ta hevn, ikke spar noen! take revenge, spare no one!
    • han sparer seg aldri i arbeidet he never spares himself in his work
