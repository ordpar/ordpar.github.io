skjerpe I homograph 1

verbverb

to sharpen · to tighten, to toughen · to heighten, to whet

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av skarp (II)sharp · keen · harsh, biting · live (of ammunition) from skarp (II)sharp · keen · harsh, biting · live (of ammunition)

  2. gjøre streng og skarp (II, 8)harsh to make strict and harshskarp (II, 8)

  3. gjøre mer oppmerksom to make more alert
