viktig

adjectiveadjektiv

important · self-important, conceited

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk, samme opprinnelse som vektigweighty, important, compelling · heavy, of considerable weight from Low German, of the same origin as vektigweighty, important, compelling · heavy, of considerable weight
