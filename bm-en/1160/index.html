åsted

noun neuter

crime scene · the scene (the place where something happened)

Inflection

Etymology

av norrønt á ‘på’, opprinnelig ‘på stedet’from Old Norse á 'on', originally 'on the spot'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i jus: sted der noe straffbart har gått for segin law: the place where a criminal act has taken place, the scene of a crime
    • politiet kom raskt til åstedetthe police quickly arrived at the scene
  2. i jus: sted som det er tvist om i en sivil rettssakin law: the place in dispute in a civil case
  3. sted der noe har hendtthe place where something has happened, the scene
    • vassdraget er åsted for dragkampen mellom naturvernere og veibyggerethe watercourse is the scene of a tug-of-war between conservationists and road builders
