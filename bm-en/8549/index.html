bøte

verb

to mend, to patch · to remedy, to put right, to make amends for · to pay as a fine, to pay for

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt bǿta; av bota fine · a remedy · penance · a patchOld Norse bǿta; from bota fine · a remedy · penance · a patch
