bøte

verbverb

to mend, to patch · to remedy, to put right, to make amends for · to pay as a fine, to pay for

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt bǿta; av bota fine · a remedy · penance · a patch Old Norse bǿta; from bota fine · a remedy · penance · a patch

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. sette bot (4)a patch på noe som er sundt;
    sy igjen en flenge;
    sette i stand;
    lappeto mend     to put a patchbot (4) on something that is torn;
    to sew up a rip;
    to put back in order;
    to mendlappe
    • bøte buksa si to patch one's trousers
    • binde og bøte fiskegarn to tie and mend fishing nets
  2. rette på;
    gjøre godt igjen     to put right;
    to make amends for
    • bøte på gammel urett to redress an old injustice
    • bøte på skaden to repair the damage
  3. betale i bot (2)a fine;
    gi vederlagremuneration, payment · something given in return for     to pay as a finebot (2);
    to give compensationvederlag for
    • bøte 200 kr to pay a fine of 200 kroner
    • bøte med livet to pay with one's life
