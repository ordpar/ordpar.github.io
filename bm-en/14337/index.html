fengsle

verb

to imprison · to captivate

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sette i fengselto put in prison
  2. vekke sterk interesse hosto arouse strong interest in
    • han ble fengslet av personligheten henneshe was captivated by her personality
    som adjektiv i presens partisipp:as an adjective in the present participle:
