underskrive

verb
Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. skrive under på (som tegn på godkjenning eller samtykke);
    sette navnetrekket sitt under;
    signere (1)to sign, undertegneto sign    to put one's name to (as a mark of approval or consent);
    to write one's signature at the bottom;
    to signsignere (1), to sign one's nameundertegne
    • underskrive oppropetto sign the petition
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • det underskrevne skjøtetthe signed deed
  2. i overført betydning: gå god forto vouch for · to guarantee · to answer for;
    garantere, forsikre    figuratively: to vouch forgå god for;
    to guarantee, to affirm
    • han kunne underskrive at hvert evige ord var santhe could swear that every single word was true
