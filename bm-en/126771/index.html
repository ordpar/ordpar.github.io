bli revet bort/vekk

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. dø brått og uventet;
    omkomme    to die suddenly and unexpectedly;
    to perish     Eng. phraseto be snatched away
    • hun ble revet bort i ung aldershe was taken from us at a young age
    • familien ble brutalt revet vekkthe family was brutally torn away
  2. forsvinne fra markedetto disappear from the market Eng. phraseto be snapped up
    • billettene ble revet bortthe tickets sold out in no time
    • varene ble revet vekk fra hyllenethe goods flew off the shelves
