oppunder

preposition

up under, right up against · at the foot of (a mountain, the shore) · just before (a date)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. mot undersiden avup against the underside of
    • ligge med hendene oppunder hodetto lie with one’s hands under one’s head
    • bjelkene oppunder taketthe beams up under the ceiling
  2. mot nedre del avtowards the lower part of
    • en gård oppunder fjelleta farm at the foot of the mountain
    • oppunder landclose in to shore
  3. like førjust before
    • en dag oppunder jula day just before Christmas
  4. brukt som adverb: i nærheten avused as an adverb: close to
    • det er ikke det beste han har prestert, men det ligger tett oppunderit is not the best he has done, but it comes very close
