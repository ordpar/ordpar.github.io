“bredde” is an inflected form of:

  • bre III homograph 3, breie III homograph 3 verbverb (past, perfect participle definite and plural)(preteritum, perfektum partisipp bestemt og flertall) to stretch out or unfold (in order to cover something) strekke eller folde ut (for å dekke noe)
  • bre II homograph 2 verbverb (past, perfect participle definite and plural)(preteritum, perfektum partisipp bestemt og flertall) Norwegian only smøre med bek eller tjære
