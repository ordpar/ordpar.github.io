bestille

verb

to order · to book · to reserve

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk ‘stille, anbringe’from Low German 'to place, to put'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre avtale om, be om;
    tinge (1)to book, reservereto reserve, to book · to set aside    to order, to ask for;
    to booktinge (1), to reservereservere
    • bestille varerto order goods
    • bestille bordto book a table
    • bestille billetterto book tickets
    • bestille time hos tannlegento make an appointment with the dentist
  2. utretteto accomplish, to achieve, to get done, gjøreto do · to make;
    beskjeftige seg med    to accomplish, to achieveutrette, to dogjøre;
    to occupy oneself with
    • hva bestiller du her?what are you doing here?
    • er du ikke snill, skal du få med meg å bestilleif you don't behave, you'll have me to deal with

Set phrases

som bestilt i rette øyeblikk; perfektat just the right moment; perfect Eng. phrasejust what the doctor ordered
  • det fine været kom som bestiltthe lovely weather could not have come at a better time
