omgås

verb

to socialise with, to mix with · to handle, to deal with

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ha selskapelig samkvem med;
    være sammen med    to socialise with;
    to spend time with
    • de omgås ikke naboene sinethey don't mix with their neighbours
  2. være opptatt av;
    pønske på    to be preoccupied with;
    to be scheming about
    • de omgikkes med planer om eget husthey were entertaining plans of a house of their own
  3. bruke, behandleto use, to handle
    • de nye stoffene må omgås med varsomhetthe new substances must be handled with care
