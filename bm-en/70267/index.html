fronte

verbverb

to front, to be the public face of · to spearhead, to champion · to take a public stand against

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. være offentlig talsperson for to be the public spokesperson for
    • bedriften skal frontes av den nye direktøren the company is to be fronted by the new director
  2. danne front (3)a front mot, imøtegå offentlig to form a frontfront (3) against, to oppose publicly
    • aktivistene fronter dumping av oljeplattformer the activists are campaigning against the dumping of oil platforms
    • vi har vært for feige til å fronte høyreekstremistene we have been too cowardly to stand up to the right-wing extremists
  3. gå i brodden for to spearhead
    • fronte en sak to champion a cause
