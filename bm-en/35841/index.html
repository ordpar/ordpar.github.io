Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. si seg villig til noe;
    gi løfte om     to declare oneself willing to do something;
    to make a promise about
    • love noen noe to promise someone something
    • jeg kan ikke love mirakler I can't promise miracles
    • lov meg at du ikke blir sen! promise me you won't be late!
    • love å gjøre noe to promise to do something
    • love å holde tett to promise to keep quiet
    • love å være snill to promise to be good
    • love for mye to promise too much
    • holde det en lover to keep one's promises
  2. se ut til å bli, gi varsel, håp om;
    jamfør lovendepromising     to look set to become, to give warning or hope of;
    compare lovendepromising
    • dette lover godt this bodes well
  3. forsikreto assure · to insure, garantereto guarantee · to vouch for to assureforsikre, to guaranteegarantere
    • jeg lover at dette skal bli siste gangen I promise this will be the last time
    • det skal jeg love deg! I can assure you of that!
    • jeg skal love for at ... I can tell you that ...
    • han lovet på at han skulle sette ting på plass he swore he would put things right
