munter

adjective

cheerful, merry · cheering, amusing

Inflection

Etymology

fra tyskfrom German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i godt humør;
    lystig, livlig, spøkefull    in a good mood;
    merry, lively, playful
    • en munter fyra cheerful fellow
    • være i muntert lagto be in jolly company
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • de forteller muntert om hva de har opplevdthey tell cheerfully of what they have been through
  2. som muntrer oppcheering, uplifting
    • muntre fargercheerful colours
    • muntre historieramusing stories
    • en munter melodia lively tune
