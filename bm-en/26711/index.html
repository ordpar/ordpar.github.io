innstille

verb

to cancel · to set · to be disposed towards

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stille inn et apparat, instrument eller lignende;
    stille (V, 8)to adjust, regulereto regulate · to adjust · to zone    to set an apparatus, instrument or the like;
    to adjuststille (V, 8), to regulateregulere
    • termostaten må innstilles nøyaktigthe thermostat must be set precisely
  2. gjøre klar til å gjennomføre;
    forberede mentalt    to get ready to carry something out;
    to prepare mentally
    • være innstilt på en hard kampto be braced for a hard fight
    • vi får innstille oss på å leve sparsommeligwe shall have to prepare ourselves to live frugally
  3. ha en viss holdning tilto have a certain attitude towards something
    • være vennlig innstilt overfor noeto be kindly disposed towards something
    • jeg er positivt innstilt til forslagetI look favourably on the proposal
  4. tilråto recommend, foreslåto suggest · to propose · to nominateto recommendtilrå, to nominateforeslå
    • søkeren ble innstilt som nummer to til stillingenthe applicant was recommended as the second choice for the post
  5. slutte med;
    stoppe, avlyse    to discontinue;
    to halt, to cancel
    • innstille alle utbetalingerto suspend all payments
    • innstille skytingento cease fire
    • da det ble mørkt, måtte vi innstille letingenwhen it got dark, we had to call off the search
    • bedriften må innstille videre driftthe company must cease further operations
    • siste bussavgang er innstiltthe last bus departure has been cancelled
