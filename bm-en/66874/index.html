vasse

verb
Inflection

Etymology

av vadeto wade · to be rolling in, to be awash withfrom vadeto wade · to be rolling in, to be awash with

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gå i vann, snø eller lignende som når opp over føttene;
    vadeto wade · to be rolling in, to be awash with    to walk through water, snow or the like that comes up over one's feet;
    to wadevade
    • vasse over elvato wade across the river
    • vasse i vanndammerto wade through puddles
    • vi vasset gjennom lyngenwe waded through the heather
  2. i overført betydning: ha rikelig av noein a figurative sense: to have plenty of something
    • de vasser i pengerthey are rolling in money
