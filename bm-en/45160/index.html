plapre

verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra tysk, lydord from German, an onomatopoeic word

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. snakke fort;
    skravle, vrøvle     to talk fast;
    to chatter, to prattle
    • de plapret om de samme tingene hver gang de møttes they prattled on about the same things every time they met
  2. røpe hemmelighet;
    sladre     to let out a secret;
    to blab
    • plapre til politiet to spill the beans to the police
