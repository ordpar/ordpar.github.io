trossamfunn, trussamfunn

noun neuter

a religious community, a faith community, a denomination

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

gruppe mennesker med samme (variant av) religiongroup of people sharing the same religion (or the same variant of one)
  • det mosaiske trossamfunnthe Jewish faith community
  • han brøt med kirken og dannet et nytt trossamfunnhe broke with the church and founded a new religious denomination
