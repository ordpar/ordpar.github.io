holdbar

adjective
Inflection

Etymology

av tysk haltbar; av holdeto hold · to keep · to hold, to put on (an event)from German haltbar; from holdeto hold · to keep · to hold, to put on (an event)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som holder seg i god stand;
    som bevarer sin gode kvalitet    which stays in good condition;
    which keeps its good quality
    • disse skoene har vært utrolig holdbarethese shoes have been incredibly hard-wearing
    • pølsene er holdbare til 15. marsthe sausages keep until 15 March
  2. som er godt underbygd;
    forsvarligsound, safe, up to standard · justifiable, defensible    which is well founded;
    justifiable, defensibleforsvarlig
    • påstanden viste seg ikke å være holdbarthe claim turned out not to be tenable
