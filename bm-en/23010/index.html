hell I homograph 1

noun neutersubstantiv intetkjønn

luck, good fortune · a stroke of luck, a lucky coincidence

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra dansk, jamfør norrønt heill ‘varsel, lykke’; beslektet med hel (II)whole, entire · intact, unbroken · completely, entirely (adverb {helt}) from Danish, compare Old Norse heill ‘omen, good fortune’; related to hel (II)whole, entire · intact, unbroken · completely, entirely (adverb {helt})
