mellomrom

noun neuter

a gap · a space · an interval

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør rom (III)room · spacecompare rom (III)room · space

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. fysisk avstand mellom to individer, gjenstander eller punkterthe physical distance between two individuals, objects or points
    • ha stort mellomrom mellom fortenneneto have a big gap between one's front teeth
    • føye sammen to ord til ett, uten mellomromto join two words into one, without a space
  2. tidsrom mellom to tidspunkter, perioder eller hendelser;
    opphold, pause    the stretch of time between two points in time, periods or events;
    a break, a pause
    • med få ukers mellomroma few weeks apart
    • med jevne mellomromat regular intervals
