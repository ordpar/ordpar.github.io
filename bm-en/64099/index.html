ulik

adjective

different, unlike · various · unequal

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ikke lik, forskjellig (fra)not alike, different (from)
    • godene er ulikt fordeltthe goods are unequally distributed
    • dette er ulikt deg – utypisk for degthis is unlike you – uncharacteristic of you
    • være ulik sin farto be unlike one's father
  2. i flertall: noensome · any · someone, something, flereseveral, a number of · morein the plural: somenoen, severalflere
    • ulike filosofiske retningervarious philosophical schools of thought

Set phrases

likt og ulikt alt mulig, både det ene og det andreall sorts of things, one thing and another Eng. phrasethis and that
