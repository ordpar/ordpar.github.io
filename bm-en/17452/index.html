frakoble, frakople

verb

to disconnect

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

bryte forbindelse mellom noe, koble frato break the connection between things, to disconnect
  • jernbanevogna ble frakobletthe railway carriage was uncoupled
  • pasienten ble frakoblet respiratorthe patient was taken off the ventilator
  • de er fullstendig frakoblet virkelighetenthey are completely disconnected from reality
