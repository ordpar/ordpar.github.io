mareritt

noun neuter

a nightmare

Inflection

Etymology

av marea mare (a folk-belief spirit that sits on a sleeper) og ritta cycle race · a ride, a ride on horsebackfrom marea mare (a folk-belief spirit that sits on a sleeper) and ritta cycle race · a ride, a ride on horseback

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. vond drøma bad dream
    • få mareritt om nattato have nightmares at night
  2. uhyggelig tilstand eller opplevelsea dreadful situation or experience
    • krigens marerittthe nightmare of the war
    • den reisen var et eneste marerittthat journey was one long nightmare
