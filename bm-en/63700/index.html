ublandet, ublanda

adjective

unmixed, undiluted, neat · unalloyed, unqualified

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ikke tilsatt eller blandet med noenot added to or mixed with anything
    • ublandet whiskyneat whisky
  2. hel, fullstendigentire, complete
    • en ublandet fornøyelsean unalloyed pleasure
