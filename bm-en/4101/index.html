bakerst

adjectiveadjektiv

furthest back, at the very back · rearmost, hindmost

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

opprinnelig superlativ av bak (III)behind · at the back, in the back; jamfør bakrerear · back · hind originally the superlative of bak (III)behind · at the back, in the back; compare bakrerear · back · hind

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. lengst bak;
    motsatt fremst (1)frontmost     furthest back, rearmost;
    opposite of frontmostfremst (1)
    • i bakerste rekke in the very back row
  2. brukt som adverb: used as an adverb:
    • sitte bakerst i salen to sit right at the back of the hall
  3. brukt som substantiv: used as a noun:
    • de bakerste hørte ingenting those at the very back heard nothing
