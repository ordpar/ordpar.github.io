mot III homograph 3

preposition

towards · against

Etymology

norrønt í mót, i móti, opprinnelig ‘i møte (med)’; jamfør mot (II)meeting og imotagainst · towardsOld Norse í mót, i móti, originally 'in meeting (with)'; compare mot (II)meeting and imotagainst · towards

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i møte med;
    i retning (fram til)    towards, so as to meet;
    in the direction of (right up to)
    • de kom gående mot ossthey came walking towards us
    • kjøre mot byento drive towards the city
    • snu seg mot venstreto turn to the left
    • arbeide mot et målto work towards a goal
  2. i motsatt retning som;
    motsatt med (II, 4)in the same direction as    in the opposite direction to;
    opposite of in the same direction asmed (II, 4)
    • gå mot vindento walk against the wind
  3. (bort til og) i fysisk kontakt med(over to and) in physical contact with
    • bilen støtte mot en fjellveggthe car struck a rock face
    • lene seg mot veggento lean against the wall
  4. rett framfordirectly opposite, facing
    • stolene står rett mot hverandrethe chairs are directly opposite one another
  5. om tid: fram til;
    like før    of time: up to;
    just before
    • det lir mot vårspring is drawing near
    • mot slutten av periodentowards the end of the period
  6. i forholdet til noen eller noe;
    overfor (4)towards;
    jamfør med (II, 12)with (in a reciprocal relationship)    in relation to someone or something;
    towardsoverfor (4);
    compare med (II, 12)with (in a reciprocal relationship)
    • være ondskapsfull mot noento be malicious towards someone
    • de er snille mot hverandrethey are kind to each other
    • oppførselen hans mot meg var uklanderlighis behaviour towards me was impeccable
    • være allergisk mot bjørkepollento be allergic to birch pollen
  7. for å rette på eller forebygge noeto remedy or prevent something
    • et middel mot rustan agent against rust
    • medikamenter mot hodepinemedication for headaches
  8. for å unngåto avoid, to guard against
    • forsikre seg mot ulykkerto insure oneself against accidents
    • advare mot lommetyverto warn against pickpockets
  9. i konkurranse med;
    med noen som motstander    in competition with;
    with someone as an opponent
    • konkurrere mot noento compete against someone
    • spille kamp mot et topplagto play a match against a top team
    • partene stod steilt mot hverandrethe parties stood firmly opposed to one another
  10. med mål om å motarbeide eller bekjempe noe;
    jamfør for (V, 3)in support of    with the aim of opposing or combating something;
    compare for (V, 3)in support of
    • være mot noeto be against something
    • stemme mot noeto vote against something
    • forbud mot røykinga ban on smoking
  11. i strid medcontrary to, in conflict with
    • dette er mot all fornuftthis is against all reason
    • dette er mot min viljethis is against my will
  12. i forhold til;
    sammenlignet med    in relation to;
    compared with
    • mot henne blir du bare barnetcompared with her, you're just a child
    • forslaget falt med seks mot ni stemmerthe motion was defeated by six votes to nine
    • dette er ikke noe mot hva det kunne ha værtthis is nothing compared to what it could have been
  13. som vederlag forin return for, in exchange for
    • beløpet refunderes mot kvitteringthe amount is refunded against a receipt
    • bli satt fri mot kausjonto be released on bail
