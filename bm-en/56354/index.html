sprette I homograph 1

verb

to spring · to leap · to pop

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt sprettaOld Norse spretta

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre en hurtig, brå bevegelse;
    hoppe, fare, fyke;
    jamfør sprettende (1)bouncing    to make a quick, sudden movement;
    to leap, to dart, to fly;
    compare sprettende (1)bouncing
    • sprette fram på gulvetto come bounding out across the floor
    • fisken spretterthe fish is jumping
    • knappene spratt ut av jakkathe buttons popped off the jacket
    • det var så vondt at tårene sprattit hurt so much that the tears sprang to my eyes
  2. springe ut;
    spire;
    jamfør sprettende (2)coming into leaf    to burst into leaf;
    to sprout;
    compare sprettende (2)coming into leaf
    • løvet har sprettet utthe foliage has come out
  3. stå opp;
    renne (II, 4)to rise    to come up;
    to riserenne (II, 4)
    • sola spretterthe sun pops up
