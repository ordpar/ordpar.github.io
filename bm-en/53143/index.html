skjønne

verbverb

to understand · to realise · to get

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt skynja; av skjønn (I)discretion · judgement · an official appraisal Old Norse skynja; from skjønn (I)discretion · judgement · an official appraisal

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

ha greie på;
forstå, innse to have a grasp of;
to understand, realise
  • ikke skjønne et ord to not understand a word
  • dette er vanskelig, skjønner du this is difficult, you see
  • skjønne at noe er på ferde to realise that something is afoot
  • jeg skjønte ikke hva hun mente I didn't understand what she meant
  • har du skjønt hva problemet er ennå? have you figured out what the problem is yet?
