stange

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt stangaOld Norse stanga

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. støte eller renne hodet eller hornene mot noe;
    skalle (IV)to headbutt    to knock or ram one's head or horns into something;
    to headbuttskalle (IV)
    • geitene stanger hverandrethe goats are butting each other
    • stange hodet mot dørkarmento bang one's head against the door frame
    • hun stanget ballen rett i målshe headed the ball straight into the goal
  2. i overført betydning: bli stående fast;
    ikke komme seg videre med noe    figuratively: to get stuck;
    to make no headway with something
    • køen stod og stangetthe queue was at a standstill
    • stange hodet mot lover og paragraferto bang one's head against laws and regulations

Set phrases

stange i taket møte en hindring som stopper vekst, utvikling eller lignendeto run into an obstacle that halts growth, development or the like Eng. phraseto hit a ceiling
  • markedet stanger i taketthe market is bumping up against a limit
