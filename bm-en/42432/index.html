oppgi

verbverb

to state, to give (details) · to give up, relinquish

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gi opplysninger om;
    gi opp (1)to state     to give information about;
    to stategi opp (1)
    • oppgi navn og adresse to give one's name and address
    • farten ble oppgitt til 90 km/t the speed was given as 90 km/h
    brukt som adjektiv used as an adjective
    • sjekke alle oppgitte opplysninger to check all the information provided
  2. avstå fra, gi slipp på;
    gi opp (2)to give up;
    jamfør oppgittexasperated, fed up · despondent, disheartened, resigned     to relinquish, to let go of;
    to give upgi opp (2);
    compare oppgittexasperated, fed up · despondent, disheartened, resigned
    • oppgi all motstand to give up all resistance
    • den syke var oppgitt av legene the patient had been given up on by the doctors
