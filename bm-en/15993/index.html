forbrenne I homograph 1

verb
Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk; av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excessfrom Low German; from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. brenne oppto burn up
    • tørt tre forbrenner lettdry wood burns up easily
    • gassen har forbrentthe gas has burned up
  2. forbinde seg kjemisk med et annet stoff, særlig oksygen, under varmeutviklingto combine chemically with another substance, especially oxygen, giving off heat
    • næringsstoffene forbrant i kroppenthe nutrients burned up in the body
