fritid

noun masculine or feminine

free time · spare time · leisure

Inflection

Etymology

av fri (II)free · off, on leave (from work) · clear of, rid offrom fri (II)free · off, on leave (from work) · clear of, rid of

Senses and Example Sentences

tid da en har fri fra arbeid, skole eller lignendetime when one is free from work, school or the like
  • ha lite fritidto have little free time
  • utnytte fritiden sinto make good use of one's free time
  • det er slikt jeg gjør i fritidenthat is the sort of thing I do in my spare time
