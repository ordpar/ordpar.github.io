nat

noun masculine
Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk, opprinnelig ‘søm, sammenføyning’; beslektet med nålneedle · pin (a fastening or a badge)from Low German, originally 'seam, joint'; related to nålneedle · pin (a fastening or a badge)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. fuge mellom plankene i et dekk eller en skipssidethe seam between the planks of a deck or a ship's side
    • båten var gissen i natenethe boat had dried out and was leaking at the seams
  2. søm over et seila seam across a sail
