dom I homograph 1

noun masculine

a judgement · a sentence · a verdict

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt dómr, jamfør tysk tun og engelsk do ‘gjøre’; jamfør -dom-dom, -hood, -ness, -shipOld Norse dómr, compare German tun and English do; compare -dom-dom, -hood, -ness, -ship

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. endelig avgjørelse av en rettssakthe final decision in a court case
    • avsi en domto pass a sentence
    • fengsles uten lov og domto be imprisoned without trial
    • dommen lyder på to års fengselthe sentence is two years in prison
    • anke en domto appeal a judgement
    • en mild doma lenient sentence
  2. kritisk bedømmelse, vurderingan assessment · an evaluation · a judgementa critical appraisal, an assessmentvurdering
    • være hard i sin domto be harsh in one's judgement
    • felle en dom over noento pass judgement on someone
