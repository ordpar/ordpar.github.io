brenne ut

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: brenneto burn, to be on fire · to sting, to smart · to be passionate (about)
  1. brenne til det ikke er noe igjen to burn until nothing is left Eng. phraseto burn out
    • bålet har brent ut the bonfire has burned out
    • bilen brant ut the car was gutted by fire
  2. om sykdom: slutte å være aktiv of an illness: to stop being active Eng. phraseto burn itself out
    • leddgikta hennes har brent ut her rheumatoid arthritis has burned itself out
