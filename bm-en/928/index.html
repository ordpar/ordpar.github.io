åkeC, level C

verbverb

to end up, to land · to go, to travel

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra svensk; jamfør aketo sledge, to toboggan from Swedish; compare aketo sledge, to toboggan

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. komme eller ende i en ny posisjon, for eksempel i fengsel;
    havne (I)to end up, to land     to come or end up in a new position, for example in prison;
    to landhavne (I)
    • laget åkte ned enda en divisjon the team went down yet another division
    • hun stod på sikker plass i valget og åkte inn i bystyret she had a safe place on the list in the election and went straight onto the city council
    • han åker inn og ut av fengsel he goes in and out of prison
    • han smuglet sprit og åkte rett inn he smuggled liquor and went straight inside
  2. fare (II, 1)to go, gli (1)to slide to gofare (II, 1), to slidegli (1)
    • varebilen åkte rett i grøfta the van went straight into the ditch
  3. dra (13)to go to godra (13)
    • åke til Oslo to go to Oslo
