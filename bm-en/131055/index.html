bli å

to be going to · can be (found, reached)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. brukt for å uttrykke passiv eller at noe skal eller bør skjeused to express the passive, or that something is to or should happen
    • vi blir å finne utenfor butikkenwe can be found outside the shop
    • hun ble å treffe på kontoretshe could be reached at the office
  2. brukt for å uttrykke framtid;
    komme til å (1)to be going to    used to express the future;
    to be going tokomme til å (1)
    • han blir å reise i morgenhe is going to travel tomorrow
    • hun ble å komme litt seinereshe was going to come a little later
