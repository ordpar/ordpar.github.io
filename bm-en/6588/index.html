blotte

verb

to bare, to expose · to reveal, to lay bare

Inflection

Etymology

gjennom dansk; fra lavtyskthrough Danish; from Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre en kroppsdel synlig, særlig ved å fjerne et klesplaggto make a part of the body visible, especially by removing a garment
    • han blottet sin muskuløse overkropphe bared his muscular torso
    • blotte sitt hode for kongento bare one's head before the king
  2. vise fram;
    blottstilleto expose;
    blottleggeto lay bare    to display;
    to exposeblottstille;
    to lay bareblottlegge
    • blotte sin uvitenhetto reveal one's own ignorance
    • et angrepsspill som blottet forsvaretan attacking style of play that left the defence exposed
  3. i uttrykketin the expression

Set phrases

være blottet for mangle fullstendigto lack completely Eng. phraseto be devoid of
  • være blottet for selvtillitto have no self-confidence at all
  • en fotballkamp blottet for spenninga football match completely devoid of excitement
