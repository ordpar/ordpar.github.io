blande II homograph 2

verbverb

to mix · to confuse, to mix up

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt blanda Old Norse blanda

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. helle, røre sammen to pour or stir together
    • blande farger to mix colours
    • blande sand og jord to mix sand and soil
    • blande mel med sukker og salt to mix flour with sugar and salt
  2. forveksle, gå surr i to confuse, to muddle up
    • blande drøm og virkelighet to confuse dream and reality
    • gleden var blandet med bekymring the joy was mixed with worry
