blande II homograph 2

verb

to mix · to confuse, to mix up

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt blandaOld Norse blanda

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. helle, røre sammento pour or stir together
    • blande fargerto mix colours
    • blande sand og jordto mix sand and soil
    • blande mel med sukker og saltto mix flour with sugar and salt
  2. forveksle, gå surr ito confuse, to muddle up
    • blande drøm og virkelighetto confuse dream and reality
    • gleden var blandet med bekymringthe joy was mixed with worry
