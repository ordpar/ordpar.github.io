handikap, handikapp

noun neuter
Inflection

Etymology

etter engelsk handicap, opprinnelig hand in the cap ‘hånden i luen’after English handicap, originally hand in the cap

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i idrett (for eksempel riding, seilsport eller golf): tiltak for å utjevne vinnersjansene i en konkurranse ved at svakere deltakere får fordeler (for eksempel flere slag i golf), eller at de sterkeste får tilleggsvansker (for eksempel distansetillegg i travsport)in sport (for example riding, sailing or golf): an arrangement for evening out the chances of winning a competition, by giving weaker competitors an advantage (extra strokes in golf, for example) or the strongest an added difficulty (such as a longer distance in harness racing)
    • stille med handikapto compete off a handicap
    • hesten startet med handikapthe horse started with a handicap
    • en golfspiller med 15 i handikapa golfer with a handicap of 15
  2. uheldig omstendighet;
    ulempedisadvantage, drawback    an unfortunate circumstance;
    a disadvantageulempe
    • dårlige språkkunnskaper er et klart handikap i denne jobbenpoor language skills are a clear handicap in this job
  3. eldre betegnelse for funksjonshemningdisabilityan older term for disabilityfunksjonshemning
