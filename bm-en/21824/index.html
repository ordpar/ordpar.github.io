hage I homograph 1

noun masculine

garden

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt hagi i betydning 2Old Norse hagi in sense 2

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. (inngjerdet) jordstykke (ved hus, gård) der en kan dyrke frukt, bær, grønnsaker eller prydplanteran (enclosed) piece of ground (by a house or farm) where one can grow fruit, berries, vegetables or ornamental plants
    • en hage med mange blomstera garden with lots of flowers
    • ha en hage å stelle medto have a garden to look after
    • gå ut i hagento go out into the garden
    • jobbe i hagento work in the garden
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som frukthagean orchard, grønnsakhagea vegetable garden, kjøkkenhagea kitchen garden
  2. inngjerdet stykke beitemark;
    havn (II)pasture    a fenced-in piece of grazing land;
    pasturehavn (II)
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som havnehagea fenced-in pasture, hestehagea horse paddock

Set phrases

Edens hage paradisisk steda paradisiacal place Eng. phrasethe Garden of Eden
  • øya er en Edens hage, en perlethe island is a Garden of Eden, a gem
