gro fast

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bli værende i ro så lenge at en blir hengende fast i underlaget eller en annen gjenstandto stay still so long that one becomes stuck to the surface below or to another object
    • hun var som grodd fast i gulvetshe was as if rooted to the floor
    • klærne begynte å gro fast i såretthe clothes were beginning to stick to the wound
  2. i overført betydning: bli værende på et sted eller i en tilstand så lenge at en ikke greier å forlate stedet eller endre tilstandfiguratively: to remain in a place or a state so long that one can no longer leave it or change it
    • jeg ønsker ikke å gro fast noe stedI don't want to get stuck in one place
    • gro fast i sofaen foran tv-ento take root on the sofa in front of the TV
