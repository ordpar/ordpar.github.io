under II homograph 2

prepositionpreposisjon

under · below · during

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt undir, opprinnelig komparativ av norrønt und ‘under’ Old Norse undir, originally a comparative of Old Norse und 'under'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. på undersiden av;
    nedenfor (1)below, innunder (2)underneath     on the underside of;
    belownedenfor (1), underneathinnunder (2)
    • gjemme seg under senga to hide under the bed
    • ligge under dyna to lie under the duvet
    • svømme under vannet to swim underwater
    • hun bor to etasjer under dem she lives two floors below them
    • ligge gjemt under teppet to lie hidden under the blanket
    • kjenne gresset under føttene to feel the grass under one's feet
    • stille seg under tak to take shelter under a roof
  2. med lavere antall eller verdi enn;
    mindre eller færre enn;
    motsatt over (1)above     with a lower number or value than;
    smaller or fewer than;
    opposite of aboveover (1)
    • temperaturen holdt seg under null hele måneden the temperature stayed below zero all month
    • hun er under 20 år she is under 20 years old
    • jakka kostet under 400 kr the jacket cost under 400 kroner
    • det bor under 300 personer i bygda fewer than 300 people live in the village
  3. på baksiden eller innsiden av;
    innenfor (1)inside     on the back or inside of;
    compare innenfor (1)inside
    • ha skitt under neglene to have dirt under one's nails
    • holde en mappe under armen to hold a folder under one's arm
    • være naken under jakka to be naked under one's jacket
    • ha på en tynn trøye under genseren to wear a thin shirt under one's sweater
  4. ved (nedre) kanten av at the (lower) edge of
    • bo under fjellet to live at the foot of the mountain
    • sitte under et tre to sit under a tree
  5. innenfor tidsrommet av;
    samtidig med, i løpet av     within the time span of;
    at the same time as, in the course of
    • rasjonere mat under krigen to ration food during the war
    • være skadd under mesterskapet to be injured during the championship
    • holde seg taus under middagen to stay silent during dinner
    • ulykken inntraff under besøket i hovedstaden the accident happened during the visit to the capital
  6. som rangerer lavere enn;
    som hører til;
    styrt av, underordnet (2)subordinate to     ranking lower than;
    belonging to;
    governed by, subordinate tounderordnet (2)
    • ha mange ansatte under seg to have many employees under one
    • under generalens kommando under the general's command
    • være under amatørmessig ledelse to be under amateurish management
    • seile under norsk flagg to sail under the Norwegian flag
    • dette sorterer under et annet departement this comes under another ministry
  7. utsatt for;
    ledsaget av     exposed to;
    accompanied by
    • godta noe under tvil to accept something with reservations
    • være under tilsyn to be under supervision
    • arbeide under press to work under pressure
    • leve under elendige forhold to live under miserable conditions
    • under forutsetning av noe on condition of something
    • under alle omstendigheter under all circumstances
  8. som er gjenstand for;
    underlagtsubject to, subordinate to     undergoing, in the process of;
    subject tounderlagt
    • boka er under trykking the book is being printed
    • holde noen under oppsikt to keep someone under observation
    • saken er under behandling the matter is under consideration
  9. forbundet med, kjent ved;
    ved (II, 3)associated with     connected with, known by;
    associated withved (II, 3)
    • gå under navnet Gulosten to go by the name Gulosten
    • skrive under pseudonym to write under a pseudonym
  10. (like) før (just) before
    • like under jul just before Christmas
