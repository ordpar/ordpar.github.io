avansement

noun neuter
Inflection

Etymology

fra fransk; jamfør avansereto advance · to be promotedfrom French; compare avansereto advance · to be promoted

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. opprykk til høyere stilling eller grad;
    forfremmelsea promotion (to a higher rank or post)    advancement to a higher post or rank;
    a promotionforfremmelse

  2. i idrett: det å gå videre, for eksempel i en turneringin sport: the act of progressing, for example in a tournament
