utover

preposition

out across · beyond · as (a period) goes on

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. videre ut i området for;
    lenger ut mot, på eller i    further out into the area of;
    further out towards, on or in
    • spre seg utover hele landetto spread across the whole country
    • seile utover fjordento sail out along the fjord
    • renne utover bakkento run out across the ground
  2. brukt som adverb: i retning mot omgivelseneused as an adverb: in a direction towards the surroundings
    • elva flommet utoverthe river flooded outwards
    • svømme utover mot holmento swim out towards the islet
  3. framover i tid;
    på ett eller flere senere tidspunkt    forward in time;
    at one or more later points in time
    • regnbyger utover dagenshowers of rain as the day goes on
    • det kom mye snø utover vinterena lot of snow fell as the winter wore on
  4. ut fra noe og nedoverout from something and downwards
    • falle utover en skrentto fall over the edge of a steep slope
    • fjellet hang utover gårdenthe mountain overhung the farm
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • fjellet hang utoverthe mountain jutted out
  5. lenger eller mer ennfurther or more than
    • tiltak utover det vanligemeasures beyond the ordinary
    • utover fristenbeyond the deadline
