forresten, for resten

adverbadverb

by the way · come to think of it · anyway

EtymologyOpphav

av for (IV)for · because og restrest · remainder · leftovers; opprinnelig med betydning ‘hva det øvrige angår’ from for (IV)for · because and restrest · remainder · leftovers; originally meaning ‘as regards the rest’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

brukt for å uttrykke tilføyelse eller innskytelse: dessutenbesides · in addition, what is more, ved nærmere ettertanke, apropos (II, 1)speaking of which used to express an addition or an aside: besidesdessuten, on second thoughts, speaking of whichapropos (II, 1)
  • det kan forresten være det samme anyway, it doesn't matter
  • forresten er det ikke min sak come to think of it, it's none of my business
  • det var forresten en ting til jeg ville nevne by the way, there was one more thing I wanted to mention
  • har du forresten hørt det nye? have you heard the latest, by the way?
