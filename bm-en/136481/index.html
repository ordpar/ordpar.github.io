gi gass

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. tråkke ned gasspedalen slik at farten øker;
    gasse (III, 3)to open the throttle    to press down the accelerator so that the speed increases;
    to open the throttlegasse (III, 3)     Eng. phraseto step on the gas
    • ved forbikjøring må du gi litt ekstra gasswhen overtaking you need to give it a bit more throttle
  2. sette opp tempoet, yte mer;
    gi jernet (2)to give it your all    to step up the pace, to put in more effort;
    to give it your allgi jernet (2)     Eng. phraseto step on the gas
    • skøyteløperen gav gass ut av siste svingthe skater put on a burst of speed coming out of the final bend
