bakenfor, bakafor

preposition

behind, beyond

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. på baksiden av;
    bak (III)behind · at the back, in the back, bortenforbeyond · on the other side of;
    hinsidesbeyond    at the back of;
    behindbak (III), on the other side ofbortenfor;
    beyondhinsides
    • hun stod rett bakenfor megshe was standing right behind me
    • bakenfor mytenebehind the myths
  2. til et sted på baksidento a place at the back
    • gå bakenfor husetto go round to the back of the house
  3. i overført betydning: til grunn for, til motivasjon forfiguratively: underlying, providing the motivation for
    • det ligger noe bakenfor de nye lovenethere is something behind the new laws
    • bakenfor det helebehind it all
  4. brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • jentene stod fremst og guttene bakenforthe girls stood at the front and the boys behind
