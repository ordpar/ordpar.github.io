tyne

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt týna, av tjón ‘skade’Old Norse týna, from tjón 'damage'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre ende på;
    ødelegge, utrydde;
    drepe    to put an end to;
    to destroy, to wipe out;
    to kill
    • slagbjørnen hadde tynt en sauthe marauding bear had killed a sheep
  2. behandle brutalt;
    pine, plage;
    mobbe    to treat brutally;
    to torture, to torment;
    to bully
    • de tyner naboguttenthey are bullying the boy next door
  3. drive til å yte mest muligto push to the limit
    • tyne kroppen maksimaltto push the body to its absolute limit
    • tyne arbeiderne for å øke produksjonento drive the workers hard to increase production

Set phrases

These set phrases have not been translated yet. They are shown as Bokmålsordboka has them, in Norwegian.

tyne ut
  1. presse ut;
    (nesten) bruke opp
    • tyne ut de siste kreftene
    • det er grenser for hvor mye vi kan tyne ut av ressursene
  2. gjøre utarmet;
    pine ut
    • tyne ut matjorda
