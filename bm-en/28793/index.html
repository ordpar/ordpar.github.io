karneval

noun neuter

carnival · fancy-dress party, masked ball

Inflection

Etymology

fra italiensk, omdannet av gammelitaliensk carnelevare ‘det å ta bort kjøttet’ (i fasten)from Italian, altered from Old Italian carnelevare ‘the taking away of meat’ (during the fast)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i katolske land: fest siste uka før fasten (I, 2)the fasting seasonin Catholic countries: a festival in the last week before the fasting seasonfasten (I, 2)

    • karneval feires før fasten+carnival is celebrated before Lent
  2. maskeballa masked balla masked ballmaskeball
