legge om kursen

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. forandre retningto change direction Eng. phraseto alter course
    • skipet la om kursen noen graderthe ship altered course by a few degrees
  2. forandre plan eller holdningto change one's plan or attitude Eng. phraseto change course
    • regjeringen må legge om kursenthe government has to change course
