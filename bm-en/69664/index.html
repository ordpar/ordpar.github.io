yte II homograph 2

verb

to provide · to render · to perform

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt ýta ‘skyve fram eller ut’, av utout · outwardsOld Norse ýta 'to push forward or out', from utout · outwards

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi, bidra medto give, to contribute
    • yte hjelpto provide help
    • yte noen rettferdighetto do someone justice
  2. klare, prestere, utføreto manage, to perform, to carry out
    • han ytte sitt beste under konkurransenhe gave his best during the competition
