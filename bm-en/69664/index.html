yte II homograph 2

verbverb

to provide · to render · to perform

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt ýta ‘skyve fram eller ut’, av utout · outwards Old Norse ýta 'to push forward or out', from utout · outwards

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gi, bidra med to give, to contribute
    • yte hjelp to provide help
    • yte noen rettferdighet to do someone justice
  2. klare, prestere, utføre to manage, to perform, to carry out
    • han ytte sitt beste under konkurransen he gave his best during the competition
