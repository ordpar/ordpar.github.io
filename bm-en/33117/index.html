kvelde

verb

to grow dark, to draw towards evening · to turn in for the night, to finish work for the day · to have supper

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt kveldaOld Norse kvelda

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bli kveld;
    mørkne    to draw towards evening;
    to grow dark
    • det kveldet alt da vi droevening was already falling when we left
    • det kveldesevening is drawing in
  2. slutte arbeidet for dagen;
    gå til ro for kvelden    to finish work for the day;
    to settle down for the evening
    • de gamle har kveldetthe old folk have turned in for the night
  3. spise kveldsmatto eat supper
    • vi kveldet tidligwe had supper early
  4. i overført betydning: døfiguratively: to die
    • nå har han kveldethe has passed away now
  5. i overført betydning: slutte å virkefiguratively: to stop functioning
    • bilen har kveldathe car has packed up
