synes

verb

to think, to feel (in one's own opinion) · to seem · to be visible, to show

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt sýnastOld Norse sýnast

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. være synlig;
    visesto show, to be visible    to be visible;
    to showvises
    • det vil synes at jeg akkurat har gråttit will show that I've just been crying
    • flekken syntes heldigvis ikke så godtluckily the stain didn't show much
    • sola synes så vidt bak fjelletthe sun is barely visible behind the mountain
  2. se ut til å være;
    virke, framstå som    to appear to be;
    to seem, to come across as
    • det syntes nokså klart hva utfallet vil bliit seemed fairly clear what the outcome would be
    • det hele synes noe vanskeligthe whole thing seems somewhat difficult
    • livet har ofte syntes meningsløstlife has often seemed meaningless
  3. ha inntrykk av;
    mene    to have the impression;
    to be of the opinion
    • jeg synes du ser godt utI think you look good
    • jeg syntes det var rartI thought it was strange
    • hva synes dere?what do you think?
  4. finne for godt;
    like, ønske    to see fit;
    to like, to wish
    • gjøre som du selv synesdo as you see fit

  • jeg synes synd i de eldreI feel sorry for the elderly
