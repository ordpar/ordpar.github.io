kommandere

verb

to order about, to give orders to · to command, to be in command of

Inflection

Etymology

gjennom fransk, fra middelalderlatin, fra latin con- og mandare ‘overlate’; jamfør kon-con-, co-through French, from Medieval Latin, from Latin con- and mandare ‘to entrust’; compare kon-con-, co-

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. pålegge noen å gjøre noe;
    beordre (1)to give orders to    to order someone to do something;
    to give orders tobeordre (1)
    • jeg lar meg ikke kommandereI won’t be ordered around
  2. ha kommando (2)command overto have commandkommando (2) over
    • kommandere et kompanito command a company
