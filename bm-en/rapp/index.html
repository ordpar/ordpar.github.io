“rapp” is an inflected form of:

  • rappe I homograph 1 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to smack · to hit slå, smekke
  • rappe II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to hurry, to get a move on (in rappe seg)
  • rappe III homograph 3 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to swipe, to pinch, to snatch raske til seg
  • rappe IV homograph 4 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to render (a wall), to plaster with lime mortar dekke med et tynt lag kalkmørtel
  • rappe V homograph 5 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to quack snadre
  • rappe VI homograph 6 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to rap (perform rap music) framføre rapp
