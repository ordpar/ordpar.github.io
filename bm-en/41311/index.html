nydelig

adjective

lovely · beautiful · delicious

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk ‘behagelig’, påvirket av nyteto enjoy · to partake of, to consumefrom Low German ‘pleasant’, influenced by nyteto enjoy · to partake of, to consume

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. velsmakendetasty;
    lekker (1)delicious    tastyvelsmakende;
    deliciouslekker (1)
    • maten var nydeligthe food was lovely
    • kakene ser nydelige utthe cakes look delicious
  2. svært pen;
    vakker, skjønn    very pretty;
    beautiful, lovely
    • en nydelig ungea beautiful child
    • for en nydelig utsikt!what a gorgeous view!
    • høsten var nydeligthe autumn was glorious
  3. veldig bra;
    utmerketexcellent    very good;
    excellentutmerket
    • en nydelig prestasjona superb performance
    • hun satte inn en nydelig skåringshe put away a beautiful goal
  4. brukt ironisk for å uttrykke skepsis eller mild kritikkused ironically to express scepticism or mild criticism
    • du er meg en nydelig enyou're a fine one, you are
    • en nydelig historiea fine story indeed
