synge

verb

to sing

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt syngjaOld Norse syngja

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. frambringe toner med stemmen, særlig slik at de blir til en melodi med ordto produce notes with the voice, especially so that they form a melody with words
    • synge en sangto sing a song
    • de synger i et korthey sing in a choir
    • alle sang medeveryone sang along
    • de synger falsktthey sing out of tune
    • han har gått og sunget i flere århe has been singing for several years
    • jeg går og synger på en barnesangI keep singing a children's song to myself
    • vi skal synge julen innwe are going to usher in Christmas with song
  2. lage lyder som minner om sangto make sounds resembling singing
    • fuglene sangthe birds were singing
    • gresshoppene sang langs veikantenthe grasshoppers were chirping along the roadside
    • jeg synes nordlendingene synger slik når de snakkerI think people from northern Norway sing like that when they talk
  3. klinge (II, 1)to ring, gjalleto resound, to echo, to ring out;
    smelle (III, 1)to bang    to ringklinge (II, 1), to resoundgjalle;
    to bangsmelle (III, 1)
    • det sang i stålet når hammeren traffthe steel rang when the hammer struck
  4. suse (1)to rush, klukke (3)to gurgleto rushsuse (1), to gurgleklukke (3)
    • bekken synger i sommerkveldenthe brook sings in the summer evening
    • det suste og sang for ørenethere was a rushing and singing in his ears
  5. dikte (1)to write poetryto write poetrydikte (1)
    • naturen som dikteren synger omthe nature that the poet sings of
