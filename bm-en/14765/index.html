finne IV homograph 4

verbverb

to find

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt finna Old Norse finna

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. komme over, treffe på, oppdage to come across, run into, discover
    • finne en skatt to find a treasure
    • finne igjen noe en har mistet to find something you had lost
    • finne noen død to find someone dead
    • jeg fant hammeren i boden I found the hammer in the shed
    • de finner ikke veien they can't find the way
    • har dere funnet en plass å spise? have you found a place to eat?
  2. oppnå (1)to attain to attainoppnå (1)
    • finne formen to find one's form
    • finne trøst to find comfort
  3. komme fram til, tenke ut, utlede to arrive at, to work out, to deduce
    • finne ut av noe to figure something out
    • finne en løsning to find a solution
    • finne fram til folk to track down people
    • finne fram til løsninger to arrive at solutions
  4. synes (3)to think, vurdereto assess, to evaluate · to consider (doing something) · to value, to appraise to thinksynes (3), to assessvurdere
    • finne noe mistenkelig to find something suspicious
    • finne tiden moden to consider the time ripe
    • finne noen skyldig to find someone guilty
    • finne grunn til to find reason to
