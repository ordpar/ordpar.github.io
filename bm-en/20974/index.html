gripe

verb

to grab, to seize · to catch · to move, to grip (emotionally)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt grípaOld Norse grípa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ta tak i;
    trive    to take hold of;
    to grab
    • gripe øksato grab the axe
    • gripe tak i noeto take hold of something
    • gutten grep ballenthe boy caught the ball
    • han grep om håndleddet henneshe grasped her wrist
  2. i overført betydning: benytte seg avfiguratively: to make use of
    • gripe sjansento seize the chance
    • gripe mulighetene som dukker oppto seize the opportunities that come along
  3. fakketo nabto nabfakke
    • grip tyven!catch the thief!
    • gripe noen på fersk gjerningto catch someone red-handed
  4. gjøre sterkt inntrykk på;
    bevege, fengsle;
    jamfør grepetdeeply moved og gripendemoving, touching, gripping    to make a strong impression on;
    to move, to captivate;
    compare grepetdeeply moved and gripendemoving, touching, gripping
    • appellen grep forsamlingenthe appeal moved the gathering
    • være sterkt grepet av noeto be deeply moved by something

gjøreto set about, to tackle;
to do Eng. phraseto go about something

gripe etter
  1. strekke seg etterto reach out for
    • hun grep etter en blyantshe reached for a pencil
  2. komme med;
    nevne    to come up with;
    to mention     Eng. phraseto grasp at
    • han griper etter den første og beste unnskyldningen etter tapetafter the defeat he grasps at the first excuse that comes to hand
gripe inn gå imellom;
ta affæreto step in;

  • hun grep inn og fikk stoppet den nye ordningenshe intervened and managed to stop the new arrangement
gripe inn i hverandre
  1. passe inn i hverandreto fit into one another Eng. phraseto interlock
    • tannhjulene griper inn i hverandrethe cogwheels mesh with one another
  2. ha sammenheng med hverandreto be connected with one another Eng. phraseto be intertwined
    • historiene griper inn i hverandrethe stories are intertwined

  • han griper seg i å lyttehe catches himself listening in
  • hun skulle til å si noe, men grep seg i detshe was about to say something, but stopped herself

to fall back on Eng. phraseto resort to
  • gripe til voldto resort to violence
