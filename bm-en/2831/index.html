arve II homograph 2

verb

to inherit

Inflection

Etymology

av arvan inheritance · heredity · a legacy, heritagefrom arvan inheritance · heredity · a legacy, heritage

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. få noe til eie fra (avdød) personto receive something as one's own from a (deceased) person
    • arve tronen etter sin morto inherit the throne from one's mother
    • arve en formueto inherit a fortune
    • arve klærto inherit clothes
  2. få, ta overto get, to take over
    • den nye regjeringen arvet mange problemer fra den forrigethe new government inherited many problems from the previous one
  3. få overført egenskaper eller særtrekk (gjennom kjønnsceller) fra et opphavsindividto have qualities or traits passed on (through the germ cells) from a parent organism
    • jenta har arvet nesen til faren og det gode humøret til morenthe girl has inherited her father's nose and her mother's good temper

Set phrases

arve ned la gå i arv;
gi i arvto let something pass on as an inheritance;
to leave something to one's heirs Eng. phraseto hand down
  • huset arves ned til neste generasjonthe house is handed down to the next generation
  • familiebedriften er arvet nedthe family business has been passed down
