sett II homograph 2

noun neuter

a start · a leap · a jolt

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. hopp, sprang;
    rykk    a jump, a leap;
    a jerk
    • hunden gjorde et sett framthe dog made a leap forward
  2. uvilkårlig rykk i kroppenan involuntary jolt of the body
    • det gikk et sett gjennom henneshe gave a start
    • han våknet med et setthe woke with a start
