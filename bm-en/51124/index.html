sesong

noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

a season

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

gjennom fransk saison ‘årstid’, ‘såtid’; fra latin, av serere ‘så’ through French saison ‘season’, ‘sowing time’; from Latin, from serere ‘to sow’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. periode eller årstid for et bestemt arbeid, en bestemt virksomhet eller en tilbakevendende prosess a period or time of year for a particular kind of work, a particular activity or a recurring process
    • sesong for skreifiske the season for cod fishing
    • skiløperne våre har alt begynt sesongen our skiers have already started their season
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som badesesongthe swimming season, the bathing season, høysesongpeak season, high season, the peak time, pollensesongthe pollen season, the hay fever season, turistsesongthe tourist season
  2. rekke av episoder i en tv-serie a run of episodes in a TV series
    • de ventet spent på sesong fire they were eagerly awaiting season four
