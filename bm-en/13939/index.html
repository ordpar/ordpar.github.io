fart

noun masculine or feminine

speed, pace · drive, go, life

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk vart, beslektet med fare (II)to go, to travel · to rush, to race; samme opprinnelse som ferda journey, an expedition · conduct, behaviour · a procession, a partyfrom Low German vart, related to fare (II)to go, to travel · to rush, to race; same origin as ferda journey, an expedition · conduct, behaviour · a procession, a party
