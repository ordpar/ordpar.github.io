mene, meine

verb

to think, to be of the opinion · to mean · to intend

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt meina; fra lavtyskOld Norse meina; from Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ha i tankene;
    tenke på;
    sikte til    to have in mind;
    to think of;
    to refer to
    • det mener du ikke!you don't mean that!
    • si det en menerto say what one means
    • jeg skjønner ikke hva du menerI don't understand what you mean
  2. ha til hensiktto intend
    • hun mente ikke å gjøre noe galtshe didn't mean to do anything wrong
    • det var ikke så galt ment som det var sagtit wasn't meant as badly as it came out
    • mene alvorto be in earnest
    • han mente det godthe meant well
  3. tro, synesto believe, to think
    • jeg mener at jeg så det i avisen i gårI think I saw it in the paper yesterday
    • han visste ikke hva han skulle mene om den sakenhe didn't know what to think about the matter
  4. brukt for å framheve noe som et faktumused to emphasise something as a fact
    • ja, det skulle jeg mene!yes, I should think so!

Set phrases

mene på ha til hensiktto intend Eng. phraseto mean to
  • hun var ment på å reiseshe was intending to travel
