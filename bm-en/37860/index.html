mene, meine

verbverb

to think, to be of the opinion · to mean · to intend

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt meina; fra lavtysk Old Norse meina; from Low German

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. ha i tankene;
    tenke på;
    sikte til     to have in mind;
    to think of;
    to refer to
    • det mener du ikke! you don't mean that!
    • si det en mener to say what one means
    • jeg skjønner ikke hva du mener I don't understand what you mean
  2. ha til hensikt to intend
    • hun mente ikke å gjøre noe galt she didn't mean to do anything wrong
    • det var ikke så galt ment som det var sagt it wasn't meant as badly as it came out
    • mene alvor to be in earnest
    • han mente det godt he meant well
  3. tro, synes to believe, to think
    • jeg mener at jeg så det i avisen i går I think I saw it in the paper yesterday
    • han visste ikke hva han skulle mene om den saken he didn't know what to think about the matter
  4. brukt for å framheve noe som et faktum used to emphasise something as a fact
    • ja, det skulle jeg mene! yes, I should think so!

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

mene på ha til hensikt to intend Eng. phraseto mean to
  • hun var ment på å reise she was intending to travel
