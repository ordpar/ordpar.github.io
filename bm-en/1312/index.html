ålreit, all right

adjective

all right, OK · decent, likeable

Inflection

Etymology

fra engelsk ‘helt rett’from English 'completely right'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. helt i ordencompletely fine
    • det er ålreit for megthat is fine by me
  2. om person: grei, påliteligof a person: nice, dependable
    • en ålreit fyra decent guy
  3. brukt som interjeksjon: ja vel, okused as an interjection: all right, OK
    • ålreit, kom igjen!all right, let's go!
