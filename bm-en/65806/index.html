utspill, utspell

noun neuter

an initiative, an overture · a move · a lead (in cards)

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å spille ut, for eksempel kort eller ballthe act of playing something out, for instance a card or a ball
  2. handling eller ytring som setter noe i gang;
    initiativ (1)an initiative    an action or statement that sets something in motion;
    an initiativeinitiativ (1)
    • regjeringen kom med flere utspill i valgkampenthe government made several policy overtures during the election campaign

Set phrases

ha utspillet skulle ta neste skrittto be the one to take the next step Eng. phraseto have the ball in one's court
  • de har nå utspilletit is their move now
