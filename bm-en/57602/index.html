strek

noun masculine

line, stroke · prank, stunt

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt strikOld Norse strik

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. risset eller tegnet linjea scratched or drawn line
    • tegne en rett strekto draw a straight line
    • lage streker på arketto make lines on the sheet
    • det var skåret inn streker i tømmeretlines had been cut into the timber
    • læreren setter strek under alle feilthe teacher underlines every mistake

  3. startstrekthe starting linethe starting linestartstrek

  4. smal stripea narrow stripe
