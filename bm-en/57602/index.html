Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. risset eller tegnet linje a scratched or drawn line
    • tegne en rett strek to draw a straight line
    • lage streker på arket to make lines on the sheet
    • det var skåret inn streker i tømmeret lines had been cut into the timber
    • læreren setter strek under alle feil the teacher underlines every mistake
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som blyantstreka pencil line, krittstreka chalk line, krøllstreka wavy line, skråstreka slash
  2. en tegners linjeføring (1)linework a draughtsman's lineworklinjeføring (1)
    • han har en elegant strek he has an elegant line
    • her ser vi ham i tegnerens strek here we see him drawn in the illustrator's own line
  3. startstrekthe starting line the starting linestartstrek
    • løperne stod på streken the runners stood on the line
  4. smal stripe a narrow stripe
    • en fugl med hvite streker på siden a bird with white streaks along its side
  5. linje i (tenkt) gradinndeling a mark in an (imagined) scale of degrees
    • noen streker for langt a few notches too far
  6. kompasstreka compass point a compass pointkompasstrek
    • vinden dreide noen streker mot vest the wind veered a few points to the west
  7. overraskende, listig eller rampete handling;
    påfunn;
    puss (III)a prank, spikk (II)a practical joke     an unexpected, quick-witted act;
    a stunt;
    a prankpuss (III), a practical jokespikk (II)
    • en artig strek an amusing prank
    • gjøre mange gale streker to get up to all sorts of mischief
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som fantestreka mischievous prank, genistreka stroke of genius, narrestreka foolish caper, revestreka cunning trick, skøyerstreka practical joke
