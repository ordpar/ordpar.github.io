utbredt

adjective
Inflection

Etymology

jamfør utbreto spread · to disseminatecompare utbreto spread · to disseminate

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. spredt eller foldet ut;
    lagt utover    spread or unfolded;
    laid out
    • utbredte vingeroutspread wings
  2. alminnelig (2)widespread, vanlig (2)common;
    kjent    widespreadalminnelig (2), commonvanlig (2);
    well known
    • et utbredt syna widely held view
    • korrupsjon var svært utbredt i landetcorruption was very widespread in the country
    • utbredte oppfatningerwidespread beliefs
