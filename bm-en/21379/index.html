grådig

adjective

greedy · really, terribly (as an intensifier)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt gráðugr, av gráðr ‘begjær, hunger’Old Norse gráðugr, from gráðr ‘desire, hunger’

Senses and Example Sentences
