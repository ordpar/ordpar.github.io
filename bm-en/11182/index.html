dunke

verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

trolig av norrønt duna ‘dønne, brake’ probably from Old Norse duna 'to rumble, to boom'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gi en (hul) bankelyd;
    banke (II, 3)to pound     to make a (hollow) thumping sound;
    to poundbanke (II, 3)
    • hjertet dunket his heart was thumping
  2. slå, støte eller sparke uten at det lager sterk lyd to hit, bump or kick without making a loud noise
    • dunke hodet mot noe to bump one's head against something
    • dunke noen i ryggen to thump someone on the back
