dunke

verb
Inflection

Etymology

trolig av norrønt duna ‘dønne, brake’probably from Old Norse duna 'to rumble, to boom'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi en (hul) bankelyd;
    banke (II, 3)to pound    to make a (hollow) thumping sound;
    to poundbanke (II, 3)
    • hjertet dunkethis heart was thumping
  2. slå, støte eller sparke uten at det lager sterk lydto hit, bump or kick without making a loud noise
    • dunke hodet mot noeto bump one's head against something
    • dunke noen i ryggento thump someone on the back
