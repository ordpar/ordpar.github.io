foregå

verb

to take place · to go on

Inflection

Etymology

av fore-fore-from fore-fore-

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gå foranto go ahead, to lead the way
    • foregå med et godt eksempelto lead by example
  2. finne sted, gå for segto take place, to go on
    • stevnet foregår på Bislettthe meet is being held at Bislett
    • hva er det som foregår her?what is going on here?
    • handlingen foregår i Sør-Frankrikethe story is set in the south of France
    • pågripelsen foregikk uten dramatikkthe arrest went off without any drama
    • planleggingen har foregått i årevisthe planning has been going on for years
