elektrisk

adjective

electric, electrical

Inflection

Etymology

av nylatin electricus, (vis) electrica ‘elektrisk (kraft)’, laget av den engelske fysikeren W. Gilbert, 1540–1603; opprinnelig gresk elektron ‘rav’from New Latin electricus, (vis) electrica ‘electric (force)’, coined by the English physicist W. Gilbert, 1540–1603; originally Greek elektron ‘amber’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som frambringer, inneholder, leder eller drives av elektrisitetwhich produces, contains, conducts or is powered by electricity
    • elektrisk oppvarmingelectric heating
    • elektrisk lyselectric light
    • elektrisk ladningelectric charge
    • elektrisk gjerdean electric fence
    • elektriske artiklerelectrical goods
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • boligene varmes opp elektriskthe homes are heated electrically
  2. i overført betydning: intenst, spentfiguratively: intense, charged
    • stemningen var elektriskthe atmosphere was electric
    • samspillet mellom de to var elektriskthe chemistry between the two was electric
