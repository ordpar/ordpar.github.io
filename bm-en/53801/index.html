skulle

verb

shall, will, to be going to · to be to, must · should (in the past form)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt skuluOld Norse skulu

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. om framtid: komme til åused about the future: to be going to
    • det skal bli forandringer her i bygdathere are going to be changes here in the village
    • vi skal reise i morgenwe're leaving tomorrow
  2. brukt for å uttrykke påbud, befaling eller krav;
    måtte (2)must    used to express an order, command, or demand;
    mustmåtte (2)
    • du skal pusse tennene dine hver kveldyou must brush your teeth every evening
    • du skal si meg hva det eryou are to tell me what it is
    • sannheten skal framthe truth must come out
    • jeg skulle hilse fra farfather sends his regards
  3. brukt for å uttrykke ønske eller oppfordring;
    burde (1)should    used to express a wish or encouragement;
    shouldburde (1)
    • du skulle hjelpe broren dinyou should help your brother
    • alle skulle gjøre sin plikteveryone should do their duty
  4. brukt for å uttrykke løfte eller forsikringused to express a promise or assurance
    • jeg skal nok ventedon't worry, I'll wait
    • det skal nok gå brait'll all work out fine
  5. brukt for å stadfeste noeused to affirm something
    • jeg skal si det gikk braI must say it went well
  6. ha ord på seg for noeto have a reputation for something
    • han skal være flinkhe has a reputation for being capable
  7. brukt for å vise høflighetused to express politeness
    • skal det være en kopp kaffe til?would you like another cup of coffee?
  8. brukt for å uttrykke formodning eller mulighetused to express supposition or possibility
    • det skulle vel la seg gjørethat ought to be possible
    • han skal være rikhe's said to be rich
    • det skal være ekte gullit's supposedly real gold
    • det skal du regne medyou can count on that
    • det skulle la seg gjøreit should be doable
  9. brukt sammen med adverb (og uten hovedverb) for å uttrykke retningused with an adverb (and without a main verb) to express direction
    • jeg skal hjemI'm going home
    • hva skulle han nå der?what was he going there for?
