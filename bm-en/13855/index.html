fare II homograph 2

verbverb

to go, to travel · to rush, to race

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt fara Old Norse fara

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. forflytte seg;
    reise, dra;
    jamfør farendetravelling, wayfaring     to move from one place to another;
    to travel, to go;
    compare farendetravelling, wayfaring
    • fare til byen to go into town
    • de for sin vei they went on their way
    • fare vidt omkring to travel far and wide
    • la alt håp fare to abandon all hope
    • ungene farer fra dør til dør og selger lodd the kids go from door to door selling raffle tickets
  2. sveipe over eller gjennom to sweep over or through something
    • fare over åkeren to sweep across the field
    • fare gjennom boka to skim through the book
  3. bevege seg raskt;
    suse, fyke, renne     to move quickly;
    to whizz, to fly, to race
    • toget for forbi the train rushed past
    • komme farende to come rushing along
    • det for kaldt gjennom henne a chill shot through her
  4. befatte seg (med) to deal (with), to occupy oneself (with)
    • fare med sladder to spread gossip
    • fare stille med noe to keep quiet about something
