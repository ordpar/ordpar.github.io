tetne

verb

to clog up, to become blocked · to thicken, to grow denser

Inflection

Etymology

av tettdense · close · tightfrom tettdense · close · tight

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bli tett eller tettereto become clogged or more clogged
    • røret har tetnetthe pipe has clogged up
  2. bli (mer) kompaktto become (more) dense
    • tåken tetnerthe fog is thickening
