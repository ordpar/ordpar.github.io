grave III homograph 3

verb

to cure (fish)

Inflection

Etymology

samme opprinnelse som grave (II)to dig · to delve, to dig around fordi fisken tidligere ble gravet nedsame origin as grave (II)to dig · to delve, to dig around because the fish used to be buried in the ground
