forsøke

verbverb

to try · to attempt

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk, fra tysk ‘søke mot et mål’; av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess og søketo look for, to search for · to apply for · to seek from Low German, from German 'to make for a goal'; from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess and søketo look for, to search for · to apply for · to seek
