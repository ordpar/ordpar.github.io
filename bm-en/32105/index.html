krigsmakt

noun masculine or feminine

a warring power, a belligerent state · the armed forces, the military · military force

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stat som deltar i krig eller har stor evne til å føre krig;
    jamfør makt (2)power (a state)    state that takes part in a war or has great capacity to wage war;
    compare makt (2)power (a state)

    jamfør makt (5)fighting forces    a forcestyrke (I, 5) with the equipment to be deployed in war;
    compare makt (5)fighting forces

  3. makt (1)power som utøves i krigpowermakt (1) exercised in war
