tukt

noun feminine or masculine
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt tykt; fra lavtyskOld Norse tykt; from Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. streng oppdragelse eller atferdsnorm fra forelder eller annen formynder;
    disiplin (1)discipline, kustusfirm control, age (I, 2)obedience    strict upbringing or standard of behaviour imposed by a parent or other guardian;
    disciplinedisiplin (1), firm controlkustus, obedienceage (I, 2)
    • holde streng tuktto keep strict discipline
    • bli holdt under kirkens tuktto be held under the discipline of the church
    • bøye seg for foreldrenes tuktto bow to one's parents' discipline
  2. tukt (1)strict discipline med bruk av kroppslig straffstrict disciplinetukt (1) with the use of corporal punishment
    • bli utsatt for tuktto be subjected to corporal punishment
    • på den tiden var det vanlig å praktisere tuktat that time it was common to administer corporal punishment
