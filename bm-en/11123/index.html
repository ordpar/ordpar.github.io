dukke II homograph 2

verb

to duck · to dive, to go under

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk; jamfør dykketo dive, to submergefrom Low German; compare dykketo dive, to submerge

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bøye eller huke seg nedto bend or crouch down
    • dukke for et slagto duck a blow
    • dukke seg inn gjennom dørento duck in through the door
  2. duppe ned i eller under vannto dip into or under water
    • dukke noen underto push someone under
    • dukke seg ned i isvannetto plunge into the icy water
  3. sette grundig på plass, ydmyketo put firmly in one's place, to humiliate
    • han ble kraftig dukkethe was thoroughly taken down a peg
  4. gå under vann;
    dykketo dive, to submerge    to go under water;
    to divedykke
    • ubåten dukketthe submarine dived
    • dukke etter noeto dive for something
