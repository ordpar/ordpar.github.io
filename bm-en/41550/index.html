nøde

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt neyða; jamfør nødtobliged, forced (to) · to have toOld Norse neyða; compare nødtobliged, forced (to) · to have to

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. tvinge, presseto force, to press
    • du nøder meg til å si opp stillingenyou are forcing me to resign my post
    • han nøddes til å selge gårdenhe was forced to sell the farm
  2. overtaleto persuade, to talk (someone) intoto persuadeovertale
    • han nødet gjestene til å forsyne seghe pressed the guests to help themselves
    • vi lot oss ikke nøde lengewe did not need much persuading
