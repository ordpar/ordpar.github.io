nøde

verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt neyða; jamfør nødtobliged, forced (to) · to have to Old Norse neyða; compare nødtobliged, forced (to) · to have to

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. tvinge, presse to force, to press
    • du nøder meg til å si opp stillingen you are forcing me to resign my post
    • han nøddes til å selge gården he was forced to sell the farm
  2. overtaleto persuade, to talk (someone) into to persuadeovertale
    • han nødet gjestene til å forsyne seg he pressed the guests to help themselves
    • vi lot oss ikke nøde lenge we did not need much persuading
