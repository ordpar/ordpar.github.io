jaså

interjection

oh really?, is that so? · I see

Senses and Example Sentences

brukt for å uttrykke forbauselse, ironisk tilslutning eller lignendeused to express surprise, ironic agreement or the like
  • jaså, er det den karen?oh, so it's that chap, is it?
  • nei jaså, sier du detwell I never, you don't say
