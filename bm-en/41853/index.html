ofre

verbverb

to sacrifice · to devote · to spend

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt offra; av latin offerre ‘bære fram for’ Old Norse offra; from Latin offerre ‘to bring before’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. bære fram et offer (1)an offering to present an offeringoffer (1)
    • ofre til avguder to sacrifice to idols
  2. gi pengebidrag to give a monetary contribution
    • ofre til misjonen to donate to the mission
  3. gi avkall på to give up
    • de ofret livet for det de trodde på they sacrificed their lives for what they believed in
  4. bruke, vie to spend, to devote
    • han ofrer mye tid på trening he devotes a lot of time to training
    • det har jeg ikke ofret en tanke I haven't given it a thought

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

ofre seg for gå helt inn for to commit oneself fully to something Eng. phraseto devote oneself to
  • han ofret seg for kunsten he devoted himself to art
