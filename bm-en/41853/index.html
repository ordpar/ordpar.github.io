ofre

verb

to sacrifice · to devote · to spend

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt offra; av latin offerre ‘bære fram for’Old Norse offra; from Latin offerre ‘to bring before’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bære fram et offer (1)an offeringto present an offeringoffer (1)
    • ofre til avguderto sacrifice to idols
  2. gi pengebidragto give a monetary contribution
    • ofre til misjonento donate to the mission
  3. gi avkall påto give up
    • de ofret livet for det de trodde påthey sacrificed their lives for what they believed in
  4. bruke, vieto spend, to devote
    • han ofrer mye tid på treninghe devotes a lot of time to training
    • det har jeg ikke ofret en tankeI haven't given it a thought

Set phrases

ofre seg for gå helt inn forto commit oneself fully to something Eng. phraseto devote oneself to
  • han ofret seg for kunstenhe devoted himself to art
