skaffe I homograph 1

verb

to get, to obtain · to provide, to find (something for someone)

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

få tak i;
sikre segto get hold of;
to secure for oneself
  • vi må skaffe hjelpwe must get help
  • han skaffet dem noe å spisehe got them something to eat
  • skaffe noen en jobbto find someone a job
  • skaffe seg utstyrto get oneself some equipment
  • hun skaffet seg kundershe acquired customers
  • de skaffer seg problemerthey are making trouble for themselves
