bonde

noun masculine

farmer · pawn (in chess)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt búandi, bóndi, opprinnelig presens partisipp av búa ‘fastboende’Old Norse búandi, bóndi, originally the present participle of búa 'permanently settled'
