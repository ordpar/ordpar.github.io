konstituere

verbverb

to appoint temporarily, to appoint in an acting capacity · to constitute, to formally set up, to make up

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra latin ‘fastsette’ from Latin ‘to establish’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. tilsette midlertidig to appoint temporarily
    • konstituere noen i en stilling to appoint someone to a post on a temporary basis
  2. brukt som adjektiv: fungerende;
    forkortet kst.     used as an adjective: acting;
    abbreviated kst.
    • konstituert konsernsjef acting chief executive
  3. organisere i faste former to organise into a settled form
    • konstituere en stortingsgruppe to formally constitute a parliamentary group
    brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
    • holde et konstituerende møte to hold an inaugural meeting
  4. utgjøre eller danne hovedtrekk i to make up or form the main features of
    • felles minne til en nasjon konstituerer felles fortid a nation's shared memory constitutes a shared past
