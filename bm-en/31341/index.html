konstituere

verb

to appoint temporarily, to appoint in an acting capacity · to constitute, to formally set up, to make up

Inflection

Etymology

fra latin ‘fastsette’from Latin ‘to establish’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. tilsette midlertidigto appoint temporarily
    • konstituere noen i en stillingto appoint someone to a post on a temporary basis
  2. brukt som adjektiv: fungerende;
    forkortet kst.    used as an adjective: acting;
    abbreviated kst.
    • konstituert konsernsjefacting chief executive
  3. organisere i faste formerto organise into a settled form
    • konstituere en stortingsgruppeto formally constitute a parliamentary group
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • holde et konstituerende møteto hold an inaugural meeting
  4. utgjøre eller danne hovedtrekk ito make up or form the main features of
    • felles minne til en nasjon konstituerer felles fortida nation's shared memory constitutes a shared past
