innimellom

preposition

now and then, occasionally · in among, in between · here and there, in places

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. inne blant, inne mellomin among, in between
    • føle seg innestengt innimellom de høye huseneto feel hemmed in among the tall buildings
  2. brukt som adverb: inn mellomused as an adverb: in between
    • det var trangt, men vi kom så vidt innimellomit was a tight fit, but we just barely got in between
  3. brukt som adverb: her og derused as an adverb: here and there
    • innimellom var veien speilglattin places the road was like a sheet of ice
  4. brukt som adverb: av og til, en gang iblantused as an adverb: now and then, once in a while
    • vi arbeidet hardt, men innimellom slo vi oss løswe worked hard, but now and then we let our hair down
