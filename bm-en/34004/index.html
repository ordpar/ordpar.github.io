latter

noun masculine

laughter · a laugh

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt hlátr; samme opprinnelse som le (III)to laughOld Norse hlátr; same origin as le (III)to laugh

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å lelaughter
    • bryte ut i latterto burst out laughing
    • han brast ut i latterhe burst into laughter
    • en kald, hånlig lattera cold, scornful laugh
    • veksle mellom latter og gråtto alternate between laughter and tears
    • en god latter forlenger liveta good laugh lengthens your life
    • latteren runget gjennom salenthe laughter rang through the hall
    • latteren sitter løstlaughter comes easily
  2. noe som en ler avsomething one laughs at
    • han var til latterhe was a laughing stock
