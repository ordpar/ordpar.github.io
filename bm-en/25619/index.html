idet

subjunction

just as · as · since

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. innleder en leddsetning som uttrykker at noe skjer samtidig;
    i samme øyeblikk som;
    da (II, 1)when    introduces a subordinate clause expressing that something happens at the same time;
    at the very moment when;
    compare da (II, 1)when
    • idet vi kom, dro dejust as we arrived, they left
    • han tok på seg jakka idet det ringte påhe was putting on his jacket just as the doorbell rang
  2. innleder en leddsetning som uttrykker årsak;
    siden (I, 2)since, fordi (1)because    introduces a subordinate clause expressing cause;
    sincesiden (I, 2), becausefordi (1)
    • ulykken skjedde idet føreren ikke så i speiletthe accident happened because the driver did not look in the mirror
    • idet han visste for lite om saken, ville han ikke uttale segsince he knew too little about the matter, he did not want to comment
