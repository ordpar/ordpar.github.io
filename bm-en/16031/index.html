forby

verb

to forbid, to ban

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt fyrirbjóða ‘by mot noe’; av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess og by (II)to offer · to invite · to bidOld Norse fyrirbjóða 'to bid against something'; from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess and by (II)to offer · to invite · to bid

    nekte (1)to refuse    not to allow;
    to refusenekte (1)

  2. hindreto prevent, to stop · to obstruct, to hold upto prevent, to stophindre
