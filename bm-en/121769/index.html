legge inn

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sette inn;
    installere (1)to install    to put in;
    to installinstallere (1)
    • legge inn vann og elektrisitetto lay on water and electricity
  2. plassere i eller innenfor noeto place in or inside something
    • hun la inn snus i munnenshe put snus in her mouth
    • vi la inn resultatene i databasenwe entered the results into the database
    • de velger å legge inn en pause i studienethey choose to build a break into their studies
  3. gi beskjed om;
    melde    to give notice of;
    to report
    • legge inn bud på en leilighetto put in a bid on a flat
    • legge inn en protestto lodge a protest
