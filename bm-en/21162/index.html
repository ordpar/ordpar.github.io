grunnlegge

verb

to found, to establish · to base (something) on

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. begynne på;
    etablere, skape, danne    to start up;
    to establish, to create, to form
    • grunnlegge et hjemto found a home
    • hun var i sin tid med på å grunnlegge orkesteretin her day she helped to found the orchestra
  2. bygge (på), tufte (II)to found on, to base on · to lay the foundations ofto build (on), to found on, to base ontufte (II)
    • bevegelsen er grunnlagt på radikale ideerthe movement is founded on radical ideas
