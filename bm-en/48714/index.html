rive II homograph 2

verb

to tear, to rip · to tear down, to demolish · to grate

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt rífaOld Norse rífa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. slite i to eller flere biter;
    flerre, spjære, flenge    to tear into two or more pieces;
    to rip, to rend, to slash
    • rive sund noeto tear something to shreds
    • bjørnen rev i hjel sauthe bear mauled sheep to death
    • rive seg sund på en spikerto tear oneself open on a nail
  2. gni sund;
    skrape (II, 1)to scrape, raspe (1)to grate    to shred by rubbing;
    to scrapeskrape (II, 1), to grateraspe (1)
    • rive ostto grate cheese
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • revne gulrøttergrated carrots
  3. gnito rub, ripe (III, 2)to strike (a match)to rubgni, to strike (a match)ripe (III, 2)
    • rive av en fyrstikkto strike a match
  4. slite, svito ache, to burn
    • gikta rev og slet i kroppenthe gout gnawed and tore at his body
    • brennevinet rev i halsenthe spirits burned his throat
  5. rykke, sliteto jerk, to wrench
    • rive seg i håretto tear at one's hair
    • rive av seg luato yank off one's cap
    • rive noen over endeto knock someone over
    • stormen rev vekk husetthe storm tore the house away
  6. rasere, jevne med jordato demolish, to raze to the ground
    • rive et husto demolish a house
    • kvartalet bør rivesthe block ought to be torn down
  7. velte, dytte nedto knock over, to knock down
    • rive et hinderto knock down a hurdle
    • høydehopperen rev i første forsøkthe high jumper knocked the bar off on the first attempt
