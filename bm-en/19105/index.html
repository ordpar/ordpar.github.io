garderobe

noun masculine

changing room · cloakroom · wardrobe

Inflection

Etymology

av fransk garder ‘vokte’ og robe ‘kjole’from French garder 'to guard' and robe 'dress'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. rom til å ha ytterklær i, særlig i offentlig bygninga room for outdoor clothing, especially in a public building
    • henge fra seg kåpen i garderobento hang up one's coat in the cloakroom
  2. omkledningsroma changing room for skuespillere, idrettsutøvere, skoleelever eller lignendea changing roomomkledningsrom for actors, athletes, schoolchildren or the like
    • garderoben til fotballagetthe football team's dressing room
    • gå til garderoben for å skifteto go to the changing room to change
  3. de klærne en person eierthe clothes a person owns
    • ha en stor garderobeto have a large wardrobe
    • fornye garderobento refresh one's wardrobe
  4. garderobeskapa large wardrobea large wardrobegarderobeskap
    • frittstående garderoberfree-standing wardrobes
