atskillig, adskillig

adjectiveadjektiv

quite a lot of, a fair amount of · quite a few, a good many · considerably

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

trolig fra dansk adskillig; jamfør norrønt atskiljanligr ‘ulik’ probably from Danish adskillig; compare Old Norse atskiljanligr ‘different’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. i entall, ofte som substantiv: (ganske) mye, ikke så lite in the singular, often as a noun: (quite) a lot, not a little
    • det var atskillig å utsette på planen there was quite a lot to criticise about the plan
    • han hadde atskillig penger he had a fair amount of money
    som adverb: used as an adverb:
    • atskillig bedre, større considerably better, bigger
  2. i flertall, ofte som substantiv: (ganske) mange, ikke så få in the plural, often as a noun: (quite) a good many, not a few
    • atskillige mener det quite a few people think so
    • atskillige av de tilstedeværende several of those present
    • hun hadde atskillige bøker she had a good many books
