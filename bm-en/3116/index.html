atskillig, adskillig

adjective

quite a lot of, a fair amount of · quite a few, a good many · considerably

Inflection

Etymology

trolig fra dansk adskillig; jamfør norrønt atskiljanligr ‘ulik’probably from Danish adskillig; compare Old Norse atskiljanligr ‘different’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i entall, ofte som substantiv: (ganske) mye, ikke så litein the singular, often as a noun: (quite) a lot, not a little
    • det var atskillig å utsette på planenthere was quite a lot to criticise about the plan
    • han hadde atskillig pengerhe had a fair amount of money
    som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • atskillig bedre, størreconsiderably better, bigger
  2. i flertall, ofte som substantiv: (ganske) mange, ikke så fåin the plural, often as a noun: (quite) a good many, not a few
    • atskillige mener detquite a few people think so
    • atskillige av de tilstedeværendeseveral of those present
    • hun hadde atskillige bøkershe had a good many books
