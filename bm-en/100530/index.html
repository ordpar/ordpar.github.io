kalles

verb

to be summoned, to be called in · to be called, to be known as

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bli innkaltto be summoned
    • pasienten kalles inn til kontroll om en ukethe patient will be called in for a check-up in a week
  2. bli kalt;
    gå under navn av    to be called;
    to go by the name of
    • Ghana kaltes tidligere GullkystenGhana was formerly called the Gold Coast
    • hvitkål kalles også vinterkålwhite cabbage is also known as winter cabbage
