“lur” is an inflected form of:

  • lure I homograph 1 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to wonder · to fool, to trick · to lurk vente på en anledning
  • lure II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to nap, to doze ta seg en lur
