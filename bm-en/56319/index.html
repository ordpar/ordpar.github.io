spre, sprede

verb

to spread · to scatter, disperse

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. strø utover;
    jamfør spredtscattered, sparse    to scatter about;
    compare spredtscattered, sparse
    • spre gjødselto spread fertiliser
  2. fordeleto distribute · to divide up, to share outto distributefordele

  3. gjøre kjent;
    utbreto disseminate    to make known;
    to disseminateutbre

  4. framkalle (1)to bring aboutto bring aboutframkalle (1)
