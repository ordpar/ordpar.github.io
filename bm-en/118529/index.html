jo II homograph 2

adverb

after all, you know, of course · the … the … (jo … desto …)

Senses and Example Sentences

brukt trykklett: da (vel), nå, nok, som kjent;
jamfør dess (II)all the (more, softer, …) · the (in the paired 'the … the …')used unstressed: after all, you know, no doubt, as is well known;
compare dess (II)all the (more, softer, …) · the (in the paired 'the … the …')
  • det er jo bare tullthat's just nonsense, of course
  • det er jo slik det erwell, that's the way it is
  • det skulle jo bare mangleit's the least one could do
  • det er jo det jeg sierbut that's what I'm saying
  • det er jo nettopp det du har gjortbut that's exactly what you've done
  • der kommer de alt, jothere they come already, you see
  • hun er jo over 70 årshe's over 70, after all
  • de er jo så snillethey're so kind, you know
