rutine

noun masculine

routine · procedure · experience

Inflection

Etymology

fra fransk opprinnelig ‘kunnskap om ruten’from French, originally ‘knowledge of the route’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ferdighet eller dyktighet oppnådd ved øvelse;
    erfaring, øvelse;
    jamfør rutinertseasoned    skill or proficiency gained through training;
    experience, practice;
    compare rutinertseasoned
    • hun har den rutinen vi trenger på lagetshe has the experience we need on the team
    • det er viktig å opparbeide seg en rutineit is important to build up some experience
  2. plan en følger regelmessig eller i bestemte situasjoner;
    fast måte å gjøre noe på    a plan one follows regularly or in particular situations;
    a set way of doing something
    • de hadde faste rutiner for rengjøringthey had set routines for cleaning
    • kontoret må få bedre rutiner for saksbehandlingthe office needs better procedures for handling cases
    • rutinen var å sove i seks timer og arbeide i seksthe routine was to sleep for six hours and work for six
  3. ytre, mekanisk ferdighet;
    mekanisk, automatisert handling eller oppførsel;
    vane (1)a habit, tralta rut    outward, mechanical skill;
    mechanical, automated action or behaviour;
    a habitvane (1), a ruttralt
    • arbeidet er blitt ren rutinethe work has become pure routine
    • hun hadde en fast rutine hver morgenshe had a fixed routine every morning
  4. i IT: samling instrukser i et datamaskinprogramin IT: a set of instructions within a computer program
