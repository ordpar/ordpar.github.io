enda I homograph 1

subjunction

even though, although

Senses and Example Sentences

innleder en leddsetning som uttrykker innrømmelse;
selv omeven though · although · even if, til tross for atintroduces a subordinate clause expressing a concession;
even thoughselv om, despite the fact that
  • han er sterk enda han er litenhe is strong even though he is small
  • enda hun prøver, får hun det ikke tilalthough she tries, she can't manage it
  • enda det ikke er den beste boka hans, er det den som har solgt mestalthough it isn't his best book, it's the one that has sold the most
