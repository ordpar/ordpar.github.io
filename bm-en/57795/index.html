strømme I homograph 1

verb

to stream, to pour · to flock

Inflection

Etymology

av dansk strømme, jamfør norrønt streymafrom Danish strømme, compare Old Norse streyma

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. flyte i sterk strøm;
    flomme (1)to be in flood    to flow in a strong current;
    to be in floodflomme (1)
    • vannet strømmer inn i kjellerenwater is pouring into the cellar
    • regnet strømmet nedthe rain streamed down
    • tårene strømmetthe tears flowed
  2. komme i store mengderto come in great numbers
    • bidragene strømmer inndonations are pouring in
    • kundene strømmer til den nye butikkencustomers are flocking to the new shop
    • folk strømmet ut av konsertsalenpeople streamed out of the concert hall
    • gamle minner strømmet påold memories came flooding back
  3. om lyd eller andre sanseinntrykk: lyde (I)to sound · to go · to read, velleto well outof sound or other sense impressions: to soundlyde (I), to well outvelle
    • det strømmet musikk ut gjennom vinduetmusic came pouring out through the window

Set phrases

strømme over gi tydelig uttrykk for noeto express something openly Eng. phraseto overflow with
  • strømme over av begeistringto overflow with enthusiasm
