premie

noun masculine

a prize · an insurance premium

Inflection

Etymology

av latin pre- og emere ‘ta’; jamfør pre-pre- (before, in front of)from Latin pre- and emere 'to take'; compare pre-pre- (before, in front of)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. belønninga reward, pris (I, 2)a prizea rewardbelønning, a prizepris (I, 2)
    • hun vant mange premier i turnshe won many prizes in gymnastics
    • det var sølvtøy i premiethe prize was silverware
  2. gevinsta gain · a profit · a prizea prizegevinst
    • det er store premier i tippingthere are big prizes in the football pools
  3. avgift for forsikring (3)insurancea fee for insuranceforsikring (3)
    • selskapet hever premienethe company is raising its premiums
