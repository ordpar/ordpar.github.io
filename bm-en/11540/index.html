dømme

verbverb

to judge · to sentence, to convict · to referee

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt dǿma; av dom (I)a judgement · a sentence · a verdict Old Norse dǿma; from dom (I)a judgement · a sentence · a verdict

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. avsi dom (I, 1)a judgement i en rettssak;
    idømmeto sentence someone to, to impose a penalty on someone     to pass a judgementdom (I, 1) in a court case;
    to sentence (someone) toidømme
    • bli dømt skyldig to be found guilty
    • dømme noen til fengsel to sentence someone to prison
    • hun ble dømt til å betale saksomkostningene she was ordered to pay the legal costs
    • den tiltalte ble dømt til døden the defendant was sentenced to death
    brukt som substantiv: used as a noun:
    • den dømte the convicted person
  2. utpeke vinneren eller passe på at reglene blir fulgt i en konkurranse to pick the winner, or see that the rules are followed, in a competition
    • dømme en kamp to referee a match
    • dømme i en fiolinkonkurranse to judge a violin competition
    • dommeren dømte frispark the referee awarded a free kick
  3. gjøre seg opp en mening om;
    vurdereto assess, to evaluate · to consider (doing something) · to value, to appraise, bedømmeto judge · to assess     to form an opinion about;
    to assessvurdere, to judgebedømme
    • dømme ut fra seg selv to judge others by oneself
    • det får andre dømme om that is for others to judge
    • etter alt å dømme er det umulig by all accounts it is impossible
  4. sette seg til doms over;
    laste (II)to blame · to reproach     to set oneself up as a judge over;
    to blamelaste (II)
    • dømme andre strengere enn seg selv to judge others more harshly than oneself
    brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
    • ha en dømmende holdning to have a judgemental attitude
