vørde, vøre

verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt virða, vyrða Old Norse virða, vyrða

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. sette noe eller noen høyt;
    akte (3)to esteem, verdsette (2)to value;
    respektere (1)to respect     to think highly of something or someone;
    to esteemakte (3), to valueverdsette (2);
    to respectrespektere (1)
    • han blir mindre vørdt enn de andre he is held in lower regard than the others
    • hun vørder ikke noen ting she has no regard for anything
  2. ta hensyn til;
    bry seg om;
    enseto notice, to take notice of, to heed     to take into consideration;
    to care about;
    to take notice ofense
    • du skal ikke vørde slikt snakk you shouldn't pay any attention to talk like that
    • hun vørder ikke å svare ham she doesn't bother to answer him
