stemning

noun masculine or feminine

mood · atmosphere · sentiment

Inflection

Etymology

av stemme (II)to vote · to be right, to add up · to tunefrom stemme (II)to vote · to be right, to add up · to tune

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sinnstilstanda state of mind, a mental state som preger en person eller gruppestate of mindsinnstilstand that characterises a person or a group
    • være i god stemningto be in a good mood
    • selskapet var i løftet stemningthe party was in high spirits
    • stemningen i salen var høythe mood in the hall was buoyant
  2. holdning, innstillingattitude, disposition
    • greie å snu stemningento manage to turn the mood around
    • det var stemning for å utsette sakenthere was support for postponing the matter
  3. preg (1)character, atmosfære (2)atmospherecharacterpreg (1), atmosphereatmosfære (2)
    • månen skapte en trolsk stemningthe moon created a magical atmosphere
