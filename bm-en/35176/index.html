likeså

adverbadverb

likewise · just as

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. på samme måte;
    like ens (2)likewise     in the same way;
    likewiselike ens (2)
    • gå du bort og gjør likeså (Luk 10,37) go and do likewise (Luke 10:37)
    • gulvet var malt hvitt, og veggene likeså the floor was painted white, and the walls the same
    • for meg har nyheten likeså vært gledelig for me too the news has been welcome
  2. i samme grad, like mye;
    like (III, 1)equally, liksåjust as, if not more so     to the same degree, just as much;
    equallylike (III, 1), just as, if not more solikså
    • de ble likeså godt stående helt stille they might just as well remain standing perfectly still
    • jeg kan likeså gjerne gå hjem I might just as well go home
    • mistilliten til alt nytt var likeså stor som kjærligheten til alt det kjente the mistrust of everything new was just as great as the love of everything familiar
