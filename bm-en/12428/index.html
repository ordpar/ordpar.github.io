ellers

adverb

otherwise · else · apart from that

Etymology

av ellerorfrom elleror

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i motsatt fallotherwise, if not
    • stans, ellers går det galtstop, or it'll go wrong
    • det var Mona, hvem ellers?it was Mona, who else?
    • vi må gå, ellers kommer vi for sentwe have to go, or we'll be late
  2. i andre tilfeller;
    vanligvis    in other cases;
    usually
    • gjøre som ellersto do as usual
    • den ellers så pratsomme Elsa satt taus i en krokElsa, normally so talkative, sat silent in a corner
    • nei, ellers takk!no, thanks all the same!
  3. på andre måter;
    utenom det nevnte;
    dessuten    in other respects;
    apart from what has been mentioned;
    besides
    • ellers går det braapart from that, things are fine
    • var det ellers noe?was there anything else?
    • Norge og verden ellersNorway and the rest of the world
  4. forrestenby the way · come to think of it · anywayby the wayforresten
    • jeg er ellers i byen på den tidenas it happens, I'll be in town at that time
