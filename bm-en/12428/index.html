ellers

adverbadverb

otherwise · else · apart from that

EtymologyOpphav

av elleror from elleror

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. i motsatt fall otherwise, if not
    • stans, ellers går det galt stop, or it'll go wrong
    • det var Mona, hvem ellers? it was Mona, who else?
    • vi må gå, ellers kommer vi for sent we have to go, or we'll be late
  2. i andre tilfeller;
    vanligvis     in other cases;
    usually
    • gjøre som ellers to do as usual
    • den ellers så pratsomme Elsa satt taus i en krok Elsa, normally so talkative, sat silent in a corner
    • nei, ellers takk! no, thanks all the same!
  3. på andre måter;
    utenom det nevnte;
    dessuten     in other respects;
    apart from what has been mentioned;
    besides
    • ellers går det bra apart from that, things are fine
    • var det ellers noe? was there anything else?
    • Norge og verden ellers Norway and the rest of the world
  4. forrestenby the way · come to think of it · anyway by the wayforresten
    • jeg er ellers i byen på den tiden as it happens, I'll be in town at that time
