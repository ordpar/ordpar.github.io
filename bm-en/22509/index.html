haste

verb

to be urgent · to hurry

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk hastenfrom Low German hasten

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. skynde segto hurry;
    være i travel eller rastløs bevegelse    to hurryskynde seg;
    to be in busy or restless motion
    • alle hastet av stedeveryone hurried off
    • elva haster mot havetthe river rushes towards the sea
    • folkene haster fra sted til stedpeople hurry from place to place
  2. være presserendeto be urgent
    • sakene hastet veldigthe matters were very urgent
    • det haster med å finne en løsningthere is an urgent need to find a solution
