overrumple

verbverb

to catch off guard, to take by surprise

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

etter tysk, opprinnelig ‘overfalle med larm’; jamfør rumleto rumble modelled on German, originally ‘to attack with a din’; compare rumleto rumble

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

komme uforvarende på;
overraske (1)to come upon unexpectedly to catch off guard;
to come upon unexpectedlyoverraske (1)
  • overrumple en fiende to catch an enemy off guard
  • spørsmålene hennes overrumplet meg her questions took me by surprise
brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
  • et overrumplende initiativ a surprise initiative
  • en overrumplet keeper a goalkeeper caught off guard
