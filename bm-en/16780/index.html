forsegle

verbverb

to seal

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk; av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess og segl (I)a seal from Low German; from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess and segl (I)a seal

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. lukke med segl (I)a seal to close with a sealsegl (I)
    • brevet var forseglet the letter was sealed
  2. validereto validate med segl (I, 2)a seal to validatevalidere with a sealsegl (I, 2)
    • forsegle et dokument to seal a document
  3. dekke slik at innholdet ikke kommer ut to cover so that the contents cannot get out
    • forsegle en melkekartong to seal a milk carton
    • forsegle en krukke to seal a jar
  4. stenge for adgang;
    plombereto seal (with a security seal), låse (1)to lock     to close off access;
    to seal (with a security seal)plombere, to locklåse (1)
    • politiet forseglet døra til huset the police sealed the door of the house
  5. i overført betydning: fullbyrdeto complete, validereto validate gjennom en gest figuratively: to completefullbyrde, to validatevalidere through a gesture
    • forsegle en avtale med et håndtrykk to seal an agreement with a handshake
    • forsegle sin kjærlighet med et kyss to seal one's love with a kiss
