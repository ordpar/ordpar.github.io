forsegle

verb

to seal

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk; av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess og segl (I)a sealfrom Low German; from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess and segl (I)a seal
