“gir” is an inflected form of:

  • gi verbverb (present)(presens) to give · to hand (over) · to yield, to produce la få, (over)rekke, levere
  • gire I homograph 1 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) Norwegian only skifte gir
  • gire II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) Norwegian only ha gir
  • gire III homograph 3 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) Norwegian only skjære ut, avvike fra kursen, frivillig eller ufrivillig
