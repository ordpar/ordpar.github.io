alt i ett

again and again, constantly · all-in-one, all in one place

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: enone
  1. stadig constantly Eng. phraseagain and again
    • han så bak seg alt i ett he kept looking behind him
  2. samling av flere opplysninger, funksjoner eller lignende a collection of several pieces of information, functions or the like Eng. phraseall-in-one
    • i denne læreboka finner du alt i ett om dette emnet in this textbook you find everything about this subject in one place
