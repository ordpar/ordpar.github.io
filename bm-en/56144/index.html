spise II homograph 2

verbverb

to eat · to have (a meal)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt spiza ‘forsyne med mat’ Old Norse spiza 'to supply with food'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. innta næring i form av mat, føde eller måltid;
    ete (1)to eat, sette til livsto consume, fortære (1)to devour     to take in nourishment in the form of food, sustenance or a meal;
    to eatete (1), to consumesette til livs, to devourfortære (1)
    • spise et stykke brød to eat a piece of bread
    • de spiser frokost they are having breakfast
    • vi spiste middag klokka fem we had dinner at five o'clock
  2. kunne eller ville ha noe som mat;
    tåle (3)to tolerate (a food), like (IV)to like · to enjoy     to be able or willing to have something as food;
    to tolerate (a food)tåle (3), to likelike (IV)
    • hun spiser ikke kjøtt she doesn't eat meat
    • han spiser bare glutenfri mat he only eats gluten-free food
  3. i overført betydning: bruke opp;
    ta     figuratively: to use up;
    to take
    • sykkelfeltet spiser litt av fortauet the cycle lane eats into the pavement a little
    • formuesskatten spiser verdiene i selskapet the wealth tax is eating away at the company's assets
    • tv-titting spiser mye tid watching TV eats up a lot of time
