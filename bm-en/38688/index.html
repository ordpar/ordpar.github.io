miste

verb

to lose · to miss

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt mist, av misse; i betydingen ‘komme for sent’ etter engelskOld Norse mist, from misse; in the sense ‘to arrive too late’, after English

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ikke lenger ha;
    rote bort, tape    to no longer have;
    to mislay, to lose
    • miste nøkleneto lose one's keys
    • miste håretto lose one's hair
    • miste synetto lose one's sight
    • miste foreldrene sineto lose one's parents
    • miste håpetto lose hope
    • miste oversiktento lose track
    • vi har ingen tid å mistewe have no time to lose
    • de mister motetthey lose heart
    • de mistet alt de eide i brannenthey lost everything they owned in the fire
    • tre personer har mistet livetthree people have lost their lives
  2. komme for sent tilto arrive too late for
    • miste bussento miss the bus

Set phrases

miste munn og mæle bli stum;
ikke få fram et ordto be struck dumb;
to be unable to get a word out Eng. phraseto be left speechless
miste seg selv tape eller svekke egen personlighet eller identitetto lose or weaken one's own personality or identity Eng. phraseto lose one's sense of self
