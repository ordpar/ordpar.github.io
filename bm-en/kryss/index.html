“kryss” is an inflected form of:

  • krysse I homograph 1 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to cross · to tick · to intersect merke med et kryss
  • krysse II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to croze · to cut the groove in a barrel stave lage kryss (i tønnestaver)
