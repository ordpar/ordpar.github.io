lindre

verb

to relieve, to ease, to alleviate

Inflection

Etymology

av tysk lindern; beslektet med linnmildfrom German lindern; related to linnmild

Senses and Example Sentences

gjøre noe mindre plagsomt eller smertefullt;
mildne, lette, dempeto make something less troublesome or painful;
to soften, to relieve, to ease
  • lindre smerterto relieve pain
  • sorgen kunne lindres over tidthe grief could be eased over time
