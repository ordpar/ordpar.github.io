skyld I homograph 1

noun feminine or masculine

blame, fault · guilt · debt

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt skuld, skyldOld Norse skuld, skyld

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ansvar for skade, feil, uhell eller lignenderesponsibility for damage, a fault, an accident or the like
    • gi noen skylden for noeto blame someone for something
    • legge skylden på andreto put the blame on others
    • ta på seg skylden for noeto take the blame for something
    • være uten skyld i det som skjeddeto be blameless in what happened
    • det var jeg som fikk skylden for uhelletI was the one who got the blame for the accident
  2. synd, feilsin, wrongdoing
    • bekjenne sin skyldto confess one's guilt
  3. gjeld (I, 1)debtdebtgjeld (I, 1)
    • sitte i skyld til oppover øreneto be up to one's ears in debt
  4. avgift, skatt eller ytelse, særlig på fast eiendom;
    jamfør landskyldland rent, ground rent (paid by a tenant farmer)    a due, tax or payment, especially on real property;
    compare landskyldland rent, ground rent (paid by a tenant farmer)
