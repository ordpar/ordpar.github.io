full gass

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: gassgas · the accelerator · throttle
  1. full fart eller framdrift på bil eller annet kjøretøy full speed or acceleration in a car or other vehicle Eng. phrasefull throttle
    • kjøre for full gass to drive at full throttle
    • gi full gass opp bakken to floor it up the hill
  2. maksimal innsats, kraft eller ytelse maximum effort, force or performance Eng. phraseflat out
    • bandet gav full gass på scenen the band gave it everything on stage
    • det er full gass i oljebransjen for tiden the oil industry is booming at the moment
