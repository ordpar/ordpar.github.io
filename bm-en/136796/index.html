trille terningB2, level B2

to rate, to score (in a review, with a die score from one to six) · to decide at random · to toss a coin

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: terningdice, die · cube (of food or the like)

    • hvis jeg skulle trille terning, hadde konserten fått terningkast tre if I were to give it a dice score, the concert would have got a three
  2. avgjøre noe tilfeldig to decide something at random Eng. phraseto toss a coin
    • de må ha trillet terning for å komme til denne avgjørelsen they must have rolled dice to arrive at this decision
