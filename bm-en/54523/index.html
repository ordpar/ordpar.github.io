slokne, slukne

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt sloknaOld Norse slokna

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. slutte å brenne eller lyseto stop burning or shining
    • bålet har sloknetthe campfire has gone out
    • lampa sloknetthe lamp went out
  2. blekne (1)to grow pale, svinneto fade away, to dwindle, to vanishto grow paleblekne (1), to fade awaysvinne
    • stjernene sloknetthe stars went out
  3. miste sin glansto lose its lustre
    • øynene hennes sloknetthe light went out of her eyes
    • smilet slokner i ansiktetthe smile fades from his face
  4. i overført betydning: sovneto fall asleepfiguratively: to fall asleepsovne

    dø (1)to die    figuratively: to stop living;
    to diedø (1)

  6. gå i stå;
    stanse    to come to a standstill;
