påvise

verb

to demonstrate · to detect · to point out

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bevise eksistensen av noeto prove the existence of something
    • han påviser i boka si at teorien er riktigin his book he demonstrates that the theory is correct
    • det ble påvist en direkte forbindelse mellom dema direct connection between them was established
  2. utpeketo single out, to pick out · to designate, to appoint · to identify, angito state · to specify · to inform onto point oututpeke, to indicateangi
    • påvise ulykkesstedetto point out the site of the accident
