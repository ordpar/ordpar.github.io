nyte

verb

to enjoy · to partake of, to consume

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt njótaOld Norse njóta

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ha stor glede av;
    kjenne velbehag og lyst av    to take great pleasure in;
    to feel contentment and desire from
    • nyte den vakre utsiktento enjoy the beautiful view
    • han nøt virkelig livethe really enjoyed life
    • vi må nyte finværet når vi har detwe have to make the most of the good weather while it lasts
    • jeg nyter morgenen med utsikt over fjordenI enjoy the morning with a view over the fjord
  2. få eller ha nytte eller fordel avto get or have benefit or advantage from
    • hun nyter stor tillit i befolkningenshe enjoys great trust among the population
    • nyte visse rettigheterto enjoy certain rights
  3. smake, spise, innta eller lignende, ofte med stort velværeto taste, eat, consume or the like, often with great pleasure
    • de nøt et glass vin til matenthey had a glass of wine with their food
    • hun nyter alltid en sigarett etter middagshe always enjoys a cigarette after dinner
    • nyte rusmidlerto take intoxicants

Set phrases

nyte godt av ha fordel avto have the benefit of Eng. phraseto benefit from
  • de ansatte nøt godt av de gode velferdsordningenethe employees benefited from the good welfare schemes
