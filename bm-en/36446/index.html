lære III homograph 3

verbverb

to learn · to teach

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt læra Old Norse læra

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gi kunnskap i noe;
    undervise;
    øve opp i     to give someone knowledge of something;
    to teach;
    to train in
    • lære barna å lese to teach the children to read
    • hun lærte ham å sykle she taught him to ride a bike
    • de har lært barna sine tålmodighet they have taught their children patience
  2. tilegne seg kunnskap om eller ferdighet i noe;
    gradvis bli i stand til å gjøre eller forstå noe;
    erfare     to acquire knowledge of or skill in something;
    to gradually become able to do or understand something;
    to find out by experience
    • lære alfabetet to learn the alphabet
    • hun har lært å spille piano she has learnt to play the piano
    • jeg vil lære meg fransk I want to learn French
    • ha lett for å lære to be a quick learner
    • ha tungt for å lære to be a slow learner
    • lære av feilene sine to learn from one's mistakes
    • hun sprang av sted det forteste hun hadde lært she ran off as fast as her legs could carry her
    • hun lærte seg å leve med sykdommen she learnt to live with her illness
  3. hevde som en sannhet;
    forkynne     to assert as a truth;
    to preach
    • dualismen lærer at mennesket er delt i to deler dualism teaches that the human being is divided into two parts
