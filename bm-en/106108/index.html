gå unna

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: gåto walk · to go · to run, to work (of a machine) · to turn out, unnaaway (from) · clear of, out of the way · aside
  1. holde seg borte fra;
    vike for noen eller noe     to keep away from;
    to give way to someone or something     Eng. phraseto get out of the way
  2. foregå i raskt tempo;
    gå fort     to proceed at a rapid pace;
    to go fast
    • forberedelsene gikk unna på et par timer the preparations were done in a couple of hours
  3. få rask avsetning to sell quickly Eng. phraseto fly off the shelves
    • varene gikk unna the goods flew off the shelves
