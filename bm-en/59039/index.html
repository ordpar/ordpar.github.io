synde

verb

to sin, to offend against

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

begå synd (1)a sin;
gjøre noe som blir oppfattet som umoralsk eller galt;
bryte lov, regel eller lignendeto commit a sinsynd (1);
to do something regarded as immoral or wrong;
to break a law, rule or the like
  • synde mot Gudto sin against God
  • de har syndet mot det sjette budetthey have sinned against the sixth commandment
  • mange bilister synder mot parkeringsforbudetmany drivers offend against the parking ban
