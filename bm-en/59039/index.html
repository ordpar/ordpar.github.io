synde

verbverb

to sin, to offend against

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

begå synd (1)a sin;
gjøre noe som blir oppfattet som umoralsk eller galt;
bryte lov, regel eller lignende to commit a sinsynd (1);
to do something regarded as immoral or wrong;
to break a law, rule or the like
  • synde mot Gud to sin against God
  • de har syndet mot det sjette budet they have sinned against the sixth commandment
  • mange bilister synder mot parkeringsforbudet many drivers offend against the parking ban
