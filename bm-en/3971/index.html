avvise

verb

to reject · to turn down · to turn away

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. nekte adgangto refuse entry to
    • hotellet måtte avvise mange gjesterthe hotel had to turn away many guests
    • bli avvist i dørato be turned away at the door
  2. si nei til;
    avslåto turn down, to reject · to decline;
    forkasteto reject · to discard · to throw out    to say no to;
    to turn downavslå;
    to rejectforkaste
    • avvise et tilbudto turn down an offer
    • retten avviste ankenthe court dismissed the appeal
    • kroppen avviste den nye nyrenthe body rejected the new kidney
  3. tilbakeviseto refute, to rebut, to disproveto refutetilbakevise
    • avvise en beskyldingto deny an accusation
    • avvise alle forsøk på tilnærmelserto rebuff all attempts at advances
    • bli avvistto be rejected
