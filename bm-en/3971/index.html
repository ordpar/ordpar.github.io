avvise

verbverb

to reject · to turn down · to turn away

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. nekte adgang to refuse entry to
    • hotellet måtte avvise mange gjester the hotel had to turn away many guests
    • bli avvist i døra to be turned away at the door
  2. si nei til;
    avslåto turn down, to reject · to decline;
    forkasteto reject · to discard · to throw out     to say no to;
    to turn downavslå;
    to rejectforkaste
    • avvise et tilbud to turn down an offer
    • retten avviste anken the court dismissed the appeal
    • kroppen avviste den nye nyren the body rejected the new kidney
  3. tilbakeviseto refute, to rebut, to disprove to refutetilbakevise
    • avvise en beskylding to deny an accusation
    • avvise alle forsøk på tilnærmelser to rebuff all attempts at advances
    • bli avvist to be rejected
