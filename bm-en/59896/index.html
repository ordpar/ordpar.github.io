tape II homograph 2

verb

to lose

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt tapaOld Norse tapa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. lide tap, misteto suffer a loss, to lose
    • tape en formueto lose a fortune
    • tape interessen for noe(n)to lose interest in something or someone
    • tape noe(n) av syne – miste øyekontakten med; se syn (I)sightto lose sight of something or someone – to no longer be able to see; see syn (I)sight
    • tape terreng – bli drevet tilbake, miste oppslutningto lose ground – to be driven back, to lose support
    • ikke være tapt bak en vogn – se vogncarriageto be nobody's fool – see vogncarriage
  2. lide nederlag, bli slåttto suffer defeat, to be beaten
    • tape en krig, en sak, en cupkampto lose a war, a case, a cup match
    som adjektiv i presens partisipp:as an adjective in the present participle:
    • den tapende partthe losing party
  3. refleksivt:used reflexively:
    • hun har tapt segshe has lost her looks
    • foredraget taper seg på trykkthe lecture loses something in print
    • tråkket tapte seg i ura – ble borte, utydeligthe path faded out in the scree – disappeared, became indistinct
    • hun har tapt seg flere kilo – gått flere kilo ned i vektshe has lost several kilos – gone down several kilos in weight

Set phrases

gi tapt gi opp, komme til kortto give up, to fall short Eng. phraseto admit defeat
  • laget måtte gi tapt i finalenthe team had to concede defeat in the final
gå tapt gå til spille, bli borteto go to waste, to vanish Eng. phraseto be lost
  • fire menneskeliv gikk taptfour lives were lost
tape seg
  1. forringes i utseende eller kvalitet;
    falme    to deteriorate in looks or quality;
    to fade     Eng. phraseto go downhill
    • han taper seg med årenehe is losing his looks as the years go by
    • orkesteret har tapt segthe orchestra isn't what it used to be
  2. bli (gradvis) borte;
    svinne hen    to (gradually) disappear;
    to dwindle away
    • veien taper seg i tett tåkethe road disappears into thick fog
    • minnene tapte seg med årenethe memories faded over the years
