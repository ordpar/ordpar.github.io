overlate

verb

to leave (something to someone) · to hand over, to give up · to abandon (to a fate)

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. la få til eie, bruk eller lignende;
    avstå, overdra    to let someone have something to own, to use or the like;
    to cede, to make over
    • overlate plassen sin til noento give up your seat to someone
  2. overlate ansvar for noe;
    la noen ta seg av eller utføre noe    to hand over responsibility for something;
    to let someone see to or carry out something
    • jeg overlot til henne å avgjøre sakenI left it to her to settle the matter
  3. gi over til;
    utlevere    to give over to;
    to surrender
    • overlate noen til sin skjebneto abandon someone to their fate

Set phrases

overlate til seg selv la noen greie seg selvto let someone fend for themselves Eng. phraseto leave someone to their own devices
