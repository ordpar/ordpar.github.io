overlate

verbverb

to leave (something to someone) · to hand over, to give up · to abandon (to a fate)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk from Low German

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. la få til eie, bruk eller lignende;
    avstå, overdra     to let someone have something to own, to use or the like;
    to cede, to make over
    • overlate plassen sin til noen to give up your seat to someone
  2. overlate ansvar for noe;
    la noen ta seg av eller utføre noe     to hand over responsibility for something;
    to let someone see to or carry out something
    • jeg overlot til henne å avgjøre saken I left it to her to settle the matter
  3. gi over til;
    utlevere     to give over to;
    to surrender
    • overlate noen til sin skjebne to abandon someone to their fate

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

overlate til seg selv la noen greie seg selv to let someone fend for themselves Eng. phraseto leave someone to their own devices
