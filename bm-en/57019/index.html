stenge II homograph 2

verbverb

to close · to shut · to block

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt stengja, av stanga pole · a rod · a bar Old Norse stengja, from stanga pole · a rod · a bar

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. lukke igjen (og låse) to close up (and lock)
    • stenge døra to shut the door
  2. bli lukket (II, 2)closed og låst to be closedlukket (II, 2) and locked
    • kafeen stenger klokka 20 the café closes at 8 pm
  3. blokkere, hindre, sperre to block, to obstruct, to bar
    • stenge for utsynet to block the view
    • kryssere stengte havneinnløpet cruisers blocked the entrance to the harbour
  4. fange fisk i steng (1)a barrier net to catch fish in a barrier netsteng (1)
    • stenge 70 hl sild to net 70 hectolitres of herring

  2. skille ut og sperre tilgang to separate off and bar access to Eng. phraseto cordon off
    • branntomta er stengt av the fire site has been cordoned off
