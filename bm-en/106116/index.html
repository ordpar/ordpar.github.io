urent trav

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: travtrot, uren
  1. brudd på regelen om at hesten skal gå i trav (I, 1)a trot i travløp a breach of the rule that the horse must keep to a trottrav (I, 1) in a trotting race Eng. phrasea break in stride
    • bli diskvalifisert for urent trav to be disqualified for breaking gait
  2. uredelig virksomhet;
    ureglementert eller skittent spill     dishonest dealings;
    irregular or dirty play     Eng. phrasefoul play
    • ha mistanke om urent trav i saken to suspect foul play in the case
