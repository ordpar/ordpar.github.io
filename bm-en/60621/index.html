tidsmessig

adjective

relating to time, temporal · up-to-date, contemporary, modern

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som har med tid å gjørewhich has to do with time
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • tidsmessig er det lite å sparein terms of time there is little to save
  2. som passer for eller tilfredsstiller tidens kravsuited to, meeting the demands of the times (for example in fashion); up to date
    • vi trenger et mer tidsmessig lovverkwe need a more up-to-date body of legislation
  3. som passer med tidens smak eller mote;
    moderne (2)modern    which suits the taste or fashion of the day;
    modernmoderne (2)
    • et tidsmessig interiøra contemporary interior
    • ha tidsmessige meningerto have up-to-date views
