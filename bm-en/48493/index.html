ri I homograph 1

noun masculine or feminine

a fit · a spell

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt hríðOld Norse hríð

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. plutselig smerte eller sinnsstemning;
    anfall, raptus    a sudden pain or change of mood;
    a fit, a mad impulse
    • få en rito have a fit
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som fødselsria labour pain, a contraction, hosteria coughing fit
  2. kort stunda short while
  3. brukt som etterledd i sammensetninger: periode med hardt værused as a final element in compounds: a period of harsh weather
    also found in compounds:i ord som frostria fit of chills, a shivering fit · a cold snap, a spell of frosty weather
