urett I homograph 1

noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

injustice, a wrong · being in the wrong

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. urimelig handlemåte;
    urettferdighet;
    overgrep     an unreasonable way of acting;
    injustice;
    a violation
    • gjøre urett mot noen to do someone an injustice
    • de har gjort meg urett they have wronged me
    • det ble begått urett mot tolvåringen an injustice was done to the twelve-year-old

    feiltakelse, mistaka mistake, an error, a blunder;
    feilgrepa blunder     the fact of being wrong;
