stå på

to be going on, to be happening · to keep at it, to work hard

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. hende, foregåto happen, to take place Eng. phraseto go on
    • hva er det som står på her?what is going on here?
    • bråket stod ikke lenge på før politiet komthe commotion had not gone on for long before the police arrived
  2. henge i;
    jobbe hardt    to persevere;
    to work hard     Eng. phraseto keep at it
    • de jobber og står på for å rekke fristenthey are working flat out to meet the deadline
