feie

verb

to sweep

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk vegen, norrønt fægja ‘gjøre ren, klar’; beslektet med fagerfair, beautiful, lovelyfrom Low German vegen, Old Norse fægja 'to make clean, clear'; related to fagerfair, beautiful, lovely

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sope, rengjøre med kost eller lignendeto sweep, to clean with a brush or the like
    • feie pipato sweep the chimney
    • hun feiet trappashe swept the stairs
  2. drive (III, 5)to drive away, jage (2)to chase awayto drive awaydrive (III, 5), to chase awayjage (2)
    • feie andre til sideto sweep others aside
  3. fyke (2)to dash, fare (II, 3)to rushto dashfyke (2), to rushfare (II, 3)
    • vinden feide gjennom dalenthe wind swept through the valley
    • hun feide forbishe swept past
    • bevegelsen har feid over store deler av verdenthe movement has swept across large parts of the world

  • fei for din egen dør!look to your own affairs first!
