stand I homograph 1

noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

condition · state · station

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av eldre stande ‘stå’, jamfør stå (III)to stand · to be, to be situated (of a thing in a place) · to say, to be written (of a text) from older stande 'to stand', compare stå (III)to stand · to be, to be situated (of a thing in a place) · to say, to be written (of a text)
