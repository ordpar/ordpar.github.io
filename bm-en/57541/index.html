stramme

verb

to tighten · to be tight

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre stram;
    spenne (II, 1)to stretch taut    to make taut;
    to stretch tautspenne (II, 1)
    • stramme klessnorato tighten the clothes line
    • stramme beltetto tighten the belt
  2. være eller bli trangto be or become tight
    • buksa strammet rundt livetthe trousers were tight around the waist

Set phrases

stramme inn gjøre (noe) knappereto make (something) tighter Eng. phraseto tighten up
  • stramme inn utgifteneto cut back on spending
stramme inn på gjøre (noe) knappereto make (something) tighter Eng. phraseto tighten up on
  • stramme inn på kostnadeneto cut back on costs
stramme opp stive (seg) opp;
irettesette;
refse (en annen)to brace (oneself) up;
to reprimand;
to chastise (someone else)
  • vi får stramme dem opp littwe'll have to give them a bit of a talking-to
