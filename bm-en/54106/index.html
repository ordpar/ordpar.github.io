skål I homograph 1

noun feminine or masculine

a bowl · a toast

Inflection

Etymology

Senses and Example Sentences

    det å skåle (II)to drink a toast, to toast, to clink glasses    a glass or cup of drink that one drinks as a congratulation, as a wish of good luck, in tribute or the like;
    the act of drinking a toastskåle (II)
    • drikke jubilantens skålto drink to the guest of honour
    • utbringe en skål for brudeparetto propose a toast to the bride and groom
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som kjøpskåla toast drunk to seal a deal, kranseskåla topping-out party

  4. den delen av en bh som støtter eller omslutter et bryst;

    a cupcup (2)
