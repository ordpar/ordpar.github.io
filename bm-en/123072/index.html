stridende

adjectiveadjektiv
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av stride (III)to struggle, contend (with) · to fight, battle · to go against, conflict with from stride (III)to struggle, contend (with) · to fight, battle · to go against, conflict with

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. som kjemper i krig eller annen væpnet konflikt fighting in a war or other armed conflict
    • stridende soldater soldiers in combat
  2. brukt som substantiv: stridsdyktig soldat;
    soldat i kamp     used as a noun: a soldier fit for combat;
    a soldier in battle
    • krigen var hard både for sivile og stridende the war was hard on civilians and combatants alike
  3. som stridesfighting one another;
    som er uenige     fighting one anotherstrides;
    in disagreement with each other
    • de stridende partene møttes i rettssalen the opposing parties met in the courtroom
