tarvelig

adjective

shabby, plain, cheap · mean, contemptible

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt þarfligr ‘nødvendig’; av tarva needOld Norse þarfligr 'necessary'; from tarva need

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. billig, simpel, fattigsligcheap, plain, shabby
    • en tarvelig kjolea shabby dress
    • dette er tarvelige greierthis is shoddy stuff
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • leve tarveligto live frugally
  2. sjofel, usselmean, contemptible
    • tarvelig oppførselshabby behaviour
    • et tarvelig menneskea mean-spirited person
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • det var tarvelig gjortthat was a rotten thing to do
