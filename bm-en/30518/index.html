knipe II homograph 2

verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. klype (II)to pinch to pinchklype (II)
    • knipe seg i armen to pinch one's arm
    • knipe av veken på et lys to pinch off the wick of a candle
  2. gripe eller ta raskt to grab or seize quickly
    • katten knep en mus the cat caught a mouse
    • bli knepet i nasking to be caught shoplifting
    • knipe seieren to snatch the victory
  3. klemme, presse to squeeze, to press
    • knipe sammen munnen to press one's lips together
    • knipe seg foran konkurrentene to squeeze ahead of one's competitors
  4. holde hardt;
    være vanskelig     to be tight;
    to be difficult
    • det kniper med kontanter money is tight
    • det knep med å bli ferdig i tide it was a struggle to finish in time
  5. spare unødig to skimp unnecessarily
    • knipe på maten to skimp on food
