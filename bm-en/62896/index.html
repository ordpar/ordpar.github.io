tur I homograph 1

noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

trip, outing, walk · turn

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

gjennom fransk; fra gresk tornos ‘sirkel’ through French; from Greek tornos ‘circle’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. plass i rekkefølge one's place in a sequence, one's turn
    • det er min tur nå! it's my turn now!
    • passe sin tur i køen to keep one's place in the queue
    • stå for tur to be next in line
    • gå etter tur to take turns
    • vente på tur to wait one's turn
  2. kortere eller lengre reise, ferd eller utflukt a journey, trip or outing, short or long
    • ta en tur til byen to take a trip into town
    • gå en tur to go for a walk
    • sykle seg en tur to go for a bike ride
    • gå på tur to go on a hike
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som bytura trip into town, an outing to the city, fottura hike, a walking trip, helgetura weekend trip, a weekend break, skitura ski trip, a skiing outing
  3. hver av flere omganger i et forløp;
    vending, gang     each of several rounds in a course of events;
    a go, a time
    • de gikk flere turer for å hente alle tingene they made several trips to fetch everything
  4. avgrenset del av dans a distinct section of a dance, a figure
    • lære alle turene i reinlender to learn all the figures of the schottische
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som firetura set dance in four figures, sekstura folk dance in six figures, the music for it
