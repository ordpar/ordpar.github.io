digge

verbverb

to love · to be really into

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

trolig gjennom svensk; fra engelsk slang dig ‘sette pris på, like’ probably through Swedish; from English slang dig 'to appreciate, to like'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

like svært godt;
sette stor pris på to like very much;
to be really into
  • digge rock to love rock music
  • grillmat som barna digger barbecue food that the kids are mad about
