innunder

preposition

under, underneath, in under · just before

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. (inn) under;
    bort under    (in) under;
    over under
    • komme innunder traktoren og bli skaddto get in under the tractor and be injured
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • kom innunder så du ikke blir våtcome in under here so you don’t get wet
  2. (inne) under;
    borte under    (in) under;
    away under
    • gjemme seg innunder en lemto hide underneath a hatch
    • innunder trærne var det skyggethere was shade under the trees
  3. like førjust before
    • innunder jul slo været omjust before Christmas the weather turned
