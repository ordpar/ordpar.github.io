innunder

prepositionpreposisjon

under, underneath, in under · just before

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. (inn) under;
    bort under     (in) under;
    over under
    • komme innunder traktoren og bli skadd to get in under the tractor and be injured
    brukt som adverb: used as an adverb:
    • kom innunder så du ikke blir våt come in under here so you don’t get wet
  2. (inne) under;
    borte under     (in) under;
    away under
    • gjemme seg innunder en lem to hide underneath a hatch
    • innunder trærne var det skygge there was shade under the trees
  3. like før just before
    • innunder jul slo været om just before Christmas the weather turned
