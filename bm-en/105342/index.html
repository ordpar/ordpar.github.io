ta på sparket

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sparke direkte videreto kick the ball straight on without stopping it Eng. phraseto take the ball first time
    • ta ballen på sparketto take the ball on the volley
  2. prøve å mestre (noe) uten forberedelse, eller uten å nøleto try to handle something without preparation, or without hesitating Eng. phraseto wing it
    • hen tok utfordringen på sparketthey took on the challenge on the spur of the moment
