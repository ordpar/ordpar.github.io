rettlede, rettleie

verb
Inflection

Etymology

av rett (III)right, correct · straight · straight, directly og lede (II)to lead · to run, to manage · to conductfrom rett (III)right, correct · straight · straight, directly and lede (II)to lead · to run, to manage · to conduct

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. hjelpe med råd, opplysninger og lignende;
    lede på rett lei;
    veilede (1)to guide    to help with advice, information and the like;
    to lead in the right direction;
    to guideveilede (1)
    • kontoret rettleder om reglenethe office provides guidance on the rules
  2. i utdanning: gi faglige råd og informasjon;
    veilede (2)to supervise;
    jamfør rettleder (3)a supervisor    in education: to give academic advice and information;
    to superviseveilede (2);
    compare rettleder (3)a supervisor
    • han rettleder de nye studentenehe supervises the new students
    • hun rettledet meg da jeg skrev masteroppgavenshe supervised me while I was writing my master's thesis
