erkjenne

verb

to acknowledge · to admit · to recognise, apprehend

Inflection

Etymology

fra tyskfrom German

    oppfatte noe som det er    to arrive at certain knowledge of;

  2. vedgå (1)to concede, innrømmeto concedevedgå (1), to admit

    • erkjenne straffskyld for grovt ranto plead guilty to aggravated robbery
