platt

adjective
Inflection

Etymology

gjennom lavtysk og fransk; fra gresk platys ‘flat’through Low German and French; from Greek platys ‘flat’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. simpel og uten raffinement;
    smakløs, flau, plump (II, 2)crude, tactless    coarse and lacking refinement;
    tasteless, lame, crude, tactlessplump (II, 2)
    • en platt vitsa lame joke
    • platt humorcrass humour
  2. med vinden rett bakfrawith the wind straight from behind
    • seile plattto sail before the wind
  3. brukt som adverb: flatt, utstraktused as an adverb: flat, stretched out
    • legge seg platt nedto lie down flat
  4. brukt som forsterkende adverb: helt, aldelesused as an intensifying adverb: completely, utterly
    • platt umuligutterly impossible
