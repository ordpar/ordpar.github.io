fond II homograph 2

noun neuter

a fund · a store, a wealth (of something)

Inflection

Etymology

samme opprinnelse som fond (I)stock (in cooking) · a backdrop, a backgroundthe same origin as fond (I)stock (in cooking) · a backdrop, a background

    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som aksjefondequity fund, krisefondcrisis fund, kulturfondcultural fund

    overflodan abundance    a large quantity;
    an abundanceoverflod
