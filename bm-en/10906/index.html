dristig

adjectiveadjektiv

bold, daring · risqué (of dress or pictures)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av lavtysk drist(e) ‘dristig(e)'; jamfør dristeto venture, to dare (only in fixed expressions) from Low German drist(e) 'bold'; compare dristeto venture, to dare (only in fixed expressions)
