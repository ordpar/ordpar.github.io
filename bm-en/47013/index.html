pytt II homograph 2

interjection

oh well · never mind

Senses and Example Sentences

brukt for å uttrykke at en er likeglad med noeused to express that one is unconcerned about something
  • pytt, ikke verrenever mind, it's not that bad
  • å pytt, det går nok braoh well, I'm sure it'll be fine
