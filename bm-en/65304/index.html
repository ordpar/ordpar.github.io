utfordre

verb

to challenge

Inflection

Etymology

etter tysk; lavtyskafter German; Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. oppfordre sterkt til handling;
    eggeto incite, to egg on, to spur on, hisse (2)to incite (to battle)    to call on someone forcefully to act;
    to egg onegge, to incite (to battle)hisse (2)
    • utfordre noen til duellto challenge someone to a duel
    • han utfordret alle mosjonistene til å delta i løpethe challenged all the recreational runners to take part in the race
  2. være eller opptre kritisk overfor noen (slik at de reagerer);
    provosere (2)to provoke    to be or act critical towards someone (so that they react);
    to provokeprovosere (2)
    • utfordre de etablerte sannheteneto challenge established truths
  3. stille krav tilto make demands on
    • bli utfordret intellektueltto be intellectually challenged
    • utviklingen utfordrer samfunnetdevelopments are challenging society
