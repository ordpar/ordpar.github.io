mure II homograph 2

verb

to build in brick or stone · to lay bricks

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt múra; av mura wall (of brick or stone) · masonry, brickwork · a barrierOld Norse múra; from mura wall (of brick or stone) · masonry, brickwork · a barrier

Senses and Example Sentences

sette opp en mur eller en bygning av murstein og med bindemiddel i fugeneto build a wall or a building out of brick, with mortar in the joints
  • mure et husto build a house in brick
  • mure igjen en døråpningto brick up a doorway
  • mure opp en ny pipeto build a new chimney

Set phrases

mure seg inne isolere seg fra omverdenento isolate oneself from the outside world Eng. phraseto shut oneself away
