adjective

open

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt opinn ‘vendt oppover’; beslektet med oppup · open (with verbs of opening) · up, completely (marking a process finished)Old Norse opinn 'turned upwards'; related to oppup · open (with verbs of opening) · up, completely (marking a process finished)
