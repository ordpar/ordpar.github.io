tulle II homograph 2

verbverb

to joke, to be kidding · to mess about · to wrap, to wind

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. rulle, svøpe, vikle to roll, to wrap, to wind
    • tulle tråden på snella to wind the thread onto the reel
  2. floke to tangle
    • skjorta var tullet sammen som en liten ball the shirt was bundled up into a little ball
  3. handle dumt eller planløst;
    rote omkring, surre, vase     to act foolishly or aimlessly;
    to mess about, to bumble, to muddle
    • jeg har bare gått og tullet på jobben i dag I've just been messing about at work today
    • tulle seg bort i noe ulovlig to get mixed up in something illegal
  4. ta feil, si noe dumt to be mistaken, to say something silly
    • nei, nå tuller jeg visst no, now I've got it wrong
  5. ikke mene alvor;
    tøyse, vase     not to be serious;
    to joke, to talk nonsense
    • ikke hør på henne – hun bare tuller don't listen to her – she's only joking
    • tulle med jentene to fool around with the girls

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

tulle bort
  1. kaste eller sløse vekk to throw away or squander Eng. phraseto fritter away
    • tulle bort penger to fritter money away
  2. rote eller somle bort to misplace or lose through carelessness
    • tulle bort nøklene to mislay the keys
tulle seg bort/vekk gå seg vill to lose one's way Eng. phraseto get lost
  • de tullet seg bort i skogen they got lost in the forest
  • partiet har tullet seg vekk i politikken the party has lost its way politically
tulle seg inn
  1. sette seg fast to become stuck Eng. phraseto get caught up in
    • tauet tullet seg inn i propellen the rope got tangled in the propeller
  2. pakke seg inn to wrap oneself up
    • tulle seg inn i et teppe to wrap oneself up in a blanket
