samvær

noun neuter

time together, being together, company · contact, access (with a child, in family law)

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør norrønt samveracompare Old Norse samvera

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å være sammen;
    lag, selskap    being together;
    company, companionship
    • sosialt samværsocial interaction
    • seksuelt samværsexual intercourse
    • ha selskapelig samværto spend time together socially
    • takk for samværet!thank you for the time together!
  2. i jus: det at et barn og den forelderen som barnet ikke bor sammen med, er sammen til avtalte tiderin law: the arrangement by which a child and the parent the child does not live with spend time together at agreed times, contact, access
