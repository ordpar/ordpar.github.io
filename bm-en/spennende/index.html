“spennende” is an inflected form of:

  • spenne II homograph 2 verbverb (present participle)(presens partisipp) to buckle, strap, fasten · to tense, flex, tighten · to span, range, stretch… spile eller strekke ut
  • spenne III homograph 3 verbverb (present participle)(presens partisipp) to kick støte til med foten
