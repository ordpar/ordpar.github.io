surre I homograph 1

verbverb

to buzz, to hum · to muddle, to get things mixed up · to fry gently

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

lydord, beslektet med svirreto whirr onomatopoeic word, related to svirreto whirr

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. dure svakt;
    summe (I)to buzz, to hum     to drone softly;
    to buzz, to humsumme (I)
    • fluene surret i solveggen the flies were buzzing against the sunlit wall
    • det surret av alle slags tungemål the air buzzed with all kinds of languages
  2. steke ved svak varme to fry over a low heat
    • småørret surret i rømme small trout fried gently in sour cream
  3. løpe rundt;
    svirre (3)to whirl     to spin round;
    to whirlsvirre (3)
    • rokkehjulet surret og gikk the spinning wheel whirred round and round
    • disse tankene surret i hodet ustanselig these thoughts kept going round and round in his head
  4. rote, tulle to muddle, to mess about
    • du bare surrer you’re just getting muddled
    • surre rundt med kompisene to knock about with one’s mates
