snakk

noun masculine or neuter

talk · conversation · nonsense

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk snackfrom Low German snack

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å snakke;
    prat, samtale    the act of talking;
    chat, conversation
    • gi seg i snakk med noento strike up a conversation with someone
    • komme i snakk med noento get talking with someone
    • oppholde noen med snakkto keep someone occupied with talk
  2. rykte, sladderrumour, gossip
    • snakket går om dem på bygdathere is talk about them in the village
  3. tøv, tøysnonsense, rubbish
    • hva er det for snakk?what kind of nonsense is that?
