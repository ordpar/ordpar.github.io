kose

verbverb

to enjoy oneself, to have a cosy time · to cuddle

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

påvirket av tysk (lieb)kosen ‘kjæle’; jamfør kausea thimble (metal ring protecting a rope's eye) influenced by German (lieb)kosen ‘to cuddle’; compare kausea thimble (metal ring protecting a rope's eye)

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. ha det hyggelig, nyte velværet to have a nice time, to enjoy oneself
    • de koste seg virkelig i ferien they really enjoyed themselves on holiday
    • drikke kaffe og kose seg to have a coffee and relax
    • hunden ligger og koser seg i sola the dog is lying in the sun enjoying itself
  2. kjæleto cuddle, kjærtegneto caress to cuddlekjæle, to caresskjærtegne
    • kose med hunden to cuddle with the dog
    • paret satt og koste i sofaen the couple sat cuddling on the sofa
  3. gjøre det hyggelig to make things pleasant
    • kose for en to make things nice for someone
