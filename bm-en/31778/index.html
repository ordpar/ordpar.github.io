kose

verb

to enjoy oneself, to have a cosy time · to cuddle

Inflection

Etymology

påvirket av tysk (lieb)kosen ‘kjæle’; jamfør kausea thimble (metal ring protecting a rope's eye)influenced by German (lieb)kosen ‘to cuddle’; compare kausea thimble (metal ring protecting a rope's eye)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ha det hyggelig, nyte velværetto have a nice time, to enjoy oneself
    • de koste seg virkelig i ferienthey really enjoyed themselves on holiday
    • drikke kaffe og kose segto have a coffee and relax
    • hunden ligger og koser seg i solathe dog is lying in the sun enjoying itself
  2. kjæleto cuddle, kjærtegneto caressto cuddlekjæle, to caresskjærtegne
    • kose med hundento cuddle with the dog
    • paret satt og koste i sofaenthe couple sat cuddling on the sofa
  3. gjøre det hyggeligto make things pleasant
    • kose for ento make things nice for someone
