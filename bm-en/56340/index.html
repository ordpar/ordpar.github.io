sprenge

verb

to blow up, to blast · to burst, to force open · to exceed, to overload · to salt (fish, meat)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt sprengja, opprinnelig ‘få til å springe’Old Norse sprengja, originally 'to cause to burst'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. få til å eksplodere eller springe i stykkerto cause to explode or burst apart
    • de sprengte en minethey set off a mine
    • sprenge noe i luftato blow something up
    • vi sprenger ut en tunnelwe are blasting out a tunnel
    • naboen har sprengt vekk fjellthe neighbour has blasted away some rock
  2. bryte (opp)to break (open)
    • sprenge en låst dørto force a locked door
    • han sprenger lenkenehe bursts his chains
  3. splitte (2)to split upto split upsplitte (2)
    • koalisjonen ble sprengtthe coalition was torn apart
  4. ødelegge ved overanstrengelseto ruin through over-exertion
    • hun ønsker ikke å sprenge stemmen når hun syngershe does not want to wreck her voice when she sings
  5. overskride, overbelasteto exceed, to overload
    • sprenge et budsjettto blow a budget
    • alle hoteller er sprengtall the hotels are full to bursting
  6. presse, trengeto press, to push
    • sprenge seg forbito force one's way past
    • gråten sprengte i brystetthe sobs were bursting in his chest
  7. ri eller kjøre fortto ride or drive fast
    • hun kaster seg på hesten og sprenger i veishe leaps onto the horse and gallops off
  8. salte;
    jamfør lettsprengtlightly salted, saltsprengtlightly salted    to salt;
    compare lettsprengtlightly salted, saltsprengtlightly salted
    • sprenge fiskto salt fish
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • sprengt torsksalted cod

    also found in compounds:i ord som gråsprengtflecked with grey, rødsprengtruddy or bloodshot

Set phrases
