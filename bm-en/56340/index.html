sprenge

verbverb

to blow up, to blast · to burst, to force open · to exceed, to overload · to salt (fish, meat)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt sprengja, opprinnelig ‘få til å springe’ Old Norse sprengja, originally 'to cause to burst'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. få til å eksplodere eller springe i stykker to cause to explode or burst apart
    • de sprengte en mine they set off a mine
    • sprenge noe i lufta to blow something up
    • vi sprenger ut en tunnel we are blasting out a tunnel
    • naboen har sprengt vekk fjell the neighbour has blasted away some rock
  2. bryte (opp) to break (open)
    • sprenge en låst dør to force a locked door
    • han sprenger lenkene he bursts his chains
  3. splitte (2)to split up to split upsplitte (2)
    • koalisjonen ble sprengt the coalition was torn apart
  4. ødelegge ved overanstrengelse to ruin through over-exertion
    • hun ønsker ikke å sprenge stemmen når hun synger she does not want to wreck her voice when she sings
  5. overskride, overbelaste to exceed, to overload
    • sprenge et budsjett to blow a budget
    • alle hoteller er sprengt all the hotels are full to bursting
  6. presse, trenge to press, to push
    • sprenge seg forbi to force one's way past
    • gråten sprengte i brystet the sobs were bursting in his chest
  7. ri eller kjøre fort to ride or drive fast
    • hun kaster seg på hesten og sprenger i vei she leaps onto the horse and gallops off
  8. salte;
    jamfør lettsprengtlightly salted, saltsprengtlightly salted     to salt;
    compare lettsprengtlightly salted, saltsprengtlightly salted
    • sprenge fisk to salt fish
    brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
    • sprengt torsk salted cod
  9. brukt som etterledd i sammensetninger: som har et stenk av used as a final element in compounds: which has a touch of
    in words such as:i ord som gråsprengtflecked with grey, rødsprengtruddy or bloodshot

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

sprenge seg overanstrenge seg to overexert oneself
  • han akter ikke å sprenge seg ved å sykle i høyt tempo he does not intend to wear himself out cycling at high speed
