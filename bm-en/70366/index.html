maks

adjective

at most, max · brilliant, first-rate · the maximum

Inflection

Etymology

kortform av maksimalmaximum · greatest possible og maksimummaximum · at most (as an adverb)short form of maksimalmaximum · greatest possible and maksimummaximum · at most (as an adverb)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. maksimalt god, topp (II)top, first-class · great!as good as it gets, first-ratetopp (II)
    • filmen var helt maksthe film was absolutely brilliant
    • alle ytte maks innsatseveryone gave it their very best effort
  2. brukt som adverb: maksimalt, høyst (2)at mostused as an adverb: at maximum, at mosthøyst (2)
    • betale maks 10 000 krto pay 10,000 kroner at most
  3. brukt som substantiv: høyest mulig sum, verdi eller lignendeused as a noun: the highest possible sum, value or the like
    • 10 000 kr er maks10,000 kroner is the maximum
