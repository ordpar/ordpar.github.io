slikke

verb

to lick

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. berøre med tungen;
    la tungen gli over;
    sleike (1)to lick    to touch with the tongue;
    to run one's tongue over something;
    to licksleike (1)
    • kua slikker saltthe cow is licking salt
    • katten slikker segthe cat is licking itself
    • maten var så god at de slikket av tallerkenenthe food was so good that they licked the plate clean
    • de slikket i seg isenthey licked up their ice cream
  2. bevege seg som tungerto move like tongues
    • flammene slikket oppover veggenthe flames licked up the wall
