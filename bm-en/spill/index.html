“spill” is an inflected form of:

  • spille II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to spill · to waste la renne eller falle ned (så det blir søl eller ikke kan brukes)
  • spille III homograph 3, spelle III homograph 3 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to play · to gamble blinke, glitre, funkle
