smøre

verbverb

to spread · to lubricate · to wax

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt smyrja; av smørbutter Old Norse smyrja; from smørbutter

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. stryke eller gni utover to spread or rub onto a surface
    • smøre smør på brødskiva to spread butter on a slice of bread
    • han smører matpakke he is making a packed lunch
    • hun smurte salve på såret she rubbed ointment into the wound
  2. sette inn med smurning to coat with a greasy or waxy preparation
    • smøre ski to wax skis
    • smøre seg inn med solkrem to rub oneself with sunscreen
  3. ha smøremiddel på to apply a lubricant to
    • smøre en lås to oil a lock
  4. denge, jule, slå to thrash, to beat up, to hit
    • smøre noen opp to beat someone up
    • smøre til noen to give someone a clout
  5. bestikketo bribe, to buy off to bribebestikke
    • dørvakten måtte smøres litt før vi slapp inn the doorman had to be slipped something before we were let in
  6. lage noe i en fart to make something in a hurry
    • smøre sammen et maleri to dash off a painting
    • han smurte opp hytta i en fart he threw the cabin up in no time
