lett

adjectiveadjektiv

easy · light (in weight) · slight, slightly

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt léttr Old Norse léttr

    motsatt tung (1)heavy     having relatively low weight;
    opposite heavytung (1)
    • en lett bør a light load

    • lett søvn light sleep
