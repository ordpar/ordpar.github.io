doble

verbverb

to double

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av dobbeltdouble · twice as much · doubly from dobbeltdouble · twice as much · doubly

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gjøre dobbelt så stor eller tallrik;
    fordoble     to make twice as large or twice as numerous;
    to double
    • formuen hennes er mer enn doblet her fortune has more than doubled
  2. i kortspill: fordoble motspillers melding eller innsats in card games: to double an opponent's bid or stake
    • doble en melding to double a bid
  3. brukt som etterledd i sammensetninger: øke så mange ganger som førsteleddet viser used as a final element in compounds: to increase by as many times as the first element shows
    in words such as:i ord som femdobleto quintuple, mangedobleto multiply many times over
