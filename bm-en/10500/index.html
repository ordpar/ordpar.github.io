doble

verb

to double

Inflection

Etymology

av dobbeltdouble · twice as much · doublyfrom dobbeltdouble · twice as much · doubly

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre dobbelt så stor eller tallrik;
    fordoble    to make twice as large or twice as numerous;
    to double
    • formuen hennes er mer enn doblether fortune has more than doubled
  2. i kortspill: fordoble motspillers melding eller innsatsin card games: to double an opponent's bid or stake
    • doble en meldingto double a bid
  3. brukt som etterledd i sammensetninger: øke så mange ganger som førsteleddet viserused as a final element in compounds: to increase by as many times as the first element shows
    also found in compounds:i ord som femdobleto quintuple, mangedobleto multiply many times over
