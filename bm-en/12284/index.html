eldst

adjective

oldest, eldest, earliest

norrønt ellztr, superlativ av gammelold; jamfør eldreolder · elderly, fairly old · senior, longer-servingOld Norse ellztr, superlative of gammelold; compare eldreolder · elderly, fairly old · senior, longer-serving
