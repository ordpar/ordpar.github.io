hvelve

verb

to turn upside down · to capsize · to arch, to vault

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt hvelfaOld Norse hvelfa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. danne et hvelvto arch, to form a vault
    • himmelen hvelver seg over jordathe sky arches over the earth
    brukt som adjektivused as an adjective
    • et hvelvet taka vaulted ceiling
  2. snu (noe) så bunnen kommer oppto turn (something) upside down
    • hvelve glassene etter å ha vasket demto turn the glasses upside down after washing them
  3. kantreto capsizeto capsizekantre
    • båten hvelvetthe boat capsized
