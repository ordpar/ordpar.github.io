gevinst

noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

a gain · a profit · a prize

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra tysk; av vinne (III)to win · to gain · to be able to from German; from vinne (III)to win · to gain · to be able to

  1. vinninggain, fortjenesteprofit · earnings · merit, credit gainvinning, profitfortjeneste
