drive seg

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. presse seg selv til å gjennomføre;
    arbeide hardt    to force oneself to see something through;
    to work hard     Eng. phraseto push oneself
    • de driver seg selv til dødethey are working themselves to death
    • jeg drev meg selv stadig videreI kept pushing myself further and further
  2. flytte seg framover ved å sparke, presse eller dytte fra bakken eller annet underlagto move forward by kicking, pressing or pushing off from the ground or some other surface Eng. phraseto propel oneself
    • de drev seg fram over isenthey propelled themselves across the ice
