strøk

noun neuter

area · stroke · coat of paint

Inflection

Etymology

av stryketo stroke · to iron · to cross out, to delete · to fail (an exam) · to sweep, to dashfrom stryketo stroke · to iron · to cross out, to delete · to fail (an exam) · to sweep, to dash
