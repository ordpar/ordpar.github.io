tine II homograph 2

verbverb

to thaw, to defrost, to melt · to thaw out, to loosen up

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt þíðna Old Norse þíðna

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. få til å bli myk eller rennende to cause to become soft or runny; to thaw
    • tine isen to melt the ice
    • tine frosne rør to thaw frozen pipes
    • tine opp frosne koteletter to defrost frozen chops
    • tine fisk i vann to thaw fish in water
  2. bli myk eller rennende to become soft or runny
    • sola får snøen til å tine the sun makes the snow thaw
    • telen tiner opp om våren the frost in the ground thaws in spring
  3. bli blid og pratsom;
    tø oppto loosen up     to become friendly and talkative;
    to loosen uptø opp
    • gjestene tinte opp da de fikk servering the guests thawed out once the refreshments were served
