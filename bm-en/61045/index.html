tine II homograph 2

verb

to thaw, to defrost, to melt · to thaw out, to loosen up

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt þíðnaOld Norse þíðna

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. få til å bli myk eller rennendeto cause to become soft or runny; to thaw
    • tine isento melt the ice
    • tine frosne rørto thaw frozen pipes
    • tine opp frosne koteletterto defrost frozen chops
    • tine fisk i vannto thaw fish in water
  2. bli myk eller rennendeto become soft or runny
    • sola får snøen til å tinethe sun makes the snow thaw
    • telen tiner opp om vårenthe frost in the ground thaws in spring
  3. bli blid og pratsom;
    tø oppto loosen up    to become friendly and talkative;
    to loosen uptø opp
    • gjestene tinte opp da de fikk serveringthe guests thawed out once the refreshments were served
