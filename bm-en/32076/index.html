kreve

verb

to demand · to require, to call for · to claim

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt krefjaOld Norse krefja

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. be om noe på en myndig måte;
    forlange at noe blir gjort, satt i verk eller lignende;
    gjøre krav på    to ask for something in an authoritative manner;
    to demand that something be done, put into effect or the like;
    to lay claim to
    • kreve betalingto demand payment
    • kreve tilbake et lånto call in a loan
    • hun krevde erstatningshe demanded compensation
    • de krever oppreisningthey are demanding redress
    • sjefen krever full innsatsthe boss demands full commitment
    • kreve sin rettto claim one's right
  2. være nødvendig for at noe skal eksistere, fungere eller lykkes;
    fordre, forutsette (2)to depend on    to be necessary for something to exist, function or succeed;
    to call for, to depend onforutsette (2)
    • situasjonen krever handlingthe situation calls for action
    • arbeidet krever orden og innsiktthe work requires order and insight
  3. forårsake et stort tap;
    koste (II, 3)to cost    to cause a great loss;
    to costkoste (II, 3)
    • ulykken krevde mange menneskelivthe accident claimed many lives
