utpeke

verbverb

to single out, to pick out · to designate, to appoint · to identify

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

trekke fram, peke ut, plukke ut to single out, to point out, to pick out
  • bli utpekt til leder to be designated as leader
  • ransmannen ble utpekt av bankfunksjonæren the robber was identified by the bank employee
