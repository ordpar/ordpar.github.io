lukte

verbverb

to smell

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt lukta; av lukt (I)smell · odour · sense of smell Old Norse lukta; from lukt (I)smell · odour · sense of smell

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. (ha evne til å) kjenne lukt (to have the ability to) perceive a smell
    • lukte på en rose to smell a rose
    • jeg kunne lukte at hun nettopp hadde tatt en røyk I could smell that she had just had a cigarette
  2. sende ut;
    gi fra seg lukt;
    ange (II)to give off a scent     to emit;
    to give off a smell;
    to give off a scentange (II)
    • det lukter godt her it smells good in here
    • det luktet kokt kål i oppgangen there was a smell of boiled cabbage in the stairwell
