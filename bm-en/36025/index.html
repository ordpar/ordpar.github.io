lukte

verb

to smell

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt lukta; av lukt (I)smell · odour · sense of smellOld Norse lukta; from lukt (I)smell · odour · sense of smell

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. (ha evne til å) kjenne lukt(to have the ability to) perceive a smell
    • lukte på en roseto smell a rose
    • jeg kunne lukte at hun nettopp hadde tatt en røykI could smell that she had just had a cigarette
  2. sende ut;
    gi fra seg lukt;
    ange (II)to give off a scent    to emit;
    to give off a smell;
    to give off a scentange (II)
    • det lukter godt herit smells good in here
    • det luktet kokt kål i oppgangenthere was a smell of boiled cabbage in the stairwell
