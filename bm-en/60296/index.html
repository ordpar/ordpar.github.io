tenke

verbverb

to think · to intend, to plan · to imagine

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av norrønt þekkja med påvirkning fra lavtysk denken from Old Norse þekkja with influence from Low German denken

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. binde sammen forestillinger til tanker, drive tankevirksomhet;
    resonnereto reason, to think through     to connect ideas into thoughts, to engage in thinking;
    to reason, to think throughresonnere
    • tenke klart to think clearly
    • tenke farlige tanker to think dangerous thoughts
    • tenke i bilder to think in images
    • han tenker ikke før han taler he doesn't think before he speaks
    • sitte og tenke to sit thinking
    • den var stor, tenkte hun it was big, she thought
    brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
    • et tenkende menneske a thinking person
    • tenkende vesener thinking beings
  2. rette bevisstheten mot noe to turn one's attention to something
    • hun tenkte ikke noe mer over det she didn't think about it any further
    • han kom til å tenke på broren sin he happened to think of his brother
    • prøve å ikke tenke på det to try not to think about it
  3. forestille seg, se for seg to imagine, to picture to oneself
    • la oss tenke oss følgende: … let us imagine the following: …
    • det er lett å tenke seg en slik løsning it's easy to imagine such a solution
    • jeg kan tenke meg at det var vanskelig I can imagine it was difficult
    brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
    • et tenkt tilfelle a hypothetical case
    • for å ta et tenkt eksempel to take a hypothetical example
  4. ha forsett om, ha i sinne;
    akte (4)to mean to     to intend, to have in mind;
    to mean toakte (4)
    • hva har du tenkt å gjøre? what do you intend to do?
    • tenke på å gå to be thinking of leaving
    • jeg tenker meg til fjells i helgen I'm planning to head up to the mountains this weekend
    • jeg har tenkt å flytte I intend to move
  5. ha omsorg for, ta hensyn til to care about, to be considerate of
    • ikke tenk på meg don't worry about me
    • prøv å tenke litt på andre iblant try to think of others now and then
  6. ha som synspunkt;
    mene (3)to believe, synes (3)to have the impression     to hold as one's opinion;
    to believemene (3), to have the impressionsynes (3)
    • hva tenker du om saken? what do you think about the matter?
    • ikke bry seg med hva andre tenker not to care what others think
    • de tenkte det var lurt å starte tidlig they thought it was wise to start early
    • jeg tenker det er for tidlig å si noe sikkert I think it's too early to say anything for certain
  7. brukt for å uttrykke forundring used to express astonishment
    • tenk det! imagine that!
    • tenke seg til at du er her! just think, you're here!
