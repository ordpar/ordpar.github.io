tenke

verb

to think · to intend, to plan · to imagine

Inflection

Etymology

av norrønt þekkja med påvirkning fra lavtysk denkenfrom Old Norse þekkja with influence from Low German denken

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. binde sammen forestillinger til tanker, drive tankevirksomhet;
    resonnereto reason, to think through    to connect ideas into thoughts, to engage in thinking;
    to reason, to think throughresonnere
    • tenke klartto think clearly
    • tenke farlige tankerto think dangerous thoughts
    • tenke i bilderto think in images
    • han tenker ikke før han talerhe doesn't think before he speaks
    • sitte og tenketo sit thinking
    • den var stor, tenkte hunit was big, she thought
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • et tenkende menneskea thinking person
    • tenkende vesenerthinking beings
  2. rette bevisstheten mot noeto turn one's attention to something
    • hun tenkte ikke noe mer over detshe didn't think about it any further
    • han kom til å tenke på broren sinhe happened to think of his brother
    • prøve å ikke tenke på detto try not to think about it
  3. forestille seg, se for segto imagine, to picture to oneself
    • la oss tenke oss følgende: …let us imagine the following: …
    • det er lett å tenke seg en slik løsningit's easy to imagine such a solution
    • jeg kan tenke meg at det var vanskeligI can imagine it was difficult
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • et tenkt tilfellea hypothetical case
    • for å ta et tenkt eksempelto take a hypothetical example
  4. ha forsett om, ha i sinne;
    akte (4)to mean to    to intend, to have in mind;
    to mean toakte (4)
    • hva har du tenkt å gjøre?what do you intend to do?
    • tenke på å gåto be thinking of leaving
    • jeg tenker meg til fjells i helgenI'm planning to head up to the mountains this weekend
    • jeg har tenkt å flytteI intend to move
  5. ha omsorg for, ta hensyn tilto care about, to be considerate of
    • ikke tenk på megdon't worry about me
    • prøv å tenke litt på andre iblanttry to think of others now and then
  6. ha som synspunkt;
    mene (3)to believe, synes (3)to have the impression    to hold as one's opinion;
    to believemene (3), to have the impressionsynes (3)
    • hva tenker du om saken?what do you think about the matter?
    • ikke bry seg med hva andre tenkernot to care what others think
    • de tenkte det var lurt å starte tidligthey thought it was wise to start early
    • jeg tenker det er for tidlig å si noe sikkertI think it's too early to say anything for certain
  7. brukt for å uttrykke forundringused to express astonishment
    • tenk det!imagine that!
    • tenke seg til at du er her!just think, you're here!
