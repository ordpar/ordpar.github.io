ultimo

adverbadverb

EtymologyOpphav

fra latin, av ultimus ‘sist’ from Latin, from ultimus 'last'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. i siste tredel av en måned;
    jamfør medioin the middle third of a month og primo (III)in the first third of a month     in the last third of a month;
    compare medioin the middle third of a month and primo (III)in the first third of a month
    • brevet ble sendt ultimo mai the letter was sent at the end of May
  2. om forfallsdag for verdipapirer: siste hverdag i måneden;
    jamfør medio (2)the 15th of the month or the nearest working day, primo (III, 2)the first working day of a month     of the maturity date for securities: the last working day of the month;
    compare medio (2)the 15th of the month or the nearest working day, primo (III, 2)the first working day of a month
