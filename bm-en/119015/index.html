kaste kortene

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. slutte å spille eller gi opp et kortspill fordi en ikke vil eller kan spille kortene en harto stop playing or give up a card game because one will not or cannot play the cards one holds Eng. phraseto throw in one's hand
  2. gi opp (en sak, et standpunkt eller lignende)to give up (a cause, a position or the like) Eng. phraseto throw in the towel
    • hun kastet kortene da hun tapte valgetshe threw in the towel when she lost the election
