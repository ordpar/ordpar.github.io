hatt

noun masculine

a hat · a cap (of a mushroom, a mountain top) · a hat (a role one wears)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt hǫttr, hattrOld Norse hǫttr, hattr

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. hodeplagg med pulla crown og (vanligvis) bremheadwear with a crownpull and (usually) a brim
    • hun hadde hatt med slørshe was wearing a hat with a veil
    • han tok hatten av da han kom innhe took his hat off when he came in
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som bowlerhattbowler hat, cowboyhattcowboy hat, flosshatttop hat, panamahattPanama hat, tyrolerhattTyrolean hat

    • fjellene med sine hvite hatterthe mountains with their white caps
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som røykhattchimney cowl, skumhatthead of foam
  3. i overført betydning: rolle (I, 4)a role;
    verv (II, 1)a duty    in a figurative sense: a rolerolle (I, 4);
    a dutyverv (II, 1)
    • hun sjonglerer mange ulike hattershe juggles many different hats
    • han er en person med flere hatter i norsk kulturlivhe is a person who wears several hats in Norwegian cultural life
