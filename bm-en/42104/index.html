omgang I homograph 1

noun masculine

round · half · a going-over

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt umgangr; av omgåto circumvent, to get around, to sidestep, to bypassOld Norse umgangr; from omgåto circumvent, to get around, to sidestep, to bypass

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. avdeling, rundea part, a round
    • laget vant første omgangthe team won the first half
    • presidenten ble valgt i annen omgangthe president was elected in the second round
    • spille en omgang pokerto play a round of poker
  2. medfart, prylrough treatment, a beating
    • gi noen en omgang julingto give someone a thrashing
  3. behandling, stelltreatment, tending
    • la skoene få en omgang skokremto give the shoes a coat of polish
  4. rundganga round, circulation from one to the next, rotation, tur (I, 1)one's turnrotationrundgang, one's turntur (I, 1)
    • ledervervet gikk på omgang blant medlemmenethe chairmanship rotated among the members
  5. rad av masker på et strikketøya row of stitches in a piece of knitting
    • strikke fem omganger på rundpinneto knit five rounds on a circular needle
