bre III homograph 3, breie II homograph 2

verb
Inflection

Etymology

jamfør dansk brede, norrønt breiða, av breiðr; jamfør bredwide · broad · wide-rangingcompare Danish brede, Old Norse breiða, from breiðr; compare bredwide · broad · wide-ranging

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. strekke eller folde ut (for å dekke noe)to stretch out or unfold (in order to cover something)
    • hun bredte ut armeneshe spread out her arms
    • høna bredte vingene over kyllingenethe hen spread her wings over the chicks
    • bre over seg dynato pull the duvet over oneself
  2. legge utoverto lay out over a surface
    • rake og bre høyto rake and spread hay
