ærend

noun neuter

errand · message, what one has come to say

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt erendiOld Norse erendi

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. noe en har fore;
    oppdragan assignment · a task · a mission, gjøremåltask, chore · things to do    something one has to do;
    an assignmentoppdrag, a taskgjøremål
    • ha et ærend i byento have an errand in town
    • utføre et ærendto carry out an errand
