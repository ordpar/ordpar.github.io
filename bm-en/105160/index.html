slappe av

to relax · to take it easy

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. hvile;
    kople av    to rest;
    to unwind     Eng. phraseto relax
    • slappe av med en bokto relax with a book
  2. bli mindre ivrig;
    roe ned    to become less eager;
    to calm down     Eng. phraseto take it easy
    • iveren har slappet avthe enthusiasm has died down
    • slapp av! Det går brarelax! It'll be fine
