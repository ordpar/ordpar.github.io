innbille

verb

to make someone believe (something untrue) · to fool, to delude

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk inbilden eller tysk einbildenfrom Low German inbilden or German einbilden

Senses and Example Sentences

få til å tro (uten grunnlag i virkeligheten)to make someone believe (something with no basis in reality)
  • prøv ikke å innbille meg slikt vrøvl!don't try to make me believe such nonsense!

Set phrases

innbille seg ha en falsk forestilling om noeto have a false notion of something Eng. phraseto delude oneself
  • jeg innbiller meg at det skal gå braI imagine it will turn out all right
  • han innbilte seg at han var ung igjenhe imagined he was young again
