ryddig

adjective

tidy, neat · orderly, clear · well-organised, methodical (of a person)

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. preget av orden;
    i orden, stelt    characterized by order;
    in order, neat
    • rommet var ryddigthe room was tidy
    • det var så ryddig og fint ute på gårdsplassenit was so neat and tidy out in the farmyard
  2. ordnet, klar;
    oversiktlig (1)easy to take in at a glance    orderly, clear;
    easy to take in at a glanceoversiktlig (1)
    • en ryddig lederstila straightforward leadership style
    • saken ble håndtert på en ryddig måtethe matter was handled in a proper manner
  3. om person: som har orden og system i sakene sine;
    systematisk    of a person: who keeps order and system in their affairs;
    systematic
    • ha et ryddig hodeto have a clear head
    • være ryddig i tankegangento be methodical in one's thinking
    • en ryddig persona well-organised person
