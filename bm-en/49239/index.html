ryddig

adjectiveadjektiv

tidy, neat · orderly, clear · well-organised, methodical (of a person)

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. preget av orden;
    i orden, stelt     characterized by order;
    in order, neat
    • rommet var ryddig the room was tidy
    • det var så ryddig og fint ute på gårdsplassen it was so neat and tidy out in the farmyard
  2. ordnet, klar;
    oversiktlig (1)easy to take in at a glance     orderly, clear;
    easy to take in at a glanceoversiktlig (1)
    • en ryddig lederstil a straightforward leadership style
    • saken ble håndtert på en ryddig måte the matter was handled in a proper manner
  3. om person: som har orden og system i sakene sine;
    systematisk     of a person: who keeps order and system in their affairs;
    systematic
    • ha et ryddig hode to have a clear head
    • være ryddig i tankegangen to be methodical in one's thinking
    • en ryddig person a well-organised person
