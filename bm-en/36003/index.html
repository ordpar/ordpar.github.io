lukke II homograph 2

verb

to close · to shut

Inflection

Etymology

fra dansk; jamfør norrønt lykja og norrønt lúkafrom Danish; compare Old Norse lykja and Old Norse lúka

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ha igjen;
    motsatt åpneto open    to shut;
    the opposite of to openåpne
    • kan du lukke vinduet?can you close the window?
    • jeg glemte å lukke igjen døraI forgot to shut the door
    • de hadde pakket så mye at det var vanskelig å lukke koffertenthey had packed so much that the suitcase was hard to close
    • hun lukket bøkene for kveldenshe closed her books for the evening
  2. stenge (II, 1)to close, låse (1)to lockto closestenge (II, 1), to locklåse (1)
    • butikken lukker klokka 17the shop closes at 5 pm
    • han har lukket seg inne i seg selvhe has withdrawn into himself
  3. brukt som adjektiv: stengtused as an adjective: closed
    • en lukket døra closed door
    • hun er en veldig lukket personshe is a very closed-off person
    • saken ble diskutert i et lukket møtethe matter was discussed in a closed meeting
    • han er med i en lukket gruppehe belongs to a closed group
