banke II homograph 2

verb

to knock · to beat · to pound

Inflection

Etymology

lydord, intensivform av norrønt banga ‘slå, hamre’onomatopoeic, an intensive form of Old Norse banga 'to strike, to hammer'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. svinge, bevege hånd eller redskap raskt mot noe og treffe;
    dunketo thump, kakketo rap    to swing, to move one's hand or a tool quickly against something and hit it;
    to thumpdunke, to rapkakke
    • banke på dørato knock on the door
    • banke en finger mot tinningento tap a finger against one's temple
  2. i overført betydning: formidle et budskap gjentatte gangerfiguratively: to put a message across by repeating it over and over
    • banke inn i arbeiderne hvor viktige de erto drum into the workers how important they are
  3. lage rytmiske lyder eller signaler;
    slå (II, 1)to bang, pulsere (1)to throb    to make rhythmic sounds or signals;
    to bangslå (II, 1), to throbpulsere (1)
    • motoren bankerthe engine is knocking
    • hjertet banket hardthis heart was pounding hard
  4. gi juling (1)a beating;
    gi bank (III, 2)a hiding;
    denge (1)to thrash    to give a beatingjuling (1);
    to give a hidingbank (III, 2);
    to thrashdenge (1)
    • bli banket oppto be beaten up
    • de dyttet ham i bakken og banket hamthey pushed him to the ground and beat him

Set phrases

bank i bordet! (sagt samtidig som en banker på noe av tre) motvirke at et optimistisk utsagn utfordrer overnaturlige makter slik at hellet snur(said while knocking on something wooden) to keep an optimistic statement from provoking supernatural powers and turning one's luck Eng. phrasetouch wood
  • de har, bank i bordet, aldri blitt skaddthey have, touch wood, never been injured

banke på
  1. kakke på en dør, port eller lignende med fingerknokene for å signalisere at en ønsker å komme innto rap on a door, gate or the like with one's knuckles to signal that one wants to come in Eng. phraseto knock on the door
    • han gikk rett inn uten å banke påhe walked straight in without knocking
  2. i overført betydning: henvende seg; komme; oppsøkefiguratively: to approach someone; to come; to seek someone out Eng. phraseto come knocking
    • dårlig samvittighet banket snart på døren hennesa guilty conscience soon came knocking at her door
