drukne

verbverb

to drown · to be swamped, to be lost, to be drowned out

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt drukna; beslektet med drikke (III)to drink Old Norse drukna; related to drikke (III)to drink

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. dø ved å bli kvalt i vann eller annen væske;
    bliveto drown     to die by suffocating in water or another liquid;
    to drownblive
    • drukne i elva to drown in the river
    • falle over bord og drukne to fall overboard and drown
    • de gikk gjennom isen og druknet they went through the ice and drowned
  2. drepe ved å kvele i vann to kill by suffocating in water
    • hun ble anklaget for å ha prøvd å drukne sitt eget barn she was accused of having tried to drown her own child
    • han orket ikke mer av livet og druknet seg he could not face life any longer and drowned himself
  3. bli overlesset, nedgravd eller overdøvet;
    forsvinne eller bli mindre eller svakere     to be swamped, buried or drowned out;
    to disappear or become smaller or weaker
    • han druknet i farens frakk he was lost in his father's coat
    • drukne i mengden to be lost in the crowd
    • drukne i reklame to be swamped with advertising
    • ordene hennes druknet i den høye musikken her words were drowned out by the loud music
  4. få til å bli mindre eller forsvinne;
    døyve (1)to deaden, dempe (1)to dampen     to cause to become smaller or to disappear;
    to deadendøyve (1), to dampendempe (1)
    • drukne sorgene i alkohol to drown one's sorrows in alcohol
