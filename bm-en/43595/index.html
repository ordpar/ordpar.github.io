overgi

verb

to surrender (something) · to hand over

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt yfirgefa ‘gi opp’Old Norse yfirgefa 'to give up'

Senses and Example Sentences

oppgi, gi i fiendens henderto give up, to hand over into enemy hands
  • festningen ble overgittthe fortress was surrendered

Set phrases

overgi seg oppgi kampen;
kapitulere (1)to capitulateto give up the fight;
to capitulatekapitulere (1) Eng. phraseto surrender
  • overgi seg uten motstandto surrender without resistance
