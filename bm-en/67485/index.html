velling

noun feminine or masculine
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt vellingr; av velleto well upOld Norse vellingr; from velleto well up

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. suppe av gryn eller mel kokt i melk eller vanna soup of grain or flour boiled in milk or water
    • sild og vellingherring and gruel
  2. flytende massea runny, sloppy mass

Set phrases

gjøre/lage vei i vellinga gjøre noe raskt og effektivt;
få ting unnato do something quickly and efficiently;
to get things out of the way Eng. phraseto make short work of it
