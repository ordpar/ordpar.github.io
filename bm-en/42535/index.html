opplyse

verb

to inform, to state · to light up · to enlighten

Inflection

Etymology

av oppup · open (with verbs of opening) · up, completely (marking a process finished) og lyse (II)to shine · to shed light · to proclaimfrom oppup · open (with verbs of opening) · up, completely (marking a process finished) and lyse (II)to shine · to shed light · to proclaim

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi lys (fra en lyskilde);
    lyse opp    to give light (from a light source);
    to light up
    • hele gata er opplyst fra klokka åtte om kveldenthe whole street is lit up from eight in the evening
  2. gi informasjon;
    forklare, meddele, informere, fortelle    to give information;
    to explain, to communicate, to inform, to tell
    • politiet opplyser at tyven nå er fangetthe police report that the thief has now been caught
    • opplyse om plikter og rettigheterto give information about obligations and rights
    • begge parter er opplyst på forhåndboth parties have been informed in advance
    • firmaet er tilstrekkelig opplyst om problemenethe firm has been sufficiently informed about the problems
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • foredraget var svært opplysendethe lecture was very informative
  3. gi kunnskap eller innsikt;
    utdanne    to give knowledge or insight;
    to educate
    • opplyse folketto enlighten the people
    • målet med forestillingen er å underholde og opplysethe aim of the performance is to entertain and inform
  4. veiledeto guide
    • be Gud opplyse segto ask God for guidance
