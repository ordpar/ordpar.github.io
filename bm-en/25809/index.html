ille

adverb

badly · bad · terribly

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt illaOld Norse illa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. vondt, ubehagelig, fælt;
    motbydelig    badly, unpleasantly, horribly;
    disgustingly
    • det luktet illeit smelled bad
    • det så ille utthings looked bad
    • være ille tilredtto be badly knocked about
  2. uheldig, dårlig, elendigunfortunately, poorly, wretchedly
    • det stod ille til med hennethings were in a bad way with her
    • det gikk dem illethings turned out badly for them
    • ja ja, dette var jo ikke så illeoh well, this wasn't so bad after all
    • det var ille at de ikke komit was a shame that they did not come
    • være ille til moteto feel uneasy
  3. vondt, fiendtligcruelly, with hostility
    • der handlet han ille mot ossthere he treated us badly
    • det var ikke ille mentit was not meant unkindly
    • snakke ille om andreto speak ill of others
  4. brukt forsterkende: svært, veldigused as an intensifier: very, extremely
    • hun ble så ille sykshe became so terribly ill
    • være i ille dårlig humørto be in a really bad mood
  5. brukt som adjektiv: stygg, fæl, dårlig;
    hard (5)harsh    used as an adjective: ugly, awful, bad;
    harshhard (5)
    • få ille medfartto be badly treated
    • ha en ille oppvekstto have a rough upbringing
