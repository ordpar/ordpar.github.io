ille

adverbadverb

badly · bad · terribly

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt illa Old Norse illa

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. vondt, ubehagelig, fælt;
    motbydelig     badly, unpleasantly, horribly;
    disgustingly
    • det luktet ille it smelled bad
    • det så ille ut things looked bad
    • være ille tilredt to be badly knocked about
  2. uheldig, dårlig, elendig unfortunately, poorly, wretchedly
    • det stod ille til med henne things were in a bad way with her
    • det gikk dem ille things turned out badly for them
    • ja ja, dette var jo ikke så ille oh well, this wasn't so bad after all
    • det var ille at de ikke kom it was a shame that they did not come
    • være ille til mote to feel uneasy
  3. vondt, fiendtlig cruelly, with hostility
    • der handlet han ille mot oss there he treated us badly
    • det var ikke ille ment it was not meant unkindly
    • snakke ille om andre to speak ill of others
  4. brukt forsterkende: svært, veldig used as an intensifier: very, extremely
    • hun ble så ille syk she became so terribly ill
    • være i ille dårlig humør to be in a really bad mood
  5. brukt som adjektiv: stygg, fæl, dårlig;
    hard (5)harsh     used as an adjective: ugly, awful, bad;
    harshhard (5)
    • få ille medfart to be badly treated
    • ha en ille oppvekst to have a rough upbringing
