lure I homograph 1

verb

to wonder · to fool, to trick · to lurk

Inflection

Etymology

trolig fra lavtysk luren ‘lure på, bedra’probably from Low German luren ‘to lie in wait for, to deceive’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. vente på en anledning;
    holde seg skjult og vokte på noe    to wait for an opportunity;
    to stay hidden and keep watch on something
    • katten lurer på fuglenethe cat is lying in wait for the birds
    • det lurer farer hvor en går og stårdangers lurk wherever you go
    • vi visste ikke hva som lurte rundt hjørnetwe did not know what was lurking round the corner
  2. spekulere eller gruble påto speculate or ponder about
    • hun lurte på hva han egentlig hadde ment med det han sashe wondered what he had really meant by what he said
  3. tvile (1)to doubt, to feel uncertainto doubt, to feel uncertaintvile (1)
    • nå begynner jeg snart å lure på om de kommerI am beginning to doubt whether they are coming
  4. bruke list;
    smette (II)to slip, to dart, to nip, snike (I, 1)to sneak    to use cunning;
    to slipsmette (II), to sneaksnike (I, 1)
    • lure seg til å gjøre noeto contrive to do something on the sly
    • de lurte seg unna alt arbeidetthey sneaked out of all the work
    • vi lurte oss forbi vaktenwe slipped past the guard
  5. narreto fool, to trick, to deceive, bedra (1)to defraud, snyte (I, 2)to cheatto foolnarre, to defraudbedra (1), to cheatsnyte (I, 2)
    • han er lett å lurehe is easy to fool
    • vi lot oss lure til å tro at det var santwe were tricked into believing it was true
    • du lurer ikke meg!you can't fool me!
