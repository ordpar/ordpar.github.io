fnugg

noun neuter
Inflection

Etymology

fra dansk, jamfør norrønt fnykr ‘stank’; svensk fnyk ‘støvkorn’ og fnokkpappus (tuft of hairs on a seed)from Danish, compare Old Norse fnykr 'stench'; Swedish fnyk 'speck of dust' and fnokkpappus (tuft of hairs on a seed)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. liten, lett partikkelsmall, light particle
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som snøfnuggsnowflake, støvfnuggspeck of dust
  2. grann (I)a scrap, a tiny bit, sneva touch, a hint, a tracea scrap, a tiny bitgrann (I), a touch, a hint, a tracesnev
    • jeg begriper ikke et fnuggI don't understand a thing
    • ikke det fnuggnot the slightest bit
