ennå

adverbadverb

still · yet

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt ennú, av enn (III)-ever (whoever, whatever, wherever) · even, still og nå (II)now · nowadays, at present · well, you know (unstressed particle); jamfør enda (II)even (before a comparative) · another, one more · still, yet Old Norse ennú, from enn (III)-ever (whoever, whatever, wherever) · even, still and nå (II)now · nowadays, at present · well, you know (unstressed particle); compare enda (II)even (before a comparative) · another, one more · still, yet

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

fremdeles på det tidspunktet;
enda (II, 1)yet still at that point in time;
yetenda (II, 1)
  • hun lever ennå she is still alive
  • det er ennå billetter igjen there are still tickets left
  • jeg har ennå til gode å se henne sint I have yet to see her angry
  • vi har ennå ikke hørt noe we still haven't heard anything
  • det var ennå ikke for sent it was not too late yet
  • ordningen var ennå ikke godkjent the scheme had not yet been approved
