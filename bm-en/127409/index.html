ha noe på samvittigheten

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. være ansvarlig for noe kritikkverdig, ofte ulovlig;
    være skyldig i noe    to be responsible for something blameworthy, often illegal;
    to be guilty of something     Eng. phraseto have something on one's conscience
    • han hadde flere innbrudd på samvittighetenhe had several burglaries on his conscience
  2. være opphavet til noeto be the one behind something Eng. phraseto have something to one's name
    • hun har flere filmer på samvittighetenshe has several films to her name
