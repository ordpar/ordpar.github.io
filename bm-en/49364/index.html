rømme II homograph 2

verb

to escape · to run away · to flee

Etymology

norrønt rýma ‘gjøre rommeligere, rydde, flytte’; beslektet med rom (III)room · space og rom (IV)roomy · spacious · ampleOld Norse rýma 'to make roomier, to clear, to move'; related to rom (III)room · space and rom (IV)roomy · spacious · ample
