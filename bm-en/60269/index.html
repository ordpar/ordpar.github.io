temmelig

adverbadverb

rather · fairly · pretty

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk ‘passende’ from Low German ‘suitable’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

i stor grad;
ganske, nokså to a considerable degree;
quite, fairly
  • vi var temmelig slitne we were rather tired
  • det er temmelig nøyaktig et år siden it was pretty much exactly a year ago
