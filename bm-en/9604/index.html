dele II homograph 2

verb

to share · to divide

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt deila(st); opprinnelig fra lavtyskOld Norse deila(st); originally from Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stykke opp en helhet i mindre enheter;
    kløyve, skille    to break a whole up into smaller units;
    to split, to separate
    • rommet er delt i tothe room is divided in two
    • hun delte eplet i små bitershe cut the apple into small pieces
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • delte meningerdivided opinions
  2. dividereto divide · to ponder, to mull things over, to ditherto dividedividere
    • gange og deleto multiply and divide
    • 18 delt på 6 er 318 divided by 6 is 3
  3. skifte (II, 5)to divide up an estate, fordele;
    levere    to divide up an estateskifte (II, 5), to distribute;
    to hand out
    • de deler på oppgavenethey share the tasks between them
    • slektningene delte arven mellom segthe relatives divided the inheritance among themselves
    • dele utbyttet liktto share the profits equally
  4. ha felles eierskap over;
    ha sammen    to have joint ownership of;
    to have in common
    • dele romto share a room
    • idrettsutøverne delte seierenthe athletes shared the victory
    • dele omsorgen for barnato share the care of the children
  5. meddele, fortelle, informere;
    gi andre innsikt i    to communicate, to tell, to inform;
    to give others insight into
    • dele et innlegg på sosiale medierto share a post on social media
    • de delte sine innerste tankerthey shared their innermost thoughts
    • dele strategien med resten av lagetto share the strategy with the rest of the team
    • han var ikke redd for å dele meningene sinehe was not afraid to share his opinions
