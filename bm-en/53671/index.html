skrubbe II homograph 2

verbverb

to scrub, to scour · to graze, to scrape (the skin) · to brake, to slow down (by dragging)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

sammenheng med norrønt hrjúfr ‘knudrete, ru’ connected with Old Norse hrjúfr 'knobbly, rough'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gni hardt;
    skureto scour     to scrub, to rub hard;
    to scourskure
    • skrubbe golvet to scrub the floor
    • skrubbe noen på ryggen to scrub someone's back
  2. få skrubbsår to graze, to scrape (the skin)
    • hun skrubbet seg på kneet she grazed her knee
  3. minske fart;
    bremse     to slow down;
    to brake
    • han skrubber med beina for å bremse kjelken he drags his feet to brake the sledge
