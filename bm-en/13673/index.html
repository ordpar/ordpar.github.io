falle II homograph 2

verbverb

to fall · to drop, to decline · to be killed in battle

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt falla Old Norse falla

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. komme eller være i bevegelse nedover to come or be moving downwards
    • snøen falt the snow fell
    • tårene falt tears fell
    • håret faller av his hair is falling out
  2. dette over ende;
    velte (II, 1)to overturn     to topple over;
    to overturnvelte (II, 1)
    • falle over ende to fall over
    • falle på baken to fall on one's backside
    • falle og slå seg to fall and hurt oneself
  3. bli opphevet;
    bli gitt opp     to be annulled;
    to be abandoned
    • straffen faller bort the penalty lapses
    • la gamle prinsipp falle bort to let old principles go
  4. bli erobret, overvunnet, styrtet eller vraket;
    lide nederlag     to be conquered, defeated, overthrown or rejected;
    to suffer defeat
    • regjeringen falt the government fell
    • festningen falt the fortress fell
    • forslaget falt på sin egen urimelighet the proposal was defeated by its own absurdity
  5. dø (1)to die to diedø (1)
    • falle i krigen to be killed in the war
  6. bli redusert;
    minketo decrease, to diminish, to dwindle     to be reduced;
    to decreaseminke
    • temperaturen falt i natt the temperature fell during the night
    • prisene faller prices are falling
  7. treffe;
    komme     to land;
    to come
    • saken faller inn under § 5 the case falls under section 5
    • falle utenfor mønsteret to fall outside the pattern
    • 17. mai faller på en mandag 17 May falls on a Monday
    • ansvaret faller på meg the responsibility falls on me
    • falle i hendene på noen to fall into someone's hands
    • falle i unåde to fall into disfavour
    • det falt en ro over henne a calm came over her
    • det falt mange harde ord many harsh words were spoken
    • dommen faller på mandag the verdict comes on Monday
  8. forme seg;
    passe (V, 1)to fit     to take shape;
    to fitpasse (V, 1)
    • gardinene faller fint the curtains hang nicely
    • skaftet faller godt i hånden the handle sits well in the hand
    • falle i smak to go down well
    • falle heldig ut to turn out well
  9. virke, være eller bli to seem, to be or to become
    • arbeidet faller lett for ham the work comes easily to him
    • det faller naturlig å ta det opp nå it feels natural to raise it now
    • tiden falt lang time dragged
