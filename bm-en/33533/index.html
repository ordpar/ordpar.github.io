lagre

verb

to store · to age (cheese, wine)

Inflection

Etymology

av lagera warehouse, a storeroom · stock · a bearingfrom lagera warehouse, a storeroom · stock · a bearing

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. legge eller ha på lager;
    oppbevare    to put or have in storage;
    to keep
    • lagre materiale til senereto store material for later
  2. få til å modne ved å la ligge en periode under bestemte forholdto bring to maturity by leaving it for a period under particular conditions
    • noen oster og viner bør lagres en stundsome cheeses and wines should be aged for a while
  3. samle og ta vare påto collect and keep
    • lagre opplysningerto store information
  4. henge eller feste i lager (3)a bearing (for a shaft)to hang or fix in a bearing (for a shaft)lager (3)
    • akslingen er lagret i begge enderthe shaft is mounted in bearings at both ends
