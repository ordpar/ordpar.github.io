strende I homograph 1

verb
Inflection

Etymology

av strandbeach · shore, opprinnelig ‘gå langs stranda’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. streife (1)to move from place to place with no particular destination;
    to travel about, to wander    , fare (II, 1)to move from one place to another;
    to travel, to go;
    compare farende    , farteto travel about, to roam
    • de strender rundt
  2. gå (fort);
    strene (1)
    • strende forbi
  3. streife (2)to touch lightly and briefly;
    to graze, to brush against    , snerte (3)
    • strende borti noe
