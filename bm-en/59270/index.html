søle II homograph 2

verb

to spill, to make a mess, to soil · to squander, to waste, to fritter away

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. væte ut eller skitne til noe med noe vått og skittent, for eksempel vann, gjørme eller lignende;
    grise (2)to make a mess, skitneto dirty    to wet or soil something with something wet and grimy, for example water, mud, or the like;
    to make a messgrise (2), to dirtyskitne

    • de sølte med malingenthey made a mess with the paint
  2. kaste bort;
    rote (II, 4)to potter about, somleto dawdle, tulle (II, 3)to mess about    to squander, to waste;
