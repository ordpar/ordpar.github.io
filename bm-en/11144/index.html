dumhetB2, level B2

noun masculine or femininesubstantiv hankjønn eller hunkjønn

stupidity, foolishness · stupid thing, folly (an act or remark)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av dumstupid, dumb from dumstupid, dumb

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. manglende forstand eller omtanke lack of intelligence or thought
    • det var hans egen dumhet it was his own stupidity
  2. toskete, uklok handling, ytring eller lignende a silly, unwise act, remark or the like
    • den sinnataggen kunne lett gjøre en dumhet that hothead could easily do something stupid
    • komme med dumheter to come out with stupid remarks
    • begå en dumhet to commit a folly
    • ingen dumheter nå! no stupidity now!
