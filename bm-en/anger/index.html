“anger” is an inflected form of:

  • ange II homograph 2 verbverb (present)(presens) to give off a scent gi fra seg ange
  • ange I homograph 1 nounsubstantiv (plural indefinite)(flertall ubestemt) a (pleasant) smell (god) lukt
