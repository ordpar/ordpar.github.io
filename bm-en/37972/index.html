mer, mere

adjectiveadjektiv

more · any more

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt meiri, komparativ av myemuch, a lot of · a lot, greatly; jamfør mestmost · mostly, most often · almost Old Norse meiri, comparative of myemuch, a lot of · a lot, greatly; compare mestmost · mostly, most often · almost

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. i større mengde, omfang eller utstrekning;
    i tillegg     in greater quantity, scope or extent;
    in addition, extra
    • kjøpe mer mat to buy more food
    • vil du ha mer kaffe? would you like more coffee?
    • de har fått mer penger they've got more money
    • det gjorde mer skade enn nytte it did more harm than good
    • han har vel ikke mer vett he surely has no more sense than that
    • det er ikke mer å si there's nothing more to say
    • streve mer enn før to struggle more than before
    • du må trene mer you need to exercise more
    • få mer enn nok av noe to get more than enough of something
  2. ut over en viss grense i tid;
    lenger (2)any longer     beyond a certain point in time;
    any longerlenger (2)
    • de bor ikke her mer they don't live here any more
    • jeg vil ikke dra dit mer I don't want to go there any more
  3. brukt til å danne komparativ (I)the comparative used to form the comparativekomparativ (I)
    • mer innviklet more complicated
    • vi skal bli mer aktive we're going to become more active
    • han har blitt mer selvstendig he's become more independent
