mer, mere

adjective

more · any more

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt meiri, komparativ av myemuch, a lot of · a lot, greatly; jamfør mestmost · mostly, most often · almostOld Norse meiri, comparative of myemuch, a lot of · a lot, greatly; compare mestmost · mostly, most often · almost

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i større mengde, omfang eller utstrekning;
    i tillegg    in greater quantity, scope or extent;
    in addition, extra
    • kjøpe mer matto buy more food
    • vil du ha mer kaffe?would you like more coffee?
    • de har fått mer pengerthey've got more money
    • det gjorde mer skade enn nytteit did more harm than good
    • han har vel ikke mer vetthe surely has no more sense than that
    • det er ikke mer å sithere's nothing more to say
    • streve mer enn førto struggle more than before
    • du må trene meryou need to exercise more
    • få mer enn nok av noeto get more than enough of something
  2. ut over en viss grense i tid;
    lenger (2)any longer    beyond a certain point in time;
    any longerlenger (2)
    • de bor ikke her merthey don't live here any more
    • jeg vil ikke dra dit merI don't want to go there any more
  3. brukt til å danne komparativ (I)the comparativeused to form the comparativekomparativ (I)
    • mer innvikletmore complicated
    • vi skal bli mer aktivewe're going to become more active
    • han har blitt mer selvstendighe's become more independent
