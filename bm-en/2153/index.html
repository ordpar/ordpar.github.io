anta

verb

to assume · to accept · to take on

Inflection

Etymology

fra tysk ‘ta til seg’from German ‘to take to oneself’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. formode, gå ut frato suppose, to assume
    • jeg antar at hun har reistI assume she has left
    • det er grunn til å anta at …there is reason to assume that …
    • funnene er langt eldre enn først antattthe finds are far older than first thought
    • jeg skulle anta at hun er rundt 40I would guess she is around 40
    • la oss anta at du har rettlet us assume that you are right
    • han antas å være dødhe is presumed dead
  2. godtato accept · to agree to, godkjenneto approve · to pass, to accept as validto acceptgodta, to approvegodkjenne
    • få antatt en bokto have a book accepted for publication
    • få et teaterstykke antatt til oppførelseto have a play accepted for performance
  3. få (en viss karakter)to take on (a certain character)
    • anta et preg av komikkto take on a comic quality
    • saken antok etter hvert store dimensjonerthe affair gradually took on large proportions
    • anta en fastere formto take on a firmer shape
