fire II homograph 2

verb

to lower · to back down

Inflection

Etymology

gjennom lavtysk; fra fransk virer ‘dreie’through Low German; from French virer 'to turn'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. heise ned, la sige;
    senketo lower · to reduce, to cut · to sink (a ship)    to haul down, to let out gradually;
    to lowersenke
    • fire flaggetto haul down the flag
    • fire seg ned i et tauto lower oneself down a rope
  2. gi etter, slå avto give in, to relent
    • han firer aldrihe never backs down
    • fire på kraveneto relax one's demands

Set phrases

ikke fire/vike en tomme stå fast på standpunktet sittto stand firm on one's position Eng. phrasenot to give an inch
