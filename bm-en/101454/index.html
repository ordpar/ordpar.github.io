kjøre drosje

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: drosjea taxi, a cab
  1. arbeide som drosjesjåfør to work as a taxi driver Eng. phraseto drive a taxi
    • i studietiden kjørte han drosje i helgene as a student he drove a taxi at weekends
  2. reise med drosje to travel by taxi Eng. phraseto take a taxi
    • vi kjørte drosje til flyplassen we took a taxi to the airport
