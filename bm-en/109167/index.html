gud bedre

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. brukt i utropused in exclamations Eng. phrasegood Lord
    • gud bedre oss for et værgood Lord, what weather!
    • gud bedre for en artist hun ergood Lord, what an artist she is!
  2. sant å si;
    oppriktig talt    to tell the truth;
    frankly
    • det får en da gud bedre finne ut av selvfrankly, you'll just have to work that out for yourself
