skjære seg

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. briste, sprekketo break, to crack
    • melka skar segthe milk curdled
    • motoren skar segthe engine seized up
    • stemmen skar seghis voice cracked
  2. slå feil, gå i ståto fail, to grind to a halt Eng. phraseto go wrong
    • opplegget skar segthe plan fell apart
  3. ta en viss retningto take a certain course
    • skiene skar seg ned i snøenthe skis cut down into the snow
    • båten skjærer seg fram i bølgenethe boat cuts its way through the waves
    • Sognefjorden skjærer seg 20 mil inn i landetthe Sognefjord cuts 200 kilometres inland
