sleike

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt sleikjaOld Norse sleikja

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stryke over eller rense med tungen;
    slikketo lick    to stroke or clean with the tongue;
    to lickslikke
    • sleike seg rundt munnento lick around one's mouth
    • sleike av fingreneto lick one's fingers
    • maten var så god at de sleikte tallerkenenthe food was so good that they licked the plate
  2. smigre (1)to flatter, smisketo fawnto flattersmigre (1), to fawnsmiske
