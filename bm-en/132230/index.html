slå an tonen

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. spille første tone i et musikkstykketo play the first note of a piece of music Eng. phraseto strike the opening note
  2. vise hvordan noe skal gå;
    gi rammene for noe    to show how something is going to go;
    to set the framework for something     Eng. phraseto set the tone
    • ledelsen slo raskt an tonen for møtetthe management quickly set the tone for the meeting
    • den første låten slår an tonen for hele konsertenthe first song sets the tone for the whole concert
