slå an tonen

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: tonea note (musical) · a tone (of voice or of writing) · a tune · a tinge, a hue
  1. spille første tone i et musikkstykke to play the first note of a piece of music Eng. phraseto strike the opening note
  2. vise hvordan noe skal gå;
    gi rammene for noe     to show how something is going to go;
    to set the framework for something     Eng. phraseto set the tone
    • ledelsen slo raskt an tonen for møtet the management quickly set the tone for the meeting
    • den første låten slår an tonen for hele konserten the first song sets the tone for the whole concert
