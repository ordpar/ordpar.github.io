vite

verbverb

to know

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt vita Old Norse vita

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. ha kjennskap til;
    ha greie på     to have knowledge of;
    to be aware of
    • la noen få vite hva en mener to let someone know what one thinks
    • jeg visste ikke at de hadde flyttet I didn't know they had moved
    • vet du hvordan det gikk? do you know how it went?
    • det kan være løgn, for alt jeg vet it may be a lie, for all I know
    • ikke si det til henne du vet don't tell her, you know
    • så vidt du vet det! just so you know!
    • de visste ikke om det they didn't know about it
    • han skulle bare visst hva jeg tenkte if only he knew what I was thinking
  2. ha forstand på;
    ha innsikt i     to have an understanding of;
    to have insight into
    • ikke vite bedre to not know any better
    • du vet ikke hva du snakker om you don't know what you're talking about
    • er ikke dette sløsing, så vet ikke jeg if this isn't a waste, then I don't know what is
    • de vet å innrette seg they know how to adapt
    • vi visste ikke mye om konsekvensene we didn't know much about the consequences
  3. være sikker på to be sure of
    • vite noe med seg selv to know something deep down
    • en kan aldri vite you can never be sure
    • det er ikke godt å vite hva en skal tro it's hard to know what to believe
    • jeg visste det ville gå slik I knew it would turn out this way
