fanden I homograph 1

noun uninflectable

Etymology

trolig av frisisk fannen ‘frister’probably from Frisian fannen ‘tempter’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. personifikasjon av det onde;
    i bestemt form entall: Guds motstander, Djevelen    a personification of evil;
    in the definite singular: God's adversary, the Devil
    • hun fryktet verken Gud eller Fandenshe feared neither God nor the Devil
  2. brukt i banningswearing · bad language;
    faen (I, 1)the devil, fan (II)the devil · damn (as an oath)    used in swearingbanning;
    the devil – compare faen (I, 1)the devil and fan (II)the devil · damn (as an oath)
    • fanden ta deg!the devil take you!
    • se for fanden til å skjerpe dere!will you bloody well get your act together!
