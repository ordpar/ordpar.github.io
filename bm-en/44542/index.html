pelle

verbverb

to peel, to shell (by hand) · to pick, to gather (berries) · to pick at, to poke at

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

samme opprinnelse som pille (III)to poke, to pick the same origin as pille (III)to poke, to pick

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. pirke, grave;
    pille (III, 1)to pick at something     to poke, to dig about;
    to pick at somethingpille (III, 1)
    • pelle seg i nesa to pick one's nose
  2. plukke, rive, slite;
    pille (III, 2)to strip     to pluck, to tear, to pull;
    to strippille (III, 2)
    • pelle noe fra hverandre to pick something apart
    • pelle reker to peel prawns
  3. plukke eller sanke (noe smått, særlig bær) to pick or gather (small things, especially berries)
    • pelle bær to pick berries
