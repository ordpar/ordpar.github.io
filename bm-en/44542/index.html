pelle

verb

to peel, to shell (by hand) · to pick, to gather (berries) · to pick at, to poke at

Inflection

Etymology

samme opprinnelse som pille (III)to poke, to pickthe same origin as pille (III)to poke, to pick

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. pirke, grave;
    pille (III, 1)to pick at something    to poke, to dig about;
    to pick at somethingpille (III, 1)
    • pelle seg i nesato pick one's nose
  2. plukke, rive, slite;
    pille (III, 2)to strip    to pluck, to tear, to pull;
    to strippille (III, 2)
    • pelle noe fra hverandreto pick something apart
    • pelle rekerto peel prawns
  3. plukke eller sanke (noe smått, særlig bær)to pick or gather (small things, especially berries)
    • pelle bærto pick berries
