angi

verbverb

to state · to specify · to inform on

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av lavtysk angeven from Low German angeven

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. opplyse om, nevne, tilkjennegi to state, to mention, to indicate
    • angi farten to state the speed
    • som tidligere angitt as stated earlier
    • angi verdien av noe to specify the value of something
    • angi kilde to give a source
    • angi som grunn to give as a reason
    • angi nærmere to specify in more detail
  2. melde (II, 1)to report to the police, røpeto give away · to reveal · to betray, tysteto inform on someone to report to the policemelde (II, 1), to give awayrøpe, to inform on someonetyste
    • bli angitt av sine egne to be turned in by one's own people
    • angi seg selv to turn oneself in
    • angi en to inform on someone
