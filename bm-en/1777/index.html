angi

verb

to state · to specify · to inform on

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk angevenfrom Low German angeven

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. opplyse om, nevne, tilkjennegito state, to mention, to indicate
    • angi fartento state the speed
    • som tidligere angittas stated earlier
    • angi verdien av noeto specify the value of something
    • angi kildeto give a source
    • angi som grunnto give as a reason
    • angi nærmereto specify in more detail
  2. melde (II, 1)to report to the police, røpeto give away · to reveal · to betray, tysteto inform on someoneto report to the policemelde (II, 1), to give awayrøpe, to inform on someonetyste
    • bli angitt av sine egneto be turned in by one's own people
    • angi seg selvto turn oneself in
    • angi ento inform on someone
