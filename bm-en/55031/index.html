snakke

verbverb

to talk · to speak

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av lavtysk snacken, opprinnelig lydord from Low German snacken, originally onomatopoeic

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. prate, samtale;
    jamfør snakkesto talk to each other, to speak later · to be talked about, to be a topic of conversation     to chat, to converse;
    compare snakkesto talk to each other, to speak later · to be talked about, to be a topic of conversation
    • snakke med hverandre to talk to each other
    • vi snakket sammen på telefon we talked on the phone
    • jeg snakker på vegne av flere I speak on behalf of several people
    • snakke om andre ting to talk about other things
    • det er ikke noe å snakke om there's nothing to talk about
    • vi har aldri snakket om det we've never talked about it
  2. skravle to chatter, to prattle
    • snakke i ett kjør to talk non-stop
    • snakke seg fra noe to talk one's way out of something
    • han snakker i vei om hobbyen sin he talks away about his hobby
  3. bruke sin taleevne to use one's power of speech
    • barnet kunne ikke snakke ennå the child couldn't speak yet
    • snakke rent to speak clearly
    • snakke russisk to speak Russian
    • snakk høyere! speak up!
  4. drøfte to discuss
    • snakke forretninger to talk business
  5. sladre to gossip
    • de snakket om henne over hele byen the whole town was talking about her
  6. brukt i utrop om noe storartet used in exclamations about something splendid
    • snakk om flaks! talk about luck!
    • du snakker om flott forestilling! talk about a great show!

snakke til
  1. henvende seg til to address someone
    • politikeren snakker til hele landet the politician is speaking to the whole country
  2. irettesette to reprimand Eng. phraseto tell someone off
    • det nyttet ikke å snakke til bråkmakerne it was no use telling the troublemakers off

  • de snakket ut om det de hadde opplevd they talked openly about what they had experienced
