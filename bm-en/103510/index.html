kutte ut

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. slutte med;
    fjerne, sløyfe    to give up;
    to remove, to leave out     Eng. phraseto cut out
    • kutte ut alkohol og sigaretterto give up alcohol and cigarettes
    • 150 årsverk skulle kuttes ut150 full-time positions were to be cut
  2. slutte, stanseto stop, to quit Eng. phraseto cut it out
    • hold opp, da, kutt ut!stop it, will you, cut it out!
    • kan du ikke bare kutte ut!why don't you just pack it in!
