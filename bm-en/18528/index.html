fyllest

noun masculine

to pull one's weight, to do one's fair share (in gjøre fyllest for seg) · to be done in full, to be fulfilled (in skje fyllest)

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk vulleisten, opprinnelig ‘utrette til fulle’from Low German vulleisten, originally 'to accomplish in full'
