nettopp

adverb

just · exactly · precisely

Etymology

av nett (II)neat, trim · pretty og oppup · open (with verbs of opening) · up, completely (marking a process finished), opprinnelig ‘helt opp, akkurat tilstrekkelig’from nett (II)neat, trim · pretty and oppup · open (with verbs of opening) · up, completely (marking a process finished), originally 'right up, just enough'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. nøyaktig (2)exactly, akkurat (1)justexactlynøyaktig (2), justakkurat (1)
    • nettopp, slik er det!precisely, that's how it is!
    • det er nettopp det jeg menerthat is exactly what I mean
    • hun er ikke nettopp den flinkeste i klassenshe is not exactly the cleverest in the class
  2. for et øyeblikk siden;
    i sted    a moment ago;
    just now
    • de gikk nettoppthey just left
  3. i øyeblikket;
    akkurat nå    at the moment;
    right now
    • jeg står nettopp og venterI'm just standing here waiting
  4. om et øyeblikkin a moment
    • filmen skulle nettopp begynnethe film was just about to start
