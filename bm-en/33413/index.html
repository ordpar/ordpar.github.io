kåpe

noun masculine or feminine
Inflection

Etymology

gjennom norrønt kápa, fra latin capa; jamfør kappe (I)a cloak, a cape · a gown (academic or official) · a pelmet, a valance · a mantle (of a mollusc)through Old Norse kápa, from Latin capa; compare kappe (I)a cloak, a cape · a gown (academic or official) · a pelmet, a valance · a mantle (of a mollusc)
