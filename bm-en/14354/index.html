fenomen

noun neuter

a phenomenon

Inflection

Etymology

fra gresk av phainesthai ‘vise seg’from Greek, from phainesthai 'to appear'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det som oppfattes av sansene, i motsetning til ‘tingen i seg selv’, foreteelsethat which is perceived by the senses, as opposed to 'the thing in itself'; an occurrence
    • som fenomen betraktetconsidered as a phenomenon
    • et universelt fenomena universal phenomenon
    • nordlys er et vanlig fenomen i Nord-Norgethe northern lights are a common phenomenon in Northern Norway
    • fenomenenes verdenthe world of phenomena
  2. noe merkelig og ualminneligsomething strange and out of the ordinary
    • hun er et fenomen til å strikke – oppsiktsvekkende dyktigshe is a phenomenon at knitting – remarkably skilled
    • fenomenet hildringthe phenomenon of mirage
