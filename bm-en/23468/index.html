hete III homograph 3

verbverb

to be called · to be named

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt heita Old Norse heita

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. ha som navn to have as one's name
    • jeg heter Leah, hva heter du? my name is Leah, what's yours?
    • han heter Christian etter bestefaren he is named Christian after his grandfather
    • hva het firmaet du arbeidet for? what was the name of the company you worked for?
    • hovedstaden i Norge heter Oslo the capital of Norway is called Oslo
    • det fjellet der heter Svartkollen that mountain over there is called Svartkollen
    • avisen heter Nordlys the newspaper is called Nordlys
    • det er så sant som jeg heter Eva that is as true as my name is Eva
  2. kalles (2)to be called to be calledkalles (2)
    • det er noe som heter anstendighet there is such a thing as decency
    • det er ikke noe som heter gidder ikke, kom igjen! there's no such thing as 'can't be bothered', come on!
    • være levende interessert i alt som heter teater og ballett to be passionately interested in anything to do with theatre and ballet
    • hun kan ikke eie det som heter samvittighet she doesn't have a shred of conscience
    • sykdommen tappet meg for alt som heter energi the illness drained me of every last bit of energy
  3. ha som (korrekt) språklig betegnelse to have as its (correct) term
    • hva heter det på norsk? what is it called in Norwegian?
    • det heter ‘nitid’, ikke ‘nitidig’ it is nitid 'meticulous', not nitidig
