overgang

noun masculine

a transition · a crossing · a transfer · a conversion

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør norrønt yfirgangrcompare Old Norse yfirgangr

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sted der en kan komme seg over en vei, en jernbane og anneta place where one can get across a road, a railway and the like
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som fjelloverganga mountain pass, fotgjengeroverganga pedestrian crossing (or footbridge)
  2. stedet der unnarennet går over i slettathe point where the landing slope passes into the flat
    • hopperen var ustø i overgangenthe jumper was unsteady over the transition
  3. fortsatt reise med annet tog, annen buss eller lignendecontinued travel on another train, bus or the like
    • billetten gir rett til fri overgang fra trikk til bussthe ticket entitles you to a free transfer from tram to bus
  4. det å gå over til noe annetthe act of going over to something else
    • hennes overgang til katolisismenher conversion to Catholicism
    • overgang fra skole til arbeidslivthe transition from school to working life
  5. kortvarig, forbigående tilstand;
    mellomstadium    a short-lived, temporary state;
    an intermediate stage
    • overgangen mellom romantikk og realismethe transition between Romanticism and Realism
    • det er bare en overgangit's only a passing phase
