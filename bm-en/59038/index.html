synd

noun feminine or masculine

a shame · a pity · sin

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt syndOld Norse synd

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i religion: handling eller tanke som strider mot Guds viljein religion: an act or a thought that goes against God's will
    • de fikk tilgivelse for sine synderthey received forgiveness for their sins
    • Kristus døde for våre synderChrist died for our sins
    • jeg må bekjenne mine synderI must confess my sins
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som arvesyndoriginal sin, dødssynddeadly sin
  2. handling som blir oppfattet som umoralsk eller ulovlig;
    ulovlighetan illegal act, forgåelsea transgression    an act that is regarded as immoral or unlawful;
    an illegal actulovlighet, a transgressionforgåelse
    • jeg har mange synder på samvittighetenI have many sins on my conscience
    • han begikk den synden å snakke i mobilen mens han kjørte bilhe committed the sin of talking on his mobile while driving
  3. brukt som adjektiv: sørgelig, illeused as an adjective: sad, unfortunate
    • nei, så synd!oh, what a shame!
    • det var synd at det skulle ende slikit was a pity that it had to end like that
    • det vil være synd for dem om det ikke gårit would be a shame for them if it doesn't work out
