a IV homograph 4

preposition

in, on, with (in Italian loan phrases such as a cappella)

Etymology

fra italiensk, samme opprinnelse som ad (II)regarding; jamfør a (III)at (a price of) · of, each (a quantity per unit)from Italian, of the same origin as ad (II)regarding; compare a (III)at (a price of) · of, each (a quantity per unit)
