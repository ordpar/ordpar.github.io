lense II homograph 2

verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av nederlandsk og lavtysk lenzen; jamfør lens (II)out of, empty, cleaned out · dry, pumped out of water from Dutch and Low German lenzen; compare lens (II)out of, empty, cleaned out · dry, pumped out of water

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

tømme, særlig for vann to empty, especially of water
  • lense en båt to bail out a boat
  • lense en gruve for vann to drain a mine of water
  • pumpa lenser ikke the pump isn't pumping the water out
