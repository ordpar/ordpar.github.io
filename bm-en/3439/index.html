avfall

noun neuter

waste · refuse, rubbish

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt affall ‘verditap, avslag’, opprinnelig ‘noe som falt av’; jamfør tysk AbfallOld Norse affall ‘loss of value, price reduction’, originally ‘something that fell off’; compare German Abfall

Senses and Example Sentences

(verdiløse) rester fra industri, husholdning eller lignende;
rask (I, 1)refuse, søppel (1)rubbish(worthless) leftovers from industry, households and the like;
refuserask (I, 1), rubbishsøppel (1)
  • avfall fra slaktingwaste from slaughtering
  • kaste avfallet i en søppeldunkto throw the waste in a rubbish bin
