trenge

verb

to need · to force one's way, to push

Inflection

Etymology

av trang (II)cramped, tight · narrow · straitened, hard upfrom trang (II)cramped, tight · narrow · straitened, hard up

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ha behov for;
    behøve;
    jamfør trengendeneedy, in need og trengtin need of something, feeling an urge for something    to have need of;
    to need, to require;
    compare trengendeneedy, in need and trengtin need of something, feeling an urge for something
    • barn trenger melkchildren need milk
    • jeg kan trenge litt hjelpI could use some help
    • de trenger ikke å bry segthey don't need to bother
  2. presse (II, 1)to press, trykketo presspresse (II, 1), to push
    • de trenger seg sammen om vinnerenthey crowd together around the winner
    • han trengte på for å komme først i køenhe pushed to get to the front of the queue
    • tårene trenger seg framthe tears force their way out
    • hun har trengt seg inn i et husshe has forced her way into a house
  3. i overført betydning: streve med å komme fram til noefiguratively: to struggle to reach or grasp something
    • hun forsøkte å trenge inn i det vanskelige stoffetshe tried to penetrate the difficult material
