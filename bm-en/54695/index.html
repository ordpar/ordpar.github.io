smake

verbverb

to taste · to try, to sample

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk from Low German

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. ha en bestemt smak (1)taste to have a particular tastesmak (1)
    • suppa smakte vondt the soup tasted bad
    • røye smaker omtrent som ørret Arctic char tastes much like trout
  2. bli eller være godt to be or become enjoyable
    • nå smakte det virkelig med kaffe a coffee really hit the spot just then
    • nå skal det virkelig smake med ferie a holiday is going to be really welcome now
  3. prøve smaken på noe;
    drikke eller spise en liten porsjon     to try the taste of something;
    to drink or eat a small amount
    • han smakte på ølet he tasted the beer
    • vil du smake et stykke? would you like to try a piece?
    • smake seg fram til den beste iskremen to taste your way to the best ice cream
  4. nyte (3)to consume to consumenyte (3)
    • ikke smake alkohol not to touch alcohol
  5. få føle;
    kjenne     to get to experience;
    to know at first hand
    • hun fikk smake både seier og nederlag she tasted both victory and defeat
  6. tenke over;
    prøve ut;
    vurdere     to think over;
    to try out;
    to weigh up
    • han smaker på ordet he savours the word

    to resemble     Eng. phraseto smack of

to look promising
