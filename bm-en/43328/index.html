orientere

verb

to brief, to inform · to orient, position · to do orienteering

Inflection

Etymology

fra fransk, opprinnelig ‘bygge (kirke) med fasade mot øst’; jamfør orientthe east, the Orientfrom French, originally 'to build (a church) with its façade facing east'; compare orientthe east, the Orient

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. plassere eller vende i en bestemt retningto place or turn in a particular direction
    • husene er orientert nord–sørthe houses are oriented north–south
    • orientere kartetto orient the map
  2. i landmåling: tegne inn himmelretningene på kartin surveying: to mark the cardinal directions on a map
  3. sette inn i;
    opplyse (2)to inform, veilede (1)to advise    to acquaint someone with something;
    to informopplyse (2), to adviseveilede (1)
    • formannen vil orientere medlemmene om sakenthe chairman will brief the members on the matter
    • hun ble orientert om den siste utviklingenshe was informed of the latest developments
  4. drive med orientering (4)orienteering;
    delta i orienteringsløp    to do orienteeringorientering (4);
    to take part in orienteering races
