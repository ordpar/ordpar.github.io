bykse

verb

to bound, to leap

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt byxa sér ‘hoppe’, trolig gjennom lavtysk; fra middelhøytysk bückezen ‘hoppe som en bukk’Old Norse byxa sér ‘to jump’, probably through Low German; from Middle High German bückezen ‘to jump like a billy goat’

Senses and Example Sentences

gjøre et byksa sudden leap;
springe, hoppeto make a sudden leapbyks;
to run, to jump
  • bykse ut gjennom dørato bound out through the door
  • bykse over bekkento leap across the stream
  • han bykset opp trappahe bounded up the stairs
  • hunden bykset tilthe dog gave a sudden jump
