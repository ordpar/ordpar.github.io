slapp

adjective

slack, limp · listless, worn out · lax, half-hearted

Inflection

Etymology

trolig fra lavtysk slap ‘kraftlaus’probably from Low German slap 'feeble'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som ikke er stram;
    som henger løst    not taut, slack;
    hanging loose
    • seilene hang slappethe sails hung limp
    • slappe tøylerslack reins
    • en slapp holdninga slouching posture
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • armene hang slapt nedhis arms hung limply down
  2. kraftløsweak, feeble, drained, matt (II, 2)worn out;
    trøtt, veik    drainedkraftløs, worn outmatt (II, 2);
    tired, weak
    • slapp i beinaweak in the legs
    • et slapt håndtrykka limp handshake
  3. doven (1)indolent, likeglad, latindolentdoven (1), indifferent, lazy
    • en slapp fyra slacker
    • være slapp på skolento be lazy at school
  4. lite effektivnot very effective
    • kontrollen var slappthe checks were lax
  5. preget av liten etterspørsel;
    treg    marked by low demand;
    sluggish
    • aksjemarkedet er slapt for tidenthe stock market is slack at the moment
