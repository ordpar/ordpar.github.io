karve III homograph 3

verb
Inflection

Etymology

beslektet med lavtysk kerven ‘skjære’related to Low German kerven ‘to cut’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. skjære i småbiter, hakke med knivto cut into small pieces, to chop with a knife
    • karve sund noeto chop something up
    • karve i pipato shred tobacco into one's pipe
  2. skjære ut (i tre eller bein)to carve (in wood or bone)
    • hun karvet navnet inn i treetshe carved the name into the tree
