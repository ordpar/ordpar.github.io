fordømme

verb

to condemn, to denounce · to damn

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt fordǿma; fra lavtyskOld Norse fordǿma; from Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i religiøst språk: dømme til evig straffin religious language: to condemn to eternal punishment
    brukt som substantiv:used as a noun:
    • de fordømtethe damned
  2. ta avstand fra;
    klandre skarpt    to distance oneself from;
    to condemn harshly
    • fordømme terrorhandlingerto condemn acts of terrorism
