begå

verb

to commit (a crime or other wrongful act) · to make (a mistake), to do (something foolish)

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk ‘gå hen til’from Low German ‘to go over to’

Senses and Example Sentences

gjøre seg skyldig i;
utøve;
utføreto make oneself guilty of;
to perpetrate;
to carry out

  • begå en dumhetto do something stupid

  • rusavhengige som begår kriminalitetdrug addicts who commit crimes
  • hun begikk den feilen at hun stolte på hamshe made the mistake of trusting him
