tone I homograph 1

noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

a note (musical) · a tone (of voice or of writing) · a tune · a tinge, a hue

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt tóni ‘tone, musikalsk lyd’; opprinnelig fra gresk tonos ‘spenning, spent streng, tone’ Old Norse tóni 'tone, musical sound'; originally from Greek tonos 'tension, a taut string, tone'
