gå ned for telling

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bli slått ut av funksjon;
    gå svært dårlig for    to be knocked out of action;
    to go very badly for someone     Eng. phraseto go down for the count
    • løperen gikk ned for telling mot slutten av løpetthe runner went down for the count towards the end of the race
    • datasystemene gikk ned for full tellingthe computer systems went down completely
  2. bli svært syk;
    bli skadet    to fall seriously ill;
    to be injured     Eng. phraseto go down for the count
    • spilleren fikk en albue i ansiktet og gikk ned for tellingthe player took an elbow to the face and went down for the count
    • jeg går alltid ned for full telling når jeg får influensaI always go right down for the count when I get flu
