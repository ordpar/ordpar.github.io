justC, level C

adverbadverb

exactly, precisely · just, a moment ago (of time)

EtymologyOpphav

gjennom lavtysk; fra latin juste, av justus ‘rettferdig, rett’ av jus ‘rett’ through Low German; from Latin juste, from justus 'just, right', from jus 'right, law'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. akkurat (1)exactly, nettopp (1)precisely;
    jamfør just somjust as · just as if, as though     exactlyakkurat (1), preciselynettopp (1);
    compare just somjust as · just as if, as though
    • ja, det er just slik det er yes, that is just how it is
    • det var just ikke så enkelt it was not exactly that simple
  2. om tid: for et øyeblikk siden;
    nettopp (2)just now     of time: a moment ago;
    just nownettopp (2)
    • just nå kom de they arrived just now
