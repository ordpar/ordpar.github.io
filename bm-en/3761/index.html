avsette

verb

to depose · to dismiss (from office) · to set aside

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi avskjed (fra et embete, en stilling, et verv);
    si opp    to dismiss (from an office, a post, a position of trust);
    to remove from office
    • avsette presidentento depose the president
  2. reservere for et visst formålto set aside for a particular purpose
    • på budsjettet er det avsatt et visst beløp til uforutsette utgiftera certain sum has been set aside in the budget for unforeseen expenses
  3. selgeto sellto sellselge
    • det var ikke noe problem å avsette varenethere was no problem selling the goods
  4. avleireto deposit in layersto deposit in layersavleire
