toppe seg

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stikke opp;
    lage topp    to stick up;
    to form a peak
    • skyene topper segthe clouds are towering up
    • snøen toppet seg utenfor vinduetthe snow was heaped up outside the window
  2. nå et høydepunkt;
    kulminereto culminate    to reach a peak;
    to culminatekulminere
    • dramaet toppet seg i et mordthe drama ended in a murder
    • handelen topper seg i desembertrade peaks in December
    • kaoset toppet seg da statsministeren skrev ut nyvalgthe chaos came to a head when the prime minister called a new election
