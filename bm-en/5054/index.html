beintfram, beintfrem, bentfram, bentfrem

adjectiveadjektiv

straightforward, forthright · straight out, plainly, downright

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av bein (II)straight · direct og framforward · forth, out (into view) · all the way, to the destination from bein (II)straight · direct and framforward · forth, out (into view) · all the way, to the destination
