signere

verb

to sign

Inflection

Etymology

fra latin, av signum ‘tegn’from Latin, from signum ‘sign’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. skrive under;
    sette navnetrekket sitt på    to sign;
    to put one's signature on
    • brevet var signert av styreformannenthe letter was signed by the chairman of the board
    • forfatteren vil være til stede og signere boka sithe author will be present and sign her book
  2. stadfeste opphav, særlig med signatur, initialer eller lignendeto confirm the origin of something, especially with a signature, initials or the like
    • maleriet var signert Edvard Munchthe painting was signed Edvard Munch
  3. skrive kontrakt eller avtale medto sign a contract or agreement with
    • fotballklubben signerte en ny spissthe football club signed a new striker
