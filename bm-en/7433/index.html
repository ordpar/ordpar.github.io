brenne I homograph 1

verb

to burn, to be on fire · to sting, to smart · to be passionate (about)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt brennaOld Norse brenna

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. være i brann;
    stå i flammer;
    flamme (II, 1)to blaze;
    jamfør brennende (1)burning    to be on fire;
    to be in flames;
    to blazeflamme (II, 1);
    compare brennende (1)burning
    • låven brennerthe barn is on fire
    • det brenner hos naboenthere is a fire at the neighbours'
    • adventslysene brant i stakenthe Advent candles were burning in the candlestick
    • det vil ikke brenne i ovnenthe fire will not catch in the stove
  2. lyse som ild;
    skinne kraftig    to glow like fire;
    to shine intensely
    • sola brant på himmelenthe sun blazed in the sky
  3. være eller kjennes hetto be or feel hot
    • huden brant av feberhis skin was burning with fever
    • blodet brenner i årenethe blood is burning in his veins
    • føle jorda brenne under føtteneto feel the ground burning under one's feet
  4. svi (I, 1)to smart, verke (1)to acheto smartsvi (I, 1), to acheverke (1)
    • såret brennerthe wound is burning
    • halsen brant av tørsthis throat was burning with thirst
  5. ha sterke følelser for noe;
    være intenst opptatt av noe;
    kjenne sterk trang eller lyst;
    jamfør brennende (2)ardent    to have strong feelings about something;
    to be intensely engaged with something;
    to feel a strong urge or longing;
    compare brennende (2)ardent
    • brenne for en sakto be passionate about a cause
    • brenne av lyst til å hjelpeto be burning with a desire to help
    • hun brant for faget sittshe was passionate about her subject
    • hun brant etter å komme i gangshe was itching to get started

Set phrases

brenne inne miste livet i husbrannto lose one's life in a house fire Eng. phraseto burn to death
  • hele familien brant innethe whole family perished in the fire
brenne inne med
  1. ikke få solgtto fail to sell Eng. phraseto be left with something on one's hands
    • brenne inne med alle vareneto be left with all the goods unsold
  2. ikke få uttrykt noe en vil ha framto fail to get out something one wants to say
    • hun brant inne med ideenshe never got to air her idea
brenne ned
  1. brenne til det er oppbruktto burn until nothing is left Eng. phraseto burn down
    • lysene brant nedthe candles burned down
  2. bli tilintetgjort av brannto be destroyed by fire Eng. phraseto burn to the ground
    • hele kvartalet brant nedthe whole block burned to the ground
brenne opp brenne til det ikke er noe igjento burn until nothing remains Eng. phraseto burn up
  • bilen eksploderte og brant oppthe car exploded and burned up
brenne ut
  1. brenne til det ikke er noe igjento burn until nothing is left Eng. phraseto burn out
    • bålet har brent utthe bonfire has burned out
    • bilen brant utthe car was gutted by fire
  2. om sykdom: slutte å være aktivof an illness: to stop being active Eng. phraseto burn itself out
    • leddgikta hennes har brent uther rheumatoid arthritis has burned itself out
