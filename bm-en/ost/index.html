“ost” is an inflected form of:

  • ose verbverb (perfect participle masculine / feminine and neuter)(perfektum partisipp hankjønn / hunkjønn og intetkjønn) to reek (of) · to give off smoke or steam · to ooze, radiate (a quality) dampe, dunste
  • oste verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) Norwegian only
