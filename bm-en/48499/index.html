ribbe II homograph 2

verb
Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. plukke eller pille fjærene avto pull or pick the feathers off
    • ribbe en høneto pluck a hen
  2. ta fra;
    berøve, plyndre    to take away from;
    to deprive, to plunder
    • ribbe et husto strip a house bare
    • være ribbet for ære og anseelseto be stripped of honour and standing
