bestå

verbverb

to pass (a test) · to endure · to last

InflectionVis bøyning

PronunciationUttale

beståˊ

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk; av stå (III)to stand · to be, to be situated (of a thing in a place) · to say, to be written (of a text) from Low German; from stå (III)to stand · to be, to be situated (of a thing in a place) · to say, to be written (of a text)
