forutsatt at

subjunction

provided that · assuming that · on condition that

Senses and Example Sentences

innleder en leddsetning som uttrykker vilkår;
på betingelse av at, hvis (II, 1)ifintroduces a subordinate clause expressing a condition;
on condition that, ifhvis (II, 1)
  • vi skal grille i morgen forutsatt at været blir brawe're going to have a barbecue tomorrow, provided the weather is good
