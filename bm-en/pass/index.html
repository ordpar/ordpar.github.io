“pass” is an inflected form of:

  • passe III homograph 3 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to pass melde pass i kortspill
  • passe IV homograph 4 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to look after, to mind · to keep an eye on ha tilsyn med
  • passe V homograph 5 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to fit · to suit, to be convenient være riktig eller egnet
  • passe VI homograph 6 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to pass i lagspill, for eksempel fotball: sende ballen videre til medspiller
