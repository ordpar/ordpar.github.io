hinder

noun neuter

obstacle · hindrance · jump

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt hindrOld Norse hindr

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å hindrethe act of hindering
    • være til hinder forto be an obstacle to
    • det er ingenting til hinder for at du kan reisethere is nothing to stop you from travelling
  2. noe som hindrersomething that gets in the way, an obstacle
    • ikke noe hinder er uoverstigelig for henneno obstacle is insurmountable for her
  3. i hesteveddeløp, friidrett: oppbygd hindringin horse racing and athletics: a purpose-built obstacle
    • passere det siste hinderet i fin stilto clear the last jump in fine style
    • 3000 m hinderthe 3000 m steeplechase
