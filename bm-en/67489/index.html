vellykket, vellykt, vellykka

adjective

successful

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som har et ønsket eller lykkelig utfall;
    med godt eller gunstig resultat;
    til forskjell fra mislykketunsuccessful · failed · botched    which has a desired or happy outcome;
    with a good or favourable result;
    as opposed to unsuccessfulmislykket
    • en vellykket festa successful party
    • operasjonen var vellykketthe operation was a success
    • forsøket på etterligning var ikke særlig vellykketthe attempt at imitation was not very successful
  2. som gjør stor suksess;
    suksessriksuccessful, suksessfullsuccessful, framgangsrikthriving    who achieves great success;
    successful, thrivingframgangsrik – compare suksessriksuccessful and suksessfullsuccessful
    • hun er utvilsomt et vellykket barnshe is without doubt a child who has turned out well
    • ha en svært vellykket karriereto have a very successful career
