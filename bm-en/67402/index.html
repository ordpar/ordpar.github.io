vel III homograph 3

adverb

probably, I suppose (unstressed particle) · well · a good, rather too (before a quantity)

Etymology

norrønt vel; trolig beslektet med viljewill · willingness · determinationOld Norse vel; probably related to viljewill · willingness · determination
