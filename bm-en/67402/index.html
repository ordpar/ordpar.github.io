vel III homograph 3

adverbadverb

probably, I suppose (unstressed particle) · well · a good, rather too (before a quantity)

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt vel; trolig beslektet med viljewill · willingness · determination Old Norse vel; probably related to viljewill · willingness · determination

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. god, bra good, well
    • gjøre vel mot noen to do good to someone
    • ville andre vel to wish others well
    • føle seg vel to feel comfortable
    • komme vel med to come in handy
    • alt vel? all well?
  2. nøye (I, 2)closely, grundigthorough · thoroughly closelynøye (I, 2), thoroughlygrundig
    • tenke seg vel om to think it over carefully
    • se vel etter to look closely
  3. fullt ut;
    rikelig (1)amply, ettertrykkeligemphatic, forceful, firm, god (12)perfectly well     fully, completely;
    amplyrikelig (1), emphaticallyettertrykkelig, perfectly wellgod (12)
    • vente både vel og lenge to wait long and hard
    • du vet meget vel at jeg ikke kan kommentere saken you know perfectly well that I can't comment on the matter
    • lønnen var vel fortjent the pay was well deserved
    • museet er vel verdt en tur the museum is well worth a visit
  4. i overkant (av);
    for (V, 16)too, altfor (1)far too, drøy (4)a good     rather too much; a bit over;
    toofor (V, 16), far tooaltfor (1), a gooddrøy (4)
    • det ble vel mye baksnakking, syns jeg that was rather too much gossip, I think
    • talen var litt vel lang the speech was a little too long
    • det er vel hundre deltakere påmeldt there are a good hundred participants signed up
    • det skjedde for vel et år siden it happened a good year ago
  5. rett nok;
    riktignok     true enough;
    admittedly
    • vel er det vanskelig, men ikke umulig it's true it's difficult, but not impossible
    • vel har jeg gjort tabber før, men denne gangen går det nok bedre true, I've made mistakes before, but this time it'll probably go better
  6. brukt for å uttrykke konstatering eller oppsummering used to state or sum something up
    • vel, så sier vi det slik well, let's put it that way then
    • vel, har du mer å si? well, do you have more to say?
    • vel vel, da snakkes vi i morgen well well, we'll talk tomorrow then
  7. brukt for å uttrykke noe som synes opplagt: da (III, 5)after all used to express something that seems obvious: after allda (III, 5)
    • hva er det dere har som de andre mangler? Entusiasme, vel! what do you have that the others lack? Enthusiasm, of course!
    • hva er vel bedre enn at … what could be better than …
    • det ser du vel! surely you can see that!
  8. brukt for å uttrykke noe sannsynlig: nok (I)enough · probably, I suppose, troliglikely · probably, venteligto be expected, expected · probably, presumably, likely used to express something probable: I supposenok (I), probablytrolig, presumablyventelig
    • de greier det vel they'll probably manage it
    • slikt kan vel skje that sort of thing can happen, I suppose
    • det blir vel til at vi reiser I expect we'll end up going
  9. brukt for å uttrykke at noe ønskes bekreftet eller klarlagt used to express a wish for confirmation or clarification
    • du kommer vel i kveld? you're coming tonight, aren't you?
    • du er vel ikke syk? you're not ill, are you?
  10. brukt i utrop for å rose eller ønske noen lykke used in exclamations to praise or wish someone well
    • vel hjem da! get home safe!
    • lev vel! farewell!
