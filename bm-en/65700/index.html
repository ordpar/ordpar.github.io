utsatt

adjectiveadjektiv

vulnerable, exposed · postponed, deferred · released (into the wild)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

jamfør setteto put · to place · to set og utsetteto postpone · to expose compare setteto put · to place · to set and utsetteto postpone · to expose

    risikofylt     that is easily affected, vulnerable;
