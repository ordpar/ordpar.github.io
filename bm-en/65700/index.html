utsatt

adjective

vulnerable, exposed · postponed, deferred · released (into the wild)

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør setteto put · to place · to set og utsetteto postpone · to exposecompare setteto put · to place · to set and utsetteto postpone · to expose

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som en venter medthat is deferred
    • gå opp til utsatt prøveto sit a deferred exam
  2. som er satt ut (2)released into the wildwhich has been released into the wildsatt ut (2)
    • utsatt fiskeyngelreleased fish fry
  3. som lett rammes;
    risikofylt    that is easily affected, vulnerable;
    exposed, risky
    • kuling på utsatte stedergale-force winds in exposed places
    • gården ligger utsatt til for rasthe farm lies exposed to landslides
    • ha en utsatt stillingto hold a vulnerable position
