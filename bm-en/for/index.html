“for” is an inflected form of:

  • fare II homograph 2 verbverb (past)(preteritum) to go, to travel · to rush, to race forflytte seg
  • fôre II homograph 2, fore II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to feed (animals) · to feed (material into something) gi fôr
  • fôre III homograph 3, fore III homograph 3 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to line (a garment) · to clad, to pack out sy fôr i
