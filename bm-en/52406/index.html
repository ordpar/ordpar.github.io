skake

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt skakaOld Norse skaka

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bevege fort fram og tilbake eller opp og ned;
    riste hardt    to move quickly back and forth or up and down;
    to shake hard
    • huset skaket i stormenthe house shook in the storm
    • han tok slyngelen i kragen og skaket hamhe grabbed the scoundrel by the collar and shook him
    • hestene skaket segthe horses shook themselves
    • skake på segto give oneself a shake
  2. humpe (1)to bump alongto bump alonghumpe (1)
    • traktoren skaker av sted over åkerenthe tractor jolts along across the field
  3. skremme opp;
    sjokkere;
    ryste (1)to shake;
    jamfør skakendeharrowing    to startle;
    to shock;
    to shakeryste (1);
    compare skakendeharrowing
    • opplevelsen skaket hennethe experience shook her
