gate I homograph 1

noun feminine or masculine

a street

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt gataOld Norse gata

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ferdselsåre i by eller tettsted med husrekker på begge sider;
    forkortet g. i adresser    a thoroughfare in a town or built-up area with rows of houses on both sides;
    abbreviated g. in addresses
    • gå ut på gatato go out into the street
    • gå gate opp og gate nedto walk up and down the streets
    • krysse gatato cross the street
    • bo i Kongens gateto live in Kongens gate
    • butikken ligger rett over gatathe shop is right across the street
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som bakgatea back street, a backstreet, gågatea pedestrian street, a pedestrianised street, hovedgatea main street, a high street
  2. ryddet stripe i skoga cleared strip in a forest
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som branngatea firebreak, kraftgatea power line clearing, a cleared strip under a power line
