gro

verb

to grow (of plants, hair and the like) · to heal (of a wound) · to sprout, to germinate

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt gróaOld Norse gróa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. begynne å vokse fra frø, frukt eller knoll;
    spire (II)to sprout    to start growing from a seed, a fruit or a tuber;
    to sproutspire (II)
    • frøet grorthe seed is germinating
  2. vokse fram, utvikle seg;
    finnes på et sted    to come up, to develop;
    to be found in a place
    • gresset grorthe grass is growing
    • skjegget gror godthis beard is coming along nicely
    • muggen gror fram på vegger og i vinduskarmermould is growing on the walls and in the window sills
  3. i overført betydning: vokse fram, utvikle seg, finnesfiguratively: to spring up, to develop, to be found
    • nye miljøbevegelser gror framnew environmental movements are springing up
    • i byen har det grodd opp et rikt litterært miljø de siste årenea rich literary scene has grown up in the city over the past few years
  4. om sår: leges, heles, bli bra;
    fylles med nytt kroppsvev    of a wound: to heal, to mend, to get better;
    to fill in with new tissue
    • såret har ikke begynt å gro endathe wound hasn't started to heal yet
    • kuttet gror fintthe cut is healing nicely
  5. få til å vokse;
    la vokse    to make something grow;
    to let something grow
    • gro skjeggto grow a beard

Set phrases

gro fast
  1. bli værende i ro så lenge at en blir hengende fast i underlaget eller en annen gjenstandto stay still so long that one becomes stuck to the surface below or to another object
    • hun var som grodd fast i gulvetshe was as if rooted to the floor
    • klærne begynte å gro fast i såretthe clothes were beginning to stick to the wound
  2. i overført betydning: bli værende på et sted eller i en tilstand så lenge at en ikke greier å forlate stedet eller endre tilstandfiguratively: to remain in a place or a state so long that one can no longer leave it or change it
    • jeg ønsker ikke å gro fast noe stedI don't want to get stuck in one place
    • gro fast i sofaen foran tv-ento take root on the sofa in front of the TV
gro igjen/til vokse til med vegetasjon;
tetne med skittto become overgrown with vegetation;
to become clogged with dirt
  • hagen er grodd tilthe garden has run wild
  • vannet er i ferd med å gro igjenthe lake is slowly growing over
gro ned være i ro så lenge eller bli brukt så lite at noe blir dekket med rot, skitt, vegetasjon eller lignendeto stand undisturbed for so long, or be used so little, that something becomes covered with mess, dirt, vegetation or the like
  • gro ned i skittto be buried in dirt
