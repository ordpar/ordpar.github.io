kline

verb

to smear · to snog, to make out · to press up against

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt klínaOld Norse klína

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stryke over med noe vått eller fett;
    smøre (1)to spread    to smear with something wet or greasy;
    to spreadsmøre (1)
    • ungene klinte smør utover bordetthe children smeared butter all over the table
    • kline på noe malingto slap on some paint
    • kline seg til med noeto get oneself smeared with something
  2. klemme (II, 3)to squeeze, presse (II, 3)to push i en viss retningto squeezeklemme (II, 3), to pushpresse (II, 3) in a certain direction
    • kline seg opp til veggento press oneself up against the wall
  3. kysse (1)to kiss, kjæleto cuddleto kisskysse (1), to cuddlekjæle
    • paret stod og klintethe couple were snogging
