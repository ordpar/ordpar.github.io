oppta

verb

to take up · to admit · to preoccupy

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi plass;
    innlemme    to make room for;
    to admit
    • oppta nye medlemmer i foreningento admit new members to the association
  2. ta imot;
    notere    to receive;
    to record
    • oppta bestillingerto take orders
    • oppta store lånto take out large loans
    • oppta forklaringto take a statement
  3. legge beslag påto take up
    • jeg skal ikke oppta mer av tiden dinI won't take up any more of your time
  4. interessereto interest
    • saken opptar allethe matter is on everyone's mind
