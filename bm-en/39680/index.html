mye

adjectiveadjektiv

much, a lot of · a lot, greatly

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt mykit, mikit, av mikill ‘stor’; jamfør meget (II)very, mermore · any more og mestmost · mostly, most often · almost Old Norse mykit, mikit, from mikill 'big'; compare meget (II)very, mermore · any more and mestmost · mostly, most often · almost

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. som fins i stor mengde eller i sterk grad found in large quantity or to a high degree
    • mye bær a lot of berries
    • mye regn a lot of rain
    • mye arbeid a lot of work
    • blir det mye bry for deg? will it be a lot of trouble for you?
    • det fikk du ikke mye glede av you didn't get much pleasure out of that
  2. brukt som substantiv: stor mengde, stor del (av);
    mange (ting)     used as a noun: a large amount, a large part (of);
    many (things)
    • spise mye to eat a lot
    • dobbelt så mye twice as much
    • det er ikke mye som skal til it doesn't take much
    • det er ikke mye å rope hurra for it's nothing to shout about
    • hun sier ikke så mye she doesn't say much
    • mye kunne ha vært gjort annerledes much could have been done differently
    • mye av pengene er borte much of the money is gone
    • få mye for pengene to get a lot for your money
    • ville det være for mye forlangt? would that be too much to ask?
    • hvor mye koster det? how much does it cost?
    • hvor mye er klokka? what time is it?
  3. brukt som adverb: i høy grad;
    sterkt, ofte     used as an adverb: to a high degree;
    strongly, often
    • det varierer mye it varies a lot
    • vi omgås mye we see a lot of each other
    • du må hilse så mye! be sure to give my regards!
    • det koster for mye it costs too much
    • han har vært her mye i det siste he's been here a lot lately
  4. brukt framfor adjektiv og adverb i komparativ: i høy grad, svært used before an adjective or adverb in the comparative: to a high degree, much
    • mye bedre much better
    • jeg vil mye heller være hjemme I'd much rather be at home
  5. brukt i streng og irettesettende tiltale used in stern, reproachful address
    • nå kommer du, så mye du vet det! you're coming now, just so you know it!
