framkalle, fremkalle

verb

to evoke, to bring about, to induce · to develop (photographs)

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. kalle fram, forårsaketo call forth, to bring about
    • framkalle minnerto evoke memories
    • framkalle munterhetto provoke amusement
  2. få fram bilder ved hjelp av kjemikalierto bring out images by means of chemicals
    • framkalle en filmto develop a film
