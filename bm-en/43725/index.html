overse

verb

to overlook · to ignore

Inflection

Etymology

fra tysk eller lavtysk; jamfør seto see · to look · to watchfrom German or Low German; compare seto see · to look · to watch

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ikke bli oppmerksom på;
    ikke se    to fail to notice;
    to miss
    • overse en trykkfeilto overlook a typo
  2. late som en ikke ser;
    se bort fra;
    ringeakte    to pretend not to see;
    to disregard;
    to slight
    • hun overser meg fullstendigshe ignores me completely
