tilby

verb

to offer

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør by (II)to offer · to invite · to bidfrom by (II)to offer · to invite · to bid

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. si seg villig til å gi, låne bort, selge eller lignendeto declare oneself willing to give, lend, sell or the like
    • de tilbød oss å låne huset mens de var bortethey offered to let us borrow the house while they were away
    • gjestene ble tilbudt drinker som velkomstthe guests were offered drinks as a welcome
  2. si seg villig til å gjøre noeto declare oneself willing to do something
    • de tilbyr hjelpthey are offering help
    • han tilbød styret å fratre stillingenhe offered the board his resignation

Set phrases

  • tilby seg å hjelpeto offer to help
