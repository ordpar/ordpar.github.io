vurdering

noun feminine or masculine

an assessment · an evaluation · a judgement

Inflection

Etymology

av vurdereto assess, to evaluate · to consider (doing something) · to value, to appraisefrom vurdereto assess, to evaluate · to consider (doing something) · to value, to appraise

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å fastsette en viss (økonomisk) verdithe act of setting a certain (financial) value
  2. det å gjøre et (omtrentlig) overslag over størrelsen, omfanget eller kvaliteten av noethe act of making an (approximate) estimate of the size, extent or quality of something
  3. det å tillegge noe eller noen en bestemt verdithe act of attributing a particular value to something or someone
  4. gjøre seg opp en mening om et forhold, en tilstand, en hendelse, en utvikling eller lignende;
    bedømmelsean assessment, a judgement, an evaluation    the forming of an opinion about a situation, a state, an event, a development or the like;
    a judgementbedømmelse
    • være uenig i vurderingen deresto disagree with their assessment
  5. det å veie for og imotthe act of weighing the pros and cons
