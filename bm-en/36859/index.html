mang

determiner quantifier

many a, many · much, all sorts of things

Inflection

Etymology

av mellomnorsk mangr, trolig fra østnordisk, og ubestemt artikkelfrom Middle Norwegian mangr, probably from East Scandinavian, and the indefinite article

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i entall: en av flerein the singular: one of several
    • han har tatt mangt et tungt takhe has done many a hard day's work
    • vi har hatt mang en trivelig stundwe have had many a pleasant time
    • mang en ung jente måtte ut i tjenestemany a young girl had to go into service
    • mang en sommerdag drog han til fjellson many a summer's day he went up into the mountains
    • det hendte mang en gangit happened many a time
  2. i nøytrum: mye forskjelligin the neuter: many different things
    • de fortalte mangt og myethey talked about all sorts of things
    • ha mangt å læreto have much to learn
  3. i flertall: atskillige, ikke så fåin the plural: several, quite a few
    • mange andremany others
    • vi måtte gå mange gangerwe had to go many times
