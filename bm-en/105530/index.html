strekke ut

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. tøye ut, for eksempel etter treningto loosen up the muscles, for example after exercise Eng. phraseto stretch out
  2. om tid: vare lengeof time: to last a long time
    • valgkampen burde vært strukket lenger ut i tidthe election campaign should have been spread out over a longer period
