ensporet, enspora

adjective
Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. med ett spor (2)a way in the form of a railway track, a (prepared) ski trail or the like
    • en ensporet jernbane
  2. i overført betydning: ensidig (4)which stresses only one side of a matter;
    narrow-minded;
    as opposed to versatile, multifaceted and many-sided    , sneversynt
    • være ensporet
    • ha en ensporet tankegang
