toppe

verb

to top · to surpass · to lop the top off

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ha den høyeste eller beste rangeringento have the highest or best ranking
    • etter siste serieomgang topper Brann tabellenafter the last round of league matches Brann tops the table
    • desember topper listen når det er flest branner i landetDecember tops the list of months with the most fires in the country
    • han toppet listen over de rikeste i landethe topped the list of the richest people in the country
  2. overgå, slå (II, 3)to beat;
    være bedre enn    to surpass, to beatslå (II, 3);
    to be better than
    • denne bragden kan vi vanskelig toppethat is a feat we will find hard to top
  3. lage en topp på;
    plassere øverst    to form a top on something;
    to place on top
    • topp kaken med friske bærtop the cake with fresh berries
    • salaten var toppet med valnøtterthe salad was topped with walnuts
  4. lage eller utvikle topp eller aksto form or develop a top or an ear
    • treet topper segthe tree is putting out a crown
    • knip av de øverste skuddene slik at planten topper seg bedrepinch off the topmost shoots so that the plant forms a better top
  5. fjerne eller hogge toppen avto remove or cut off the top of
    • toppe et treto top a tree
