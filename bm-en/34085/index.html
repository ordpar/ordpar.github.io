lave

verb

to come down thick and fast, to pour down · to hang thick, to be laden

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt lafa; beslektet med lav (I)lichenOld Norse lafa; related to lav (I)lichen

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. henge i stor mengde eller i klaser;
    bugneto be laden    to hang in great quantity or in clusters;
    to be ladenbugne
    • treet laver av knopperthe tree is bursting with buds
  2. drysse ned i mengder, falle i store flakto come down in masses, to fall in big flakes
    • snøen laver nedthe snow is coming down thick and fast
