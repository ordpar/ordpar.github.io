vis-à-vis, vis-a-vis

preposition

Etymology

fra franskfrom French

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. overfor (1)facing, rett imotfacingoverfor (1), directly opposite
    • sitte vis-à-vis hverandre i kupeento sit opposite each other in the compartment
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • de flyttet inn i huset vis-à-visthey moved into the house opposite
  2. i overført betydning: i forholdet til;
    når det gjelder;
    overfor (4)towards    figuratively: in relation to;
    as regards;
    towardsoverfor (4)
    • stå friere vis-à-vis utlandetto be in a freer position vis-à-vis other countries
