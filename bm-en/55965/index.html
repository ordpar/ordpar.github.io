spenne II homograph 2

verb

to buckle, strap, fasten · to tense, flex, tighten · to span, range, stretch over

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt spennaOld Norse spenna

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. spile eller strekke utto stretch or spread out taut
    • han spenner ut et lerrethe stretches a canvas taut
  2. trekke sammen;
    stramme    to contract;
    to tighten
    • kroppsbyggeren spente musklenethe bodybuilder flexed his muscles
  3. feste med spenne (I)buckle · hair slide, hairclip · brooch, ornamental pin, reim eller lignendeto fasten with a bucklespenne (I), a strap or the like
    • sjåføren spenner fast setebeltetthe driver fastens the seat belt
    • hun spente hesten for sledenshe harnessed the horse to the sledge
    • spenne et belte om livetto buckle a belt around one's waist
  4. bøye i spenn (I, 1)tensionto bend into tensionspenn (I, 1)
    • spenne hanen på et geværto cock the hammer of a rifle
  5. omfavneto embrace
    • spenne om noen med armeneto put one's arms around someone
  6. omfatteto comprise · to cover · to includeto coveromfatte
    • spenne vidtto cover a wide range
    • historien spenner over et stort tidsromthe story spans a long period
    • alderen kan spenne fra seks til 30 årages can range from six to 30
  7. anstrengeto strainto strainanstrenge
    • spenne sine evner til det yttersteto strain one's abilities to the utmost
  8. heve, intensivereto raise, to heighten
