spenne II homograph 2

verbverb

to buckle, strap, fasten · to tense, flex, tighten · to span, range, stretch over

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt spenna Old Norse spenna

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. spile eller strekke ut to stretch or spread out taut
    • han spenner ut et lerret he stretches a canvas taut
  2. trekke sammen;
    stramme     to contract;
    to tighten
    • kroppsbyggeren spente musklene the bodybuilder flexed his muscles
  3. feste med spenne (I)buckle · hair slide, hairclip · brooch, ornamental pin, reim eller lignende to fasten with a bucklespenne (I), a strap or the like
    • sjåføren spenner fast setebeltet the driver fastens the seat belt
    • hun spente hesten for sleden she harnessed the horse to the sledge
    • spenne et belte om livet to buckle a belt around one's waist
  4. bøye i spenn (I, 1)tension to bend into tensionspenn (I, 1)
    • spenne hanen på et gevær to cock the hammer of a rifle
  5. omfavne to embrace
    • spenne om noen med armene to put one's arms around someone
  6. omfatteto comprise · to cover · to include to coveromfatte
    • spenne vidt to cover a wide range
    • historien spenner over et stort tidsrom the story spans a long period
    • alderen kan spenne fra seks til 30 år ages can range from six to 30
  7. anstrengeto strain to strainanstrenge
    • spenne sine evner til det ytterste to strain one's abilities to the utmost
  8. heve, intensivere to raise, to heighten
    • de spente forventningene for høyt they pitched their expectations too high
