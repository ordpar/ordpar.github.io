bryte ned

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: bryteto break · to wrestle
  1. rive over ende to tear down
    • de hadde brutt ned gjerdet they had torn the fence down
  2. løse opp to dissolve, to decompose Eng. phraseto break down
    • bryte ned fett to break down fat
  3. svekke, ødelegge to weaken, to destroy Eng. phraseto wear down
    • bryte ned motstanden to wear down the resistance
