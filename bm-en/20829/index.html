greie I homograph 1

noun feminine or masculine

thing · gadget · business, affair

Inflection

Etymology

av greie (III)to manage · to be able to · to sort out betydning 4 fra svenskfrom greie (III)to manage · to be able to · to sort out sense 4, from Swedish

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. orden, skikkorder, proper shape
    • det er ingen greie på detthere is no rhyme or reason to it
    • det er ikke greie på noe som helst der i gårdennothing at all is in order in that household
  2. redskap, innretning, hjelperåda tool, a device, a contrivance
    • en greie til å fjerne rusta gadget for removing rust
  3. sak, forholda matter, a business
    • det er fine greierthat is a fine business
    • fæle greiera nasty business
    • det var ikke rare greieneit was nothing much to speak of
  4. noe en liker eller er flink til;
    spesialitet    something one likes or is good at;
    a speciality
    • gammeldans er ikke hennes greieold-time dancing is not her thing
