saga

noun masculine

saga · story, history

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt saga ‘utsagn, fortelling’Old Norse saga 'statement, tale'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. norrøn, særlig islandsk, prosafortelling med mer eller mindre historisk grunnlagan Old Norse, especially Icelandic, prose narrative with a more or less historical basis
    • sagaen om Olav Tryggvasonthe saga of Olav Tryggvason
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som fornaldarsagaan Old Norse legendary saga set before the ninth century, islendingesagaan Icelandic saga about Icelanders in the Viking Age, kongesagaa saga telling the history of a king
  2. beretning om noe som hører svunne tider til;
    historie;
    skjebne, livsløp    an account of something belonging to times long past;
    history;
    fate, course of a life
    • Norges sagathe history of Norway
    • hans saga ble korthis story was soon over
