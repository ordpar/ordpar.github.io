forakte

verb

to despise · to look down on, to hold in contempt

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk, opprinnelig ‘ikke akte’from Low German, originally ‘not to esteem’

Senses and Example Sentences

ha lave tanker om, se ned på;
ringeakteto disdain, misliketo disliketo have a low opinion of, look down on;
to disdainringeakte, to dislikemislike
  • forakte svakhetto despise weakness
  • forakte seg selvto despise oneself
  • forakte sitt eget opphavto despise one's own origins
