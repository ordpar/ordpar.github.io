selvstendig, sjølstendig

adjective

independent · self-employed

Inflection

Etymology

etter tysk selbständig, opprinnelig ‘som står, består for seg selv’after German selbständig, originally 'that stands, exists on its own'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som tenker og handler uten å rette seg etter andre;
    som selv bestemmer;
    fri, uavhengig, ubundet    who thinks and acts without deferring to others;
    who makes their own decisions;
    free, independent, unconstrained
    • en selvstendig statan independent state
    • jeg er blitt en mer selvstendig personI have become a more independent person
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • tenke selvstendigto think for oneself
  2. original (II, 2)originaloriginaloriginal (II, 2)
    • han stod fram som en selvstendig forfatterhe emerged as a writer with a voice of his own
    • de har knapt tenkt en selvstendig tankethey have hardly had a thought of their own
  3. som driver på egen hånd eller for egen regningwho operates on their own or on their own account
    • hun er selvstendig næringsdrivendeshe is self-employed
