angå

verbverb

to concern · to have to do with

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk from Low German

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

være av betydning, interesse for;
gjelde (I)to be valid, to apply · to be about, to concern · to count, to matter to be of importance or interest to;
to be about, to concerngjelde (I)
  • en sak som angår alle a matter that concerns everyone
  • det angår ikke saken that has nothing to do with the matter
  • hva meg angår, er dette slutt på affæren as far as I'm concerned, this is the end of the affair
  • hva angår det meg? what's that got to do with me?
  • de det angikk those whom it concerned
