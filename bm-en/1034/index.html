åpenbar

adjective

obvious, evident, plain · evidently, clearly (adverbially)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt opinberr; av lavtysk openbarOld Norse opinberr; from Low German openbar

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som er helt tydelig for alle;
    klar (I, 7)clear    which is perfectly plain to everyone;
    clearklar (I, 7)
    • en åpenbar løgna blatant lie
    • en åpenbar misforståelsean obvious misunderstanding
    • åpenbare manglermanifest shortcomings
  2. brukt som adverb: tydeligvisevidently, apparently · clearly, obviouslyused as an adverb: evidently, apparentlytydeligvis
    • han somlet og hadde åpenbart god tidhe was dawdling and evidently had plenty of time
    • han var åpenbart berusethe was clearly drunk
