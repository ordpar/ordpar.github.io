dommer

noun masculine

judge · referee · umpire

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt dómari; av dom (I)a judgement · a sentence · a verdictOld Norse dómari; from dom (I)a judgement · a sentence · a verdict

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. person som dømmer i rettssaker eller tvistera person who passes judgement in court cases or disputes
    • dommerne i lagmannsrettenthe judges in the court of appeal
  2. person som dømmer i konkurranser, leker eller lignendea person who judges in competitions, games and the like
    • dommeren blåste av kampenthe referee blew the whistle to end the match
  3. person som sier sin mening om noe;
    kritiker    a person who gives their opinion on something;
    a critic
    • gjøre seg til dommer over noento set oneself up as a judge over someone
  4. om bibelske forhold: israelittisk stammehøvdingof biblical matters: an Israelite tribal chieftain
    • Gideon var en dommerGideon was a judge
