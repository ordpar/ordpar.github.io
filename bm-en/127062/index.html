samfunnsansvar

noun neuter

social responsibility · public responsibility

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ansvar som person, institusjon, virksomhet eller lignende har for at noe kommer samfunnet til godethe responsibility a person, institution, business or the like has for making sure that something benefits society
    • private virksomheter som tar samfunnsansvarprivate businesses that take social responsibility
  2. noe som det offentlige har ansvar forsomething the public sector is responsible for
