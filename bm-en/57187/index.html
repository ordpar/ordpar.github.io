stille V homograph 5

verb

to place, to set, to position · to put forward, to pose (a question) · to provide, to field · to adjust, to tune

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt stilla, påvirket av tyskOld Norse stilla, influenced by German

Senses and Example Sentences

    anbringe (1)to put    to lay, place or set something in a certain place or in a certain way;
    to putanbringe (1)
    • de stiller opp stigen langs veggenthey stand the ladder against the wall
    • de stilte ut varene sinethey displayed their goods
  2. gå inn på eller ta en viss plass;
    sette i en viss situasjon    to take up or occupy a certain position;
    to put into a certain situation
    • hun hadde stilt seg i veien for demshe had stood in their way
    • han stilte dem overfor et valghe faced them with a choice

    • ledelsen stilte representantene fritt ved avstemningenthe management gave the representatives a free hand in the vote
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • hun er godt stiltshe is well off
