øse IV homograph 4, ause II homograph 2

verbverb

to ladle, to scoop · to bail out (a boat) · to pour down (of rain)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av dansk øse; norrønt ausa from Danish øse; Old Norse ausa
