sikkerhet

noun feminine or masculine

safety · security · certainty

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. trygghet mot fare, angrep eller lignende;
    det å være sikker (1)safe    security against danger, attack or the like;
    the state of being safesikker (1)
    • de ble bragt i sikkerhetthey were brought to safety
    • det gjelder landets sikkerhetit concerns national security
  2. trygghet i opptreden;
    fasthet, presisjon    assurance in one's manner;
    steadiness, precision
    • utføre arbeidet med stor sikkerhetto carry out the work with great assurance
  3. det å være sikker (4)sure;
    visshetcertainty, sure knowledge    the state of being suresikker (4);
    certaintyvisshet
    • vite noe med sikkerhetto know something for certain
  4. garanti (2)a guarantee, pant (II, 1)collaterala guaranteegaranti (2), collateralpant (II, 1)
    • stille sikkerhet for et lånto put up security for a loan
