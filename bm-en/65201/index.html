utelukke

verb

to rule out · to exclude

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stenge ute eller nekte deltakelse i noe;
    ekskludereto exclude    to shut out or deny someone participation in something;
    to excludeekskludere
    • mange kvinner er utelukket fra yrkeslivetmany women are shut out of working life
    • bli utelukket fra foreningento be barred from the association
  2. regne som umulig;
    eliminere som et alternativ    to regard as impossible;
    to eliminate as an option
    • dette utelukker enhver annen forklaringthis rules out any other explanation
    • politiet utelukket ikke at noe kriminelt hadde funnet stedthe police did not rule out that something criminal had taken place
