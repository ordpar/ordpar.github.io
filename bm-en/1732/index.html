ane II homograph 2

verbverb

to have an idea, to have a clue · to sense, to make out dimly · to suspect

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk from Low German

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

ha en (svak) følelse av, ha en tanke om, ha kjennskap til to have a (faint) sense of, to have a notion of, to have some knowledge of
  • du aner ikke hvor slitsomt det er! you have no idea how exhausting it is!
  • han aner ingenting he suspects nothing
  • jeg aner ikke hvor de er I have no idea where they are
  • et eventyr vi bare aner konturene av an adventure whose outlines we can only just make out
ha mistanke om to suspect
  • det ante meg at du ville komme I had a feeling you would come
