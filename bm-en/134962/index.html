tone ut

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: toneto ring out, to sound · to tint, to tone
  1. dø hen;
    opphøre     to die away;
    to come to an end     Eng. phraseto fade out
    • sangen var ikke tonet ut da applausen braket løs the song had not finished when the applause broke out
  2. la klinge ut over to let sound out across something
    • musikken tonet ut over oss the music rang out over us
