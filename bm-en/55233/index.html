snurpe

verb
Inflection

Etymology

beslektet med snerpe (II)to purse (the lips)related to snerpe (II)to purse (the lips)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. dra eller trekke sammen med tråd, snor eller lignende;
    snøre    to draw or pull together with thread, cord or the like;
    to lace up
    • snurpe sammen et hull i bukseneto stitch up a hole in one's trousers
    • snurpe sammen en sekkto draw a sack shut
  2. trekke sammen (i rynker)to draw together (in folds)
    • snurpe munnento purse one's lips
  3. fange med snurpenota purse seineto catch with a purse seinesnurpenot
    • snurpe brislingto catch sprat with a purse seine
