“bad” is an inflected form of:

  • be, bede verbverb (past)(preteritum) to ask, to request · to pray · to invite henvende seg til noen for å oppnå noe
  • bade verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to bathe · to go swimming · to have a bath ta seg et bad
