uhyre I homograph 1

noun neuter

a monster · a beast

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk; jamfør uhyre (II)enormousfrom Low German; compare uhyre (II)enormous

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. uhyggelig fabelvesen;
    stort og skremmende dyr;
    monster    eerie mythical creature;
    large and frightening animal;
    monster
    • et uhyre som spyr ilda monster that spews fire
  2. fælt, ondt menneskehorrible, wicked person
    • moren hennes var et uhyreher mother was a monster
  3. enormt stor tingenormously large thing
    • et uhyre av et skipa monster of a ship
  4. skremmende fenomenfrightening phenomenon
    • oppleve byråkratiet som et mangehodet uhyreto experience the bureaucracy as a many-headed monster
