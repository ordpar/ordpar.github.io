for V homograph 5

preposition

for · too, excessively · in front of, before

Etymology

norrønt fyr, fyrir; jamfør fore (I)afoot, in hand (in {ha noe fore})Old Norse fyr, fyrir; compare fore (I)afoot, in hand (in {ha noe fore})
