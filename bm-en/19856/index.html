gjennomsnitt

noun neuter

average · mean

Inflection

Etymology

etter tysk Durchschnitt; jamfør snitt (II)an average · a cut, an incision · a section (in a drawing) · a cut, a styleafter German Durchschnitt; compare snitt (II)an average · a cut, an incision · a section (in a drawing) · a cut, a style
