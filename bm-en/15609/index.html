flyte

verbverb

to float · to flow

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt fljóta Old Norse fljóta

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. bæres av en væske, ikke synke to be borne up by a liquid, not to sink
    • tre flyter wood floats
    • liket fløt opp the body floated to the surface
  2. strømme (I, 1)to pour, renne (II, 1)to run to pourstrømme (I, 1), to runrenne (II, 1)
    • flyte over to overflow
    • flyte med blod to run with blood
  3. gli lett;
    ha flyt     to glide easily;
    to move along smoothly
    • trafikken flyter the traffic is flowing freely
  4. bre seg utover, ligge strødd to spread out, to lie scattered about
    • flyte utover to spread out all over the place
    • rommet fløt med aviser the room was littered with newspapers
  5. være ustabil, forandre seg to be unstable, to keep changing
    • alt flyter everything is in flux

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

flyte på berge seg ved hjelp av to get by with the help of Eng. phraseto coast on
  • han flyter på sjarmen he gets by on charm
flyte sammen gli over i hverandre;
gå i ett to merge into one another;
to become one Eng. phraseto blur together
  • dagene flyter sammen the days all blur into one
