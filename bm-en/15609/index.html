flyte

verb

to float · to flow

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt fljótaOld Norse fljóta

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bæres av en væske, ikke synketo be borne up by a liquid, not to sink
    • tre flyterwood floats
    • liket fløt oppthe body floated to the surface
  2. strømme (I, 1)to pour, renne (II, 1)to runto pourstrømme (I, 1), to runrenne (II, 1)
    • flyte overto overflow
    • flyte med blodto run with blood
  3. gli lett;
    ha flyt    to glide easily;
    to move along smoothly
    • trafikken flyterthe traffic is flowing freely
  4. bre seg utover, ligge strøddto spread out, to lie scattered about
    • flyte utoverto spread out all over the place
    • rommet fløt med aviserthe room was littered with newspapers
  5. være ustabil, forandre segto be unstable, to keep changing
    • alt flytereverything is in flux

Set phrases

flyte på berge seg ved hjelp avto get by with the help of Eng. phraseto coast on
  • han flyter på sjarmenhe gets by on charm
flyte sammen gli over i hverandre;
gå i ettto merge into one another;
to become one Eng. phraseto blur together
  • dagene flyter sammenthe days all blur into one
