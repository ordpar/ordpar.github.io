overta

verb

to take over

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. få eller ta til eie (eller ta i bruk) noe som en annen har eid (eller brukt);
    ta over drift av    to acquire or take possession of (or take into use) something another person has owned (or used);
    to take over the running of
    • han overtok gården etter farenhe took over the farm from his father
  2. ta på seg ansvar, oppgave eller lignende som en annen har hatt før;
    gå inn i stilling, verv eller lignende etter en annen    to take on a responsibility, task or the like that someone else has had before;
    to step into a post, office or the like after someone else
    • overta ansvaret for noeto take over responsibility for something
    • han skal overta som statsministerhe is to take over as prime minister
  3. komme i stedet for;
    fylle plassen til    to come in place of;
    to fill the place of
    • perioden da cd-en overtok for vinylplatenethe period when the CD took over from vinyl records
