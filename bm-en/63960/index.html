uhell

noun neuter

mishap · accident · bad luck

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør hell (I)luck, good fortune · a stroke of luck, a lucky coincidencecompare hell (I)luck, good fortune · a stroke of luck, a lucky coincidence

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. hendelse som volder skade eller bry;
    mindre ulykke    an event that causes damage or trouble;
    a minor accident
    • ha et lite uhell med bilento have a little mishap with the car
    • et hendelig uhella pure accident
    • når uhellet er utewhen misfortune strikes
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som arbeidsuhella workplace accident, trafikkuhella traffic accident
  2. uflaksbad luck, uturmisfortunebad luckuflaks, misfortuneutur
    • hell i spill, uhell i kjærlighetlucky at cards, unlucky in love

Set phrases

hell i uhell heldig hendelse eller omstendighet som demper virkningen av en ugunstig hendelsea fortunate event or circumstance that softens the effect of an unfortunate one Eng. phrasea blessing in disguise
  • det var hell i uhell at de var hjemme da det begynte å brenneit was a blessing in disguise that they were at home when the fire broke out
