omskrive

verb

to rewrite, to rephrase · to circumscribe

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. skrive om;
    formulere annerledes    to rewrite;
    to phrase differently
    • lange, tunge setninger bør omskriveslong, cumbersome sentences should be rewritten
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • en omskrevet versjona rewritten version
  2. i matematikk: tegne en linje rundtin mathematics: to circumscribe, to draw a line around
    • omskrive en sirkel rundt en trekantto circumscribe a circle around a triangle
