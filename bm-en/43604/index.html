overgå

verb

to surpass, to exceed · to befall

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. nå høyere enn eller gå forbi;
    overstigeto exceed, to surpass    to reach higher than or to go past;
    to exceedoverstige
    • han overgikk alle i dyktighethe surpassed everyone in skill
    • resultatet overgikk all forventningthe result exceeded all expectations
  2. ramme (III, 1)to strike, hende (III, 2)to befallto strikeramme (III, 1), to befallhende (III, 2)
    • en stor ulykke har overgått ossa great misfortune has befallen us
