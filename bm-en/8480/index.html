noun neuter

Etymology

norrønt bǿli; beslektet med bol (II)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. oppholdssted for mennesker;

  4. bande (I, 2)a gang, a crowd;
    flokk (1)group of animals or people;
    throng, crowd    , følge (II, 2)a person or persons who accompany something, someone or one another; a party, an entourage
