urørlig

adjective

motionless, immobile · immovable, tied up · inviolable, untouchable

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som ikke beveger segwhich does not move
    • en urørlig skikkelsea motionless figure
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • stå urørlig bak et treto stand motionless behind a tree
    • ligge urørlig i sengato lie motionless in bed
  2. som ikke kan røres;
    fast (1)fixed, bunden (1)tied up    which cannot be touched;
    fixedfast (1), tied upbunden (1)
    • urørlig eiendomimmovable property
    • urørlig kapitalcapital that cannot be drawn on
  3. om religiøse forhold: så hellig at en ikke må røre ved detin religious contexts: so sacred that it must not be touched
