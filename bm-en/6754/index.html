bod

noun masculine

bu II homograph 2

noun masculine or feminine

a storeroom, a storage room · a stall, a booth

Inflection

Etymology

av dansk bod, norrønt búð; jamfør bu (I)hut, shack · the cells, military lock-upfrom Danish bod, Old Norse búð; compare bu (I)hut, shack · the cells, military lock-up

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. mindre rom eller lite hus til å oppbevare mat, utstyr og lignende ia small room or small building for storing food, equipment and the like
    • sette sykkelen i boden+to put the bicycle in the storeroom
    • leiligheten har egen bod i kjelleren+the flat has its own storage room in the basement
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som kjellerboda storeroom in a cellar, loftboda storeroom in an attic, vedboda woodshed
  2. lite hus der noe selgesa small building where something is sold, a stall
    • sette opp en bod på julemarkedet+to set up a stall at the Christmas market
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som pølseboda hot dog stand
  3. lagerhusa warehouse
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som tollboda custom house
