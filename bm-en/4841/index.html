bedre I homograph 1

adjective

better · more (space, time, luck)

Inflection

Etymology

fra dansk, norrønt betri, komparativ av bragood · well · pretty, fairly og godgood · well · plenty of; jamfør best (II)best · most (of an amount)from Danish, Old Norse betri, the comparative of bragood · well · pretty, fairly and godgood · well · plenty of; compare best (II)best · most (of an amount)
