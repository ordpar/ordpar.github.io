glemme II homograph 2, glømme II homograph 2

verb

to forget · to leave behind

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt gleyma, av glaumr ‘overstadig glede’, opprinnelig ‘være overstadig lystig (og derfor forsømme)'Old Norse gleyma, from glaumr 'boisterous joy', originally 'to be boisterously merry (and therefore neglectful)'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ikke huske;
    tape av minne    not to remember;
    to let slip from memory
    • jeg har glemt hva han heterI've forgotten what his name is
    • glemme tid og stedto lose all sense of time and place
    • glemme morsmålet sittto forget one's mother tongue
    • jeg glemmer det aldriI'll never forget it
    • har du glemt meg?have you forgotten me?
    • det skal jeg aldri glemme deg forI will never forget you for that
  2. legge igjen ved et uhellto leave behind by accident
    • glemme boka hjemmeto leave the book at home
    • jeg glemte lommeboka på butikkenI left my wallet at the shop
  3. ikke gjøre;
    forsømme, unnlate    not to do;
    to neglect, to fail to
    • glemme pliktene sineto neglect one's duties
    • glemme å slokke lysetto forget to turn off the light
  4. ikke bry seg om eller ta hensyn til;
    ikke tenke på    not to care about or take into account;
    not to think about
    • glemme gammel urettto forget old wrongs
    • glem det!forget it!
    • det kan du bare glemmeyou can just forget about that
    • du glemmer hvem du snakker medyou are forgetting who you are talking to
