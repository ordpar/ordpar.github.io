overfor

preposition

towards · to · facing · faced with

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ansikt til ansikt;
    vendt mot    face to face;
    facing
    • de stod overfor hverandrethey stood facing each other
  2. om tale eller utsagn: vendt til noenof speech or statements: addressed to someone
    • kommentere overfor pressento comment to the press
  3. i overført betydning: rett framfor noe (for eksempel en oppgave eller utfordring)figuratively: right in front of something (for example a task or a challenge)
    • bli stilt overfor et problemto be faced with a problem
  4. i forholdet til;
    mot    in relation to;
    towards
    • være ansvarlig overfor sin samvittighetto be answerable to one's conscience
    • hennes følelser overfor foreldreneher feelings towards her parents
    • være overfølsom overfor noeto be oversensitive to something
