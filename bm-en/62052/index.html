trekk II homograph 2

noun neuter

feature, trait · move · cover

Inflection

Etymology

av trekketo pull · to draw · to withdrawfrom trekketo pull · to draw · to withdraw

    compare trekkfuglmigratory bird

    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som fugletrekkbird migration, grågåstrekka migrating flock of greylag geese, gåsetrekkgoose migration

    framtrekkan advance mechanism    something that pulls or hauls something forward;
    an advance mechanismframtrekk

    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som skitrekkski lift
