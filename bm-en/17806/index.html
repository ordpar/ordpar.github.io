fremme II homograph 2

verb

to promote · to put forward · to further

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt fremja; av framforward · forth, out (into view) · all the way, to the destinationOld Norse fremja; from framforward · forth, out (into view) · all the way, to the destination

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. hjelpe fram, stimulere;
    øke, påskynde    to promote, to encourage;
    to boost, to accelerate
    • fremme en sakto promote a cause
    • tiltak som fremmer kommunens økonomimeasures that improve the municipality's finances
    • fremme salget av små leiligheterto boost the sale of small flats
    brukt som adjektivused as an adjective
    • virke fremmende på folks engasjementto have a stimulating effect on people's engagement
  2. legge fram til behandling;
    ta opp, reise (II, 3)to raise    to put forward for consideration;
    to bring up, to raisereise (II, 3)
    • fremme et forslagto put forward a proposal
    • de fremmet saken for rettenthey brought the case before the court
  3. sette i verk, gjennomføreto put into effect, to carry out
    • gi hjemmel for å fremme skjønnetto give legal authority to proceed with the appraisal
