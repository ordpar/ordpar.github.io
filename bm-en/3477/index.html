avgi

verbverb

to give off, to emit · to give, to deliver (a statement, a vote)

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gi (fra seg), produsere to give (off), to produce
    • avdelingen måtte avgi personell the unit had to give up personnel
    • ovnen avgir varme the stove gives off heat
    • avgi stemme to cast a vote
    brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
    • avgitte stemmer votes cast
  2. komme med;
    framføre     to give;
    to deliver
    • avgi en forklaring to give a statement
