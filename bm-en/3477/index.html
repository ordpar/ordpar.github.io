avgi

verb

to give off, to emit · to give, to deliver (a statement, a vote)

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi (fra seg), produsereto give (off), to produce
    • avdelingen måtte avgi personellthe unit had to give up personnel
    • ovnen avgir varmethe stove gives off heat
    • avgi stemmeto cast a vote
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • avgitte stemmervotes cast
  2. komme med;
    framføre    to give;
    to deliver
    • avgi en forklaringto give a statement
