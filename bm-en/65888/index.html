uttale II homograph 2

verb

to pronounce · to utter, to state

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør tale (II)to speak · to argue, to plead (for or against)compare tale (II)to speak · to argue, to plead (for or against)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. si, ytreto say, to utter
    • hun uttalte først en takk til deltakerneshe first uttered a word of thanks to the participants
  2. lage språklyder;
    artikulereto articulate, to enunciate    to make speech sounds;
    to articulateartikulere
    • gutten kan ikke uttale rthe boy cannot pronounce his r
  3. brukt som adjektiv: utpreget, åpenbarused as an adjective: pronounced, obvious
    • en uttalt tendens til rasismea marked tendency towards racism
    • uttalte symptomerpronounced symptoms

Set phrases

uttale seg si hva en menerto say what one thinks Eng. phraseto give one's opinion
  • han uttaler seg om alt mulighe comments on all sorts of things
