taust vitne

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: vitnewitness
  1. person som har sett noe som han eller hun ikke forteller om a person who has seen something that he or she does not speak about Eng. phrasea silent witness

  2. noe som er på et sted der noe hender something that is present in a place where something happens Eng. phrasea silent witness
    • bjørka var et taust vitne til skiftende tider the birch tree was a silent witness to changing times
