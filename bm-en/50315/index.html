se

verb

to see · to look · to watch

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt sjáOld Norse sjá

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. oppfatte med øyet;
    oppdage, få øye på    to perceive with the eye;
    to spot, catch sight of
    • hun ser ikke uten brillershe can't see without glasses
    • han så båtene i havnenhe saw the boats in the harbour
    • han ser seg selv i speilethe sees himself in the mirror
    • har du sett henne?have you seen her?
    • plutselig så jeg det lå noe i veiensuddenly I saw that something was lying in the road
    • se! Et stjerneskudd!look! A shooting star!
  2. rette blikket i en bestemt retning;
    betrakteto regard · to view, to consider · to study, to observe;
    stirre, kikke    to direct one's gaze in a certain direction;
    to observebetrakte;
    to stare, peer
    • se ut gjennom vinduetto look out through the window
    • de så på tvthey watched TV
    • hun ser i avisashe's looking through the newspaper
    • han så en filmhe watched a film
    • de så på utstillingenthey looked at the exhibition
  3. forstå (1)to understand, skjønneto grasp;
    innseto realise · to come to see    to understandforstå (1), to graspskjønne;
    to realiseinnse
    • han er rik, ser duhe's rich, you see
    • der ser du hvordan det kan gåthere you see how things can turn out
    • jeg kan ikke se at dette er noe problemI can't see that this is any problem
    • så langt jeg kan se, er det ingen problemeras far as I can see, there are no problems
  4. få lære eller oppleve, erfare;
    finne (IV, 1)to find, oppdage;
    komme fram til    to come to learn or experience;
    to findfinne (IV, 1), to discover;
    to arrive at
    • vi får se, kanskje det går brawe'll see, maybe it'll turn out fine
    • en så god avgangsklasse er noe vi sjelden sersuch a good graduating class is something we rarely see
    • jeg skjønner ikke hva hun ser i hamI don't understand what she sees in him
    • vi så hjembyen vår på en ny måtewe saw our home town in a new way
  5. legge positivt merke til;
    vise forståelse for, anerkjenne (2)to acknowledge    to take positive notice of;
    to show understanding for, to acknowledgeanerkjenne (2)
    • den nye treneren var flink til å se spillerne sinethe new coach was good at seeing his players
    • hun forlot mannen sin fordi hun ikke følte seg settshe left her husband because she didn't feel seen
    • det er viktig for en leder å se de ansatteit's important for a leader to see the employees
  6. ville (II, 1)to want, ønske (II)to wish · to want;
    foretrekketo prefer    to wantville (II, 1), to wishønske (II);
    to preferforetrekke
    • jeg ser helst at du gårI'd rather you left
    • retten så helst at partene fant en minnelig løsningthe court preferred the parties to reach an amicable settlement
  7. være sammen med;
    treffe (1)to meet, møte    to be together with;
    to meettreffe (1), to see
    • han så mora hver helghe saw his mother every weekend
    • når skal du se henne igjen?when will you see her again?
    • vi har ikke sett noe til ham i det sistewe haven't seen anything of him lately
  8. betrakte eller bedømme fra en bestemt synsvinkelto view or judge from a particular perspective
    • stort sett er folk veldig hjepsommeby and large, people are very helpful
    • teoretisk sett var det en god plantheoretically, it was a good plan
    • hun ser seg selv som en heltshe sees herself as a hero
    • du ser alltid ting på din egen måteyou always see things your own way
    • isolert sett har endringene små konsekvenserseen in isolation, the changes have minor consequences
    • faglig sett har elevene blitt bedrein academic terms, the pupils have improved
  9. tenke etter, fundereto think, ponder
    • la meg se, hvor la jeg brillene?let me see, where did I put my glasses?
