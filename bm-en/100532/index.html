snakkes

verb

to talk to each other, to speak later · to be talked about, to be a topic of conversation

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. være tema for samtaleto be a topic of conversation
    • det snakkes om løst og fastpeople talk about all sorts of things
  2. snakke med hverandreto talk to one another
    • vi snakkes i neste ukewe'll talk next week
