bitte

adverb

ever so, very (intensifier before liten, små, litt)

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

brukt forsterkende, særlig foran liten og småused as an intensifier, especially before liten and små 'little'

Set phrases

bitte liten svært litenvery small Eng. phrasetiny
  • et bitte lite husa tiny house
  • den bitte lille jentathe tiny little girl
  • hun har skrevet tekster siden hun var bitte litenshe has been writing lyrics since she was tiny
bitte litt svært litevery little Eng. phrasea tiny bit
  • gjøre bitte litt vondtto hurt just a little
  • bruke bitte litt mer tidto spend a tiny bit more time
bitte små svært småvery small (plural) Eng. phrasetiny
  • barna var bitte småthe children were tiny
