knapt

adverb

hardly, barely, scarcely · scantily, sparingly

Etymology

av knapp (II)scarce · narrow · shortfrom knapp (II)scarce · narrow · short

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bare så vidt;
    nesten ikke;
    neppe, snaut    only just;
    almost not;
    scarcely, barely
    • vi torde knapt pustewe hardly dared breathe
    • vi visste knapt hvor vi varwe barely knew where we were
  2. sparsomt, småttscantily, sparingly
    • ha knapt med tidto be short of time
