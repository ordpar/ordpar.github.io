den II homograph 2

determiner demonstrative

that · the one

Etymology

norrønt þann, þenn, akkusativ av sá ‘den’Old Norse þann, þenn, accusative of sá 'that'

Senses and Example Sentences

3. person entall femininum og maskulinum, trykksterkt;
jamfør det (II)that, dennethis, de (II)those · the3rd person singular feminine and masculine, stressed;
