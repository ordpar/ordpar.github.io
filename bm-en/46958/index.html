pusse I homograph 1

verb

to clean · to polish

Inflection

Etymology

av tysk putzenfrom German putzen

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre ren (og blank);
    rense    to make clean (and shiny);
    to cleanse
    • pusse vinduerto clean windows
    • hun pusser tenneneshe is brushing her teeth
    • vi har pusset sølvtøyetwe have polished the silverware
    • han måtte pusse nesenhe had to blow his nose
  2. stryke puss (I, 3)plaster på mur eller betongto apply plasterpuss (I, 3) to masonry or concrete
