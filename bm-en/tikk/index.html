“tikk” is an inflected form of:

  • tikke II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to tap, to nudge · to tag (in the chasing game) gi et lett slag eller støt
  • tikke III homograph 3 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to tick lage en tikkende lyd
