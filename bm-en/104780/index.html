se etter

to look for, to search for · to look after, to keep an eye on

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. passe på noen eller noeto look after someone or something Eng. phraseto keep an eye on
    • han ser etter onkelbarna sine i helgenhe's looking after his nieces and nephews this weekend
    • kan du se etter tingene mine et øyeblikk?can you watch my things for a moment?
  2. lete etter noen eller noeto search for someone or something Eng. phraseto look for
    • hun ser etter brillene sineshe's looking for her glasses
