hyggelig

adjective

nice · pleasant, lovely · friendly, likeable

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som skaper hygge;
    trivelig    that creates a cosy atmosphere;
    pleasant, nice
    • et hyggelig hjema nice home
    • takk for en hyggelig kveldthank you for a lovely evening
    • en hyggelig overraskelsea pleasant surprise
    • kan vi ikke finne et hyggeligere samtaleemne?can't we find a nicer topic of conversation?
  2. tiltalende, sympatisk, vennliglikeable, agreeable, friendly
    • et av de hyggeligste menneskene jeg kjennerone of the nicest people I know
    • de var hyggelige alle sammenthey were all nice people
