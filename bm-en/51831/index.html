sjalusi II homograph 2

noun masculine

Venetian blind, louvred blind

Inflection

Etymology

samme opprinnelse som sjalusi (I)jealousy kalt slik fordi en innenfra kunne se det som skjedde utenfor, uten å bli sett selvsame origin as sjalusi (I)jealousy, so called because from the inside one could see what was going on outside without being seen oneself

Senses and Example Sentences

skjerm eller gardin av brede, tynne, regulerbare ribbera screen or blind made of broad, thin, adjustable slats
