snever

adjective

narrow · narrow-minded, insular

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt snæfrOld Norse snæfr

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. liten, trangsmall, narrow
    • arbeide innenfor snevre rammerto work within narrow limits
  2. som omfatter få;
    engere (II)narrower    comprising only a few;
    narrowerengere (II)
    • en snever kretsa narrow circle of people
  3. trangt avgrensetnarrowly delimited
    • en snever definisjona narrow definition
    • i snevreste forstandin the narrowest sense
  4. smålig, trangsyntpetty, narrow-minded
    • et snevert miljøan insular community
