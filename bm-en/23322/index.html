herje

verb

to ravage · to run riot · to knock about

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt herja, av hær, opprinnelig ‘fare fram som en hær’Old Norse herja, from hær 'army', originally 'to advance like an army'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. forårsake ødeleggelse eller skade;
    rasere    to cause destruction or damage;
    to devastate
    • fienden herjet landetthe enemy ravaged the country
    • en ungdomsgjeng herjet og ødela i sentrum i natta gang of youths ran riot and vandalised the town centre last night
    • stormen har herjet langs kystenthe storm has raged along the coast
    • gården ble herjet av brannthe farm was ravaged by fire
    • influensa og forkjølelse herjerflu and colds are rife
  2. bråke (II, 1)to make a racket, romstereto bang about;
    behandle uvørent;
    dominere    to make a racketbråke (II, 1), to bang aboutromstere;
    to treat carelessly;
    to dominate
    • ungene herjet oppe på loftetthe kids were rampaging around up in the attic
    • du må ikke herje slik med lekene dineyou mustn't knock your toys about like that
    • de herjer med konkurrentenethey walk all over their competitors
