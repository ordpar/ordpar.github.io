bedra

verb

to deceive, to cheat, to swindle · to cheat on, to be unfaithful to

Inflection

Etymology

gjennom dansk bedrage; fra lavtysk bedregenthrough Danish bedrage; from Low German bedregen

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. svindleto swindle;
    snyte (I, 2)to cheat    to swindlesvindle;
    to cheatsnyte (I, 2)
    • han bedrog meg for flere tusen kronerhe defrauded me of several thousand kroner
  2. være utro motto be unfaithful to
    • bedra ektefellen sinto cheat on one’s spouse
  3. gi falskt inntrykkto give a false impression
    • sansene mine bedrar megmy senses are deceiving me

Set phrases

skinnet bedrar det ytre kan gi galt inntrykkoutward appearances can give the wrong impression Eng. phraseappearances can be deceptive
verden vil bedras menneskene er godtroende og lette å narrepeople are gullible and easy to fool Eng. phrasethe world wants to be deceived
