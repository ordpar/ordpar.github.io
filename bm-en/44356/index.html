passe IV homograph 4

verb

to look after, to mind · to keep an eye on

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk passen up ‘gi akt på’; av fransk passer ‘la (tiden) gå’from Low German passen up 'to pay attention to'; from French passer 'to let (the time) pass'

Senses and Example Sentences

ha tilsyn med;
vokte på;
stelleto look after;
to keep watch over;
to tend, to care for
  • passe barnto mind children
  • naboen passer huset i ferienthe neighbour looks after the house during the holidays
  • de glemte å passe tidenthey forgot to keep an eye on the time
  • pass dine egne saker!mind your own business!
  • jeg passet hunden sist torsdagI looked after the dog last Thursday
