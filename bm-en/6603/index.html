blunde

verb

to doze, to nap, to snooze

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt blunda, av blundr; jamfør blunda napOld Norse blunda, from blundr; compare blunda nap

Senses and Example Sentences

sove lett en liten stundto doze for a little while
  • prøv om du kan blunde littsee if you can get a little sleep
  • jeg har visst blundet noen minutterI must have dozed off for a few minutes
