operere

verb

to operate on (surgically) · to operate, to run (a machine) · to work, to be active

Inflection

Etymology

av latin operarifrom Latin operari

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. utføre et kirurgisk inngrep på en pasientto perform a surgical procedure on a patient
    • operere noen for kreftto operate on someone for cancer
    • bli operert for nyresteinto have an operation for kidney stones
  2. arbeide, virketo work, to be active
    • operere med store tallto work with large numbers
    • operere på børsento trade on the stock exchange
  3. betjene eller styre en maskin, et anlegg eller lignendeto run or control a machine, an installation or the like
    • operere en maskinto operate a machine
