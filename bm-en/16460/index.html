forlate

verb

to leave · to abandon

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk ‘la fare, fjerne seg fra’; av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excessfrom Low German ‘to let go, to move away from’; from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. dra bort fra;
    slutte med (å følge)    to go away from;
    to stop (following)
    • forlate stedetto leave the place
    • han forlot kone og barnhe abandoned his wife and children
    • til slutt måtte kapteinen forlate skipetin the end the captain had to abandon ship
    • etter skandalen forlot hun politikken for godtafter the scandal she left politics for good
  2. i eldre bibelspråk: tilgito forgive · to excusein older biblical language: to forgivetilgi
    • forlat oss vår skyld!forgive us our trespasses!
