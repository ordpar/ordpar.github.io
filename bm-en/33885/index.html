lappete, lappet

adjective
Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. full av lapper (I, 1)piece of cloth, leather or the like;
    patch    ;
    bøtt (1)to put a patch on something that is torn;
    to sew up a rip;
    to put back in order;
    to mend
    • en lappete skjorte
  2. i botanikk, om blad: med lapper (I, 6)an incision in a leaf that goes no more than halfway in towards the midrib
  3. brukt som etterledd i sammensetninger: med slike eller så mange lapper (I, 6)an incision in a leaf that goes no more than halfway in towards the midrib som førsteleddet nevner
    i ord som fjærlappete
