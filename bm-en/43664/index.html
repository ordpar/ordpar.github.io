overlegen

adjectiveadjektiv

superior, far better · clear, convincing (of a win) · arrogant, condescending

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk, opprinnelig ‘som ligger oppå motstanderen i en brytekamp’ from Low German, originally 'lying on top of one's opponent in a wrestling match'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. bedre, dyktigere eller lignende;
    i særklasse;
    suveren (II, 2)outstanding     better, more skilled or the like;
    in a class of one's own;
    outstandingsuveren (II, 2)
    • hun var dem helt overlegen i alt she was streets ahead of them in everything
    • en overlegen kunstner a supremely gifted artist
  2. klar (I, 7)clear clearklar (I, 7)
    • en overlegen vinner a clear winner
    brukt som adverb: used as an adverb:
    • seire overlegent to win convincingly
  3. som har store tanker om seg selv;
    storsnutet, hoven     having a high opinion of oneself;
    cocky, haughty
    • en overlegen fyr an arrogant chap
