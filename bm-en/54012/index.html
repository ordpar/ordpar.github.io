skylle II homograph 2

verbverb

to rinse · to wash, to sweep (of water)

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. flomme, fosse to flood, to gush
    • sjøen skyller over rekka the sea washes over the gunwale
  2. føre eller rive med seg to carry or sweep along
    • bølgene skylte en mann over bord the waves washed a man overboard
    • vrakgods var skylt i land wreckage had washed ashore
  3. i overført betydning: spre seg;
    gjøre seg stadig med gjeldende;
    flomme (2)to stream     figuratively: to spread;
    to make itself increasingly felt;
    to streamflomme (2)
    • sinnet skylte gjennom henne anger surged through her
    • eldrebølgen skyller over landet the wave of ageing is sweeping across the country
  4. rense i eller med vann to clean in or with water
    • skylle tøy to rinse laundry
    • hun skyller saltet av fisken she rinses the salt off the fish
    • han skylte hendene grundig he rinsed his hands thoroughly

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

skylle i seg sette til livs i store mengder to consume in large quantities Eng. phraseto gulp down
  • han skylte i seg kaffe he guzzled coffee
skylle ned
  1. svelge to swallow Eng. phraseto wash down
    • skylle ned tabletter med vann to wash tablets down with water
  2. hølje ned to pour down
    • regnet skylte ned the rain came pouring down
  3. spyle toalettet etter bruk to rinse the toilet after use Eng. phraseto flush the toilet
    • skylle ned i do to flush it down the loo
