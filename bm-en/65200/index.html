utelukket, utelukka

adjective

out of the question, ruled out · excluded, barred

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som er utestengt eller nektet å delta i noewho is shut out or barred from taking part in something
    • de utelukkede spillerne måtte reise hjemthe excluded players had to go home
  2. som ikke lar seg gjøre;
    umulig (1)impossible, utenkeliginconceivable · unthinkable    which cannot be done;
    impossibleumulig (1), inconceivableutenkelig
    • det er ikke utelukket at hun klarer eksamenit's not out of the question that she'll pass the exam
