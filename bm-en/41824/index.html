offer

noun neuter

a victim · a sacrifice · an offering

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt offr; av ofreto sacrifice · to devote · to spendOld Norse offr; from ofreto sacrifice · to devote · to spend

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gave til en guddoma gift to a deity
    • bære fram et offer for Herrento present an offering to the Lord
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som brennofferburnt offering, sonofferexpiatory sacrifice, takkofferthank offering
  2. frivillig pengegave fra kirkefolket til et visst formål;
    jamfør kollekt (1)a collection    a voluntary monetary gift from the congregation for a particular purpose;
    compare kollekt (1)a collection
    • gi et offer til menighetsarbeidetto give an offering towards the parish work
  3. uegennyttig handlinga selfless act
    • fedrelandet krever dette offeret av ossour country demands this sacrifice of us
    • det var ikke noe stort offer å slutte å røykegiving up smoking was no great sacrifice
  4. person som må bøte eller lide, ofte for andres handlinger;
    skadelidendean injured party    a person who has to pay or suffer, often for the actions of others;
    an injured partyskadelidende
    • bli offer for mordto fall victim to murder
    • krigens ofrethe victims of the war
    • han bli et offer for intrigerhe fell victim to intrigue
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som dødsoffera fatality, gjeldsoffera debt victim, mobbeoffervictim of bullying
