offer

noun neutersubstantiv intetkjønn

a victim · a sacrifice · an offering

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt offr; av ofreto sacrifice · to devote · to spend Old Norse offr; from ofreto sacrifice · to devote · to spend

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gave til en guddom a gift to a deity
    • bære fram et offer for Herren to present an offering to the Lord
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som brennofferburnt offering, sonofferexpiatory sacrifice, takkofferthank offering
  2. frivillig pengegave fra kirkefolket til et visst formål;
    jamfør kollekt (1)a collection     a voluntary monetary gift from the congregation for a particular purpose;
    compare kollekt (1)a collection
    • gi et offer til menighetsarbeidet to give an offering towards the parish work
  3. uegennyttig handling a selfless act
    • fedrelandet krever dette offeret av oss our country demands this sacrifice of us
    • det var ikke noe stort offer å slutte å røyke giving up smoking was no great sacrifice
  4. person som må bøte eller lide, ofte for andres handlinger;
    skadelidendean injured party     a person who has to pay or suffer, often for the actions of others;
    an injured partyskadelidende
    • bli offer for mord to fall victim to murder
    • krigens ofre the victims of the war
    • han bli et offer for intriger he fell victim to intrigue
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som dødsoffera fatality, gjeldsoffera debt victim, mobbeoffervictim of bullying
