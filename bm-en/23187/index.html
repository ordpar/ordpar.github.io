henge I homograph 1

verb

to hang · to hang around

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt hanga; samme opprinnelse som henge (II)to hang up · to hangOld Norse hanga; the same origin as henge (II)to hang up · to hang

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. være festet øverst og holdt oppe i en viss avstand fra gulv eller bakke (og med mulighet til å svinge eller dingle);
    sitte fast høyt oppe    to be attached at the top and held up some distance above the floor or the ground (with room to swing or dangle);
    to be fixed fast high up
    • henge og dingle i et tauto hang dangling from a rope
    • det henger mye frukt på trærnethere is a lot of fruit hanging on the trees
    • det hang malerier på alle veggenethere were paintings hanging on every wall
    • det hang klessnorer mellom husenewashing lines were strung between the houses
    • håret hang ned i øynenehis hair hung down into his eyes
    • klesvasken hang på snorathe washing was hanging on the line
    • ha et smykke hengende om halsento wear a pendant round one's neck
    • det hang noen stygge skavler oppe i bratthengetsome nasty snow cornices hung up on the steep slope
  2. holde seg svevende på samme stedto stay hovering in one place
    • en drone hang i luftaa drone hung in the air
    • røyken hang over husenethe smoke hung over the houses
    • månen henger like over åskantenthe moon hangs just above the ridge
  3. sige eller bøye seg løst eller slapt nedto sag or droop down loosely or limply
    • henge med halento let one's tail droop
    • magen hang ut over beltethis belly hung out over his belt
    brukt som adjektivused as an adjective
    • stå med hengende armerto stand with one's arms dangling
    • en rose med hengende hodea rose with a drooping head
  4. om person: være bøyd (over noe);
    være sammensunket    used about a person: to be bent (over something);
    to be slumped
    • hun hang ut av vinduetshe was leaning out of the window
    • henge over bøkeneto pore over one's books
    • sitte og henge på stolento sit slouched in one's chair
  5. oppholde seg et sted uten å ha et bestemt mål eller ærend;
    drive (III, 8)to wander about    to spend time somewhere without any particular aim or errand;
    to wander aboutdrive (III, 8)
    • ungdommer som henger på kjøpesenteret hele dagenyoung people who hang around the shopping centre all day
  6. holde seg fast og la seg slepe med;
    klamre seg til    to hold on and let oneself be dragged along;
    to cling to
    • syklisten hang etter bilenthe cyclist was hanging on behind the car
    • han hang i armen på farenhe was hanging on his father's arm
    • henge rundt halsen på noento hang round someone's neck
  7. være fast eller festetto stay stuck fast or attached
    • henge fast i noeto be stuck fast to something
    • øynene hans hang ved henne hele tidenhis eyes stayed fixed on her the whole time
    • røyklukten hang i klærnethe smell of smoke clung to the clothes
    • det henger da igjen litt av barnelærdommena little of what one learned as a child does stick after all
