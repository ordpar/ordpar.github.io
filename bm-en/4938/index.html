begge

determiner quantifier

both

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt beggja, genitiv av báðirOld Norse beggja, genitive of báðir

Senses and Example Sentences

brukt om to (personer, ting, begreper) for å uttrykke at de er omtalt sammen, eller at de naturlig hører sammenused about two (people, things, concepts) to express that they are spoken of together, or that they naturally belong together
  • begge foreldreneboth parents
  • ha vondt i begge armeneto have pain in both arms
  • på begge sider av gataon both sides of the street
  • de gikk begge tothey both went
  • hun var med begge gangeneshe came along both times
  • det var i begges interesseit was in the interests of both
  • begge deler er rettboth are right
