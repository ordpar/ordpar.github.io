lete, leite III homograph 3

verb

to look for · to search

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt leita, av líta ‘se, skue’Old Norse leita, from líta ‘to see, to behold’

Senses and Example Sentences

søke, prøve å finne;
se etter noeto search, to try to find;
to look for something
  • lete etter nøkleneto look for the keys
  • lete etter ordeneto grope for words
  • vi har lett overaltwe have looked everywhere
  • maken til jente skal en lete lenge etteryou'd have to look far and wide to find a girl like her
  • lete opp et godt bærstedto seek out a good berry-picking spot
  • politiet leter etter en savnet personthe police are searching for a missing person
  • de lette lenge før de fant rett stedthey searched for a long time before they found the right place
  • lete fram gamle fotografierto dig out old photographs
  • lete seg fram til riktig husnummerto feel one's way to the right house number
