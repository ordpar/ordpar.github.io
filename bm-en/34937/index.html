verbverb

to look for · to search

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt leita, av líta ‘se, skue’ Old Norse leita, from líta ‘to see, to behold’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

søke, prøve å finne;
se etter noe to search, to try to find;
to look for something
  • lete etter nøklene to look for the keys
  • lete etter ordene to grope for words
  • vi har lett overalt we have looked everywhere
  • maken til jente skal en lete lenge etter you'd have to look far and wide to find a girl like her
  • lete opp et godt bærsted to seek out a good berry-picking spot
  • politiet leter etter en savnet person the police are searching for a missing person
  • de lette lenge før de fant rett sted they searched for a long time before they found the right place
  • lete fram gamle fotografier to dig out old photographs
  • lete seg fram til riktig husnummer to feel one's way to the right house number
