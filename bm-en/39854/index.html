mønstre

verbverb

to scrutinise, to look over, to inspect · to field, to muster · to sign on, to take on as crew

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk; jamfør mønstera pattern · a model from Low German; compare mønstera pattern · a model

    granske kritisk;
