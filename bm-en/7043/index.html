borger

noun masculine

citizen · burgher, townsman (historical) · bourgeois, member of the propertied middle class

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk ‘bymann’; av borg (I)a castlefrom Low German 'townsman'; from borg (I)a castle

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. person med borgerrett i et land;
    statsborgera citizen    a person who holds the rights of citizenship in a country;
    a citizenstatsborger
    • staten skal beskytte sine borgere+the state must protect its citizens
    • alle borgere har stemmerett+all citizens have the right to vote
  2. om eldre forhold: innbygger i en byin earlier times: inhabitant of a town
    • byens borgere samlet seg på torget+the town's burghers gathered in the square
  3. i nyere tid: person som hører til en bestemt sosial klasse ut fra ytre kjennetegn som yrke, posisjon eller idémessig forankringin more recent times: person belonging to a particular social class judged by outward marks such as occupation, standing or ideological allegiance

Set phrases
