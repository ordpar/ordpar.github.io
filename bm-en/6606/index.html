blunke

verb
Inflection

Etymology

beslektet med blinke (II)to flash, to twinkle · to blinkrelated to blinke (II)to flash, to twinkle · to blink

Senses and Example Sentences

    • blunke lurt til noento give someone a knowing wink
  2. blinke (II, 2)to blinkto blinkblinke (II, 2)
