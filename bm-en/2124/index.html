anstrengeB2, level B2

verbverb

to strain, to tax

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra tysk; etterleddet samme opprinnelse som norrønt strengja ‘drive hardt’ from German; the final element has the same origin as Old Norse strengja 'to drive hard'

bruke krefter;
anspenneto strain (one’s attention or senses) · to exert oneself to use one's strength;
to strainanspenne
