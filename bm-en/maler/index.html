maler

Bokmål–English dictionary 5 5 articles

Article page

“maler” is an inflected form of:

  • male I homograph 1 verbverb (present)(presens) to grind, to mill · to purr · to harp on knuse til mel (med kvern), pulverisere
  • male II homograph 2 verbverb (present)(presens) to paint dekke med maling
  • mal I homograph 1 nounsubstantiv (plural indefinite)(flertall ubestemt) template · pattern, stencil arbeidsmodell som gir omrisset til en ting som skal lages
  • mal II homograph 2 nounsubstantiv (plural indefinite)(flertall ubestemt) shingle bank, a bank of pebbles at the water's edge mol
