innta

verb

to take up · to consume · to capture · to adopt

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ta innto take in
    • de nye bestemmelsene ble inntatt i reglementetthe new provisions were incorporated into the rules
  2. ta til seg mat, drikke eller lignendeto take in food, drink or the like
    • innta en bedre middagto have a fine dinner
  3. erobreto conquerto conquererobre
    • fienden inntok byenthe enemy captured the town
    • glade korpsmusikanter inntok bygdacheerful brass-band players descended on the village
  4. plassere seg påto take up a position in
    • laget inntok sisteplassen på tabellenthe team occupied last place in the table
    • publikum må innta sine plasser før klokka 19the audience must take their seats before 7 pm
  5. legge for dagento display;
    vise    to displaylegge for dagen;
    to show
    • innta en avvisende holdningto adopt a dismissive attitude
