tulle seg inn

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: tulleto joke, to be kidding · to mess about · to wrap, to wind
  1. sette seg fast to become stuck Eng. phraseto get caught up in
    • tauet tullet seg inn i propellen the rope got tangled in the propeller
  2. pakke seg inn to wrap oneself up
    • tulle seg inn i et teppe to wrap oneself up in a blanket
