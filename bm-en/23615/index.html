hindre

verb

to prevent, to stop · to obstruct, to hold up

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt hindraOld Norse hindra

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. være i veien for;
    stenge    to be in the way of;
    to block
    • gjerdet hindret dyra i å dra til skogsthe fence stopped the animals from heading into the forest
    • høye boligblokker hindrer utsiktentall blocks of flats obstruct the view
  2. hefte, sinketo hold up, to delay
    • uvær og snøfall hindret arbeidetbad weather and snowfall held up the work
    • jeg kunne ikke hindre henne fra å gåI could not stop her from leaving
  3. avvergeto avert, to prevent, forhindreto preventto avertavverge, to preventforhindre
    • vi må hindre en ny krigwe must stop another war from breaking out
    • han greide å hindre at skuta gikk på grunnhe managed to keep the ship from running aground
