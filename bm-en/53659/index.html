skrote

verb

to scrap

Inflection

Etymology

fra svenskfrom Swedish

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. lage om til skrot;
    hogge opp    to turn into scrap;
    to break up

  2. slutte å la gjeldeto scrap, to stop letting something apply
    • skrote satsingento scrap the initiative
