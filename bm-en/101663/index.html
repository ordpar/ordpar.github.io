gi etter

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. om underlag: svikte (1)to give wayof a supporting surface: to give waysvikte (1)
    • isen gav etter under hamthe ice gave way under him
    • bjelkene gav etterthe beams gave way
  2. om person: føye seg, vikeof a person: to comply, to yield Eng. phraseto give in
    • gi etter for fristelsento give in to temptation
    • til slutt gav foreldrene etterin the end the parents gave in
