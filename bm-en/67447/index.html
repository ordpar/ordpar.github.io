velge

verb

to choose · to elect · to pick

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt velja; beslektet med ville (II)to want · will (future) · would (hypothetical)Old Norse velja; related to ville (II)to want · will (future) · would (hypothetical)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. av egen vilje bestemme seg for (noe);
    gå inn forto commit to, slå inn påto embark on    to decide on (something) of one's own free will;
    to commit togå inn for, to embark onslå inn på
    • du står fritt til å kunne velgeyou are free to choose
    • velge å bli fagarbeiderto choose to become a skilled worker
    • jeg valgte å ikke si noeI chose to say nothing
    • velge ordene sine med omhuto choose one's words with care
    • jeg valgte det billigste alternativetI chose the cheapest option
    • du kan få velge hva vi skal ha til middagyou get to choose what we'll have for dinner
    • la barna velge selvlet the children choose for themselves
    • han valgte å tolke henne i beste meninghe chose to interpret her in the most favourable light
    • jeg velger å tro at de kommer snartI choose to believe they'll come soon
    • noe slikt ville hen aldri valgtthey would never have chosen anything like that
    • tross at de er ganske ulike, valgte de hverandrealthough they are quite different, they chose each other
  2. peke ut gjennom avstemning eller sjefsavgjørelse;
    utpeketo designate, utnevne (1)to appoint    to designate through a vote or a manager's decision;
    to designateutpeke, to appointutnevne (1)

    • bli valgt inn på Stortingetto be elected to the Storting
    • være demokratisk valgtto be democratically elected
    • velge noen til en rolleto choose someone for a role
    • sjefen valgte meg til å gjøre jobbenthe boss chose me to do the job
  3. gjøre bruk av;
    benytte (1)to use, anvendeto use · to apply    to make use of;
    to usebenytte (1), to employanvende
    • det gjelder å velge rett tidspunktthe key is to pick the right moment
    • velge feil dørto take the wrong door
    • i dag valgte jeg trappatoday I took the stairs
    • eksempelet er ikke tilfeldig valgtthe example was not chosen at random

Set phrases

  • den lille butikken hadde ikke så mye å velge ithe little shop didn't have much to choose from
  • folk liker å ha forskjellige ting å velge ipeople like to have different things to choose from
velge bort la være å velge noe;
velge noe annet ennto refrain from choosing something;
to choose something other than Eng. phraseto decide against
