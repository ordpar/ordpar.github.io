verdsette

verb

to value · to appreciate

Inflection

Etymology

av verd (III)value, worthfrom verd (III)value, worth

    taksereto appraise    to determine the value of;
    to appraisetaksere

  2. sette pris på;
    akte (3)to esteem    to appreciate;
