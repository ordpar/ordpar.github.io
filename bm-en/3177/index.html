au III homograph 3

interjection

ouch · oh dear

Etymology

jamfør norrønt auficompare Old Norse aufi

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i utrop, som uttrykk for fysisk smertein exclamations, as an expression of physical pain
    • au, du tråkker på meg!ouch, you’re stepping on me!
  2. brukt i uttrykk for uventet skuffelse, lei overraskelseused to express unexpected disappointment or an unwelcome surprise
    • au da, var det så mange feil?oh dear, were there really that many mistakes?
