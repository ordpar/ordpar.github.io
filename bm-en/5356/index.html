berge

verb

to rescue, to save · to salvage, to get in (a harvest) · to hang on to (a win, a lead)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt bjarga ‘frelse, redde’, opprinnelig ‘skjule’Old Norse bjarga ‘to save, to rescue’, originally ‘to hide’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. føre eller hjelpe til et trygt sted fra et sted der det er fare for skade eller ødeleggelse;
    reddeto save, to rescue, frelse (II)to save    to bring or help to a safe place from somewhere there is a risk of injury or destruction;
    to rescueredde, to savefrelse (II)
    • mannskapet på den forliste båten ble bergetthe crew of the wrecked boat were rescued
    • de berget livet ved å hoppe ut gjennom vinduetthey saved their lives by jumping out through the window
  2. bringe i hus, sikkerhetto bring in, to bring to safety
    • berge avlingento get the harvest in
  3. i idrett, i overført betydning: unngå tap, nedrykkin sport, figuratively: to avoid defeat or relegation
    • berge seierento hang on to the win

Set phrases

berge seg
  1. (greie å) forsørge seg(to manage to) support oneself Eng. phraseto get by
    • kystbefolkningen kunne berge seg med fiskthe coastal population could support themselves on fish
  2. (greie å) redde seg(to manage to) save oneself
    • de berget seg opp av sjøenthey managed to get themselves out of the sea
berge seil minke på seilføringen (i sterk vind)to reduce the amount of sail carried (in strong wind) Eng. phraseto take in sail
