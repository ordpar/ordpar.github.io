respekt

noun masculine

respect

Inflection

Etymology

fra latin ‘det å ta hensyn’, av respicere ‘se tilbake’from Latin 'the act of taking into consideration', from respicere 'to look back'

Senses and Example Sentences

aktelse, ærbødighet, lydighetesteem, deference, obedience
  • ha stor respekt for ento have great respect for someone
  • sette seg i respektto command respect
