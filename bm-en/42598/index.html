oppover

preposition

up, up along · upwards

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. mot den øvre delen av noe;
    mot noe lenger opp;
    oppetterup along    towards the upper part of something;
    towards somewhere further up;
    up alongoppetter
    • komme oppover bakkento come up the hill
    • gå oppover kirkegolvetto walk up the aisle of the church
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • de fortsatte oppoverthey carried on upwards
    • på vei oppover til toppenon the way up to the summit
  2. innover fra kysten eller i retning nordinland from the coast, or in a northerly direction
    • de dro oppover landetthey travelled up the country
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • dra oppover til Nord-Norgeto travel up to Northern Norway
    • fuglene trekker oppoverthe birds migrate northwards
