skranke

noun masculine
Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk ‘gitter, gjerde’from Low German 'grating, fence'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. smal, nokså høy benk eller vegg med åpning i bank, kontor eller rettssal;
    disk (I, 1)a counter    a narrow, fairly high bench or partition with an opening, in a bank, an office or a courtroom;
    a counterdisk (I, 1)
    • ta ut penger i skrankento withdraw money at the counter
    • skranken i en rettssalthe bar in a courtroom
  2. turnapparat med to vannrette, parallelle stenger som hviler på loddrette stolpergymnastics apparatus with two horizontal, parallel bars resting on upright posts
  3. noe som hindrer;
    grense (I, 3)a limit    something that stands in the way;
    a limitgrense (I, 3)
    • sette en skranke for noeto set a limit to something
    • bryte alle skrankerto break down all barriers
