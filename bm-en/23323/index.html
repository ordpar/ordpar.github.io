herk

noun neuter
Inflection

Etymology

jamfør norrønt harki ‘skrap’, herki ‘stakkar’compare Old Norse harki ‘rubbish’, herki ‘wretch’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. dårlige saker, skrappoor stuff, junk
    • de gamle maskinene var bare herkthe old machines were nothing but rubbish
  2. noe plagsomtsomething annoying
    • biltrafikk er noe ordentlig herkcar traffic is a real nuisance
  3. ramphooligans, pøbelriffraffhooligansramp, riffraffpøbel
