føring

noun feminine or masculine

a guideline, a directive · the lead (in a contest) · the act of guiding or conveying

Inflection

Etymology

av føre (IV)to lead · to take, to bring · to keep (records), to stockfrom føre (IV)to lead · to take, to bring · to keep (records), to stock
