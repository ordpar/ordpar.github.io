letne

verb
Inflection

Etymology

av letteasy · light (in weight) · slight, slightlyfrom letteasy · light (in weight) · slight, slightly

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bli lettere av vektto become lighter in weight
    • grønnsakene letner under lagringthe vegetables lose weight in storage
  2. om været: klarne opp;
    bli opphold    of the weather: to clear up;
    to stop raining
    • været letnet på ettermiddagenthe weather brightened in the afternoon
    • det ser ut til at det letner nåit looks as though it is letting up now
  3. løse seg oppto disperse, to clear
    • tåka letnetthe fog lifted
  4. bli gladere;
    bli mindre nedstemt eller trykkende    to grow more cheerful;
    to become less gloomy or oppressive
    • stemningen letnetthe mood lightened
    • jeg letnet flere kilo da jeg hørte at alt var i orden med demI felt several kilos lighter when I heard that they were all right
