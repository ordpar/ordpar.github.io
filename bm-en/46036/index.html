presentere

verbverb

to present · to introduce

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

gjennom fransk; fra latin, av samme opprinnelse som presens through French; from Latin, of the same origin as presens 'present tense'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. vise, legge fram to show, to put forward
    • presentere et nytt produkt to present a new product
    • presentere en regning for noen to present someone with a bill
  2. framføre (II, 1)to perform, spille (III, 6)to stage to performframføre (II, 1), to stagespille (III, 6)
    • teateret skal presentere to Ibsen-stykker the theatre will be putting on two Ibsen plays
  3. gjøre en person kjent for en annen eller for en forsamling;
    forestille (1)to present someone, introdusere (2)to introduce     to make a person known to another person or to a gathering;
    to present someoneforestille (1), to introduceintrodusere (2)
    • hun presenterte mannen sin she introduced her husband
