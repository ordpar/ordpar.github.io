oppsett

noun neuter

setup, configuration · layout · a short piece, a brief article

Inflection

Etymology

av oppsetteto set up, to erect, to stage · to postpone, to put off og setteto put · to place · to setfrom oppsetteto set up, to erect, to stage · to postpone, to put off and setteto put · to place · to set

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. måte noe er laget til eller ordnet på;
    konfigurering, innstilling, systematisering    the way something is made or arranged;
    configuration, setting, organisation
    • mobilen har et veldig enkelt oppsettthe phone has a very simple layout
    • du kan endre oppsettet hvis du ikke liker detyou can change the setup if you don't like it
  2. sted for å sette båt på landa place for pulling a boat ashore
  3. kort skriftlig innlegg;
    kort artikkel    a short written contribution;
    a brief article
