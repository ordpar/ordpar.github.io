terapi

noun masculine

therapy

Inflection

Etymology

av gresk therapeia ‘pleie, kur’from Greek therapeia 'care, cure'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. behandling av fysiske eller psykiske plager og sykdommertreatment of physical or mental complaints and illnesses
    • gå i terapi hos en psykologto be in therapy with a psychologist
    • terapien var vellykketthe therapy was a success
  2. noe som har helsebringende effekt;
    lisea balm, lindringrelief    something that has a health-giving effect;
    a balmlise, relieflindring
    • musikken er terapi for megmusic is therapy for me
