gjelde I homograph 1

verb

to be valid, to apply · to be about, to concern · to count, to matter

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt gjalda ‘betale’Old Norse gjalda 'to pay'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. være gyldig;
    være tillatt;
    være gangbar    to be valid;
    to be allowed;
    to be current
    • billetten gjelder i to ukerthe ticket is valid for two weeks
    • reglene gjelder for alle beboernethe rules apply to all the residents
    • forbudet gjaldt fra mandag til onsdagthe ban was in force from Monday to Wednesday
  2. dreie seg om;
    angåto concern · to have to do with    to be about;
    to concernangå
    • saken gjelder forsikringssvindelthe case is about insurance fraud
    • forbudet har bare gjeldt noen fåthe ban has applied to only a few
    • hva gjelder det?what is it about?
  3. bli holdt for;
    gå for;
    ha en viss verdi    to be regarded as;
    to pass for;
    to have a certain value
    • kvitteringen gjelder som garantithe receipt counts as a guarantee
    • han gjaldt for to, så sterk var hanhe counted for two men, he was that strong
  4. være av eller ha (stor) betydning;
    være viktig    to be of or have (great) importance;
    to matter
    • det er nå det gjelderthis is the moment that counts
    • i år er det pastellfarger som gjelderthis year pastel colours are the thing
    • det gjelder å ikke gi oppthe important thing is not to give up

Set phrases

gjelds ikke teller ikke;
er ikke tillatt etter reglenedoes not count;
is not allowed under the rules
  • å slå under beltestedet gjelds ikkehitting below the belt is not allowed
