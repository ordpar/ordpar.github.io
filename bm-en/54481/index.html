slippe, sleppe II homograph 2

verb

to let go of, to drop · to be spared, not to have to · to get free, to get away

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt sleppaOld Norse sleppa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bli løs eller fri;
    få bevege seg fritt    to get loose or free;
    to be allowed to move freely
    • hun slapp ut av karantenenshe got out of quarantine
    • de slapp fra forfølgerenthey got away from their pursuer
    • jeg slipper ikke fra hjemme i dagI can't get away from home today
    • han prøvde å slippe unna politiethe tried to escape the police
    • gislene ble sluppet frithe hostages were set free
    • vi slipper ikke inn i byggetwe're not allowed into the building
    • varene har sluppet gjennom kontrollenthe goods have got through the inspection
    • vi slapp tidligere på skolen i dagwe got out of school earlier today
  2. bli fritatt eller spart for;
    bli kvitt;
    unngå (1)to avoid    to be excused or spared;
    to be rid of;
    to avoidunngå (1)
    • de slapp lett fra ulykkenthey got off lightly in the accident
    • vi slipper å rydde i dagwe don't have to tidy up today
    • jeg slapp heldigvis å betale full prisluckily I didn't have to pay full price
    • de håper å slippe millitærtjenestenthey hope to get out of military service
  3. glippe (I, 1)to slip out of one's hold, to come looseto slip out of one's hold, to come looseglippe (I, 1)
    • fatet slapp ut av hendene minethe dish slipped out of my hands
  4. miste eller løsne taket i noe;
    la noe eller noen bevege seg fritt    to lose or loosen one's grip on something;
    to let something or someone move freely
    • hun slapp koppen i gulvetshe dropped the cup on the floor
    • han slipper hunden løshe lets the dog loose
    • selja slipper barkenthe sallow is shedding its bark
    • slipp meg!let go of me!
    • sykdommen har sluppet taketthe illness has loosened its grip
    • de slapp ham ut av fengseletthey let him out of prison
    • hun slapp sinnet løsshe gave free rein to her anger
  5. la seg gli;
    falle    to let oneself slide;
    to fall
    • slippe seg ned fra en grento let oneself down from a branch
  6. slutte å interessere;
    slutte å holde fast ved    to cease to interest;
    to stop clinging to
    • ideen ville ikke slippe megthe idea wouldn't leave me alone
    • de ville ikke slippe samtaleemnetthey wouldn't drop the subject
  7. bryte av;
    stanse    to break off;
    to stop
    • vi begynner igjen der vi slappwe'll start again where we left off
