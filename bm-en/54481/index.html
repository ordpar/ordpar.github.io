slippe, sleppe II homograph 2

verbverb

to let go of, to drop · to be spared, not to have to · to get free, to get away

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt sleppa Old Norse sleppa

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. bli løs eller fri;
    få bevege seg fritt     to get loose or free;
    to be allowed to move freely
    • hun slapp ut av karantenen she got out of quarantine
    • de slapp fra forfølgeren they got away from their pursuer
    • jeg slipper ikke fra hjemme i dag I can't get away from home today
    • han prøvde å slippe unna politiet he tried to escape the police
    • gislene ble sluppet fri the hostages were set free
    • vi slipper ikke inn i bygget we're not allowed into the building
    • varene har sluppet gjennom kontrollen the goods have got through the inspection
    • vi slapp tidligere på skolen i dag we got out of school earlier today
  2. bli fritatt eller spart for;
    bli kvitt;
    unngå (1)to avoid     to be excused or spared;
    to be rid of;
    to avoidunngå (1)
    • de slapp lett fra ulykken they got off lightly in the accident
    • vi slipper å rydde i dag we don't have to tidy up today
    • jeg slapp heldigvis å betale full pris luckily I didn't have to pay full price
    • de håper å slippe militærtjenesten they hope to get out of military service
  3. glippe (I, 1)to slip out of one's hold, to come loose to slip out of one's hold, to come looseglippe (I, 1)
    • fatet slapp ut av hendene mine the dish slipped out of my hands
  4. miste eller løsne taket i noe;
    la noe eller noen bevege seg fritt     to lose or loosen one's grip on something;
    to let something or someone move freely
    • hun slapp koppen i gulvet she dropped the cup on the floor
    • han slipper hunden løs he lets the dog loose
    • selja slipper barken the sallow is shedding its bark
    • slipp meg! let go of me!
    • sykdommen har sluppet taket the illness has loosened its grip
    • de slapp ham ut av fengselet they let him out of prison
    • hun slapp sinnet løs she gave free rein to her anger
  5. la seg gli;
    falle     to let oneself slide;
    to fall
    • slippe seg ned fra en gren to let oneself down from a branch
  6. slutte å interessere;
    slutte å holde fast ved     to cease to interest;
    to stop clinging to
    • ideen ville ikke slippe meg the idea wouldn't leave me alone
    • de ville ikke slippe samtaleemnet they wouldn't drop the subject
  7. bryte av;
    stanse     to break off;
    to stop
    • vi begynner igjen der vi slapp we'll start again where we left off
