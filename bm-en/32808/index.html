kunstverk

noun neuter

work of art

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør verk (II)work (a created work, a deed) · works, plant · movement, mechanismcompare verk (II)work (a created work, a deed) · works, plant · movement, mechanism

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjenstand (1)an object eller verk (II, 2)a work laget av en kunstner, for eksempel et maleri, et litterært verk eller et musikkstykkean objectgjenstand (1) or a workverk (II, 2) made by an artist, for example a painting, a piece of writing or a piece of music
    • museet stilte ut sju kunstverk av den unge kunstnerenthe museum exhibited seven works of art by the young artist
  2. gjenstand som er svært vakkert utførtan object made with exquisite craftsmanship
    • de små kakene var kunstverkthe little cakes were works of art
