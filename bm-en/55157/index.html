snill

adjective

kind · nice, good-natured · well-behaved, good

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt snjallr ‘dyktig, klok, god’Old Norse snjallr 'capable, wise, good'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. lett å omgås med;
    lydigobedient    easy to get along with;
    obedientlydig
    • være snill og medgjørligto be kind and accommodating
    • vær nå snill gutt!now be a good boy!
    • en snill hesta gentle horse
    • være blant de snilleste i klassento be among the best-behaved in the class
  2. god, kjærliggood, loving
    • en snill og omsorgsfull fara good and caring father
    • du må være litt snillere med lillebroren dinyou must be a bit kinder to your little brother
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • det var snilt gjortthat was kindly done
  3. gavmildgenerous
    • få noe av en snill onkelto get something from a generous uncle
  4. vennlig, forekommendefriendly, obliging
    • snille og hjelpsomme naboerkind and helpful neighbours
    • vil du være så snill å gjøre meg en tjeneste?would you be so kind as to do me a favour?
  5. i utrop:in exclamations:
    • men snille deg, det kan da ikke du noe for!oh come now, it isn't your fault at all!
    • skjenk i kaffe, er du snill!pour the coffee, would you!

Set phrases

vær så snill brukt i spørsmål der en ber andre om noe;
til forskjell fra vær så godhere you are · please, help yourself · like it or notused in questions in which one asks someone for something;
as distinct from vær så godhere you are · please, help yourself · like it or not Eng. phraseplease
  • kan jeg få litt vann, vær så snill?could I have some water, please?
