rett II homograph 2

noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

a right (entitlement) · justice · the law · a court of law

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt réttr Old Norse réttr

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. rettighet, lovlig krav right, legal claim
    • kreve sin rett to demand one's rights
    • stå på retten sin to stand on one's rights
    • ha rett til pensjon to be entitled to a pension
    • alle har rett til arbeid everyone has the right to work
    • være i sin gode rett to be well within one's rights
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som bruksretta right of use, usufruct, menneskeretta human right, stemmerettthe right to vote · suffrage
  2. vederlagremuneration, payment · something given in return, fyllestto pull one's weight, to do one's fair share (in {gjøre fyllest for seg}) · to be done in full, to be fulfilled (in {skje fyllest}) recompensevederlag, full measure (only in fixed expressions)fyllest
    • gjøre rett for maten to earn one's keep
  3. rettferdighet justice
    • kjempe for rett og rettferdighet to fight for right and justice
    • med en viss rett with some justification
  4. det at noe viser seg å være i samsvar med virkeligheten the fact that something turns out to match reality
    • få rett i noe to be proved right about something
    • gi noen rett i noe to grant that someone is right about something
  5. lov, regel, rettslig vedtak law, rule, legal ruling
    • gjeldende rett the law as it stands
    • lov og rett law and justice
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som folkerettinternational law, privatrettprivate law, civil law, strafferettcriminal law
  6. domstola court (of law) · a tribunal court of lawdomstol
    • møte for retten to appear in court
    • retten avsa kjennelse the court delivered its ruling
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som arbeidsrettlabour law, employment law · the labour court · the right to work, forhørsrettan examining court, a committal court, høyesteretta supreme court
