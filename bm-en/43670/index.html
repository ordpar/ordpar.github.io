overleve

verb

to survive · to outlive

Inflection

Etymology

etter lavtyskafter Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. leve lenger ennto outlive, to live longer than
    • hun overlevde mannen sinshe outlived her husband
  2. leve igjennom, slippe fra (det) med livet;
    jamfør overlevendesurviving    to live through, to escape with one's life;
    compare overlevendesurviving
    • de overlevde ulykkenthey survived the accident
    • han vil nok overlevehe'll pull through, no doubt

Set phrases

overleve seg selv bli avleggs eller foreldetto become outmoded or obsolete Eng. phraseto outlive one's usefulness
