engasjement

noun neuter

commitment · involvement · fixed-term post

Inflection

Etymology

fra fransk; jamfør engasjereto engage · to grip, captivate · to hire, take on (for a fixed period)from French; compare engasjereto engage · to grip, captivate · to hire, take on (for a fixed period)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å være svært opptatt av noe og vise det i handling;
    sterk interesse    being deeply concerned about something and showing it in action;
    strong interest
    • vise engasjement for sakento show commitment to the cause
    • ønske å skape engasjement og bevisstgjøringto want to create involvement and awareness
    • hun har et brennende engasjement for fagetshe has a burning passion for the subject
  2. det å være involvert;
    deltakelse (I, 1)participation    the state of being involved;
    participationdeltakelse (I, 1)
    • et langvarig militært engasjement i regionena long-standing military involvement in the region
    • statens engasjement i oljevirksomhetenthe state's involvement in the oil industry
  3. ansettelse for en avgrenset periodeemployment for a limited period
    • faste stillinger og engasjementerpermanent posts and fixed-term appointments
    • få seg et engasjementto land a fixed-term post
