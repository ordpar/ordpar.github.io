engasjement

noun neutersubstantiv intetkjønn

commitment · involvement · fixed-term post

InflectionVis bøyning

PronunciationUttale

engasjemanˊg oreller angasjemanˊg

EtymologyOpphav

fra fransk; jamfør engasjereto engage · to grip, captivate · to hire, take on (for a fixed period) from French; compare engasjereto engage · to grip, captivate · to hire, take on (for a fixed period)

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. det å være svært opptatt av noe og vise det i handling;
    sterk interesse     being deeply concerned about something and showing it in action;
    strong interest
    • vise engasjement for saken to show commitment to the cause
    • ønske å skape engasjement og bevisstgjøring to want to create involvement and awareness
    • hun har et brennende engasjement for faget she has a burning passion for the subject
  2. det å være involvert;
    deltakelse (I, 1)participation     the state of being involved;
    participationdeltakelse (I, 1)
    • et langvarig militært engasjement i regionen a long-standing military involvement in the region
    • statens engasjement i oljevirksomheten the state's involvement in the oil industry
  3. ansettelse for en avgrenset periode employment for a limited period
    • faste stillinger og engasjementer permanent posts and fixed-term appointments
    • få seg et engasjement to land a fixed-term post
