forklare

verb

to explain

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk ‘gjøre klar’; jamfør for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excessfrom Low German ‘to make clear’; compare for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre tydelig og forståelig;
    greie ut, opplyse, kommentere    to make clear and understandable;
    to set out, to elucidate, to comment on
    • hun er flink til å forklareshe is good at explaining
    • han forklarte dem framgangsmåtenhe explained the procedure to them
    • kan du forklare henne det?can you explain it to her?
    • det forklarer sakenthat explains it
  2. gi en strålende og guddommelig glans;
    gjøre lysende eller klar;
    renseto clean · to cleanse · to purify    to give a radiant, divine lustre to;
    to make luminous or bright;
    to cleanserense
    brukt som adjektivused as an adjective
    • han steg opp, ren og forklarethe rose up, pure and transfigured

Set phrases

forklare seg gi forklaring på noe en har gjort;
gjøre greie for segto give an explanation for something one has done;
to account for oneself Eng. phraseto explain oneself
  • forklar deg!explain yourself!
  • forklare seg for politietto give a statement to the police
