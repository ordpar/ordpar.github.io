notere

verb

to write down · to note · to record

Inflection

Etymology

fra latin, av nota; jamfør note (I)note, footnote (in a text) · (diplomatic) note · banknotefrom Latin, from nota; compare note (I)note, footnote (in a text) · (diplomatic) note · banknote

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. opptegne, skrive nedto record, to write down
    • han noterte alle opplysningene i en notisbokhe wrote down all the information in a notebook
  2. merke (II, 1)to mark, registrere (1)to registerto markmerke (II, 1), to registerregistrere (1)
    • det ble notert tre nye verdensrekorderthree new world records were recorded
    • bli notert av politietto be booked by the police
  3. føre opp som gjeldspost i et regnskap;
    debitereto debit    to enter as a debt item in an account;
    to debitdebitere
    • skal varene noteres?shall the goods be put on the account?
  4. på en børs: fastsette prisen på verdipapirer, valuta eller vareron a stock exchange: to fix the price of securities, currency or commodities
    • dollar ble notert i 7,20the dollar was quoted at 7.20
