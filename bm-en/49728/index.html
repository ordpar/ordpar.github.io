saldo

noun masculine
Inflection

Etymology

fra italiensk, opprinnelig ‘fast, hel’; samme opprinnelse som solidsturdy, solid · sound, dependable · hearty, substantialfrom Italian, originally ‘firm, whole’; same origin as solidsturdy, solid · sound, dependable · hearty, substantial

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. forskjellen mellom debet- og kreditsiden i et regnskapthe difference between the debit and credit sides in a set of accounts
    • saldoen er kr 800the balance is 800 kroner
  2. beløp til gode på en konto, kupong, et gavekort eller lignendeamount standing to one's credit on an account, coupon, gift card or the like
    • sjekke saldoen i nettbankento check the balance in one's online banking
