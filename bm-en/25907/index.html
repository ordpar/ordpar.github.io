imot

prepositionpreposisjon

against · towards

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt ímót Old Norse ímót

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

mot (III)towards · against towards, againstmot (III)
  • oksen kom imot oss the bull came towards us
  • gå rett imot vinden to walk straight against the wind
  • ta imot gjestene to receive the guests
  • hun var snill imot alle she was kind to everyone
  • kjempe imot spredning av atomvåpen to fight against the spread of nuclear weapons
brukt som adverb used as an adverb
  • fjellet lå rett imot the mountain lay directly opposite
  • vinden stod rett imot the wind was blowing right against us

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

tale noen midt imot åpent og uredd si seg uenig med en mektig person eller gruppe to disagree openly and fearlessly with a powerful person or group Eng. phraseto stand up to someone
  • tale makten midt imot to speak truth to power
  • hun talte partiledelsen midt imot she openly contradicted the party leadership
