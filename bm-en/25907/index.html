imot

preposition

against · towards

Etymology

norrønt ímótOld Norse ímót

Senses and Example Sentences

mot (III)towards · againsttowards, againstmot (III)
  • oksen kom imot ossthe bull came towards us
  • gå rett imot vindento walk straight against the wind
  • ta imot gjesteneto receive the guests
  • hun var snill imot alleshe was kind to everyone
  • kjempe imot spredning av atomvåpento fight against the spread of nuclear weapons
brukt som adverbused as an adverb
  • fjellet lå rett imotthe mountain lay directly opposite
  • vinden stod rett imotthe wind was blowing right against us

Set phrases

tale noen midt imot åpent og uredd si seg uenig med en mektig person eller gruppeto disagree openly and fearlessly with a powerful person or group Eng. phraseto stand up to someone
  • tale makten midt imotto speak truth to power
  • hun talte partiledelsen midt imotshe openly contradicted the party leadership
