kapre

verb

to hijack · to snap up, to bag

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk kapern; se kapera privateerfrom Low German kapern; see kapera privateer

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. få tak i (med kupp eller knep)to get hold of (by a coup or a trick)
    • kapre et skipto hijack a ship
    • kapre den beste stolento bag the best chair
  2. om eldre forhold: erobre med kaperskipa privateer shiphistorically: to capture with a privateer shipkaperskip
