gidde

verbverb

to be bothered (to do something)

InflectionVis bøyning

PronunciationUttale

gidˋde oreller jidˋde

EtymologyOpphav

jamfør dansk gide og norrønt geta ‘få’ compare Danish gide and Old Norse geta 'to get'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

særlig brukt i nektende setninger: ville umake seg til;
orketo have the energy for · to be bothered, to face (doing something) · to stand, to bear especially used in negative sentences: to be willing to take the trouble to;
to have the energy fororke
  • jeg gidder ikke I can't be bothered
  • at du gidder! I don't know how you can be bothered!
