fri III homograph 3

verb

to free, to deliver (from) · to spare

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt fría; av fri (II)free · off, on leave (from work) · clear of, rid ofOld Norse fría; from fri (II)free · off, on leave (from work) · clear of, rid of

Senses and Example Sentences

befri eller frita for noe uønsket;
beskytte, berge, verneto free or exempt from something unwanted;
to protect, to save, to shield
  • fri oss fra faredeliver us from danger
  • fri oss fra det ondedeliver us from evil
  • være fridd for lidelseto be spared suffering
  • fri og bevare oss!God preserve us!
