samt I homograph 1

conjunctionkonjunksjon

as well as · and also

EtymologyOpphav

tysk preposisjon samt ‘sammen med’ German preposition samt ‘together with’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

og dessuten and in addition, as well as
  • kjøpe inn korn og kraftfôr samt sukker to buy in grain and concentrated feed as well as sugar
  • han skulle ha en seng samt frokost og kveldsmat he was to have a bed as well as breakfast and supper
