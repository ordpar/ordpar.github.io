forgifte

verb

to poison

Inflection

Etymology

av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excessfrom for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre giftigto make poisonous
    • forgifte matto poison food
    • pilene var forgiftetthe arrows were poisoned
    brukt som adjektivused as an adjective
    • det forgiftede kjøttetthe poisoned meat
  2. skade eller drepe med giftto harm or kill with poison
    • bli forgiftet av gassento be poisoned by the gas
  3. i overført betydning: ødeleggefiguratively: to ruin
    • forgifte stemningento poison the atmosphere
