ansette

Article page

“ansette” is an inflected form of:

  • anse II homograph 2 verbverb (perfect participle definite and plural)(perfektum partisipp bestemt og flertall) to consider, to regard regne, betrakte
  • ansett adjectiveadjektiv (singular definite, plural)(entall bestemt, flertall) respected, well regarded, reputable som nyter alminnelig aktelse
