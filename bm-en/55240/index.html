snurre

verb

to spin, twirl · to whir

Inflection

Etymology

lydordonomatopoeic

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi en susende eller durende lydto make a whirring or droning sound
    • rokkehjulet snurrer og malerthe spinning wheel whirs and hums
  2. dreie rundt;
    svinge seg raskt;
    svirre (3)to whirl, tvinne (1)to twist (threads) together    to turn round;
    to spin quickly;
    to whirlsvirre (3), to twist (threads) togethertvinne (1)
    • snurre rundt som en snurrebassto spin round like a top
    • snurre rundt på gulvetto spin around on the floor
    • snurre glasset mellom fingreneto twirl the glass between one's fingers
    • snurre lua mellom hendeneto turn one's cap round and round in one's hands
