allmue

noun masculine

the common people, the commoners, country folk

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt almúgi, almúgrOld Norse almúgi, almúgr

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. særlig før, i bestemt form: småkårsfolket, folket på landetespecially formerly, in the definite form: the poorer classes, country folk
    • embetsmennene utsugde allmuenthe officials exploited the common people
  2. særlig om eldre forhold i sammensetninger: folke-especially of former times, in compounds: folke- 'folk-'
    also found in compounds:i ord som allmueboksamlingmunicipal library for ordinary people, allmueskoleprimary school (called folkeskole from 1889)
