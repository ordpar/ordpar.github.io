kappe II homograph 2

verb

to cut through, to chop off, to saw to length

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk; jamfør kabbe (II)to chop offfrom Low German; compare kabbe (II)to chop off

Senses and Example Sentences

hogge, skjære eller sage tvers avto chop, cut or saw crosswise through
  • kappe vedto cut firewood to length
  • kappe opp treet med motorsagto cut the tree up with a chainsaw
  • få kappet av seg den ene fotento have one foot cut off
