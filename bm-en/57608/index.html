strekke I homograph 1

verb

to pull, to strain (a muscle) · to reprimand

Inflection

Etymology

trolig fra lavtysk; jamfør strekke (II)to stretch out · to straighten out · to reach outprobably from Low German; compare strekke (II)to stretch out · to straighten out · to reach out

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. skade ved å strekke for langt;
    forstrekke (I)to strain    to injure by stretching too far;
    to strainforstrekke (I)
    • jeg strakk en lårmuskel på treningaI pulled a thigh muscle at training
  2. særlig i militæret: irettesetteto reprimand, to rebuke, to tell offespecially in the military: to reprimand, to rebukeirettesette

Set phrases

  • kreftene strakk ikke tilhis strength wasn't up to it
  • føle at en ikke har strukket tilto feel that one hasn't measured up
strekke ut
  1. tøye ut, for eksempel etter treningto loosen up the muscles, for example after exercise Eng. phraseto stretch out
  2. om tid: vare lengeof time: to last a long time
    • valgkampen burde vært strukket lenger ut i tidthe election campaign should have been spread out over a longer period
