umulig

adjectiveadjektiv

impossible · unthinkable, out of the question · impossible, unbearable (of a person)

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. som ikke eller svært vanskelig lar seg gjøre eller utføre;
    ikke mulig (1)possible, ugjørlignot feasible, impracticable, impossible to do     which cannot be done or carried out, or only with great difficulty;
    not possiblemulig (1), not feasibleugjørlig
    • få en umulig oppgave to be given an impossible task
    • det var umulig å svømme mot strømmen it was impossible to swim against the current
    brukt som adverb: used as an adverb:
    • det kan umulig være sant it can't possibly be true
    • veien er umulig lang the road is impossibly long
  2. som ikke kan forekomme eller tenkes;
    utenkeliginconceivable · unthinkable, usannsynligunlikely · improbable · unbelievably     which cannot occur or be imagined;
    inconceivableutenkelig, improbableusannsynlig
    • en umulig tanke an unthinkable idea
    • det er ikke umulig at hun blir litt forsinket it's not out of the question that she'll be a little late
  3. særlig vanskelig eller vrien;
    vrang (5)contrary, awkward, umedgjørliguncooperative, unaccommodating, obstinate     particularly difficult or trying;
    contrary, awkwardvrang (5), uncooperative, unaccommodating, obstinateumedgjørlig
    • en umulig unge an impossible child
    • ikke vær så umulig! don't be so awkward!
