sukke

verb

to sigh · to long for

Inflection

Etymology

beslektet med sugeto suck · to draw in · to soak uprelated to sugeto suck · to draw in · to soak up

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. trekke pusten dypt inn og slippe den hørbart ut igjento draw one's breath in deeply and let it out audibly
    • sukke tungtto sigh heavily
    • sukke og stønneto sigh and groan
    • sukke lettetto sigh with relief
  2. lideto sufferto sufferlide
    • sukke under åketto groan under the yoke
  3. lengteto long · to yearn · to miss (someone)to long forlengte
    • sukke etter regnto long for rain
  4. blåse (II, 1)to blow, suse (1)to rush (of wind or water)to blowblåse (II, 1), to rush (of wind or water)suse (1)
    • vinden sukket i trekronenethe wind sighed in the treetops
  5. skvulpeto lap (of water), klukke (1)to gurgleto lap (of water)skvulpe, to gurgleklukke (1)
    • det sukket og skvulpet rundt båtenthe water sighed and lapped around the boat
