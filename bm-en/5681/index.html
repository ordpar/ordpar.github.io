bety

verb

to mean · to matter, to count

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stå for;
    symbolisereto symbolise, to stand for    to stand for;
    to symbolisesymbolisere
    • hva betyr dette skiltet?what does this sign mean?
    • firkløver betyr lykkea four-leaf clover means good luck
    • hva skal dette bety?what is this supposed to mean?
  2. være ensbetydende med;
    føre til, innebære    to be synonymous with;
    to lead to, to entail
    • dette betyr kroken på døra for hele prosjektetthis spells the end for the whole project
    • et feiltrinn betyr dødenone misstep means death
    • det behøver ikke å bety at arbeidet er dårligit doesn't necessarily mean that the work is poor
  3. ha verdi eller innflytelse;
    spille en rolle    to have value or influence;
    to matter
    • han har betydd mye for Norgehe has meant a great deal to Norway
    • alle som betyr noe i norsk kulturliv, var dereveryone who counts for anything in Norwegian cultural life was there
    • samarbeidet vil bety mye for ossthe collaboration will mean a lot to us
    • som om det skulle bety noeas if that mattered
    • det betyr ingentingit doesn't matter
