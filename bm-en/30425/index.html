knapp II homograph 2

adjectiveadjektiv

scarce · narrow · short

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt knappr; fra lavtysk Old Norse knappr; from Low German

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. trang, avgrenset cramped, restricted
    • det var knapt om plassen space was tight
  2. mangelfull eller for liten insufficient or too small
    • et knapt flertall a narrow majority
    • med knapp margin by a slim margin
    • en knapp seier a narrow victory
    • matjord er en knapp ressurs arable soil is a scarce resource
  3. om tid: snau (II, 4)just under of time: just undersnau (II, 4)
    • en knapp time barely an hour
    • tida er knapp time is short
  4. om uttrykksmåte: kort, fyndig of a manner of expression: terse, pithy
    • knapp stil a terse style
