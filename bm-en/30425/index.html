knapp II homograph 2

adjective

scarce · narrow · short

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt knappr; fra lavtyskOld Norse knappr; from Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. trang, avgrensetcramped, restricted
    • det var knapt om plassenspace was tight
  2. mangelfull eller for liteninsufficient or too small
    • et knapt flertalla narrow majority
    • med knapp marginby a slim margin
    • en knapp seiera narrow victory
    • matjord er en knapp ressursarable soil is a scarce resource
  3. om tid: snau (II, 4)just underof time: just undersnau (II, 4)
    • en knapp timebarely an hour
    • tida er knapptime is short
  4. om uttrykksmåte: kort, fyndigof a manner of expression: terse, pithy
    • knapp stila terse style
