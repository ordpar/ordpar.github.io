banne

verb

to swear, to curse (use bad language)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt banna, opprinnelig ‘lyse i bann, forbanne’; av bannexcommunicationOld Norse banna, originally ‘to place under a ban, to curse’; from bannexcommunication

Senses and Example Sentences

uttrykke sterke følelser gjennom tabuordtaboo words, ofte ord knyttet til religion, kjønn osv.;
sverge (2)to curse, bruke ed (2)a swear wordto express strong feelings through taboo wordstabuord, often words connected with religion, sex and so on;
to cursesverge (2), to use a swear worded (2)
  • han bannet lavthe swore under his breath
  • banne og svergeto swear and curse

Set phrases

banne i kirken motsi eller spotte en autoritet eller noe helligto contradict or mock an authority or something held sacred Eng. phraseto commit heresy
  • å motsi lederne var å banne i kirkento contradict the leaders was heresy
banne på være sikker påto be certain of Eng. phraseto swear to it
  • det kan du banne påyou can bet on that
