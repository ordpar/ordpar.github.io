kave II homograph 2

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt kafa ‘dykke’; beslektet med kav (I)the deep seaOld Norse kafa ‘to dive’; related to kav (I)the deep sea

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. slå eller fekte rundt seg med armer eller bein;
    bevege armer og bein veldig    to flail or thrash about with one's arms or legs;
    to move one's arms and legs vigorously
    • han lå og kavde i vannethe was floundering in the water
    • han kaver seg gjennom snøenhe flounders through the snow
  2. streveto struggle, toil · to strive (for something);
    kjaseto bustle about    to strugglestreve;
    to bustle aboutkjase
    • hun kaver mye med lekseneshe struggles a lot with her homework
