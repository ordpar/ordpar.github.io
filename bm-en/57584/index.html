streife

verb
Inflection

Etymology

fra tyskfrom German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. dra fra sted til sted uten noe bestemt mål;
    farteto travel about, to roam, flakke (1)to wander    to move from place to place with no particular destination;
    to travel aboutfarte, to wanderflakke (1)
    • de streifet omkring i skogenthey roamed around in the forest
  2. berøre lett og kortvarig;
    snerte (3)to graze, sneie (2)to brush against    to touch lightly and briefly;
    to grazesnerte (3), to brush againstsneie (2)
    • hånden streifet så vidt kinnetthe hand barely brushed the cheek
    • kulen hadde bare streifet skulderenthe bullet had only grazed the shoulder
  3. se som snarest påto glance at briefly
    • hun streifer ham med blikkether gaze brushes over him
  4. omtale flyktig;
    så vidt komme inn på    to mention in passing;
    to barely touch on
    • de streifet så vidt innom spørsmåletthey only just touched on the issue
  5. om tanke, idé eller lignende: falle innof a thought, an idea or the like: to occur to someone
    • tanken har ikke streifet meg engangthe thought hasn't even crossed my mind
