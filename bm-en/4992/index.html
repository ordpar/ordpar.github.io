behandle

verb

to treat · to deal with · to handle

Inflection

Etymology

fra tysk, opprinnelig ‘håndtere’from German, originally 'to handle'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. benytte en bestemt framgangsmåte for å helbrede noen eller redusere et helseproblemto use a particular method to cure someone or ease a health problem
    • behandle en pasientto treat a patient
    • diagnostisere og behandle kreftto diagnose and treat cancer
  2. sette seg inn i en sak og komme fram til en avgjørelseto look into a matter and arrive at a decision
    • behandle et emneto deal with a subject
    • behandle en søknadto process an application
    • Høyesterett har behandlet sakenthe Supreme Court has heard the case
  3. fare fram motto act towards, to treat
    • behandle andre dårligto treat others badly
    • behandle kundene høfligto treat the customers politely
    • tenk å behandle folk slik!imagine treating people like that!
  4. bearbeideto work on, to process · to adapt, to revise (a text or play) · to work on, to win over (a person, a market)to work onbearbeide
    • bordet er behandlet med matt lakkthe table has been treated with matt varnish
  5. betjeneto serve, to attend to · to operate, to run · to service (a loan or debt), bruketo use · to spend · to tend toto operatebetjene, to usebruke
    • kunne behandle en pcto know how to handle a PC
