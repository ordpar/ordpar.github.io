slynge II homograph 2

verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt slyngja, sløngva; jamfør slenge (I)to swing, to dangle · to lurch · to hang about, to drop by Old Norse slyngja, sløngva; compare slenge (I)to swing, to dangle · to lurch · to hang about, to drop by

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. kaste noe rundt noe;
    sno, flette     to throw something around something;
    to twist, to plait
    • slynge armene rundt noen to throw one's arms around someone
  2. kaste eller slenge med stor kraft to throw or fling with great force
    • sola slynger ut enorme gasskyer the sun hurls out enormous clouds of gas
    • han ble slynget ut av båten i fart he was flung out of the speeding boat
  3. sentrifugere (for eksempel salat eller vokskaker) for å skille ut væske to spin (for example salad or honeycombs) in order to separate out liquid
    • slynge salatbladene tørre to spin the salad leaves dry
    • slynge honning to extract honey
