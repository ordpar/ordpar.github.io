prioritere

verb

to prioritise

Inflection

Etymology

av prioritetpriority · precedencefrom prioritetpriority · precedence

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi fortrinn framfor andreto give precedence over others
    • denne saken bør prioriteresthis matter ought to be prioritised
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • en prioritert oppgavea priority task
  2. sette opp i rangordningto put in order of importance
    • prioritere ønskene sineto rank one's wishes in order of priority
    • saken blir høyt prioritertthe matter is given high priority

Set phrases

prioritere ned gi lavere plassering i en rangordningto place lower in a ranking order Eng. phraseto give lower priority to
prioritere opp gi høyere plassering i en rangordningto place higher in a ranking order Eng. phraseto give higher priority to
