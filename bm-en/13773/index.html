faen I homograph 1

noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

damn, hell (as a swearword) · the devil, Satan

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt fjándinn Old Norse fjándinn

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. djevelen (1)Satan;
    fanden (I)the devil, fan (II)the devil · damn (as an oath)     Satandjevelen (1);
    the devil – compare fanden (I)the devil and fan (II)the devil · damn (as an oath)
  2. brukt forsterkende i spørsmål used as an intensifier in questions
    • hva faen skal det bety? what the hell is that supposed to mean?
    • hvem faen er det? who the hell is that?
  3. brukt i uttrykk med visse verb i konjunktiv (1)the subjunctive used in expressions with certain verbs in the subjunctivekonjunktiv (1)
    • faen spare! well, I'll be damned!
    • faen ta deg! damn you!
    • det går faen ta meg ikke an it bloody well won't do
    • han dukker faen steike meg opp he bloody well turns up
    • nå må du faen skjære meg ta deg sammen now you'd damn well better pull yourself together
  4. brukt i uttrykk som betegner en person used in expressions denoting a person
    • en fattig faen a poor sod
    • en sleip faen a slippery bastard
