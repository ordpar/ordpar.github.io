sette ut

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. slippe ut;
    spre    to let out;
    to spread
    • sette ut et rykteto start a rumour
  2. plassere ut i naturen for å øke bestandento place in the wild to increase the population Eng. phraseto release into the wild
    • sette ut yngelto release fry into the water
  3. plassere fangstredskapto set out nets, traps or other catching gear
    • sette ut garnto set out nets
  4. overlate oppdrag til andreto hand work over to others Eng. phraseto contract out
    • sette ut prosjektetto contract out the project
