soge

noun feminine or masculine
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt saga ‘utsagn, fortelling’; beslektet med si (III)to say · to tell

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. fortelling (2)what is told;
    narrative;
    short story
  2. saga (1)an Old Norse, especially Icelandic, prose narrative with a more or less historical basis
  3. historie (2)(the study of) the cultural development of humankind, or a delimited part of it

Set phrases

være ute av soga ikke lenger være aktuell;
være ferdig (3)no longer qualified, relevant or usable;
outdated, worn out, used up
