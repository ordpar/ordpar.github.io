klukke

verb

to cluck · to chuckle, to chortle · to gurgle, to lap

Inflection

Etymology

lydordonomatopoeic

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi fra seg gurglende lyderto make gurgling sounds
    • hønene klukketthe hens were clucking
  2. le med halvkvalte utbruddto laugh in half-stifled bursts
    • le så en klukkerto laugh so hard you chortle
    • klukke og leto chuckle and laugh
  3. om væske: skvulpeto lap med surklende lyderof liquid: to lapskvulpe with gurgling sounds
    • bølgene klukket mot bryggestolpenethe waves gurgled against the jetty posts
