varsle

verbverb

to notify · to warn · to announce

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av varselnotice, advance warning · an omen, a portent from varselnotice, advance warning · an omen, a portent

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. informere om fare, lovbrudd eller lignende;
    si frato let someone know · to give notice, to report     to give warning of danger, a breach of the law or the like;
    to let someone knowsi fra
    • varsle politiet to notify the police
    • de varsler med lyssignal they signal with a light
    • hun varslet om mobbing she blew the whistle on bullying
    • det ble varslet om glatte veier warnings were issued about icy roads
    • hunden varsler the dog raises the alarm
  2. gi beskjed om noe som skal skje to give notice of something that is going to happen
    • varsle streik to announce a strike
    • butikken varsler at de skal stenge the shop has announced that it is closing
    • det er varslet kuling a gale is forecast
  3. være tegn på;
    spå (II, 2)to presage     to be a sign of;
    to presagespå (II, 2)
    • uglens skrik varsler ulykke the owl's cry foretells disaster
