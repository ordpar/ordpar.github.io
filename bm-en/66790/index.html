varsle

verb

to notify · to warn · to announce

Inflection

Etymology

av varselnotice, advance warning · an omen, a portentfrom varselnotice, advance warning · an omen, a portent

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. informere om fare, lovbrudd eller lignende;
    si frato let someone know · to give notice, to report    to give warning of danger, a breach of the law or the like;
    to let someone knowsi fra
    • varsle politietto notify the police
    • de varsler med lyssignalthey signal with a light
    • hun varslet om mobbingshe blew the whistle on bullying
    • det ble varslet om glatte veierwarnings were issued about icy roads
    • hunden varslerthe dog raises the alarm
  2. gi beskjed om noe som skal skjeto give notice of something that is going to happen
    • varsle streikto announce a strike
    • butikken varsler at de skal stengethe shop has announced that it is closing
    • det er varslet kulinga gale is forecast
  3. være tegn på;
    spå (II, 2)to presage    to be a sign of;
    to presagespå (II, 2)
    • uglens skrik varsler ulykkethe owl's cry foretells disaster
