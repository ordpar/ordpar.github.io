straffe II homograph 2

verbverb

to punish · to convict · to beat

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av straffpunishment · sentence from straffpunishment · sentence

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gi straff, refse eller tukte for en misgjerning to punish, to chastise or discipline for a misdeed
    • faren straffet barna strengt når de var ulydige the father punished the children severely when they were disobedient
  2. dømme for lovbrudd to sentence for a criminal offence
    • tiltalte er ikke tidligere straffet the defendant has no previous convictions
  3. i idrett: overgå eller forbedre in sport: to surpass or improve on
    • hun straffet den tidligere rekorden med 2 tidels sekund she beat the previous record by two tenths of a second
    • utlendingene straffer oss på lengdene the foreign competitors outdo us over the long distances
    • ingen kunne straffe henne i kretsmesterskapet no one could beat her at the county championships
