straffe II homograph 2

verb

to punish · to convict · to beat

Inflection

Etymology

av straffpunishment · sentencefrom straffpunishment · sentence

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi straff, refse eller tukte for en misgjerningto punish, to chastise or discipline for a misdeed
    • faren straffet barna strengt når de var ulydigethe father punished the children severely when they were disobedient
  2. dømme for lovbruddto sentence for a criminal offence
    • tiltalte er ikke tidligere straffetthe defendant has no previous convictions
  3. i idrett: overgå eller forbedrein sport: to surpass or improve on
    • hun straffet den tidligere rekorden med 2 tidels sekundshe beat the previous record by two tenths of a second
    • utlendingene straffer oss på lengdenethe foreign competitors outdo us over the long distances
    • ingen kunne straffe henne i kretsmesterskapetno one could beat her at the county championships
