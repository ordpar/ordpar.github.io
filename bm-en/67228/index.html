veik, vek

adjectiveadjektiv

weak, feeble, frail · soft-hearted, sentimental

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt veikr; beslektet med vike (I)to give way, to yield · to move aside, to back away · to make way for Old Norse veikr; related to vike (I)to give way, to yield · to move aside, to back away · to make way for
