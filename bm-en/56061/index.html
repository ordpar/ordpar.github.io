spille III homograph 3, spelle

verbverb

to play · to gamble

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av lavtysk spelen, spillen from Low German spelen, spillen

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. blinke (II, 1)to twinkle, glitre (1)to glitter, funkleto sparkle, to twinkle, to glitter to twinkleblinke (II, 1), to glitterglitre (1), to sparklefunkle
    • sola spilte i snøkrystallene the sunlight glinted in the snow crystals
  2. drive med eller delta i lagspill eller lignende to play or take part in a team sport or the like
    • spille fotball to play football
    • de spilte mot et utenlandsk lag they played against a foreign team
    • vi spiller bra om dagen we're playing well these days
    • laget har spilt tre hjemmekamper på rad the team has played three home games in a row
  3. delta i spill med kort, terninger eller lignende to take part in a game with cards, dice, or the like
    • spille bridge to play bridge
  4. satse penger;
    vedde     to gamble;
    to bet, to wager
    • spille på travbanen to bet at the trotting track
    • ha noen kroner å spille for to have a few kroner to gamble with
  5. frambringe musikk to make music, to play
    • de spilte en sonate they played a sonata
    • gjengen har spilt plater hele kvelden the gang has been playing records all evening
    • hun spiller på fiolin she plays the violin
    • spille i korps to play in a band
  6. utføre en teaterrolle, filmrolle eller lignende;
    oppføre     to perform a stage role, film role, or the like;
    to stage, to put on
    • spille revy to perform in a revue
    • spille en rolle godt to play a part well
    • teateret spiller nå Peer Gynt the theatre is now staging Peer Gynt
  7. opptre som, te seg som;
    agere (1)to pose as     to act like, to behave as;
    to pose asagere (1)
    • spille idiot to play the idiot
  8. om fugl: frambringe paringslyd of a bird: to make its mating call
    • orrhanen spiller oppe i åsen the black grouse is calling up on the hillside
