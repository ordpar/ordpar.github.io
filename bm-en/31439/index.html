kontrahere

verb

to contract for, to order under contract · to contract, to tighten

Inflection

Etymology

av latin contrahere ‘dra sammen’; jamfør kon-con-, co-from Latin contrahere ‘to draw together’; compare kon-con-, co-

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. inngå en avtale om;
    bestille ved å skrive kontrakt    to enter into an agreement about something;
    to order something by signing a contract
    • kontrahere to nye fartøyerto contract for two new vessels
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • de kontraherende parterthe contracting parties
  2. om muskel: trekke seg sammenof a muscle: to contract
