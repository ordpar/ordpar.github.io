benk

Bokmål–English dictionary 3 3 articles

Article page

“benk” is an inflected form of:

  • benke I homograph 1 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to take a seat · to settle down ta sete
  • benke II homograph 2, beinke II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to straighten · to improve gjøre bein, rette (noe som er kroket, skjevt)
