formidling

noun masculine or feminine

the imparting, the communicating (of knowledge, art) · brokering, mediation · an agency, an intermediary body

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å formidlethe conveying or arranging of something
    • formidling av kunnskapthe imparting of knowledge
  2. institusjon som formidler noean institution that acts as an intermediary
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som arbeidsformidlingan employment agency, boligformidlinga housing service
