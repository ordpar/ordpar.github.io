trøst

noun feminine or masculine

comfort · consolation

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt traust ‘tillit’, beslektet med tro (I)belief · faith · trustOld Norse traust ‘trust’, related to tro (I)belief · faith · trust

Senses and Example Sentences

lindring eller oppmuntring i sorg eller motgangrelief or encouragement in grief or adversity
  • få trøstto be comforted
  • et trøstens orda word of comfort
  • en mager trøstcold comfort
  • det var da enda en trøstwell, that was some comfort at least
  • han trenger litt trøst nåhe needs a bit of comfort right now
