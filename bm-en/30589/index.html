knuge

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt knoka ‘slå med knokene’Old Norse knoka ‘to strike with the knuckles’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. klemme eller presse kraftig;
    jamfør knugende (1)crushing    to squeeze or press hard;
    compare knugende (1)crushing
    • knuge noe i håndento clutch something in one's hand
    • knuge sine hender i fortvilelseto wring one's hands in despair
    • hun knuger seg inntil hamshe presses herself close against him
  2. ligge trykkende over;
    jamfør knugende (2)crushing    to lie heavily on, to oppress;
    compare knugende (2)crushing
    • være knuget av skyldfølelseto be weighed down by guilt
