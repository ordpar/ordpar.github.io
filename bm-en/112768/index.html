banne i kirken

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

motsi eller spotte en autoritet eller noe hellig to contradict or mock an authority or something held sacred see:Se: banneto swear, to curse (use bad language) Eng. phraseto commit heresy
  • å motsi lederne var å banne i kirken to contradict the leaders was heresy
