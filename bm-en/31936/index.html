krangle

verb

to quarrel · to bicker · to play up

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør norrønt kranga ‘slepe seg fram’; 2 fra svenskcompare Old Norse kranga 'to drag oneself along'; 2 from Swedish

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. lage bråk (II)noise · trouble · fuss, tretteto make a fussbråk (II), to quarrel
    • krangle og småslåssto bicker and scuffle
    • krangle seg til noeto argue one's way to something
  2. ikke fungere tilfredsstillendeto fail to work properly
    • motoren krangletthe engine was playing up
    • ryggen har begynt å kranglemy back has started playing up
