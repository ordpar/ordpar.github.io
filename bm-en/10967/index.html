droppe

verb

to skip · to give up · to drop

Inflection

Etymology

fra engelsk; beslektet med dråpea dropfrom English; related to dråpea drop

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. slippe ned;
    la falle    to drop down;
    to let fall
    • droppe forsyninger i fallskjermto drop supplies by parachute
  2. om dommer i lagspill: slippe ball eller puck for å sette i gang (igjen) spilletof a referee in team sports: to release the ball or puck to (re)start play
    • han kom fram til at det beste var å stoppe spillet og droppe ballen igjenhe decided the best thing was to stop play and drop the ball again
  3. la væreto refrain from doing something · to leave something alone, to not do it;
    stå overto sit out;
    oppgito state, to give (details) · to give up, relinquish    to refrainla være;
    to sit outstå over;
    to give upoppgi
    • spis mer frukt og dropp sukkereteat more fruit and cut out sugar
    • hun droppet konserten for å spare pengershe skipped the concert to save money
    • dropp det!forget it!
    • planen ble droppetthe plan was dropped

Set phrases

droppe ut falle fra (2)to abandon;
slutte (1)to quitto abandonfalle fra (2);
to quitslutte (1) Eng. phraseto drop out
  • droppe ut av universitetetto drop out of university
