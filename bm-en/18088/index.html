frisk II homograph 2

adjectiveadjektiv

healthy, well · fresh · brisk, lively

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk; samme opprinnelse som ferskfresh · new from Low German; the same origin as ferskfresh · new

  1. som ikke har begynt å tape seg;
    fersk, god, uskjemt, ren     which has not begun to deteriorate;
    fresh, good, unspoiled, clean
    • friske egg fresh eggs
    • friske grønnsaker fresh vegetables

    • frisk luft fresh air
    • ha frisk pust to have fresh breath
  2. fornyet, usvekketundiminished, unabated renewed, undiminishedusvekket

    fersk     recently happened or made;
    fresh

  4. som har god helse;
    sunn (1)healthy, bra (2)well;

    • ha de friskeste dyrene to have the healthiest animals
  5. livlig, kvikk, feiende lively, brisk, dashing
