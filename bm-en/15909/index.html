for III homograph 3

conjunction

for · because

Etymology

sammenheng med for (V)for · too, excessively · in front of, beforeconnected with for (V)for · too, excessively · in front of, before

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sideordnende konjunksjon ved grunn, årsak:coordinating conjunction expressing reason or cause:
    • han lå, for han var sykhe was lying down, for he was ill
    • for riket er dittfor the kingdom is yours
  2. som subjunksjon:as a subordinating conjunction:
    • for di eller fordi – se fordibecausefor di or fordi 'because' – see fordibecause
