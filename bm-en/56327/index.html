sprek

adjective

fit · sprightly

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt sprækr ‘livlig’; beslektet med sprake (II)to crackleOld Norse sprækr 'lively'; related to sprake (II)to crackle

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i god fysisk form;
    sterk, spretten, spenstig    in good physical shape;
    strong, sprightly, agile
    • han var sprekere i sin ungdomhe was fitter in his youth
    • være sprek for alderento be fit for one's age
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • det var sprekt gjortthat was impressively done
  2. ved god helse;
    frisk    in good health;
    fit, well
    • jeg var syk, men nå er jeg ganske sprek igjenI was ill, but now I am feeling quite fit again
  3. kraftig og raskpowerful and quick
    • en sportslig og sprek familiebila sporty and nippy family car
