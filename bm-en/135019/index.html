i vei

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: veiroad · way · route · distance
  1. av gårde;
    av sted     off;
    on one's way
    • de la i vei they set off
    • han skulle straks i vei he was to be off straight away
  2. uten stopp;
    uhemmetunrestrained, unbridled, uninhibited     non-stop;
    unrestrained, unbridleduhemmet
    • hun snakket i vei om alt mulig she talked away about all sorts of things
    • de skrev ivrig i vei they wrote away eagerly
