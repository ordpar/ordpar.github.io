telefonere

verbverb

to phone, to call, to ring

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

jamfør telefontelephone, phone · phone call compare telefontelephone, phone · phone call

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. foreta en telefonoppringing;
    ringe (III, 2)to ring     to make a telephone call;
    to ringringe (III, 2)
    • telefonere til legen to phone the doctor
    • hun telefonerte etter drosje she phoned for a taxi
  2. snakke i telefonen to talk on the telephone
    • hun telefonerte med ambassadøren she spoke on the phone with the ambassador
