overens

adverb

to reach agreement, to get along (in komme overens) · to correspond, to match up (in stemme overens)

Etymology

fra lavtysk overen(s) ‘enig’; jamfør ens (I)alikefrom Low German overen(s) ‘in agreement’; compare ens (I)alike

Set phrases

komme overens
  1. bli enigeto reach agreement Eng. phraseto come to an agreement
    • komme overens om en pristo agree on a price
  2. gå godt sammento be on good terms with each other Eng. phraseto get along
    • de kommer godt overensthey get along well
stemme overens vise samsvarto show correspondence Eng. phraseto match up
