minere

verb

to blast with explosives · to tunnel into, to mine

Inflection

Etymology

av fransk miner; jamfør mine (II)a minefrom French miner; compare mine (II)a mine

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sprenge stein eller fjell med sprengstoffto blast rock with explosives
  2. om insekter: grave hulrom i en plantedelof insects: to tunnel cavities inside part of a plant
