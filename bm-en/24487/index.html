noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn
InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. representant for humanismen (1)an intellectual movement of the Renaissance whose aim was to create a secular and humane culture modelled on the culture of classical antiquity under renessansen
  2. person som har humanisme (2)a philosophy of life that stresses the intrinsic worth of the human being and values such as freedom, justice and truth som livssyn
  3. person som forsker i eller studerer et humanistisk fag
