verre

adjectiveadjektiv
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt verri, komparativ av ondevil · malicious · wicked, vondbad, nasty · painful, sore · evil, wicked; jamfør verst (II)worst Old Norse verri, the comparative of ondevil · malicious · wicked, vondbad, nasty · painful, sore · evil, wicked; compare verst (II)worst

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. galere, vanskeligere;
    mer plagsom, mer uheldig     worse, more difficult;
    more troublesome, more unfortunate
    • stort verre kan det ikke bli it cannot get much worse
    • jo mer du klager, jo verre blir det the more you complain, the worse it gets
    • dette blir bare verre og verre this is just getting worse and worse
    • en skjebne verre enn døden a fate worse than death
    • det kunne lett ha gått verre it could easily have gone worse
    • det som verre var what was worse
  2. dårligere, svakere;
    mer tvilsom     poorer, weaker;
    more dubious
    • pasienten er blitt verre the patient has got worse
    • mange er verre enn henne many are worse than her
  3. brukt som adverb (uten komparativ betydning): svært, veldig used as an adverb (without comparative meaning): very, extremely
    • streve verre to struggle terribly
    • jeg koste meg verre I enjoyed myself immensely
