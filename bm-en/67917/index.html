verre

adjective
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt verri, komparativ av ondevil · malicious · wicked, vondbad, nasty · painful, sore · evil, wicked; jamfør verst (II)worstOld Norse verri, the comparative of ondevil · malicious · wicked, vondbad, nasty · painful, sore · evil, wicked; compare verst (II)worst

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. galere, vanskeligere;
    mer plagsom, mer uheldig    worse, more difficult;
    more troublesome, more unfortunate
    • stort verre kan det ikke bliit cannot get much worse
    • jo mer du klager, jo verre blir detthe more you complain, the worse it gets
    • dette blir bare verre og verrethis is just getting worse and worse
    • en skjebne verre enn dødena fate worse than death
    • det kunne lett ha gått verreit could easily have gone worse
    • det som verre varwhat was worse
  2. dårligere, svakere;
    mer tvilsom    poorer, weaker;
    more dubious
    • pasienten er blitt verrethe patient has got worse
    • mange er verre enn hennemany are worse than her
  3. brukt som adverb (uten komparativ betydning): svært, veldigused as an adverb (without comparative meaning): very, extremely
    • streve verreto struggle terribly
    • jeg koste meg verreI enjoyed myself immensely

Set phrases

gjøre vondt verre gjøre noe negativt enda dårligereto make something bad even worse Eng. phraseto make matters worse
