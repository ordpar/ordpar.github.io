ansvar

noun neuter

responsibility · liability

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt andsvarOld Norse andsvar

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. (moralsk, økonomisk eller juridisk) forpliktelse til å stå til rette for noea (moral, financial or legal) obligation to answer for something
    • ha ansvar for noeto be responsible for something
    • ta ansvar for at noe blir gjortto take responsibility for getting something done
    • velte ansvaret over på andreto shift the responsibility onto others
    • på eget ansvarat one's own risk
    • stå til ansvar for det en har gjortto answer for what one has done
    • unndra seg ansvaretto evade responsibility
    • få noen til å innse sitt ansvarto make someone realise their responsibility
    • dele ansvaret for noeto share the responsibility for something
    • dette skal du få stå til ansvar for!you will be held to account for this!
    • bære ansvaret for noeto bear the responsibility for something
    • ha ansvar overfor velgerneto be accountable to the voters
    • påta seg et ansvarto take on a responsibility
    • ha ansvar for barnto have responsibility for children
    • frihet under ansvarfreedom with responsibility
    • det er mitt ansvarthat is my responsibility
    • ansvaret hvilte tungt på hennethe responsibility weighed heavily on her
    • jeg tar ikke noe ansvar for resultatetI take no responsibility for the outcome
  2. i jus: rettslig forpliktelsein law: legal liability
    • gjøre ansvar gjeldende mot bedriftento bring a claim of liability against the company
