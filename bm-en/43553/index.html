overeksponere

verb
Inflection

Etymology

jamfør overover · above · across og eksponereto expose (to something) · to display, to show off · to expose (a film)compare overover · above · across and eksponereto expose (to something) · to display, to show off · to expose (a film)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i fotografering: belyse for mye, slik at bildet blir for lyst;
    jamfør undereksponereto underexpose    in photography: to give too much light, so that the picture comes out too bright;
    compare undereksponereto underexpose
    • filmen var overeksponertthe film was overexposed
  2. (la) være for sterkt utsatt forto (let something) be too strongly exposed to
    • bli overeksponert for forurensingto be overexposed to pollution
  3. i overført betydning: legge for stor vekt på eller vise fram i overdreven gradfiguratively: to place too much emphasis on or to display to excess
    • jeg synes forfatteren overeksponerer skjevhetene i samfunnetI think the author overplays the inequalities in society
