overeksponere

verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

jamfør overover · above · across og eksponereto expose (to something) · to display, to show off · to expose (a film) compare overover · above · across and eksponereto expose (to something) · to display, to show off · to expose (a film)

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. i fotografering: belyse for mye, slik at bildet blir for lyst;
    jamfør undereksponereto underexpose     in photography: to give too much light, so that the picture comes out too bright;
    compare undereksponereto underexpose
    • filmen var overeksponert the film was overexposed
  2. (la) være for sterkt utsatt for to (let something) be too strongly exposed to
    • bli overeksponert for forurensing to be overexposed to pollution
  3. i overført betydning: legge for stor vekt på eller vise fram i overdreven grad figuratively: to place too much emphasis on or to display to excess
    • jeg synes forfatteren overeksponerer skjevhetene i samfunnet I think the author overplays the inequalities in society
