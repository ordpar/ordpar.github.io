mestre

verb

to master · to cope with · to manage

Inflection

Etymology

av mestermaster · champion · master craftsmanfrom mestermaster · champion · master craftsman

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. beherske;
    ha tak på;
    greie;
    være herre over    to master;
    to have a grasp of;
    to manage;
    to be master of
    • mestre en oppgaveto handle a task
    • hun mestret ikke situasjonenshe could not cope with the situation
    • mestre vanskeneto overcome the difficulties
    • mestre en vill hestto control a wild horse
  2. kunne (bruke) fullt utto have full command of
    • mestre et fagto master a subject
    • hun mestrer teknikkenshe has mastered the technique
    • mestre engelsk skriftlig og muntligto have a command of written and spoken English
