harmoni

noun masculine

harmony

Inflection

Etymology

av gresk harmonia ‘sammenføyning’from Greek harmonia ‘a joining together’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ordnet samklang av ulike toner;
    jamfør akkord (4)chord    ordered concord of different notes;
    compare akkord (4)chord
  2. likevekt eller samsvar mellom delene i en helhet;
    fred, ro, orden    balance or correspondence between the parts of a whole;
    peace, calm, order
    • fred og harmoni preget barndomshjemmetpeace and harmony marked their childhood home
    • harmonien mellom smakenethe harmony between the flavours
