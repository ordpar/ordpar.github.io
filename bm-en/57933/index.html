stusse II homograph 2

verb

to be taken aback, to be puzzled · to bounce · to flick (a ball) on

Inflection

Etymology

fra tysk, opprinnelig ‘(få til å) støte mot’from German, originally '(to cause to) bump against'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stanse opp og tenke seg om;
    bli forundret;
    lure på    to stop short and think;
    to be puzzled;
    to wonder
    • stusse over noeto find something odd
    • jeg stusset da jeg hørte detI was taken aback when I heard it
  2. sprette lettto bounce lightly
    • ballen stussetthe ball bounced
  3. gi et lite spark eller støtto give a slight kick or knock
    • spilleren stusset ballen viderethe player flicked the ball on
