trollpakk

noun neuter
Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. (flokk av) troll (1)in folk belief: a (large) ugly, strong and often dangerous, but stupid and gullible, human-like being that lives in the forest, in the mountains or in the sea;
    onde makter
    • Trym hersket over jotuner, tusser og trollpakk
  2. pakk (2)a rabble, hooligans, pøbel;
    jamfør troll (3)a nasty, frightening or contrary creature, especially a person
    • lommeboka hans ble stjålet av noe trollpakk
