noun masculine

hill · slope · ground

Inflection

Etymology

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. terrengskråning, særlig om side av en åsa slope in the terrain, especially the side of a ridge
    • en bratt bakkea steep hill
    • vi gikk bakke opp og bakke nedwe walked uphill and downhill
    • huset ligger oppe i bakkenthe house stands up on the hillside
  2. mark (I)open country, the woods · the ground · field, jordsoil, earth · the earth (the planet) · landthe groundmark (I), earthjord
    • falle i bakkento fall to the ground
  3. rygg på forskjellige redskap;
    motsatt egg (I, 1)the cutting edge    the back of various tools;
    the opposite of the cutting edgeegg (I, 1)
