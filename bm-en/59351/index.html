sørge

verb

to mourn · to grieve

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt syrgja; jamfør sorggrief, sorrow · mourning · worry, distressOld Norse syrgja; compare sorggrief, sorrow · mourning · worry, distress

Senses and Example Sentences

være fylt av sorg (1)grief, for eksempel ved tap eller lignendeto be filled with griefsorg (1), for instance at a loss or the like
  • sørge over tapet av en vennto mourn the loss of a friend

Set phrases

sørge for
  1. ha omsorg forto care for, to look after Eng. phraseto take care of
    • sørge for barnato look after the children
  2. ta seg av (at noe går i orden)to take charge of (making sure something gets done) Eng. phraseto see to it
    • sørge for forfriskningerto see to the refreshments
