mindre

adjective

smaller · less, fewer · minor, smallish, slight

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt minni, komparativ av litensmall, little · young · slight, minor; jamfør minstsmallest, least · at least, no less than · youngestOld Norse minni, comparative of litensmall, little · young · slight, minor; compare minstsmallest, least · at least, no less than · youngest

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ikke så stor (som)not as big (as)
    • han er mindre enn jeghe is smaller than I am
    • har du et nummer mindre?do you have a smaller size?
  2. om barn: yngreof children: younger
    • hun passet de mindre søskneneshe looked after the younger siblings
  3. heller liten i omfang eller størrelserather small in extent or size
    • en mindre pengesuma smallish sum of money
    • en mindre bya smallish town
  4. færrefewer
    • mindre enn ti eleverfewer than ten pupils
  5. brukt som adverb: i svakere grad;
    ikke så mye (som)    used as an adverb: to a lesser degree;
    not as much (as)
    • bli mindre og mindre interessertto become less and less interested
    • dess mer en strever, dess mindre tilfreds blir enthe more one strives, the less content one becomes
  6. brukt som substantiv: ganske liten mengdeused as a noun: a fairly small quantity
    • vi har mindre å gjøre enn førwe have less to do than before
    • en kan bli sint for mindreone can get angry over less
