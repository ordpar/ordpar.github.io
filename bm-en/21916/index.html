    rovetail, rumpe (3)tail, svans (1)tail     flexible, often long and narrow body part at the rear end of certain animals;
    tail – compare rovetail, rumpe (3)tail and svans (1)tail
    • logre med halen to wag its tail
  2. noe som ligner på en hale (I, 1)a tail something resembling a tailhale (I, 1)

    • kometen har en lang, lysende hale the comet has a long, glowing tail
  3. noe som følger etter noe something trailing along behind something
    • de hadde en hale med unger etter seg they had a train of children trailing after them
