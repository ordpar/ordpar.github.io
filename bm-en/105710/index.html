ta imot

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: tato take · to catch · to grab
  1. fange opp;
    motta     to catch;
    to receive
    • han tar imot ballen he catches the ball
  2. ønske velkommen (og slippe inn) to welcome (and let in)
    • ta imot gjestene to receive the guests
  3. finne seg i å få to put up with getting
    • han tok ofte imot juling he often took a beating
