ta imot

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. fange opp;
    motta    to catch;
    to receive
    • han tar imot ballenhe catches the ball
  2. ønske velkommen (og slippe inn)to welcome (and let in)
    • ta imot gjesteneto receive the guests
  3. finne seg i å fåto put up with getting
    • han tok ofte imot julinghe often took a beating
