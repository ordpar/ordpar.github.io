kronisk

adjectiveadjektiv

chronic · constant, permanent

gjennom tysk, fra gresk khronikos; se kronikkop-ed piece, opinion piece through German, from Greek khronikos; see kronikkop-ed piece, opinion piece

  1. langvariglong-lasting · prolonged · lengthy eller uhelbredeligincurable;
    motsatt akutt (II, 1)acute     long-lastinglangvarig or incurableuhelbredelig;
