innledningsvis, innleiingsvis

adjective

at the outset, to begin with · introductory, opening

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør -vis-ly · in -s, by -s (in pairs, in stages)compare -vis-ly · in -s, by -s (in pairs, in stages)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som hender i forkant av en prosesswhich takes place at the start of a process
    • den innledningsvise presiseringenthe introductory clarification
  2. brukt som adverb: til å begynne med;
    som innledning    used as an adverb: to begin with;
    by way of introduction
    • innledningsvis vil jeg understreke at jeg har full tillit til arrangørenat the outset I would like to emphasise that I have full confidence in the organiser
