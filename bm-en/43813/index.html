overvekt

noun masculine or feminine

excess weight, being overweight · a preponderance, a majority

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. vekt over det vanlige;
    for stor vekt    weight above the normal;
    excessive weight
    • hun slet med overvektshe struggled with being overweight
    • betale ekstra porto for overvektto pay extra postage for excess weight
  2. flertall, overmaktsuperior strength or numbersmajority, superior strength or numbersovermakt
    • de vant med tre stemmers overvektthey won by a majority of three votes
