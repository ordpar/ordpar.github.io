pleie II homograph 2

verb

to care for · to nurse · to tend

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt plega, gjennom dansk pleje; fra lavtyskOld Norse plega, through Danish pleje; from Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. passe og stelle et sykt eller svakt menneske eller dyrto look after and care for a sick or frail person or animal
    • pleie den syketo nurse the sick person
    • han pleide den gamle moren sinhe cared for his elderly mother
  2. stelle (en del av) kroppen så den holdes ren og sunnto tend (a part of) the body so that it stays clean and healthy
    • pleie kroppen sinto look after one's body
    • hun pleier neglene regelmessigshe takes care of her nails regularly
  3. holde noe i god standto keep something in good condition
    • hun pleide bilen med voksshe treated the car with wax
    • pleie kulturlandskapetto look after the cultural landscape
  4. holde ved like;
    dyrke (5)to cultivate    to keep up;
    to cultivatedyrke (5)
    • de pleier kontakt med vennerthey keep in touch with friends
    • ha tid til å pleie sine interesserto have time to pursue one's interests
