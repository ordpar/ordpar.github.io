tun

noun neuter

a farmyard · a courtyard · a cluster of buildings

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt tún; samme opprinnelse som engelsk town ‘by’Old Norse tún; same origin as English town

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. åpen, flat plass mellom husene på en gård eller lignende;
    gårdsplassa farmyard, a courtyard    open, flat area between the buildings on a farm or the like;
    a farmyard, a courtyardgårdsplass
    • stå ute på tunetto stand out in the yard
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som gårdstunfarmyard, inntuninner farmyard, around the dwelling houses, uttunouter farmyard, by the outbuildings
  2. sted på en gård der husene står, til forskjell fra markene omkringthe place on a farm where the buildings stand, as opposed to the fields around them
    • gå hjem i tunetto go home to the farmyard
  3. samling av hus som tilhører ett gårdsbrukgroup of houses belonging to a single farm
    • en gård med to tuna farm with two clusters of buildings
  4. samling av bygninger, institusjoner og lignendecluster of buildings, institutions and the like
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd ord som bygdetuna collection of old rural houses, helsetuna cluster of health facilities
