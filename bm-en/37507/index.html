meddele

verb
Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk, opprinnelig ‘dele med en’from Low German, originally ‘to share with someone’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. underretteto notify · to informto informunderrette
    • jeg kan meddele at vi får besøkI can announce that we're having visitors
    • vi må dessverre meddele at boka er utsolgt fra forlagetwe regret to inform you that the book is sold out at the publisher
  2. gito give · to hand (over) · to yield, to produceto givegi
    • meddele en avskjed i nådeto grant an honourable discharge
