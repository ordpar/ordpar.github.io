drikke III homograph 3

verb

to drink

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt drekkaOld Norse drekka

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. tilføre kroppen væske gjennom munnento take liquid into the body through the mouth
    • drikke en kopp teto drink a cup of tea
    • jeg drikker aldri brusI never drink fizzy drinks
    • husk å drikke nok vann!remember to drink enough water!
    • snart har jeg drukket fem kopper kaffesoon I'll have drunk five cups of coffee
    • hun drakk en liten slurk av flaskashe took a small sip from the bottle
  2. nyte alkohol;
    misbruke alkohol    to consume alcohol;
    to abuse alcohol
    • verken røyke eller drikkeneither to smoke nor to drink
    • de var ute og drakk i gårthey were out drinking yesterday
    • drikke seg fullto get drunk
    • drikke seg til motto drink to work up some courage
    • drikke med måteto drink in moderation
    • jeg skal aldri begynne å drikke igjen!I'm never going to start drinking again!
  3. om eldre forhold: feire høytid eller merkedag med drikkelaga drinking party, a drinking session, a booze-upof earlier times: to celebrate a feast day or an anniversary with a drinking partydrikkelag
    • drikke julto celebrate Christmas with drinking
    • drikke gravølto hold a wake
  4. suge til segto soak up
    • drikke av visdommens brønnto drink from the well of wisdom
    • bilen drikker mye bensinthe car guzzles a lot of petrol
