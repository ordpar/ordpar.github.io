Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. frosset vann, væske frozen water or liquid
    • vannet frøs til is the water froze to ice
    • det lå ennå is og snø i skyggen there was still ice and snow lying in the shade
    • for mange tusen år siden var landet dekket av is many thousands of years ago the land was covered in ice
    • være kald som is på beina to have feet as cold as ice
  2. isbitan ice cube, a piece of ice an ice cube, a piece of iceisbit
    • ta en drink med is i to have a drink with ice in it
  3. islag på sjø, innsjø eller elv a layer of ice on the sea, a lake or a river
    • isen på vannene er ikke sikker ennå the ice on the lakes is not safe yet
    • gå gjennom isen to fall through the ice
  4. ispinnean ice lolly, an ice cream on a stick;
    iskremice cream     an ice cream on a stickispinne;
    compare iskremice cream
    • spise is til dessert to eat ice cream for dessert
    • spise tre is to eat three ice creams
    as a suffix in words such as:som etterledd i ord som krokanispraline ice cream, sjokoladeischocolate ice cream
