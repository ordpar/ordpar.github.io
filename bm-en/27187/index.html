is

noun masculine

ice · ice cream

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt íssOld Norse íss

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. frosset vann, væskefrozen water or liquid
    • vannet frøs til isthe water froze to ice
    • det lå ennå is og snø i skyggenthere was still ice and snow lying in the shade
    • for mange tusen år siden var landet dekket av ismany thousands of years ago the land was covered in ice
    • være kald som is på beinato have feet as cold as ice
  2. isbitan ice cube, a piece of icean ice cube, a piece of iceisbit
    • ta en drink med is ito have a drink with ice in it
  3. islag på sjø, innsjø eller elva layer of ice on the sea, a lake or a river
    • isen på vannene er ikke sikker ennåthe ice on the lakes is not safe yet
    • gå gjennom isento fall through the ice
  4. ispinnean ice lolly, an ice cream on a stick;
    iskremice cream    an ice cream on a stickispinne;
    compare iskremice cream
    • spise is til dessertto eat ice cream for dessert
    • spise tre isto eat three ice creams
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som krokanispraline ice cream, sjokoladeischocolate ice cream
