forstå

verb

to understand · to grasp

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt fyrirstanda, forstanda; fra lavtysk opprinnelig ‘stå foran noe (for å undersøke det)'Old Norse fyrirstanda, forstanda; from Low German, originally ‘to stand in front of something (in order to examine it)'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. oppfatte (2)to grasp, skjønneto understand;
    kunne (1)to know    to graspoppfatte (2), to understandskjønne;
    to knowkunne (1)

    • forstår du hva jeg mener?do you understand what I mean?
    • forstå hva det dreier seg omto understand what it's about

  2. komme overens med, sympatisere medto get along with, to sympathise with
