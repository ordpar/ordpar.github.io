anbefale

verb

to recommend · to advise

Inflection

Etymology

gjennom dansk, fra tysk; av an- (I)a prefix in German loanwords og befaleto order, to command · to be in command of, to rule med betydning ‘overdra’through Danish, from German; from an- (I)a prefix in German loanwords and befaleto order, to command · to be in command of, to rule with the meaning ‘to hand over’

Senses and Example Sentences

rå (V, 2)to advise til, støtte;
gi lovord;
framheve som beste løsning eller spesielt skikketto adviserå (V, 2), to back;
to speak well of;
to single out as the best solution or as particularly suitable
  • jeg vil ikke anbefale detI wouldn't recommend it
  • legen anbefalte operasjonthe doctor recommended surgery
  • anbefale noen på det varmesteto recommend someone in the warmest terms
  • anbefale en bokto recommend a book
  • de anbefalte meg å velge et annet yrkethey advised me to choose a different profession
  • Helsedirektoratet anbefaler å spise mindre sukkerthe Norwegian Directorate of Health recommends eating less sugar
brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
  • vi følger anbefalt strategiwe are following the recommended strategy
  • anbefalt aldersgrense er 11 årthe recommended age limit is 11
  • trene mindre enn anbefaltto exercise less than recommended
