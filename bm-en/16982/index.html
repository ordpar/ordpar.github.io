forstyrre

verbverb

to disturb · to disrupt

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk ‘røre opp i noe’, av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess; etterleddet beslektet med styr (II)control · order from Low German 'to stir something up', from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess; the final element is related to styr (II)control · order
