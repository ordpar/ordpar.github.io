mate

verbverb

to feed

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. putte mat i munnen på;
    gi mat;
    fôre     to put food in someone's mouth;
    to give food;
    to feed (animals)
    • mate spedbarnet med skje to feed the baby with a spoon
    • pasienten må mates the patient has to be fed
    • hun mater hønene she feeds the hens
  2. forsyne med materiale, råstoff eller lignende to supply with material, raw material or the like
    • mate en datamaskin med opplysninger to feed data into a computer
