matnyttig

adjective

edible · that pays off, profitable

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

    spiselig    which can be used as food;
    edible

  2. som gir levebrød eller andre (økonomiske) fordelerwhich provides a livelihood or other (financial) advantages
