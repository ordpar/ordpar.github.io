“strekk” is an inflected form of:

  • strekke I homograph 1 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to pull, to strain (a muscle) · to reprimand skade ved å strekke for langt
  • strekke II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to stretch out · to straighten out · to reach out rette ut
