sluke

verb

to devour · to gulp down · to swallow up

Inflection

Etymology

trolig fra lavtyskprobably from Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. svelge grådig og fort;
    svelge i store biter eller slurker    to gulp down greedily and quickly;
    to swallow in big bites or gulps
    • sluke matento wolf down one's food
    • sluke i seg noeto gulp something down
    • han gikk under og slukte vannhe went under and swallowed water
  2. uttale utydelig eller sløyfe ord eller lyderto pronounce indistinctly or drop words or sounds
    • han mumler og sluker ordenehe mumbles and swallows his words
  3. se på eller lytte til (ukritisk og) med stor intensitetto watch or listen to (uncritically and) with great intensity
    • hun sluker ham med øyneneshe devours him with her eyes
    • de slukte hvert ordthey lapped up every word
  4. legge under seg;
    ta over    to bring under one's control;
    to take over
    • de store landene sluker de småthe big countries swallow up the small ones
  5. la forsvinne;
    bruke oppto use up;
    kreve (2)to require    to swallow up;
    to use upbruke opp;
    to requirekreve (2)
    • mørket slukte hennethe darkness swallowed her up
    • ovnen sluker en masse vedthe stove devours huge amounts of firewood
    • dette vil sluke en masse pengerthis will eat up a lot of money
    • arbeidet sluker all min tidthe work is swallowing up all my time

Set phrases

sluke rått godta noe kritikkløstto accept something uncritically Eng. phraseto swallow something whole
  • de sluker rått alt hun sierthey swallow everything she says
