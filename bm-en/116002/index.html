ha seg

to get oneself, to treat oneself to · to be the case · to have sex

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. skaffe seg;
    sørge for å få    to get oneself, to acquire;
    to make sure one gets
    • ha seg en hvilto have a rest
    • ha seg frabedtto refuse to accept something
    • ha seg en blåsto have a smoke
    • hun skal ha seg bilshe's going to get herself a car
  2. henge sammen;
    forklares    to come about;
    to be explained
    • hvordan kan dette ha seg?how can this be?
  3. kose seg;
    ha seksuell omgang    to enjoy oneself;
    to have sexual intercourse     Eng. phraseto have sex
    • de har seg på sofaen etter festenthey have sex on the sofa after the party
