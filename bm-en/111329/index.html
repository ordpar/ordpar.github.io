for noens del

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. etter noens ønske eller preferanseaccording to someone's wish or preference Eng. phrasefor one's part
    • for min del vil jeg si meg enigfor my part, I'd say I agree
    • direktøren synes for sin del at det var en god idéthe director, for his part, thought it was a good idea
    • for hans del passer det braas far as he's concerned, that's fine
  2. for noen eller noe;
    på grunn av    for someone or something;
    because of     Eng. phrasefor the sake of
    • sesongen var over for hans delthe season was over for him
    • det blir for dyrt for vår delit'll be too expensive for us
    • vi gjør ikke dette for pengenes delwe're not doing this for the sake of the money
