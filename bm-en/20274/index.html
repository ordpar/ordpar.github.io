glipp II homograph 2

noun uninflectable

to miss out on (in gå glipp av) · to miss (in gå glipp av)

Etymology

av glippe (I)to slip, to come loose · to fail, to fall throughfrom glippe (I)to slip, to come loose · to fail, to fall through

Set phrases

miste muligheten tilto fail to obtain or experience;
