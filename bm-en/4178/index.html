bakover

adverbadverb

backwards · back

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. i den retningen en har ryggen, med ryggen først;
    tilbakeback · backwards     in the direction one's back is facing, back first;
    compare tilbakeback · backwards
    • lene hodet bakover to lean one's head back
    • kamme håret bakover to comb one's hair back
    • setet kan legges bakover the seat can be reclined
    • ta et skritt bakover to take a step backwards
    • se seg bakover to look back
  2. om tid: tilbake i retning vekk fra nåtiden of time: back, away from the present
    • tenke bakover to think back
    • se, gå bakover i tid to look, go back in time
    • lenger bakover i historien further back in history
  3. brukt som preposisjon: mot baksiden, bakre enden used as a preposition: towards the back, the rear end
    • røykskyen stod bakover veien the cloud of smoke stretched back along the road
    • det lød tisking bakover rekkene whispering could be heard back along the rows
    • kikke seg bakover skuldra to glance back over one's shoulder
