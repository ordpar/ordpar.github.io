istedenfor, i stedet for

preposition

instead of · in place of

Senses and Example Sentences

i ens sted, til erstatning forin someone’s place, as a replacement for
  • vil du gå istedenfor meg?will you go instead of me?
  • få en ny kniv istedenfor den gamleto get a new knife in place of the old one
  • tilbered grønnsakene i mikrobølgeovnen i stedet for å koke eller dampe demcook the vegetables in the microwave instead of boiling or steaming them
