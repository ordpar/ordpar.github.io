bearbeide

verb

to work on, to process · to adapt, to revise (a text or play) · to work on, to win over (a person, a market)

Inflection

Etymology

av tysk bearbeitenfrom German bearbeiten

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. arbeide (videre) på eller med;
    omarbeideto rework, to revise;
    behandleto treat · to deal with · to handle    to work (further) on or with something;
    to rework, to reviseomarbeide;
    to treatbehandle
    • bearbeide en steinblokk med hammer og meiselto work a block of stone with hammer and chisel
    • få ro til å bearbeide sorgento have the peace to work through one's grief
    • bearbeide en bokto adapt a book
    • skuespillet er bearbeidet for radiothe play has been adapted for radio
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • en bearbeidet utgave av en boka revised edition of a book
    • bearbeidede produkterprocessed products
  2. påvirke, overtaleto influence, to persuade
    • bearbeide markedetto work the market
