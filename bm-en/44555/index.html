pen

adjectiveadjektiv

good-looking · pretty · nice

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. med fint og tiltalende utseende;
    velstelt     with a fine and attractive appearance;
    well-groomed
    • den peneste gutten i klassen the best-looking boy in the class
    • være pen og pyntet til festen to be nicely dressed up for the party
    • ta på seg penere klær to put on smarter clothes
    • en pen hage a pretty garden
  2. om vær: med opphold, lite vind og lite skyer of weather: dry, with little wind and few clouds
    • lettskyet, pent vær partly cloudy, fine weather
  3. respektabel;
    skikkelig (1)decent     respectable;
    decentskikkelig (1)
    • kjøpe hus i et pent strøk to buy a house in a nice neighbourhood
    brukt som adverb: used as an adverb:
    • oppfør deg pent! behave yourself!
  4. rosende, vennlig complimentary, kind
    • få pen omtale to receive favourable mention
    • det var en pen tanke that was a nice thought
    brukt som adverb: used as an adverb:
    • snakke pent om noen to speak well of someone
  5. forsiktig;
    rolig og behersket     careful;
    calm and controlled
    • politiet meldte om få ulykker og pen kjøring the police reported few accidents and orderly driving
    brukt som adverb: used as an adverb:
    • kjøre pent to drive carefully
    • han tok nederlaget pent he took the defeat well
  6. rikelig;
    tilfredsstillende;
    bra, god     ample;
    satisfactory;
    good, fine
    • få et pent utbytte to get a handsome return
    • pene resultater solid results
    • pen lønn a decent salary
  7. brukt som adverb: uten vilkår used as an adverb: without qualification
    • han ble pent nødt til å gå he simply had to leave
  8. brukt som forsterkende adverb: svært used as an intensifying adverb: very
    • de gjorde pent lite for å hjelpe til they did precious little to help out
