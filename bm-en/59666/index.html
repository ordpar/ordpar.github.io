tale II homograph 2

verb

to speak · to argue, to plead (for or against)

Inflection

Etymology

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bruke stemmen til å framføre ord og setninger;
    ytre seg;
    snakke, prate    to use one's voice to utter words and sentences;
    to express oneself;
    to speak, to talk
    • tale utydeligto speak indistinctly

    argumentere for;
    forsvare    to act as an advocate for;

  4. holde tale (I, 4)a speechto give a speechtale (I, 4)
