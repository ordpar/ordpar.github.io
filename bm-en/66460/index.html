vanne, vatne

verbverb

to water

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt vatna Old Norse vatna

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. helle eller sprute vann over to water: to pour or sprinkle water on
    • vanne åkeren to water the field
    • husk å vanne blomstene remember to water the flowers
    • vi må vanne treverket før vi begynner med pussingen we must wet the woodwork before we start sanding
  2. gi vann to water (animals): to give water to drink
    • vanne hestene to water the horses

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

vanne ut
  1. legge i vann;
    bløyte     to put in water;
    to soak
    • vanne ut salt kjøtt to soak salted meat (to draw out the salt)
  2. tilsette vann for å gjøre mildere eller drøye to add water to make something milder or make it go further Eng. phraseto water down
    • de måtte vanne ut suppa for å få nok til alle they had to water down the soup to have enough for everyone
  3. få til å miste opprinnelig verdi eller innhold;
    utvanne     to make something lose its original value or substance;
    to dilute     Eng. phraseto water down
    • vanne ut begrepet to water down the concept
