vilkår

noun neuter

a condition · terms (of an agreement) · conditions, circumstances

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk; jamfør viljewill · willingness · determination og kårliving conditions · circumstancesfrom Low German; compare viljewill · willingness · determination and kårliving conditions · circumstances

    circumstancesforhold (1), a basisgrunnlag, living conditionskår (1)
