lukt I homograph 1

noun masculine or feminine

smell · odour · sense of smell

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt lukt, fra lavtysk, opprinnelig ‘luftstrøm’; samme opprinnelse som luftair og loftattic, loft · ceiling · a traditional two-storey log storehouseOld Norse lukt, from Low German, originally 'a current of air'; same origin as luftair and loftattic, loft · ceiling · a traditional two-storey log storehouse
