savne, sakne

verb

to miss · to lack, to be without

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt sakna; beslektet med sake (III)to sue, to accuse, to chargeOld Norse sakna; related to sake (III)to sue, to accuse, to charge

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. være uten;
    mangle    to be without;
    to lack
    • ikke savne noeto lack for nothing
    • vi savner et teater her i byenwe have no theatre here in town
    • jeg har lenge savnet noen ordentlig gode skoI have gone without a really good pair of shoes for a long time
  2. oppdage at en har mistet noe, at noe er borteto discover that you have lost something, that something is gone
    • savne lommeboka sito find your wallet missing
    • to mann er savnet i fjellettwo men are missing in the mountains
    brukt som substantiv:used as a noun:
    • de savnede er funnet i god beholdthe missing have been found safe and well
  3. kjenne (smertelig) lengsel etter noe(n)to feel a (painful) longing for someone or something
    • savne selskapto long for company
    • være dypt savnetto be deeply missed
