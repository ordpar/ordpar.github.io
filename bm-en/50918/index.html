senke

verbverb

to lower · to reduce, to cut · to sink (a ship)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt søkkva Old Norse søkkva

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. få til å synke;
    jamfør søkke (III)to sink     to cause to sink, to sink;
    compare søkke (III)to sink
    • senke en båt to sink a boat
  2. fire ned to let down, to lower
    • de senket kisten i graven they lowered the coffin into the grave
  3. bøye (III, 1)to bend eller rette nedover to bendbøye (III, 1) or point downwards
    • han senket hodet i respekt he bowed his head in respect
    • kommentaren fikk henne til å senke blikket the remark made her lower her gaze
  4. sette ned;
    gjøre lavere     to reduce;
    to make lower
    • butikken senker prisene the shop is cutting its prices
    • de måtte senke kravene til eksamen they had to lower the requirements for the exam
    • han senket stemmen da de kom inn i kirken he lowered his voice as they entered the church

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

senke seg
  1. bevege seg nedover to move downwards
    • brystkassen hevet og senket seg med pusten his chest rose and fell as he breathed
  2. bre seg utover to spread out Eng. phraseto descend
    • mørket senket seg darkness fell
    • stillheten senket seg over byen silence descended on the town
senke skuldrene slappe av to relax Eng. phraseto unwind
  • da eksamen var unnagjort, kunne studentene endelig senke skuldrene with the exam out of the way, the students could finally unwind
