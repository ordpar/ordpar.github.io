bore

verb

to drill · to bore · to drive or press (something) in

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt boraOld Norse bora

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. lage eller utvide et hull med bor eller lignendeto make or widen a hole with a drill or the like
    • bore i tre, metallto drill into wood, metal
    • bore hull i noeto drill a hole in something
    • bore en brønnto drill a well
    • bore en tunnelto bore a tunnel
    • motoren er boretthe engine has been bored out
    • bore slitte sylindrerto rebore worn cylinders
    • bore etter oljeto drill for oil
    • har boret etter gasshas drilled for gas
    brukt som adjektiv, i overført betydning:used as an adjective, figuratively:
    • et borende spørsmåla probing question
  2. presse, klemme mens en vrirto press or squeeze something while twisting it
    • bore sverdet i brystet på fiendento drive the sword into the enemy's chest
    • bore hodet ned i putato bury one's head in the pillow

Set phrases

  • kula boret seg innthe bullet bored its way in
