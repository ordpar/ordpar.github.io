skjenke

verb

to pour, to serve (drink) · to give, to bestow, to present

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt skenkjaOld Norse skenkja

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. helle opp i kopp eller glassto pour into a cup or glass
    • skjenke i teto pour tea
    • hun skjenket kaffeshe poured the coffee
  2. servere alkoholholdig drikkto serve alcoholic drinks
    • ha tillatelse til å skjenke alkoholto have a licence to serve alcohol
    • skjenke noen fullto get someone drunk
    • jeg ble skjenket to stive drammerI was served two stiff drinks
  3. forære, gito present as a gift, to give
    • skjenke noen en gaveto give someone a gift
  4. ofre (4)to devoteto devoteofre (4)
    • ikke skjenke det en tankenot to give it a thought
