svirre

verb
Inflection

Etymology

fra tysk; beslektet med surre (I)to buzz, to hum · to muddle, to get things mixed up · to fry gentlyfrom German; related to surre (I)to buzz, to hum · to muddle, to get things mixed up · to fry gently

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. fare med hvinende eller surrende lydto move with a whistling or whirring sound
    • piler og spyd svirret gjennom luftaarrows and spears whizzed through the air
  2. fare hit og dit;
    flagre urolig    to dart to and fro;
    to flutter restlessly
    • barna svirrer rundt i alle retningerthe children are dashing about in all directions
    • nattsvermerne svirret rundt utelampathe moths fluttered around the outdoor lamp
  3. virvle (1)to whirl;
    rotere (1)to rotate    to whirlvirvle (1);
    to rotaterotere (1)
    • det store hjulet svirrer rundtthe big wheel spins round
  4. om utsagn, tanker eller lignende: være i raskt (og kaotisk) omløpof statements, thoughts or the like: to be in rapid (and chaotic) circulation
    • det svirret med rykter i bygdarumours were flying around the village
    • påstander svirret om øreneclaims were flying about his ears
    • spørsmålene svirrer i luftathe questions are buzzing in the air
    • tanker som svirrer rundt i hodetthoughts whirling round in one’s head
