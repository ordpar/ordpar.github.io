rygge

verb
Inflection

Etymology

av ryggback · ridge, crest · spine (of a book)from ryggback · ridge, crest · spine (of a book)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bevege seg baklengs;
    bakke (IV, 2)to back up    to move backwards;
    to back upbakke (IV, 2)
    • han rygget da han så den sinte hundenhe backed away when he saw the angry dog
  2. få til å gå eller kjøre baklengsto make something go or drive backwards
    • han rygget bilen inn i garasjenhe reversed the car into the garage

Set phrases

These set phrases have not been translated yet. They are shown as Bokmålsordboka has them, in Norwegian.
