annonsere

verb

to announce · to advertise

Inflection

Etymology

gjennom fransk; fra latin annuntiare, av ad ‘til’ og nuntius ‘sendebud’through French; from Latin annuntiare, from ad ‘to’ and nuntius ‘messenger’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre kjent;
    kunngjøreto announce, to make public, to make known    to make known;
    to announcekunngjøre
    • annonsere deltakerneto announce the participants
    • annonsere på en pressekonferanseto make an announcement at a press conference
    • hun annonserte at hun stilte til gjenvalgshe announced that she was standing for re-election
  2. lyse ut;
    avertereto place an advertisement    to advertise publicly;
    to place an advertisementavertere
    • annonsere i lokalavisento advertise in the local paper
    • annonsere på internettto advertise on the internet
    • annonsere boligen til salgsto advertise the property for sale
    • annonsere etter faglært arbeidskraftto advertise for skilled labour
