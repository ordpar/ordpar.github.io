springe

verb

to run · to leap, to fly · to burst

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt springaOld Norse springa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bevege seg raskt framover på føttene;
    løpe (1)to run    to move quickly forward on one's feet;
    to runløpe (1)
    • springe om kappto race
    • springe etter noento run after someone
    • springe opp bakkeneto run up the hills
    • han springer fra de andrehe's outrunning the others
    • hun sprang så fort hun kunneshe ran as fast as she could
    • de hadde sprunget langt innen de stoppetthey had run a long way before they stopped
    • spring for livet!run for your life!
  2. endre eller flytte seg raskt;
    hoppe, sprette, fyke    to change or move quickly;
    to jump, to spring, to fly
    • springe ut av bilento leap out of the car
    • springe over noeto skip over something
    • prisene sprang i væretprices shot up
    • øynene sprang nesten ut av hodet på demtheir eyes nearly popped out of their heads
    • døra sprang oppthe door flew open
  3. brukt som adjektiv: usammenhengendeused as an adjective: disjointed
    • et springende foredraga disjointed lecture
  4. åpne seg, folde seg utto open, to unfold
    • springe ut i full blomstto burst into full bloom
  5. eksplodere, sprekketo explode, to burst
    • brua sprang i luftathe bridge was blown up
    • båten sprang lekkthe boat sprang a leak
    • et blodkar har sprungeta blood vessel has burst
