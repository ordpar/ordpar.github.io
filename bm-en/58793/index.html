svinne

verb

to fade away, to dwindle, to vanish

Inflection

Etymology

fra tyskfrom German

Senses and Example Sentences

bli borteto fade away
  • det siste lysglimtet svantthe last glimmer of light died away
  • alt håp var svunnetall hope had gone
brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
  • ha interesse for svunne tiderto have an interest in times gone by
  • minner fra svunnen storhetmemories of vanished grandeur

Set phrases

svunnen tid gamle dager;
fjern fortid;
forgangenbygone, past, former tidthe old days;
the distant past;
bygoneforgangen times Eng. phrasedays gone by
  • minner fra en svunnen tidmemories from a bygone era
  • de drømmer om svunne tiderthey dream of times gone by
