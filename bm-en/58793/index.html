svinne

verbverb

to fade away, to dwindle, to vanish

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra tysk from German

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

bli borte to fade away
  • det siste lysglimtet svant the last glimmer of light died away
  • alt håp var svunnet all hope had gone
brukt som adjektiv: used as an adjective:
  • ha interesse for svunne tider to have an interest in times gone by
  • minner fra svunnen storhet memories of vanished grandeur

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

the distant past;
bygoneforgangen times Eng. phrasedays gone by
