sjangle

verb
Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gå ustøtt;
    vakle    to walk unsteadily;
    to stagger
    • han er så full at han sjanglerhe is so drunk that he is staggering
  2. slingre hit og ditto lurch from side to side
    • bilen sjanglerthe car is lurching
