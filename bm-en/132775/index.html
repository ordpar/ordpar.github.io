kalle opp

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi navn etter noe eller noen;
    oppkalle    to give a name taken from someone or something else;
    to name after
    • de vil gjerne kalle opp bestemoren når de får barnthey'd like to name a child after the grandmother when they have one
    • skolen er kalt opp etter forfatterenthe school is named after the author
  2. anropeto call, to hail, to call out toto call, to hailanrope
    • trafikksentralen prøvde å kalle opp skipetthe traffic control centre tried to call the ship
    • de ble kalt opp over radiothey were called over the radio
