innvie

verb

to inaugurate, to open · to let someone in on, to initiate into · to consecrate, to dedicate

Inflection

Etymology

etter tysk einweihenafter German einweihen

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. vie (1)to consecrate, hellige (3)to dedicateto consecratevie (1), to dedicatehellige (3)
    • han ble innviet til presthe was ordained a priest
  2. markere ved seremoni eller fest at noe er ferdig og skal tas i brukto mark with a ceremony or celebration that something is finished and about to be put to use
    • innvie en ny skoleto open a new school
    • lørdag skal vi innvie vår nye leilighet med en skikkelig feston Saturday we're going to christen our new flat with a proper party
  3. gjøre delaktig i;
    fortelle om    to let someone in on;
    to tell someone about
    • jeg innviet ham i planeneI let him in on the plans
