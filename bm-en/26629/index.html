innrullere

verb

to enrol, to enlist

Inflection

Etymology

etter fransk enrôlerafter French enrôler

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. føre noen inn i militær utskrivningslisteto enter someone onto a military conscription list
    • bli innrullert i Marinento be enlisted in the Navy
  2. ta opp som medlem eller deltakerto admit as a member or participant
    • la seg innrullere i det norske samfunnetto let oneself be enrolled in Norwegian society
