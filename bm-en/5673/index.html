betro

verbverb

to entrust · to confide

InflectionVis bøyning

PronunciationUttale

betroˊ

EtymologyOpphav

fra lavtysk from Low German

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. overlate ansvar for noe to entrust with responsibility for something
    • bli betrodd vervet som kasserer to be entrusted with the office of treasurer
  2. meddele i fortrolighet to tell in confidence
    • han betrodde henne sine innerste tanker he confided his innermost thoughts to her

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk

betro seg til snakke ut i fortrolighet to speak openly in confidence Eng. phraseto confide in someone
  • hun betrodde seg til meg she confided in me
