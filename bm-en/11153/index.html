dumpe I homograph 1

verb

to fail (an exam) · to dump, to drop · to flop down, to land heavily

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt dumpa ‘slå, støte’Old Norse dumpa 'to hit, to bump'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. falle tungt, deiseto fall heavily, to flop down
    • dumpe ned på en stolto flop down into a chair
  2. bli involvert i;
    komme eller treffe tilfeldig    to get involved in;
    to come across or run into by chance
    • jeg dumpet midt opp i et selskapI landed right in the middle of a party
    • han dumper borti mye rarthe stumbles across all sorts of odd things
  3. stryke til eksamento fail an exam
    • dumpe for andre gangto fail for the second time
  4. slippeto let go of, to drop · to be spared, not to have to · to get free, to get away, kaste (II)to throw · to throw awayto dropslippe, to throwkaste (II)
    • dumpe søppelto dump rubbish
    • kannene ble dumpet i sjøenthe cans were dumped in the sea
  5. vippe på en planke;
    disse (III, 3)to swing, huske (II)to rock, to bounce · to bob up and down, to pitch    to seesaw on a plank;
    to swingdisse (III, 3), to rockhuske (II)
