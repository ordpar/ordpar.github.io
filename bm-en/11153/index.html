dumpe I homograph 1

verbverb

to fail (an exam) · to dump, to drop · to flop down, to land heavily

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt dumpa ‘slå, støte’ Old Norse dumpa 'to hit, to bump'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. falle tungt, deise to fall heavily, to flop down
    • dumpe ned på en stol to flop down into a chair
  2. bli involvert i;
    komme eller treffe tilfeldig     to get involved in;
    to come across or run into by chance
    • jeg dumpet midt opp i et selskap I landed right in the middle of a party
    • han dumper borti mye rart he stumbles across all sorts of odd things
  3. stryke til eksamen to fail an exam
    • dumpe for andre gang to fail for the second time
  4. slippeto let go of, to drop · to be spared, not to have to · to get free, to get away, kaste (II)to throw · to throw away to dropslippe, to throwkaste (II)
    • dumpe søppel to dump rubbish
    • kannene ble dumpet i sjøen the cans were dumped in the sea
  5. vippe på en planke;
    disse (III, 3)to swing, huske (II)to rock, to bounce · to bob up and down, to pitch     to seesaw on a plank;
    to swingdisse (III, 3), to rockhuske (II)
