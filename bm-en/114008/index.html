i flukt

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: fluktflight, escape · an escape (from one's troubles), escapism · flight (through the air)
  1. i samsvar in accordance Eng. phrasein keeping with
    • handle i flukt med naturens premisser to act in keeping with nature's terms
  2. i samme retning eller høyde in the same direction or at the same height Eng. phrasein line with
    • boligfeltet ligger i flukt med en slette the housing estate lies level with a plain
    • åpne en pølsebod i flukt med butikken to open a hot-dog stand right alongside the shop
