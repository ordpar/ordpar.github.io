rydde vei

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. legge til rette for noen eller noe;
    gjøre klart for noen eller noe    to prepare the ground for someone or something;
    to make ready for someone or something     Eng. phraseto pave the way
    • avtalen skal rydde vei for en ny demokratisk valgt regjeringthe agreement is meant to pave the way for a new democratically elected government
    • loven har ryddet vei for nye satsingerthe law has paved the way for new initiatives
  2. lage løype eller rom slik at noen eller noe kan flytte segto make a track or space so that someone or something can move through Eng. phraseto clear a path
    • politiet måtte rydde vei gjennom menneskemengdenthe police had to clear a path through the crowd
    • han brukte machete for å rydde vei gjennom jungelenhe used a machete to cut a path through the jungle
  3. bryte opp og planere jord eller lignende for å lage veito break up and level ground or the like in order to make a road
