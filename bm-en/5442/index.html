beseire

verb

to defeat, to beat, to overcome

Inflection

Etymology

fra tysk; av seireto win, to be victorious, to triumph · to prevail, to gain the upper handfrom German; from seireto win, to be victorious, to triumph · to prevail, to gain the upper hand

Senses and Example Sentences

overvinne, seire overto overcome, to triumph over
  • beseire sine fienderto defeat one's enemies
  • beseire sine konkurrenterto beat one's competitors
  • beseire alle vanskeligheterto surmount all difficulties
