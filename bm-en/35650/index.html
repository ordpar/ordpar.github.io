-log

noun masculine
Inflection

Etymology

fra gresk, av logos ‘ord, tale, fornuft’from Greek, from logos ‘word, speech, reason’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. suffiksa suffix i betegnelse for person som har (vitenskapelig) utdanning i et bestemt fag;
    jamfør -logi-logy;
    i ord som antropologanthropologist, psykologa psychologist · a good judge of character og teologtheologian    a suffixsuffiks in the designation of a person who has (academic) training in a particular subject;
    compare -logi-logy;
    in words such as antropologanthropologist, psykologa psychologist · a good judge of character and teologtheologian
  2. suffiksa suffix i betegnelse for en bestemt type tale, samtale eller skriftstykke;
    i ord som dialogdialogue, epilogepilogue og nekrologobituary    a suffixsuffiks in the designation of a particular kind of speech, conversation or piece of writing;
    in words such as dialogdialogue, epilogepilogue and nekrologobituary
