beint fram

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: beinstraight · direct, framforward · forth, out (into view) · all the way, to the destination
  1. nøyaktig slik noe er framstilt exactly as something is presented
    • teksten er lett og beint fram the text is easy and straightforward
  2. uten å vike av;
    uten omsvøp;
    beintfram (3)straight out     without deviating;
    without mincing words;
    straight outbeintfram (3)
    • en kunstner som går beint fram an artist who takes a direct approach
    • snakke beint fram to talk straight
  3. rett og slett;
    rent, helt     simply, downright;
    purely, utterly
    • beint fram umulig downright impossible
    • de beint fram kjeder seg they're simply bored
