du

pronounpronomen

you (singular)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt þú Old Norse þú

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. 2. person entall, i tiltale second person singular, used in direct address
    • hallo, er det du (eller deg)? hello, is that you?
    • kan ikke du hjelpe meg litt? can't you help me out a bit?
    • jeg er like stor som, større enn du (eller deg) I'm as big as, bigger than you
    • hvis jeg var deg, ville jeg ha ringt først if I were you, I would have called first
    • dette ligner ikke deg this isn't like you
    • si du til hverandre – og ikke De to say du to each other – and not De 'you (polite)'
    i bønn: in prayer:
    • du Hellige Ånd, min trøster you Holy Spirit, my comforter
  2. i utrop: in exclamations:
    • du store min! good heavens!
    • du, å du ! my, oh my!
    • kjære, snille deg! oh please, I beg you!
    • du skal akte deg! you'd better watch out!
  3. en (I)one (generic) · you, people (in general) · someone, man (II)one · you (in general) · people you (in general)en (I), oneman (II)
    • du skal ikke stjele thou shalt not steal
