du

pronoun

you (singular)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt þúOld Norse þú

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. 2. person entall, i tiltalesecond person singular, used in direct address
    • hallo, er det du (eller deg)?hello, is that you?
    • kan ikke du hjelpe meg litt?can't you help me out a bit?
    • jeg er like stor som, større enn du (eller deg)I'm as big as, bigger than you
    • hvis jeg var deg, ville jeg ha ringt førstif I were you, I would have called first
    • dette ligner ikke degthis isn't like you
    • si du til hverandre – og ikke Deto say du to each other – and not De 'you (polite)'
    i bønn:in prayer:
    • du Hellige Ånd, min trøsteryou Holy Spirit, my comforter
  2. i utrop:in exclamations:
    • du store min!good heavens!
    • du, å du !my, oh my!
    • kjære, snille deg!oh please, I beg you!
    • du skal akte deg!you'd better watch out!
  3. en (I)one (generic) · you, people (in general) · someone, man (II)one · you (in general) · peopleyou (in general)en (I), oneman (II)
    • du skal ikke stjelethou shalt not steal
