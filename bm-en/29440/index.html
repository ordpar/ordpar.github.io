kjeft

noun masculine

a telling-off · gob · jaws · mouth

    trut    the mouth of an animal or a person;
    a gob

  2. munn som taleorgan;
    munn (2)the mouth    a mouth as an organ of speech;

    vid åpning;
    gap    the front part of a digging or gripping tool;
