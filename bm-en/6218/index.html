bla

verb

to leaf through · to turn the pages

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

snu bladene (i en bok eller lignende)to turn the pages (of a book or the like)

  • bla fort igjennom avisento flick quickly through the newspaper
  • bla om til neste sideto turn to the next page
