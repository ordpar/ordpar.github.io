“gjeld” is an inflected form of:

  • gjelde I homograph 1 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to be valid, to apply · to be about, to concern · to count, to matter være gyldig
  • gjelde II homograph 2 verbverb (imperative)(imperativ) to castrate, to geld fjerne eller ødelegge kjønnskjertlene hos hanndyr
