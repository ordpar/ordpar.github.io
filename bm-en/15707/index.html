flå V homograph 5

verb

to skin, to flay · to peel, to strip (bark) · to fleece, to bleed dry

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt flá; beslektet med flekke (I)to split, to strip off og flenge (II)to gash, to tearOld Norse flá; related to flekke (I)to split, to strip off and flenge (II)to gash, to tear

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ta huden avto take the skin off
    • flå en kalvto skin a calf
  2. rive bark avto strip bark off
    • flå en seljekvistto peel a willow twig
  3. plyndreto plunder · to loot · to raid, utsugeto bleed dryto plunderplyndre, to bleed dryutsuge
    • flå en til skinnetto fleece someone completely
