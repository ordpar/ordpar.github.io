ta slutt

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: sluttend · ending · the last part
  1. være over eller forbi;
    opphøre;
    stanse     to be over or past;
    to cease;
    to stop     Eng. phraseto come to an end
    • krigen tok slutt the war ended
    • streiken kan ta slutt i morgen the strike may be over tomorrow
  2. gå tom to be used up Eng. phraseto run out
    • pengene tok slutt the money ran out
    • oljen tar slutt the oil is running out
    • kreftene tok slutt his strength gave out
