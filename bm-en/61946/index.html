Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gangart der motsatt forbein og bakbein løftes samtidig fra bakken;
    til forskjell fra galoppa gallop · a galop (the dance or its tune) og skrittganga walking pace     a gait in which the opposite foreleg and hind leg are lifted from the ground at the same time;
    as opposed to a gallopgalopp and a walking paceskrittgang
    • ri i trav to ride at a trot
    • en hest i fullt trav a horse at full trot
  2. i overført betydning: løprunning · race · course, springing figuratively: a runløp, a dash
    • barna satte i trav nedover veien the children set off at a trot down the road

urent trav
  1. brudd på regelen om at hesten skal gå i trav (I, 1)a trot i travløp a breach of the rule that the horse must keep to a trottrav (I, 1) in a trotting race Eng. phrasea break in stride
    • bli diskvalifisert for urent trav to be disqualified for breaking gait
  2. uredelig virksomhet;
    ureglementert eller skittent spill     dishonest dealings;
    irregular or dirty play     Eng. phrasefoul play
    • ha mistanke om urent trav i saken to suspect foul play in the case
