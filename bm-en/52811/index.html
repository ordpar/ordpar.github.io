skinne II homograph 2

verb

to shine

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt skínaOld Norse skína

Senses and Example Sentences

lyse, stråle;
jamfør skinnendeshiningto shine, to beam;
compare skinnendeshining
  • sola skinnerthe sun is shining
  • øynene hennes skinteher eyes shone
  • snøfjella skinte i solathe snowy mountains gleamed in the sun

Set phrases

skinne gjennom
  1. vise seg, synes gjennom noe som dekkerto appear, to be visible through something covering it Eng. phraseto show through
    • trykket skinner gjennom papiretthe print shows through the paper
  2. være mulig å merke;
    være klart    to be noticeable;
    to be evident     Eng. phraseto come through
    • det skinte gjennom at hun var skuffetyou could tell she was disappointed
