sjelden II homograph 2

adverbadverb

rarely · seldom · exceptionally

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt sjaldan Old Norse sjaldan

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. ikke ofte;
    få ganger     not often;
    on few occasions
    • jeg ser henne svært sjelden I very rarely see her
    • han klipper plenen sjeldnere he mows the lawn less often
    • den musikksjanger jeg sjeldnest hører på the music genre I listen to least often
  2. brukt forsterkende (med eller uten -t): enestående, uvanlig used as an intensifier (with or without -t): exceptionally, unusually
    • hun var en sjelden pen dame she was an exceptionally pretty woman
    • dette var sjelden god mat this was unusually good food

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk
