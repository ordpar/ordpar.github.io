lage seg

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ordne seg;
    gå godt    to sort itself out;
    to go well
    • det har laget seg for hennethings have worked out for her
  2. være i ferd med å blito be in the process of becoming Eng. phraseto shape up to be
    • det laget seg til å bli en folkefestit was shaping up to be a public celebration
  3. gjøre seg ferdig;
    forberede seg    to make oneself ready;
    to prepare oneself     Eng. phraseto get ready
    • de holdt på å lage seg til å reisethey were getting ready to leave
