noun masculine

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt bǫllr ‘kule, ball’Old Norse bǫllr 'sphere, ball'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. (lett) kule av gummi, plast og lignende til å leke eller spille med(light) sphere of rubber, plastic or the like, used for play or sport
    • sparke ballen langtto kick the ball a long way
    • de liker å kaste ballthey like throwing a ball around
    • ballen dunket i gulvetthe ball thudded against the floor
  2. slag, pasning, skudd i ballspillhit, pass or shot in a ball game
    • vinne de første balleneto win the first points
    • en vanskelig balla difficult ball
    • slå lange baller inn i midtento hit long balls into the middle
  3. rund klump, kuleround lump, ball
    • tulle klærne sammen i en ballto roll the clothes up into a ball
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som snøballsnowball, raspeballa potato dumpling
  4. (rund) kjøttfull del av kroppen(round) fleshy part of the body
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som tåballthe ball of the toe, seteballa buttock, a bum cheek
