stagge

verb

to curb, to check, to restrain · to soothe, to calm

Inflection

Etymology

beslektet med stadigconstantly, continually · constant, steady og stødigsteady · stable, firm · dependable, solidrelated to stadigconstantly, continually · constant, steady and stødigsteady · stable, firm · dependable, solid

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stanse, stoppe;
    hindre    to stop, to bring to a halt;
    to hold in check
    • vi må stagge den negative utviklingenwe must curb this negative trend
  2. roe ned, berolige;
    få til å stilne    to calm down, to soothe;
    to make something subside
    • stagge et skrikende barnto soothe a screaming child
    • entusiasmen hennes var ikke til å staggeher enthusiasm could not be contained

These set phrases have not been translated yet. They are shown as Bokmålsordboka has them, in Norwegian.
