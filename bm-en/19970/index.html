gjete

verb

to herd · to tend · to keep watch on

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt gætaOld Norse gæta

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. passe husdyr (på beite) ute i markato tend livestock (at pasture) out in the fields
    • gjete sauerto herd sheep
    • han gikk og gjettehe was out herding
    • hun gjette bort et lamshe lost a lamb while herding
  2. vokte, spionereto keep watch on, to spy on
    • gjete på noento keep an eye on someone
