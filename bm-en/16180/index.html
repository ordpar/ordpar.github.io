forestille

verb

to depict, portray · to introduce

Inflection

Etymology

fra tysk ‘stille fram for en’; av fore-fore-from German ‘to put before someone’; from fore-fore-

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. presentere (3)to introduceto introducepresentere (3)
    • forestille noen for en annento introduce someone to another person
  2. bety, framstille, viseto represent, to depict, to show
    • hva skal det forestille?what is that supposed to be?
    • bildet forestiller et vinterlandskapthe picture depicts a winter landscape

Set phrases

forestille seg tenke seg noe;
se for segto imagine something;
to picture something in one's mind
  • jeg har vanskelig for å forestille meg henne på en sceneI find it hard to imagine her on a stage
  • du kan vel forestille deg reaksjonenyou can imagine the reaction
