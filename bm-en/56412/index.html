verb
Inflection

Etymology

av nederlandsk spuiten, omdannet etter spruteto spurt, to gush · to splash, to spray, to squirt · to fly up (of gravel, snow, sparks)from Dutch spuiten, reshaped after spruteto spurt, to gush · to splash, to spray, to squirt · to fly up (of gravel, snow, sparks)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sende ut væske i strålerto send out liquid in jets

    • han sprøytet maling på låvenhe sprayed paint on the barn
    • vi har sprøytet frukttrærne mot skadedyrwe have sprayed the fruit trees against pests
