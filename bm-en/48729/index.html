ro IV homograph 4

verb

to row · to backpedal, to talk one's way out of something

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt róaOld Norse róa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. drive fram et fartøy til vanns med årer;
    skysse eller frakte i robåt    to propel a vessel through the water with oars;
    to ferry or carry by rowing boat
    • ro en båtto row a boat
    • ro over fjordento row across the fjord
    • ro turister over vannetto row tourists across the water
  2. drive fiske med robåt eller annen fiskebåtto fish from a rowing boat or other fishing boat
    • han ror i Stamsundhe fishes out of Stamsund
  3. gjøre bevegelser som ligner på det å ro (IV, 1)rowingto make movements resembling rowingro (IV, 1)
    • endene rodde ute på sundetthe ducks were paddling out on the sound
    • en ørn rodde gjennom luftaan eagle was beating its way through the air
  4. prøve å snakke seg bort fra et ubehagelig tema;
    bortforklare    to try to talk one's way out of an uncomfortable subject;
    to explain away
    • politikerne rodde for harde livetthe politicians backpedalled for all they were worth
