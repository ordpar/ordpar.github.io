opprør

noun neuter

rebellion · revolt · uproar

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk rør; beslektet med røre (II)mess · stir · commotionfrom Low German rør; related to røre (II)mess · stir · commotion

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. organisert motstand, ofte voldelig, mot styresmakteneorganised resistance, often violent, against the authorities
    • folket gjorde opprør mot juntaenthe people rose up against the junta
    • det var et væpnet opprørit was an armed rebellion
  2. protest mot autoritet eller norma rebellion against authority or convention
    • punken var et opprør mot de konservativepunk was a revolt against the conservatives
    • ungdommens opprør mot foreldrenethe rebellion of the young against their parents
  3. sterk reaksjon eller sinnsstemning;
    uro    a strong reaction or state of mind;
    agitation
    • salen var i fullt opprørthe hall was in an uproar
    • nyheten satte sinnet hennes i sterkt opprørthe news threw her mind into turmoil
