spikeren i kista

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

handling eller hendelse som avgjør eller avslutter noe action or event that decides or puts an end to something see:Se: spikernail Eng. phrasethe nail in the coffin
  • sette spikeren i kista for prosjektet to hammer the final nail into the project's coffin
  • 2-0 ble spikeren i kista for laget 2-0 was the final nail in the coffin for the team
