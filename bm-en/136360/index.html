ta telling

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bli slått i bakken så hardt at en blir liggende;
    jamfør telling (2)the count (in boxing)    to be knocked to the ground so hard that one stays down;
    compare telling (2)the count (in boxing)     Eng. phraseto take the count
    • spilleren gikk rett i isen og måtte ta tellingthe player went straight down on the ice and had to take the count
  2. ta en liten pauseto take a short break Eng. phraseto take a breather
    • spilleren måtte ta telling på benken etter taklingenthe player had to take a breather on the bench after the tackle
