kjenne II homograph 2

verb

to know (be acquainted with) · to feel · to recognise

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt kenna; beslektet med kunnecan, to be able to · to know (a skill or language) · may, mightOld Norse kenna; related to kunnecan, to be able to · to know (a skill or language) · may, might
