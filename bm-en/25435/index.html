håndlanger, handlanger

noun masculine
Inflection

Etymology

etter tysk Handlanger; jamfør lange (II)to stride · to pass, to hand along · to deal (in drink or drugs)after German Handlanger; compare lange (II)to stride · to pass, to hand along · to deal (in drink or drugs)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. person som hjelper en håndverker med arbeideta person who helps a craftsman with the work
  2. (underordnet) person som blindt adlyder og utfører alt han eller hun blir bedt om;
    redskap (3)a tool;
    lakei (2)a lackey    a (subordinate) person who blindly obeys and carries out everything he or she is told to do;
    a toolredskap (3);
    a lackeylakei (2)
