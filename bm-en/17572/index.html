framstille, fremstille

verb

to depict, to portray · to produce, to manufacture

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. føre framto bring forward
    • framstille for rettento bring someone before the court
  2. gjengi kunstnerisk, gi et bilde av, skildre, illustrere;
    uttrykke (1)to express    to render artistically, to give a picture of, to depict, to illustrate;
