bite

verb

to bite

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt bítaOld Norse bíta

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. sette tennene ito sink one's teeth into
    • bite neglerto bite one's nails
    • hunden bet megthe dog bit me
    • bli bitt av myggto be bitten by mosquitoes
  2. lage (noe) eller nå et resultat med å bite (1)bitingto make (something) or achieve a result by bitingbite (1)
    • bite hull i noeto bite a hole in something
  3. om fisk: ta agnetof fish: to take the bait
    • fisken biterthe fish are biting
  4. om skarp redskap: ha evne til å skjære eller hoggeof a sharp tool: to be capable of cutting or chopping
    • ljåen biter godtthe scythe cuts well
  5. forårsake smerte;
    trenge inn    to cause pain;
    to penetrate
    • kulda biterthe cold bites
