innfri

verb

to fulfil, to meet · to keep (a promise) · to repay, to redeem

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør fri (III)to free, to deliver (from) · to sparecompare fri (III)to free, to deliver (from) · to spare

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. betale tilbake, innløseto pay back, to redeem
    • lånet må innfris innen fem årthe loan must be repaid within five years
  2. oppfylleto fulfil · to meet (a requirement) · to keep (a promise)to fulfil; to meet (a requirement)oppfylle
    • innfri våre krav!meet our demands!
    • innfri et løfteto keep a promise
    • forventningene ble ikke innfriddexpectations were not met
