helle II homograph 2

verbverb

to pour · to slope · to lean, to tilt

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt hella, av hallr ‘skrå, hellende’ Old Norse hella, from hallr 'sloping, slanting'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. slå (II, 14)to pour, tømme (II, 1)to empty out to pourslå (II, 14), to empty outtømme (II, 1)
    • helle bensin på tanken to pour petrol into the tank
    • helle ut innholdet på flaska to pour out the contents of the bottle
    • helle i seg flere flasker øl to knock back several bottles of beer
  2. skråneto slope;
    lute (I)to lean     to slopeskråne;
    to leanlute (I)
    • terrenget heller mot sjøen the terrain slopes towards the sea
    • veggen heller utover the wall leans outwards
  3. lene (II)to lean · to rest, to prop (something) against to leanlene (II)
    • helle seg bakover to lean backwards
    • helle på hodet to tilt one's head
  4. gå mot slutten to draw to a close
    • sommeren heller summer is drawing to a close
    • dagen heller mot kveld the day is drawing towards evening
