peke

verb

to point

Inflection

Etymology

beslektet med pigg (I)a spike, a spine, a point · a peak, a sharp mountain toprelated to pigg (I)a spike, a spine, a point · a peak, a sharp mountain top

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. rette finger, stokk eller lignende mot noeto point a finger, stick or the like at something
    • den lille jenta peker på gravemaskinenthe little girl is pointing at the digger
  2. være rettet mot noeto be directed at something
    • tuppen på kvisten pekte rett nedthe tip of the twig pointed straight down
    • pila pekte oppoverthe arrow pointed upwards
