klikke for

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: klikketo click (a mouse) · to make a clicking sound · to fail, to break down
  1. miste tenkeevne to lose the ability to think straight Eng. phraseto go blank
    • det klikket for henne under eksamen her mind went blank during the exam
  2. miste forstanden to lose one's reason Eng. phraseto lose one's mind
    • det klikket for ham etter at han fikk medisiner han ikke skulle ha hatt he lost his mind after being given medication he should never have had
