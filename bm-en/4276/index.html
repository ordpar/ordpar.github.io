balle III homograph 3

verb

to ball up, to bundle together · to wrap, to swathe · to bungle, to mess up

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt ballaOld Norse balla

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. klemme noe (sammen) i en ballto squeeze something (together) into a ball
    • balle klærne sammento bundle the clothes together
  2. pakke (II)to pack · to wrap noe rundt noe annet;
    tulle (II, 1)to swathe (inn)    to wrappakke (II) something around something else;
    to swathetulle (II, 1) (in)
    • balle ullteppet godt omkring segto wrap the woollen blanket tightly around oneself
    • balle varen inn i et papirto wrap the goods in a piece of paper
  3. klusse, plundreto bungle, to fumble
    • så er det noen som baller det til likevelthen someone goes and messes it up anyway
