innsyn

noun neutersubstantiv intetkjønn

access (to information), transparency · a view in, being seen into

InflectionVis bøyning

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. det at noen kan se inn i noe the possibility of seeing into something from outside
    • boligen er skjermet mot innsyn fra veien the house is screened from view from the road
  2. det å kunne skaffe seg kunnskap eller opplysninger om noe the ability to obtain knowledge or information about something
    • myndighetene krever større innsyn i private selskapers virksomhet the authorities are demanding greater transparency in the operations of private companies
