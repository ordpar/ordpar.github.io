observere

verb

to observe · to spot

Inflection

Etymology

fra latin observare ‘iaktta, gi akt på’from Latin observare ‘to watch, to pay attention to’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. holde øye med;
    iaktta    to keep an eye on;
    to observe
    • observere et fremmed fly på radarento observe a foreign aircraft on the radar
    • vaktene sitter for det meste og observererthe guards mostly just sit and watch
    • i går morges var politiet til stede og observerte trafikken på motorveiyesterday morning the police were present, observing the traffic on the motorway
  2. legge merke til;
    registrere    to notice;
    to register
    • bli observert på åstedetto be spotted at the scene of the crime
    • de observerte elg langs veienthey spotted a moose along the road
    • naboene har observert at det sjelden er folk i husetthe neighbours have noticed that there is rarely anyone in the house
