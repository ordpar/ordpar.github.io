dundre II homograph 2

verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

trolig av lavtysk dunnern ‘tordne’ probably from Low German dunnern 'to thunder'

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. lage en buldrende lyd;
    brake (II)to crash, to boom · to crack, to crackle, buldre (I, 1)to roar     to make a rumbling noise;
    to crashbrake (II), to roarbuldre (I, 1)
    • tordenen dundret the thunder rumbled
    • toget dundret forbi the train thundered past
    • dundre på døra to pound on the door
  2. slå, støte eller sparke hardt uten at det lager sterk lyd;
    dunke (2)to thump     to strike, bump or kick hard without making a loud noise;
    to thumpdunke (2)
    • hun dundret ballen i nettet she smashed the ball into the net
    • dundre inn et skikkelig slag to land a proper blow
