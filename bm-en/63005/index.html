tusle

verb

to potter about · to shuffle, to pad along · to toddle off

Inflection

Etymology

lydordonomatopoeic

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gå tassende, subbeto pad along, to shuffle
    • det tuslet ute i bislagetthere was a shuffling out in the porch
    • det tuslet og taslet i krokenethere was pattering and rustling in the corners
  2. komme eller gå på en beskjeden måteto come or go in an unassuming way
    • tusle av gårdeto toddle off
    • tusle seg inn igjento slip back inside again
  3. bevege seg stillferdig, pusleto move about quietly, to potter
    • gå og tusle for seg selvto potter about by oneself
