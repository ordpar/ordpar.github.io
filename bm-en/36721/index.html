male II homograph 2

verb

to paint

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt mála; fra lavtysk malenOld Norse mála; from Low German malen

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. dekke med malingto cover with paint
    • male husetto paint the house
    • male stolen hvitto paint the chair white
    • vi malte stua i fjorwe painted the living room last year
    • naboen maler gjerdet grøntthe neighbour is painting the fence green
    • nå har jeg malt det første strøketnow I've done the first coat
  2. lage et malerito make a painting
    • male landskapsbilderto paint landscapes
    • Tidemand har malt ‘Haugianerne’Tidemand painted ‘The Haugians’
  3. i overført betydning: framstille, uttrykkefiguratively: to portray, to express
    • overraskelsen stod malt i ansiktet hennesthe surprise was written all over her face
    • han maler situasjonen temmelig svarthe paints a rather bleak picture of the situation
