malapropos

adverb

Etymology

fransk mal à propos, av mal ‘dårlig’ og à propos ‘til saken’; jamfør apropos (II)speaking of · at just the right moment, aproposFrench mal à propos, from mal ‘bad’ and à propos ‘to the point’; compare apropos (II)speaking of · at just the right moment, apropos

Senses and Example Sentences

upassende, på uheldig tid eller sted, ubeleiliginconvenientlyinappropriately, at an unfortunate time or place, inconvenientlyubeleilig
  • bemerkningen hans var svært malaproposhis remark was very ill-timed
