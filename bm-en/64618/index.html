unnskylde

verb

to excuse · to apologise for

Inflection

Etymology

etter lavtysk entschulden ‘frita for skyld’after Low German entschulden ‘to free from guilt’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. be om unnskyldning for;
    beklageto apologise · to regret · to be sorry about;
    jamfør unnskyldsorry, excuse me    to apologise for;
    to regretbeklage;
    compare unnskyldsorry, excuse me
    • unnskylde sin glemsomhetto apologise for one's forgetfulness
    • hun unnskyldte seg fordi hun kom så sentshe apologised for arriving so late
  2. forklare eller angi årsak (til noe negativt);
    rettferdiggjøre (2)to justify    to explain or give a reason for (something negative);
    to justifyrettferdiggjøre (2)
    • uheldig oppvekstmiljø kan unnskylde myean unfortunate upbringing can excuse a lot
