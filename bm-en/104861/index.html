si opp

to hand in one's notice, to quit (a job) · to lay off, to dismiss (an employee) · to cancel, to terminate (a subscription, a contract)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. avskjedige en arbeidstakerto dismiss an employee Eng. phraseto give someone notice
    • bedriften måtte si opp flere ansattethe company had to lay off several employees
  2. gi beskjed om at en avslutter et arbeidsforholdto give notice that one is leaving one's job Eng. phraseto hand in one's notice
    • jeg har sagt opp jobben minI've quit my job
  3. avslutte eller avbestille et abonnementsubscription, en kontrakt eller lignendeto end or cancel a subscriptionabonnement, a contract or the like
    • han sa opp avisenhe cancelled his newspaper subscription
