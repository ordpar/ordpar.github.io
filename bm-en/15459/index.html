flomme, flømme

verb
Inflection

Etymology

av floma floodfrom floma flood

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ha stor vannføring;
    lage flom (1)a flood    to carry a large volume of water;
    to cause a floodflom (1)
  2. i overført betydning: strømme, velle;
    renne rikelig    figuratively: to stream, to gush;
    to flow abundantly
    • tonene flommer ut fra radioenthe music comes pouring out of the radio
    • søknadene flommet innthe applications came flooding in
    • tårene flommer nedover kinnenethe tears are streaming down her cheeks
    • nye ideer som flommer over landetnew ideas flooding across the country
    brukt som adjektivused as an adjective
    • flommende sollysstreaming sunlight
