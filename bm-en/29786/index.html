kjøp

noun neuter

purchase · a deal · a buy

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt kaup; se kaupa bargain, a good buy, a good dealOld Norse kaup; see kaupa bargain, a good buy, a good deal

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å skaffe seg noe mot betalingthe act of obtaining something in return for payment
    • drive kjøp og salgto buy and sell
  2. avtale om handelan agreement to trade
    • de gikk fra kjøpetthey backed out of the deal
  3. noe som er kjøptsomething that has been bought
    • vise fram sitt siste kjøpto show off one's latest purchase
