befinne seg

to be, to be located · to find oneself (in a situation) · to feel, to be at ease

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. oppholde seg;
    være (på et sted eller i en situasjon)    to stay;
    to be (in a place or a situation)
    • alle som befant seg i huset, ble reddeteveryone who was in the house was rescued
    • blant passasjerene befant det seg en legethere was a doctor among the passengers
    • befinne seg i en vanskelig situasjonto be in a difficult situation
  2. føle seg;
    trives    to feel;
    to be at ease
    • befinne seg brato feel well
    • hun befant seg ikke vel i den rollenshe did not feel comfortable in that role
