at I homograph 1

subjunction

that

Etymology

norrønt at, trolig av þat ‘det’Old Norse at, probably from þat 'that'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. innleder en leddsetning som gir en indirekte framstilling tilsvarende en fortellende hovedsetningintroduces a subordinate clause giving an indirect account corresponding to a declarative main clause
    • hun sier at det snørshe says that it's snowing
    • mange mener at levestandarden er høy nokmany think that the standard of living is high enough
    • presidenten sa at alle måtte gjøre sitt for landetthe president said that everyone had to do their part for the country
    • han beklager seg over at prisene er så høyehe complains that the prices are so high
  2. innleder en beskrivelse eller forklaring av ordet eller frasen som står rett førintroduces a description or explanation of the word or phrase immediately preceding
    • det at hun kom for sent, var veldig irriterendethe fact that she arrived late was very annoying
    • det faktum at de avdekket en systemsvikt, var svært graverendethe fact that they uncovered a systemic failure was very serious
    • politikken var basert på premisset at oljeprisen bare skulle økethe policy was based on the premise that the oil price would only rise
  3. innleder en leddsetning hvor ‘det’ er formelt subjekt i oversetningen (2)the superordinate clauseintroduces a subordinate clause where det 'it' is the formal subject in the superordinate clauseoversetningen (2)
    • det er trist at hun ikke kan komme i morgenit's sad that she can't come tomorrow
    • det viste seg at løven hadde rømtit turned out that the lion had escaped
  4. innleder en sammenligningssetning etter enn (I)than eller somwho, which, that · as · likeintroduces a comparative clause after thanenn (I) or assom
    • laget er ikke bedre enn at vi kan slå demthe team isn't so good that we can't beat them
    • det er bedre at folk sykler, enn at de kjører bilit's better for people to cycle than to drive a car
    • de oppfattet det som at forelesningen var sluttthey took it to mean that the lecture was over
  5. innleder en leddsetning som uttrykker følge;
    så (IV, 2)so that    introduces a subordinate clause expressing consequence;
    so thatså (IV, 2)
    • hun ble så glad at hun dansetshe was so happy that she danced
    • det gikk så fort at det kilte i magenit went so fast that my stomach lurched
    • de ble så forsinket at de ikke nådde flyetthey were so delayed that they missed the plane
  6. innleder en leddsetning som uttrykker følge, måte, hensikt eller årsak, ofte sammen med en annen subjunksjon eller preposisjon;
    jamfør etter atafter, for atso that · in order that · that, because, forutsatt atprovided that · assuming that · on condition that, slik atso that · with the result that, sånn atso that · with the result that og uten atwithout (someone) doing something · unless    introduces a subordinate clause expressing consequence, manner, purpose or cause, often together with another subordinating conjunction or preposition;
    compare etter atafter, for atso that · in order that · that, because, forutsatt atprovided that · assuming that · on condition that, slik atso that · with the result that, sånn atso that · with the result that and uten atwithout (someone) doing something · unless
    • jeg er så glad at du komI'm so glad that you came
  7. brukt i utropused in exclamations
    • at du tør!the nerve of you!
    • at de kunne være så dumme!to think they could be so stupid!
    • at du ikke har fortalt oss dette før!to think you haven't told us this before!
    • de er så fine, at!they're so lovely, they are!
