holde på

to be in the middle of, to be busy doing · to keep at it, to carry on · to hold on to, to stick to · to back, to bet on

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ikke gi seg;
    fortsette med    to not give up;
    to carry on with     Eng. phraseto keep at it
    • de får nå bare holde påthey'll just have to carry on
  2. være i ferd medto be in the process of Eng. phraseto be in the middle of
    • en pasient holdt på å døa patient was about to die
  3. ikke ville gi slipp på;
    ta vare på    to be unwilling to let go of;
    to keep     Eng. phraseto hold on to
    • de holder på standpunktet sittthey stick to their position
    • de holder på arbeidsfolkene sinethey hold on to their workers
  4. satse (1)to bet onto bet onsatse (1)
    • holde på feil hestto back the wrong horse
