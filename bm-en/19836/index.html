gjennomgående

adjective

pervasive, recurring · consistently, on the whole (as adverb) · through, running the whole way

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som bare passerer et sted uten å stoppe;
    som går over en hel strekning (uten stans eller skifte)    which merely passes through a place without stopping;
    which runs the whole way (without stops or changes)
    • gatene stenges for gjennomgående trafikkthe streets are closed to through traffic
    • en gjennomgående togstrekninga direct railway line
  2. vanlig (1)common;
    alminnelig (2)widespread;
    typisktypical · typically    commonvanlig (1);
    widespreadalminnelig (2);
    typicaltypisk
    • en gjennomgående feila recurring error
    • det gjennomgående trekket ved bransjen er lav lønn og mye arbeidthe pervasive feature of the industry is low pay and a lot of work
  3. uavbrutt fra en side til en annenunbroken from one side to the other
    • boltene er gjennomgåendethe bolts go all the way through
  4. brukt som adverb: stort sett;
    vanligvis    used as an adverb: on the whole;
    usually
    • gjennomgående er det slikby and large that is how it is
    • resultatene er gjennomgående godethe results are consistently good
