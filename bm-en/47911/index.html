reise II homograph 2

verbverb

to raise, to put up, to erect · to raise (money, an army) · to raise, to bring up (an issue, an objection)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt reisa, kausativ til rísa Old Norse reisa, causative of rísa

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. sette i oppreist stilling;
    rette opp;
    sette opp     to set in an upright position;
    to straighten;
    to put up
    • reise opp en stige to stand a ladder up
    • de reiser et nytt bygg på tomta they are putting up a new building on the site
    • han reiste seg opp fra sofaen he got up from the sofa
  2. skaffe til veie to procure
    • reise en hær to raise an army
    • klarer vi å reise nok penger til å kjøpe bygget? can we manage to raise enough money to buy the building?
  3. sette i gang;
    vekke (II)to wake (someone) · to arouse, to evoke · to revive     to set in motion;
    to arousevekke (II)
    • hun reiser en viktig debatt she sparks an important debate
    • boka reiste en rekke innvendinger mot påstanden the book raised a number of objections to the claim
  4. om fuglehund: få fugl til å lette of a gun dog: to flush a bird
    • hunden står i stand til jegeren kommanderer den til å reise fuglen the dog stays on point until the hunter orders it to flush the bird
