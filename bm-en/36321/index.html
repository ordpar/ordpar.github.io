lyse II homograph 2

verb

to shine · to shed light · to proclaim

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt lýsa; av lys (I)light · a candle og lys (II)light, bright · fair, pale (of colour or hair) · high (of a voice)Old Norse lýsa; from lys (I)light · a candle and lys (II)light, bright · fair, pale (of colour or hair) · high (of a voice)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi fra seg lys;
    skinne, stråle    to give off light;
    to shine, to beam
    • månen lyserthe moon is shining
    • det lyste fra vinduetthere was light coming from the window
    • øynene hennes lyste av gledeher eyes shone with joy
  2. i overført betydning: tydelig og klart bære preg av noe;
    vitne om    figuratively: to bear clear and obvious signs of something;
    to testify to
    • hun lyste av misunnelseshe radiated envy
    • det lyste dårlig samvittighet lang veia guilty conscience was obvious from a mile off
  3. spre lys;
    gjøre det lyst på et sted    to shed light;
    to make a place bright
    • lyse med en lyktto shine a torch
  4. kunngjøre offentlig;
    erklæreto declare;
    tillyseto give notice of    to make known publicly;
    to declareerklære;
    to give notice oftillyse
    • lyse noen fredløsto declare someone an outlaw
    • han lyste til kamphe issued a call to arms
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som avlyseto cancel, bannlyseto ban, to excommunicate, etterlyseto call for, to ask for (something that has failed to appear) · to put out a search for, to want (of the police), tinglyseto register a document officially
  5. uttrykke ønske om noe for et annet mennesketo express a wish on another person's behalf
    • lyse velsignelse over noento pronounce a blessing over someone
    • lyse fred over noento invoke peace upon someone

lyse opp
  1. spre lys utoverto cast light around Eng. phraseto light up
    • lyskasteren lyste opp på stadionetthe floodlight lit up the stadium
  2. bli oppholdsvær;
    lysne, klarne, lette    to stop raining;
    to grow lighter, to brighten, to lift     Eng. phraseto clear up
    • det lyste opp om kveldenthe weather cleared in the evening
  3. se gladere eller ivrigere utto look happier or keener Eng. phraseto light up
    • ansiktet hans lyste opphis face lit up
  4. spre glede eller interesse;
    muntre opp    to spread joy or interest;
    to cheer up     Eng. phraseto brighten up
    • de gode fagartiklene lyser opp i avisenthe good specialist articles are a bright spot in the paper
lyse ut kunngjøre at det er åpent for å søke, stemme eller lignendeto announce that applications, voting or the like are open
  • stillingen blir lyst ut neste ukethe post will be advertised next week
  • lyse ut nyvalgto call a fresh election
  • regjeringen lyser ut nye blokker for oljeletingthe government is offering new blocks for oil exploration
