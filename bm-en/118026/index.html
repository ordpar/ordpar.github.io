sette inn

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: innin · into · inwards, setteto put · to place · to set
  1. plassere under tak to place under cover
    • sette inn sykkelen to put the bike away
  2. fengsle to imprison Eng. phraseto lock up
    • de ble satt inn for fyll og bråk they were locked up for being drunk and disorderly
  3. plassere, montere to fit, to install
    • sette inn nye vinduer to install new windows
  4. plassere på konto to deposit
    • sette inn penger i banken to deposit money in the bank
  5. la komme på trykk to have something printed
    • sette inn en annonse i avisen to place an advertisement in the newspaper
  6. gni og få til å blande seg to rub and make it blend Eng. phraseto rub in
    • sette inn garnet med farger to work colours into the yarn
    • sette inn med olje to rub in oil
  7. begynne med stor kraft eller intensitet to begin with great force or intensity Eng. phraseto set in
    • uværet setter inn the storm sets in
    • stormen satte inn for alvor the storm set in in earnest
  8. bidra med to contribute
    • sette inn tiltak to bring in measures
    • sette alle krefter inn to put all one's efforts into it
    • de satte inn letemannskaper they deployed search crews
  9. lage mål to score a goal
    • han satte inn et mål he scored a goal
