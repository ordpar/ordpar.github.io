rute II homograph 2

noun feminine or masculine

a route · a service · a timetable

Inflection

Etymology

gjennom fransk; fra latin rupta (via) ‘ryddet, banet (vei)'through French; from Latin rupta (via) 'cleared, opened (road)'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. fastsatt vei som person eller transportmiddel skal følge (til bestemte tider)a fixed route that a person or means of transport is to follow (at set times)
    • SAS flyr ruten Oslo–New YorkSAS flies the Oslo–New York route
    • legge opp en rute for ferieturento plan a route for the holiday trip
  2. rutetabella timetablea timetablerutetabell

Set phrases

i rute
  1. fram og tilbake mellom to steder til bestemte tiderback and forth between two places at fixed times Eng. phraseon a scheduled service
    • båten gikk i rute mellom Bergen og Stavangerthe boat ran a scheduled service between Bergen and Stavanger
  2. som kommer (eller går) til fastsatt tidwhich arrives (or departs) at the appointed time Eng. phraseon time
    • toget er i rutethe train is on time
  3. på et bestemt trinn i en prosess i rett tidat the stage in a process that it should have reached by that time Eng. phraseon track
    • arbeidet er i rutethe work is on track
