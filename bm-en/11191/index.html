dunste

verb
Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. lukte vondtto smell bad, to reek
    • dunste av hvitløkto reek of garlic
    • det dunster av alkoholthere is a reek of alcohol
  2. eime (2)to smell (of), dampe (1)to steamto smell (of)eime (2), to steamdampe (1)
    • det dunster av vill naturthe air smells of wild country
    • det dunstet svakt av parfymethere was a faint smell of perfume
  3. fordampe eller forsvinneto evaporate or disappear
    • det øverste laget har dunstet bortthe top layer has evaporated
    • pengene dunstet vekkthe money vanished into thin air
