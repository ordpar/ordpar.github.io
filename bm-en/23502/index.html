hevde

verb

to claim, to assert · to maintain, to champion · to lay claim to

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt hefða; av hevd (II)a prescriptive right, prescriptive title, prescription · established usage, long-standing acceptanceOld Norse hefða; from hevd (II)a prescriptive right, prescriptive title, prescription · established usage, long-standing acceptance

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. vinne eller få rett til å ha eller bruke;
    gjøre krav på    to win or obtain the right to own or use something;
    to lay claim to
    • hevde odelto claim allodial rights
    • hevde jakt- og fiskerettigheterto assert hunting and fishing rights
  2. gjøre seg til talsmann for;
    påstå    to champion;
    to claim, to assert
    • dette synspunktet hevder hun konsekventshe consistently maintains this view
    • tiltalte hevdet at han handlet i god trothe defendant claimed that he acted in good faith
  3. opprettholde (1)to maintain, håndheveto enforceto maintainopprettholde (1), to enforcehåndheve
    • hevde sin rettto assert one's rights

to be among the best Eng. phraseto hold one's own
