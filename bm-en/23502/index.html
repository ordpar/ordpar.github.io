hevde

verbverb

to claim, to assert · to maintain, to champion · to lay claim to

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt hefða; av hevd (II)a prescriptive right, prescriptive title, prescription · established usage, long-standing acceptance Old Norse hefða; from hevd (II)a prescriptive right, prescriptive title, prescription · established usage, long-standing acceptance

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. vinne eller få rett til å ha eller bruke;
    gjøre krav på     to win or obtain the right to own or use something;
    to lay claim to
    • hevde odel to claim allodial rights
    • hevde jakt- og fiskerettigheter to assert hunting and fishing rights
  2. gjøre seg til talsmann for;
    påstå     to champion;
    to claim, to assert
    • dette synspunktet hevder hun konsekvent she consistently maintains this view
    • tiltalte hevdet at han handlet i god tro the defendant claimed that he acted in good faith
  3. opprettholde (1)to maintain, håndheveto enforce to maintainopprettholde (1), to enforcehåndheve
    • hevde sin rett to assert one's rights

to be among the best Eng. phraseto hold one's own
  • hevde seg bra på skolen to do well at school
  • de norske deltakerne hevdet seg med glans the Norwegian competitors acquitted themselves brilliantly
