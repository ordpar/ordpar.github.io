sannelig

adverb

certainly · really · indeed

Senses and Example Sentences

i sannhet, virkeligtruly, really
  • du har sannelig gjort ugagn nokyou have certainly done enough mischief
  • det har du sannelig rett iyou are quite right about that
  • jeg vet sannelig ikkeI really don't know
  • jo, så sanneligyes, indeed
