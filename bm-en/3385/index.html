avbryte

verb

to interrupt · to break off, to cut short

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stanse, bryte, innstilleto stop, to break off, to call off
    • avbryte feriento cut the holiday short
    • debatten ble avbrutt en timethe debate was suspended for an hour
  2. hindre en i å snakke utto stop someone from finishing what they are saying
    • ikke avbryt meg!don't interrupt me!
