hogge, hugge

verb

to chop · to hew, to carve · to strike, to sink (teeth or claws) into

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt hǫggvaOld Norse hǫggva

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. svinge eller slå et skarpt redskap mot eller inn i noe eller noento swing or strike a sharp tool at or into something or someone
    • løfte øksa og hogge tilto raise the axe and strike
    • motstanderne ble hogd nedthe opponents were cut down
    • hogge vedto chop wood
    • hogge hull på isento chop a hole in the ice
    • snekkeren hogg til et emnethe carpenter hewed a blank into shape
    • bokstavene var hogd inn i fjelletthe letters had been carved into the rock
    • båten var hogd ut av en trestammethe boat had been hewn out of a tree trunk
    • hogge seg i fotento cut one's own foot with the axe
    • hogge seg vei gjennom krattetto hack one's way through the thicket
  2. slå (II, 1)to strike, bite (1)to bite, gripe (1)to grabto strikeslå (II, 1), to bitebite (1), to grabgripe (1)
    • hogge tennene i kjøttetto sink one's teeth into the meat
    • ormen hogg i støvelen minthe snake struck my boot
    • hogge kloa i noeto sink one's claws into something
    • hun hogg tak i hamshe grabbed hold of him
  3. om fartøy: stampe (II, 4)to pitch, stange (2)to make no headwayof a vessel: to pitchstampe (II, 4), to make no headwaystange (2)
    • skipet hogg i den grove sjøenthe ship pitched in the heavy seas
    • båten lå og hogg mot bryggekantenthe boat lay knocking against the quayside

Set phrases

hogge i snakke hardt og bråttto speak harshly and abruptly
  • han hogde i så vi hoppet i stolenehe snapped so sharply that we jumped in our chairs
hogge opp ta fra hverandre (skip, biler) for å bruke materialet på annen måteto take apart (ships, cars) so that the material can be used for something else Eng. phraseto break up for scrap
hogge ut tynne ut (skog)to thin out (a forest)
  • hogge ut et skogsfeltto thin out a patch of forest
