blaffe

verb
Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk; lydordfrom Low German; onomatopoeic

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. om ild, lys: lyse opp;
    blafre (1)to flicker    of fire, light: to flare up;
    to flickerblafre (1)
  2. om flagg, seil og lignende: slå i lufta, flagre, vifteof flags, sails and the like: to beat in the air, to flutter, to wave
    • det blaffer i gardinenethe curtains are fluttering
