noun masculine

Etymology

fra engelsk (a)bout ship, opprinnelig ‘(legg) skipet om’from English (a)bout ship, originally ‘(put) the ship about’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. vending opp mot vinden så båten får vinden inn over den andre baugena turn up into the wind so that the boat takes the wind over the other bow
    • gå bautto go about
  2. strekning som en seiler med vinden inn over samme baugstretch sailed with the wind coming in over the same bow
