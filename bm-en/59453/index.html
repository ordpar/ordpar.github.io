såkalt

adjective

so-called

Inflection

Etymology

av så (V)then · so · as (in comparisons) og kalleto call, name · to call for, summonfrom så (V)then · so · as (in comparisons) and kalleto call, name · to call for, summon

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. brukt for å vise at et ord eller uttrykk er nytt, ukjent eller spesieltused to show that a word or expression is new, unfamiliar or special
    • vi har flere såkalte medietekwe have several so-called media libraries
    • prisene er regnet ut fra såkalte basispunkterthe prices are worked out from so-called basis points
    • de såkalte frie radikalenethe so-called free radicals
  2. brukt for å vise skepsis, tvil eller ironiused to express scepticism, doubt or irony
    • stedets såkalte hotellthe local so-called hotel
    • såkalt moderne kunstso-called modern art
