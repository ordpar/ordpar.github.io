minke

verb

to decrease, to diminish, to dwindle

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt minkaOld Norse minka

Senses and Example Sentences

bli mindre;
gå nedover;
avtato grow smaller;
to go down;
to diminish
  • det minker med barnetallet i skolenthe number of children at the school is falling
  • salget har minket merkbart i det sistesales have dropped noticeably lately
  • lageret vårt minkerour stock is running low
  • kuling minkende til brisgale decreasing to breeze
brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
  • minkende vinda diminishing wind
