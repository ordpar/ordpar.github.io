blomstringstid

noun masculine or feminine

the flowering season, the blossoming time · a heyday, a golden age, a flourishing period

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. tid da plante blomstrertime when a plant is in flower; flowering season
    • frukttrærnes blomstringstidthe flowering season of the fruit trees
  2. i overført betydning: tidsperiode da noe når sin fulle utvikling eller er på sitt mest produktive og aktive;
    glanstida heyday, a golden age, a prime, gullaldergolden age    figuratively: a period when something reaches its full development or is at its most productive and active;
    heydayglanstid, golden agegullalder
    • være inne i en blomstringstidto be going through a flourishing period
    • en blomstringstid for norsk industria boom time for Norwegian industry
    • en blomstringstid for litteratur og kunsta flowering of literature and art
