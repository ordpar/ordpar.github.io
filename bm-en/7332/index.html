bratt

adjective

steep · sharp

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt brattrOld Norse brattr

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. som heller kraftig;
    steil (1)steep    which slopes sharply;
    steepsteil (1)
    • en bratt fjellsidea steep mountainside
    • bratt og ulendt terrengsteep and rugged terrain
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • veien stiger brattthe road climbs steeply
    • fjellet skråner bratt ned mot fjordenthe mountain slopes steeply down towards the fjord
  2. om forhold som kan illustreres som en kurve i et diagram: brå og kraftigof something that can be shown as a curve on a graph: sudden and sharp
    • et bratt fall i oljeprisena sharp fall in the oil price
    • være forberedt på en bratt læringskurveto be prepared for a steep learning curve
    brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
    • keeperens formkurve pekte bratt oppoverthe goalkeeper's form was rising sharply
  3. om kroppsholdning: rank (1)upright, rett (III, 1)straightof posture: uprightrank (1), straightrett (III, 1)
    • hun var bratt i nakkenshe held her head high
