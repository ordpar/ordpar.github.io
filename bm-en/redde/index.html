“redde” is an inflected form of:

  • redd adjectiveadjektiv (singular definite, plural)(entall bestemt, flertall) afraid, scared · frightened som føler frykt
  • re II homograph 2, reie II homograph 2 verbverb (past, perfect participle definite and plural)(preteritum, perfektum partisipp bestemt og flertall) to make ready, to put in order gjøre klar, sette i stand
