stand III homograph 3

noun masculinesubstantiv hankjønn

an estate (of society) · a class

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

fra tysk; samme opprinnelse som stand (I)condition · state · station from German; same origin as stand (I)condition · state · station

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. om eldre forhold: hver av de sosiale gruppene samfunnet i middelalderen var delt inn i of earlier times: each of the social groups that medieval society was divided into
    • geistlighet, adel, borgere og bønder var de fire stender i middelalderens samfunn the clergy, the nobility, the burghers and the peasants were the four estates of medieval society
    • hver stand hadde sine plikter og rettigheter each estate had its own duties and rights
  2. etterledd som karakteriserer yrke;
    i ord som bondestandthe farming class, the peasantry, handelsstandthe merchant class, the business community, merchants, legestandthe medical profession, the doctors as a profession, prestestandthe priesthood, the clergy og sjømannsstandseafarers as a class, the seafaring community     a final element indicating occupation;
