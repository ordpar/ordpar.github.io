“klare” is an inflected form of:

  • klar I homograph 1 adjectiveadjektiv (singular definite, plural)(entall bestemt, flertall) clear · bright om lys, luft: strålende, blank, ren
  • klar II homograph 2 adjectiveadjektiv (singular definite, plural)(entall bestemt, flertall) ready · clear ferdig, i stand
