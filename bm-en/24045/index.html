holde

verbverb

to hold · to keep · to hold, to put on (an event)

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt halda Old Norse halda

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. ha tak i eller grep rundt to have hold of or a grip on
    • holde kjæresten i hånden to hold one's partner by the hand
    • han holdt barnet i armene he held the child in his arms
    • hold i denne planken for meg hold this plank for me
    • hold fast! hold tight!
    • holde seg fast i rekkverket to hold on to the railing
  2. få noe eller noen til å bli på en viss plass;

    • holde hesten to hold the horse

    • holde troppene samlet to keep the troops together
    • holde noe innenfor rimelige grenser to keep something within reasonable limits
    • de prøvde å holde henne fra å begynne å røyke they tried to keep her from taking up smoking

    • hun blir ofte holdt utenfor fellesskapet she is often kept out of the group

    • holde seg for ørene to cover one's ears

  4. være på et bestemt sted;
    ikke vike av fra eller komme ut av;
    bevege seg eller styre i en bestemt retning     to stay in a certain place;
    not to stray from or leave;
    to head in a certain direction
    • holde seg hjemme to stay at home

    to still have, not to lose;
    to remain unchanged;
    to defendforsvare, to maintainhevde (3)

    • holde seg på beina to stay on one's feet

    • det holder jeg for lite trolig I consider that rather unlikely
