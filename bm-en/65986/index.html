utvise

verb

to expel, to suspend · to send off (in sport) · to display, to exercise (a quality)

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. nekte fortsatt adgang;
    gi (midlertidig) utestengelse;
    vise utto send away    to refuse further admission;
    to impose a (temporary) ban;
    to send awayvise ut
    • de ble utvist fra landet etter å ha sonet straffenthey were expelled from the country after serving their sentence
    • skolen utviste eleven i tre dagerthe school suspended the pupil for three days
  2. legge for dagen;
    gå fram, vise (II, 4)to show    to display, to manifest;
    to proceed, to showvise (II, 4)
    • utvise stor forsiktighet i omgang med giftige sprøytemidlerto exercise great care when handling toxic pesticides
  3. i idrett: straffe en spiller og et lag ved at spilleren må forlate banen (midlertidig) og laget må spille med en spiller mindrein sport: to penalise a player and their team by making the player leave the field of play (temporarily) and the team play with one player fewer
    • bli utvist etter en stygg taklingto be sent off after a nasty tackle
