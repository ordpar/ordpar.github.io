beskrive

verb

to describe · to depict

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. omhandleto deal with · to be about · to cover;
    framstilleto depict, to portray · to produce, to manufacture, skildreto depict · to portray · to describe    to deal withomhandle;
    to portrayframstille, to depictskildre
    • han beskrev turen detaljerthe described the trip in detail
    • kan du beskrive ransmennene?can you describe the robbers?
    • romanen beskriver livet i en norsk fjellbygdthe novel portrays life in a Norwegian mountain village
    • det kan ikke beskrivesit defies description
  2. bevege seg i en figurto move along a geometric figure
    • planetene beskriver elliptiske banerthe planets describe elliptical orbits
  3. brukt som adjektiv: som er skrevet påused as an adjective: written on
    • et beskrevet stykke papira piece of paper with writing on it
