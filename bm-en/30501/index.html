knerte

verb

to shoot · to knock back (a drink) · to chop up

Inflection

Etymology

av knerta dramfrom knerta dram

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. hogge raskt og småttto chop with quick, small strokes
    • knerte ned noe småbjørkto chop down some small birches
  2. skyteto shoot
    • knerte og skyteto bang away and shoot
    • knerte ned en fuglto shoot down a bird
  3. slåto hit
    • knerte til noento give someone a whack
  4. drikke;
    jamfør knert (1)a nip (of spirits)    to drink;
    compare knert (1)a nip (of spirits)
    • knerte en flaske vinto knock back a bottle of wine
