røre opp

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

see:Se: røreto touch · to stir · to move
  1. uroe, skipleto disturb to unsettle, to disturbskiple Eng. phraseto stir up
    • hendelsene har rørt opp i mye gammelt grums the events have stirred up a lot of old dirt
    • de ville ikke røre opp i saken they did not want to rake up the matter
  2. rote opp fra bunnen av;
    virvle opp     to churn up from the bottom;
    to swirl up
    • flomvannet har rørt opp i havbunnen the floodwater has churned up the seabed
