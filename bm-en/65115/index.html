utbre, utbreie

verb

to spread · to disseminate

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør bre (III)to spread outcompare bre (III)to spread out

Senses and Example Sentences

få til å bre seg ut;
gjøre kjentto make something spread;
to make known
  • utbre kjennskapet til nyere litteraturto spread knowledge of more recent literature

Set phrases

utbre seg snakke mye og inngående om noe;
prate myeto speak at length and in detail about something;
to talk a lot Eng. phraseto hold forth
  • hun skal alltid utbre seg om ethvert emneshe always has to hold forth on every subject
