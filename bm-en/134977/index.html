videre

adjective

further, additional · on, onwards, furthermore

Inflection

Etymology

komparativ av vidwide · broad · far (as an adverb)the comparative of vidwide · broad · far (as an adverb)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. lenger fram;
    ytterligere    further on;
    additional
    • vedta noe uten videre diskusjonto adopt something without further discussion
    • jeg har ikke noe videre lystI don't much feel like it
  2. brukt som adverb: lenger;
    i samme retning;
    på samme måte    used as an adverb: further;
    in the same direction;
    in the same way
    • reise videreto travel on
    • la saken gå videre til neste instansto pass the case on to the next level
    • videre må en være klar over at ...furthermore, one must be aware that ...
