Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

gi tillatelse eller mulighet til å to give permission or the opportunity to see:Se: lato let, to allow · to have something done Eng. phraseto let oneself be ...
  • la seg intervjue to agree to be interviewed
  • de lot seg ikke lure they were not taken in
  • han har latt seg imponere he has let himself be impressed
  • så fort det lot seg gjøre, var de samlet igjen as soon as it could be done, they were all together again
