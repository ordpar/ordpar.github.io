-graf

noun masculine
Inflection

Etymology

av gresk graphe ‘skrift, brev’, av graphein ‘skrive’from Greek graphe ‘writing, a letter’, from graphein ‘to write’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. suffikssuffix brukt for å lage ord om apparat til å skrive eller tegne noe opp medsuffixsuffiks used to form words for a device for writing or recording something
    also found in compounds:i ord som seismografa seismograph, telegrafa telegraph
  2. suffikssuffix brukt i ord for noe opptegnet eller skrevetsuffixsuffiks used in words for something recorded or written
    also found in compounds:i ord som autografautograph, holograf (I)a holograph (a document in the author’s own hand)
  3. suffikssuffix brukt for å lage ord for yrkesbetegnelser;
    jamfør -grafi (I)-graphy    suffixsuffiks used to form words for occupational titles;
    compare -grafi (I)-graphy
    also found in compounds:i ord som fotografphotographer, geografa geographer, koreografa choreographer
