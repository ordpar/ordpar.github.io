kupp

noun neuter

a coup · a bargain, a steal · a heist

Inflection

Etymology

fra fransk coup, opprinnelig ‘slag, støt’from French coup, originally ‘blow, thrust’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. rask og lovstridig maktovertakelsea swift and unlawful seizure of power
    • generalene tok makten i et blodig kuppthe generals seized power in a bloody coup
    • regjeringen ble styrtet i kuppetthe government was overthrown in the coup
    • mindretallet gjennomførte et kupp på årsmøtetthe minority pulled off a coup at the annual meeting
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som militærkuppa military coup, statskuppa coup d’état
  2. forbrytelse som er gjort raskt og dristiga crime carried out swiftly and boldly
    • banden planla kuppet godtthe gang planned the heist well
  3. heldig kjøp;
    varp (2)a lucky find    a bargain;
    a lucky findvarp (2)
    • gjøre et kupp under salgetto pick up a bargain in the sale
    • denne bilen var et kuppthis car was a steal
