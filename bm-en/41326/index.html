nyhet

noun feminine or masculine

a piece of news · news · a novelty, a new arrival

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. hendelse som gjøres kjent for første gang;
    nyttnews    an event that is made known for the first time;
    newsnytt
    • han ville fortelle henne den gode nyheten selvhe wanted to tell her the good news himself
    • avisene bringer de triste nyhetene i dagthe papers carry the sad news today
  2. noe som er nyttsomething that is new
    • stolen er en nyhet i møbelbransjenthe chair is a new arrival in the furniture trade
