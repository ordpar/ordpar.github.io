innom

preposition

by (on a brief visit) · in at · within

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. (som snarest) inn eller inne hos, inn ved, inn på;
    om (II, 2)by way of    (just briefly) in to or in at someone's, in by, in on;
    by way ofom (II, 2)
    • de stakk innom oss på vei til hyttathey dropped in on us on the way to the cabin
    • båten går ikke innom det stedet lengerthe boat no longer calls at that place
    • hun var innom teologistudiet før hun bestemte seg for filologienshe had a spell studying theology before she settled on philology
  2. brukt som adverb: kort inn;
    inn i forbifarten    used as an adverb: briefly in;
    in while passing by
    • han kom innom på vei hjem fra jobbhe dropped in on his way home from work
  3. innenfor (1)inside;
    innen (II, 1)within    insideinnenfor (1);
    withininnen (II, 1)
    • innom dørajust inside the door
    • innom vårt distriktwithin our district
