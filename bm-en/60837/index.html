tillegg

noun neuter

addition · supplement · surcharge

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. noe som legges tilsomething added on, an addition
    • mot et tillegg i prisen kan en få enkeltromfor a surcharge on the price you can get a single room
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som alderstillegga seniority increment, a pay rise for length of service · the weight one puts on with age (jocular), driftstilleggan operating subsidy, an operating grant, lønnstillegga pay rise, a pay increase, a wage supplement
  2. supplement til dokument, bokverk, tidsskrift eller lignendea supplement to a document, book, periodical or the like
    • lovteksten med tillegg og kommentarerthe text of the law with addenda and commentary
