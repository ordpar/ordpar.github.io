hive

verb

to chuck · to fling · to haul

Inflection

Etymology

av engelsk heave; samme opprinnelse som heveto raise, to lift · to draw, to withdraw (money) · to terminate, to break offfrom English heave; of the same origin as heveto raise, to lift · to draw, to withdraw (money) · to terminate, to break off

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. løfte, heve, hale (med tau, vaier eller lignende)to lift, to raise, to haul (with a rope, wire or the like)
    • hive ankerto weigh anchor
    • hive opp en båtto hoist up a boat
  2. om båt: slingre (3)to roll, rulle (III, 5)to move with the swellof a boat: to rollslingre (3), to move with the swellrulle (III, 5)
    • skuta heiv voldsomt i den grove sjøenthe ship pitched violently in the heavy seas
  3. kaste (II, 2)to toss, slenge (II, 1)to flingto tosskaste (II, 2), to flingslenge (II, 1)
    • hive noe ut gjennom vinduetto chuck something out of the window
    • hive seg ut i noeto plunge into something
  4. kaste i søpla;
    kassere (II, 1)to discard    to throw in the rubbish;
    to discardkassere (II, 1)
    • hive matto throw food away
  5. puste inn med sterke, hørlige åndedrag;
    stønne, gispe    to breathe in with strong, audible breaths;
    to groan, to gasp
    brukt som adjektivused as an adjective
    • hivende pustheaving breath
