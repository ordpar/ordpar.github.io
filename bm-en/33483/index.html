ladning I homograph 1

noun masculine or feminine
Inflection

Etymology

av lade (II)to charge · to load a weaponfrom lade (II)to charge · to load a weapon

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. mengde med sprengstoff, for eksempel til et skytevåpena quantity of explosive, for example for a firearm
    • ladningen eksploderte for tidligthe charge went off too early
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som dynamittladninga dynamite charge, haglladninga charge of shot in a gun, sprengladningan explosive charge
  2. elektrisitetsmengde som en akkumulator, et batteri eller lignende er ladet medthe amount of electricity an accumulator, a battery or the like is charged with
    • elektrisk ladning blir målt i coulombelectric charge is measured in coulombs
  3. stor mengde;
    last (I, 1)a cargo, lass (1)a load    a large quantity;
    a cargolast (I, 1), a loadlass (1)
    • en ladning kulla load of coal
