stelle

verbverb

to look after, to care for · to tidy, to get ready

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

samme opprinnelse som stille (IV)to satisfy · to ease, to soothe · to staunch same origin as stille (IV)to satisfy · to ease, to soothe · to staunch

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gjøre ren eller ryddig;
    ordne, fjelge     to make clean or tidy;
    to put in order, to spruce up
    • stelle et barn to wash and dress a child
    • stelle huset to clean the house
    • hun steller til selskap she is getting ready to host a party
  2. holde i stand;
    passe (IV)to look after, to mind · to keep an eye on, røkteto care for     to keep in good repair;
    to look afterpasse (IV), to care forrøkte
    • stelle buskapen to tend the livestock
    • være flink til å stelle med penger to be good at handling money
    • han stelte for mora he kept house for his mother
    • han steller pent med sykkelen sin he takes good care of his bicycle
  3. innretteto set up, to arrange, to fit out to set up, to arrangeinnrette
    • vi fikk stelt det slik at alt ordnet seg we arranged things so that it all worked out
