tipse

verb

to tip off, give a tip · to tip (leave a gratuity)

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gi tips om noeto give a tip about something
    • tipse politiet om en forbrytelseto tip off the police about a crime
  2. gi drikkepengerto leave a tip
    • tipse ti prosent av regningento tip ten per cent of the bill
