forsvinne

verb

to disappear, to vanish · to go missing

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk; av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excessfrom Low German; from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess

Senses and Example Sentences

bli borte, komme bortto disappear, to go missing
  • forsvinne som dugg for solato vanish like dew before the sun
  • forsvinne i vrimmelento disappear into the crowd
  • hun forsvant for megshe vanished from my sight
  • han er sporløst forsvunnethe has vanished without a trace
  • forsvinn!get lost!
