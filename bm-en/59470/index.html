såpass, så pass

adverb

that much, to that degree · that good, reasonably

Etymology

av så (V)then · so · as (in comparisons) og pass (V)fitting, deserved (in {til pass}) · well, in good health (in {til pass})from så (V)then · so · as (in comparisons) and pass (V)fitting, deserved (in {til pass}) · well, in good health (in {til pass})

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. så mye;
    i så stor grad    that much;
    to that degree
    • er du ikke såpass til kar?aren't you man enough for that?
    • såpass får du jammen tåleyou can surely put up with that much
  2. etter måten bra, dyktig eller lignendereasonably good, capable or the like
    • det er rart han er såpass sprek fremdelesit's odd that he's still as fit as that
    • såpass til lærer skal du lete lenge etteryou'll look a long time before you find a teacher that good
