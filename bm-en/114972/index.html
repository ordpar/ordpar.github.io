liksom II homograph 2, likesom II homograph 2, lissom

adverbadverb

sort of · like · supposedly

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

på en måte, nesten, tilsynelatende in a way, almost, seemingly
  • han skulle liksom være snill he was supposedly being nice
  • det kom liksom et rødskjær på himmelen før smellet there was sort of a red glow in the sky before the bang

  • helt tilfeldig, du liksom; dette tror jeg var planlagt! completely by chance, yeah right; I think this was planned!
på liksom for syns skyld, for moro for show, for fun Eng. phrasefor pretend
