utnytte

verb

to make use of, to utilise · to exploit · to take advantage of

Inflection

Etymology

etter tyskmodelled on German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bruke eller dra fordel avto use, to take advantage of
    • utnytte naturressurseneto exploit natural resources
    • utnytte de muligheter som byr segto take advantage of the opportunities that arise
  2. misbruke til egen fordelto take advantage of to one's own benefit
    • han utnytter de gamle foreldrene økonomiskhe exploits his elderly parents financially
    • føle seg utnyttetto feel used
