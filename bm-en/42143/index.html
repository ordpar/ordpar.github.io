omkring

preposition

around · about, approximately

Etymology

norrønt umkring(is)Old Norse umkring(is)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i ring rundtaround, in a circle
    • sitte omkring bordetto sit around the table
    • ta omkring noento put one's arms around someone
  2. i forbindelse medin connection with
    • gjøre seg tanker omkring et emneto reflect on a subject
  3. utenomaround, skirting
    • snakke omkring selve problemetto talk around the real problem
  4. nære ved;
    om lag    close to;
    approximately, roughly
    • det var omkring 0°it was around 0°
    • omkring år 1900around the year 1900
  5. brukt som adverb: rundt (til alle kanter)used as an adverb: around (in every direction)
    • se seg omkringto look around
    • gå omkring i byento walk around town
    • slikt skjer aldri her omkringthat sort of thing never happens around here
