meditere

verb
Inflection

Etymology

fra latin ‘overveie’from Latin 'to ponder'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. fordype seg mentalt i noe, særlig som religiøs øvelseto immerse oneself mentally in something, especially as a religious exercise
    • meditere en halv time hver dagto meditate for half an hour every day
  2. grubleto brood, to ponder, to mull things over, grunne (IV)to ponder · to muse, to mull overto broodgruble, to pondergrunne (IV)
    • meditere over noeto muse on something
