bade

verb

to bathe · to go swimming · to have a bath

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ta seg et bad (1)a bath;
    la vann skylle over (seg selv eller andre);
    legge kroppen i vann (for å vaske den)    to take a bathbad (1);
    to let water rinse over (oneself or others);
    to put the body in water (in order to wash it)
    • bade og legge ungeneto bathe the children and put them to bed
    • bade i sjøento swim in the sea
    • bade i fontenento bathe in the fountain
  2. dyppe, skylle i en væske (I)a liquid, a fluid;
    være innhyllet av fuktighet    to dip, to rinse in a liquidvæske (I);
    to be enveloped in moisture
    • bade kjøttet i marinadeto bathe the meat in marinade
    • ligge badet i svetteto lie bathed in sweat
  3. i overført betydning: la seg innhylle, være omgitt av (noe annet enn væske)figuratively: to let oneself be enveloped, to be surrounded by (something other than liquid)
    • bygda lå badet i solthe village lay bathed in sunshine
    • bade i gjørmeto wallow in mud
    • gatene bader i søppelthe streets are awash with rubbish
