arrangere

verbverb

to arrange · to organise, to host · to stage

InflectionVis bøyning

PronunciationUttale

arangsjeˊre

EtymologyOpphav

fra fransk, av à ‘til’ og ranger ‘ordne’ from French, from à ‘to’ and ranger ‘to put in order’

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. planlegge, plassere, stelle til, tilrettelegge;
    ordneto sort out · to arrange · to put in order     to plan, to place, to prepare, to organise;
    to sort out; to put in orderordne
    • arrangere møblene i et rom to arrange the furniture in a room
    • arrangere olympiske leker to host the Olympic Games
  2. i musikk: lage et arrangement (4)an arrangement in music: to make an arrangementarrangement (4)
    • stykket var arrangert for orkester the piece was arranged for orchestra
  3. lage til på en kunstig måte;
    fingereto feign, to fake, to make up;
    stille opp     to set up artificially;
    to fakefingere;
    to stage
    • innbruddet var arrangert the burglary was staged
    • bildet er arrangert the photograph is posed

Set phrasesFaste uttrykk
