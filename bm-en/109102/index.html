bak ryggen

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

i hemmelighet, uten at en bestemt person vet om det in secret, without a particular person knowing about it see:Se: bakbehind · at the back, in the back Eng. phrasebehind someone's back
  • de har gått bak ryggen på oss they have gone behind our backs
  • diskutere noe bak noens rygg to discuss something behind someone's back
