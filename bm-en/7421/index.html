bremse II homograph 2

verb

to brake · to curb, to slow, to rein in

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. redusere eller stanse farten;
    bruke brems (II, 1)a brake    to slow down or bring to a stop;
    to use a brakebrems (II, 1)
    • sjåføren greide ikke å bremsethe driver was unable to brake
    • syklisten bremset så hardt at han veltetthe cyclist braked so hard that he came off
  2. i overført betydning: dempe;
    holde igjen    figuratively: to curb;
    to hold back
    • bremse utviklingen av sykdommento slow the progress of the disease
    • bremse veksten i norsk økonomito rein in growth in the Norwegian economy
    brukt som adjektiv:used as an adjective:
    • tiltakene hadde en bremsende effektthe measures had a dampening effect
  3. roe ned hest ved å bruke brems (II, 3)a twitch (a band tightened on the upper lip)to calm a horse by using a twitch (a band tightened on the upper lip)brems (II, 3)

Set phrases

bremse ned sette ned farten (ved å bruke brems)to reduce speed (by braking) Eng. phraseto slow down
  • bremse ned for en fotgjengerto brake for a pedestrian
bremse opp stoppe helt eller sette farten kraftig ned (ved å bruke brems)to stop completely or slow down sharply (by braking)
  • togføreren klarte ikke å bremse opp før toget traff bilenthe train driver could not brake in time before the train hit the car
  • veksten i økonomien har bremset oppgrowth in the economy has slowed sharply
