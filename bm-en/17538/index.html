framover, fremover

preposition

forwards · ahead · onwards

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i retning fram;
    bortoveralong, off down (a road, a path) · on, further along (as adverb)    in a forward direction;
    alongbortover
    • gå framover gulvetto walk forward across the floor
    brukt som adverbused as an adverb
    • falle framoverto fall forwards
  2. om bøying, stilling: fra vertikal posisjon med framsiden førstof bending, posture: from an upright position, front first
    • bøye seg framoverto bend forwards
  3. brukt som adverb: i eller på tiden som kommerused as an adverb: in or during the time to come
    • nå framoverfrom now on
  4. brukt som adverb: i utvikling mot noe bedre eller mot fullføringused as an adverb: developing towards something better or towards completion
    • arbeidet går raskt framoverthe work is progressing quickly
