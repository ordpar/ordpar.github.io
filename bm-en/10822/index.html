drepe

verb

to kill

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt drepa ‘slå, skade, drepe’; samme opprinnelse som treffeto meet · to hit · to make (a decision)Old Norse drepa 'to strike, to injure, to kill'; same origin as treffeto meet · to hit · to make (a decision)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ta livet avto take the life of
    • drepe noen med knivto kill someone with a knife
    • bli drept i trafikkento be killed in traffic
    • katten dreper fuglerthe cat kills birds
    • gjerningspersonen ble dømt for å ha skutt og drept tre personerthe perpetrator was convicted of shooting and killing three people
    brukt som substantiv:used as a noun:
    • den drepte er i 20-årsalderenthe person killed is in their twenties
  2. ødelegge (1)to destroy, to ruin, tilintetgjøreto destroy, to annihilate, to wipe outto destroy, to ruinødelegge (1), to annihilate, to wipe outtilintetgjøre
    • stadig kritikk dreper enhver tiltakslystconstant criticism kills all drive
    • ulykken drepte alle følelsene i hennethe accident killed every feeling in her

Set phrases

drepe seg ta livet sitt; miste livet; overanstrenge segto take one's own life; to lose one's life; to overexert oneself Eng. phraseto kill oneself
