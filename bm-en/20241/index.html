glideflukt

noun masculine or feminine
Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det at fugler seiler i lufta uten å bevege vingene, eller at fly flyr uten å bruke motorthe gliding of birds through the air without moving their wings, or of an aircraft flying without engine power
    • se en ørn i glidefluktto see an eagle gliding
  2. i overført betydning: det å stille og ubemerket endre synspunkt eller ståstedfiguratively: a quiet and unnoticed shift of view or standpoint
    • partiet er inne i en glideflukt mot høyrethe party is drifting steadily to the right
    • presidenten gjør glideflukt i stadig flere sakerthe president is quietly shifting ground on more and more issues
