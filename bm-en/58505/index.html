sutre

verb

to whine, to whimper, to grumble

Inflection

Etymology

beslektet med sutworry, sorrowrelated to sutworry, sorrow

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ynke seg;
    klynketo whine    to whimper;
    to whineklynke

  2. syteto grumble, to moan, to complainto grumblesyte
