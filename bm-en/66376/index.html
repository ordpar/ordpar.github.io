vandring

noun feminine or masculine

a hike, a walk · a migration · travel (of a machine part)

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. det å vandreto walk · to wander, to roam · to migratethe act of walking; migratingvandre
    • vi har vært ute på vandringwe have been out for a hike
    • laksens vandringerthe migrations of the salmon
    used as a suffix in:som etterledd i ord som fjellvandringmountain hiking, folkevandringa mass migration, utvandringemigration
  2. avstand som en maskin(del) beveger seg i løpet av en prosessthe distance that a machine (or part of one) moves during a process
