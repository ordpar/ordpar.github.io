duge

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt duga; beslektet med dyda virtue og dyktigskilled, capable · good (at something) · thoroughly, very (as an adverb)Old Norse duga; related to dyda virtue and dyktigskilled, capable · good (at something) · thoroughly, very (as an adverb)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. være til virkelig nytte;
    være dugende (1)capable    to be of real use;
    to be capabledugende (1)
    • dette duger ikkethis will not do
    • duge til noeto be good for something
    • gi hjelp som dugerto give help that really counts
  2. være flink eller sterk nok;
    være god for    to be skilled or strong enough;
    to be up to
    • la ham vise hva han duger tillet him show what he is capable of
    • hun duger ikke til den slags arbeidshe is no good at that sort of work
