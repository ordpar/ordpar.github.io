veve

verb
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt vefaOld Norse vefa

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. slå renning (II)the warp og innslag (3)the weft i en vev (I, 2)a web (threads set up for weaving) sammen til tøyto beat the warprenning (II) and the weftinnslag (3) of a web (threads set up for weaving)vev (I, 2) together into cloth
    • sitte og veveto sit weaving
    • veve et teppeto weave a rug
  2. i overført betydning: blande, involverefiguratively: to mix in, to involve
    • bli vevd inn i noeto become caught up in something
  3. prate tull;
    tulle, vrøvle    to talk nonsense;
    to joke, to talk rubbish
    • veve i hop en historieto concoct a story
    • ligge og veve i ørsketo lie there rambling in a daze
