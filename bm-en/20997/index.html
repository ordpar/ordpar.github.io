grisehus

noun neuter

a pigsty (also of a filthy, untidy room)

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. hus for griserbuilding for keeping pigs in
    • bygge nytt grisehus på gården+to build a new pigsty on the farm
    • grisehuset ligger bak låven+the pigsty is behind the barn
  2. uordentlig eller urenslig husuntidy or filthy house
    • rommet ditt er et skikkelig grisehus+your room is a real pigsty
