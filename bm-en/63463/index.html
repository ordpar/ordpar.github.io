tøve

verb

to talk nonsense, to talk rubbish · to dawdle · to felt (wool)

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt þǿfa ‘stride, slite’Old Norse þǿfa 'to struggle, to toil'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. snakke usant;
    tulle, tøyse, vrøvle    to say things that are not true;
    to mess about, to joke, to talk nonsense
    • nei, nå tøver du bareno, now you're just talking rubbish
  2. være treg;
    somleto dawdle, nøleto hesitate    to be slow;
    to dawdlesomle, to hesitatenøle
    • han stod og tøvet utenfor husethe stood dawdling outside the house
  3. behandle ull eller andre materialer slik at det filtrer seg sammen og blir tettere og tykkere;
    tove (II)to felt    to treat wool or other materials so that it mats together and becomes denser and thicker;
    to felttove (II)
