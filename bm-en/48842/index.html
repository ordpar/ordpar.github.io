romme

verb

to hold, to accommodate · to contain

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. ha plass til, gi rom tilto have room for, to accommodate
    • salen rommer mangethe hall holds a lot of people
  2. være fylt av;
    inneholde    to be filled with;
    to contain
    • hodet hans rommer mye rarthis head holds all sorts of odd things
