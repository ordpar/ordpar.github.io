ypperlig

adjective

excellent · splendid · superb

Inflection

Etymology

av eldre dansk ypper; jamfør ypperstfinest, very best · foremostfrom older Danish ypper; compare ypperstfinest, very best · foremost

Senses and Example Sentences

utmerket, glimrendeexcellent, brilliant
  • en ypperlig innsatsa splendid effort
  • et ypperlig kjøretøya superb vehicle
  • lokalene er ypperligethe premises are excellent
brukt som adverb:used as an adverb:
  • det passer meg ypperligit suits me perfectly
  • bilen fungerte ypperligthe car ran splendidly
