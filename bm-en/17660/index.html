frata

verb

to take away from · to deprive of

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

ta frato take something away from someone, to deprive someone of something
  • bli fratatt førerkortetto have one's driving licence taken away
  • frata noen gledento take away someone's pleasure
  • frata noen håpetto deprive someone of hope
  • frata noen nattesøvnento rob someone of a night's sleep
