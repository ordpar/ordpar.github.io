ankomme

verb

to arrive

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. komme fram (til)to arrive (at)
    • etter ruten skal skipet ankomme i morgenaccording to the schedule the ship is due to arrive tomorrow
    • han er nettopp ankommet fra Parishe has just arrived from Paris
    • vi ankom Bergen to timer forsinketwe arrived in Bergen two hours late
    brukt som substantiv:used as a noun:
    • den sist ankomnethe latest arrival
  2. troppe oppto turn up
    • hun ankom i shortsshe turned up in shorts
