la III homograph 3

verbverb

to let, to allow · to have something done

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

samme opprinnelse som late (I)to pretend · to leave (in a state) same origin as late (I)to pretend · to leave (in a state)

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gi lov eller anledning til å;
    tillate å;
    ikke hindre eller sette seg imot     to give permission or the opportunity to;
    to allow to;
    not to prevent or oppose
    • hun lar ingen anledning gå fra seg she never lets an opportunity slip by
    • han lot henne gå he let her go
    • han lot oss ikke komme til orde he would not let us get a word in
    • hun lot dem bare snakke she just let them talk
    • jeg beklager at jeg har latt deg vente I am sorry I have kept you waiting
  2. sørge for at noe blir gjort;
    få til å     to see to it that something gets done;
    to cause to
    • la noe være usagt to leave something unsaid
    • vi lar dette bli mellom oss we will keep this between ourselves
    • de lot advokaten sette opp et testament they had the lawyer draw up a will
  3. brukt i imperativ for å kommandere eller oppfordre til used in the imperative to give an order or an encouragement
    • la meg få se tegningen din! let me see your drawing!
    • la oss gå! let's go!
