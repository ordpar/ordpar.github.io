her

adverb

here

Etymology

norrønt hérOld Norse hér

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. på dette stedet;
    til forskjell fra derthere · where    in this place;
    as opposed to thereder
    • stå her!stand here!
    • kom her!come here!
    • her liker jeg megI like it here
    • her hjemmehere at home
    • her i landethere in this country
    • her til landsin this country
    • her ute på havetout here at sea
    • her slutter historienthis is where the story ends
    • her har du det du bad omhere's what you asked for
    • her har dere meg tilbakehere I am, back again
    • jeg vil ikke svare her og nåI don't want to answer here and now
  2. på dette feltetin this field
    • her har de vært til uvurderlig nyttehere they have been of invaluable use
    • stikkordet her er faglig kompetansethe key word here is professional expertise
  3. brukt etter pronomen eller determinativ for å framheve noen eller noe som allerede er kjent;
    jamfør herre (II)here, this here    used after a pronoun or determiner to emphasise someone or something already known;
    compare herre (II)here, this here
    • han her fyren vi traffthis guy we met
    • den her kafeenthis café here
    • disse her kommer til å visnethese here are going to wither
  4. brukt som formelt subjekt: det (III, 1)itused as a formal subject: det (III, 1)it
    • her er kaldtit's cold here
    • skal her være fest?is there going to be a party here?
  5. brukt om tid: nå (II)now · nowadays, at present · well, you know (unstressed particle), nettopp (2)just nowused about time: nå (II)now · nowadays, at present · well, you know (unstressed particle), nettopp (2)just now
    • her i dagthis very day
    • her en dagthe other day
