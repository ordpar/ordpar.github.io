ned

adverbadverb

down · downwards

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt niðr Old Norse niðr

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. fra høyere til lavere grad, nivå, trinn eller lignende;
    motsatt opp (1)up     from a higher to a lower degree, level, step or the like;
    opposite of upopp (1)
    • komme ned fra fjellet to come down from the mountain
    • huke seg ned to crouch down
    • brette ned skjorteermene to roll down one's shirtsleeves
    • sette ned prisene to lower the prices
    • en feil som trekker ned a mistake that pulls the score down
    • dette har vart ned til vår tid this has survived down to our own day
    • lynet slo ned the lightning struck
    • laget rykket ned the team was relegated
    • sige lenger ned to sink further down
  2. ut til kysten;
    sørover     out to the coast;
    southwards
    • dra ned til Frankrike to travel down to France
  3. brukt i uttrykk for at noe blir oppbevart eller lignende used in expressions for something being stored away or the like
    • fryse ned matvarer to freeze food
    • låse ned verdipapirer to lock away securities
  4. brukt i uttrykk for at noe dekkes til used in expressions for something being covered over
    • støve ned to gather dust
    • grave noe ned to bury something
    • hytta snødde nesten ned the cabin was almost buried in snow
  5. brukt i uttrykk for at noe eller noen blir undertrykt, ødelagt, tilintetgjort eller lignende used in expressions for something or someone being suppressed, destroyed, annihilated or the like
    • slå ned all motstand to crush all resistance
    • bli kjørt ned to be run over
    • ned med tyrannen! down with the tyrant!
    • rive ned en bygning to tear down a building
    • slakte ned buskapen to slaughter the livestock
    • stemme ned et forslag to vote down a proposal
    • huset brant ned the house burned down
    • bilen står og ruster ned the car sits there rusting away
    • stenge ned to shut down
    • legge ned en bedrift to close down a business
  6. brukt i uttrykk for at en handling er utført used in expressions for an action being carried out to completion
    • leiligheten er vasket ned the flat has been scrubbed clean
  7. brukt som preposisjon: nedover (1)down used as a preposition: downnedover (1)
    • gå ned trappa to go down the stairs
    • kjøre ned slalåmbakken to ski down the slalom slope

opp og ned
  1. fram og tilbake (på) back and forth (along) Eng. phraseup and down
    • gå opp og ned på gulvet to pace up and down the floor
    • vandre opp og ned gatene to wander up and down the streets
  2. fra en tilstand til en annen from one state to another Eng. phraseups and downs
    • det går opp og ned her i livet life has its ups and downs
