like III homograph 3

adverb

just as, equally · right, just · straight, all the way

Etymology

norrønt líka; jamfør lik (III)like, similar to · equal (to)Old Norse líka; compare lik (III)like, similar to · equal (to)

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i samme grad, like myeto the same degree, equally
    • de er like gamlethey are the same age
    • like myejust as much
    • jeg er like glad i vinteren som i sommerenI like winter just as much as summer
    • det var like godt at det gikk som det gikkit was just as well things turned out the way they did
  2. tett, rett, nærright, just
    • det var like før det startetit was right before it started
    • like etter dro deright afterwards they left
    • like ved grensenright by the border
    • de satt like overfor ossthey sat right opposite us
  3. fullt ut, fullstendig, aldeles;
    helt (II, 6)wholly    fully, completely, entirely;
    whollyhelt (II, 6)
    • sove like til klokka tolvto sleep right up until twelve o'clock
    • dra like til byento go right into town
    • fra 80-tallet og like fram til i dagfrom the 80s right up until today
  4. direkte (1)directlydirectlydirekte (1)
    • gå like hjemto go straight home
