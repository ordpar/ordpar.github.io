like III homograph 3

adverbadverb

just as, equally · right, just · straight, all the way

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt líka; jamfør lik (III)like, similar to · equal (to) Old Norse líka; compare lik (III)like, similar to · equal (to)

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. i samme grad, like mye to the same degree, equally
    • de er like gamle they are the same age
    • like mye just as much
    • jeg er like glad i vinteren som i sommeren I like winter just as much as summer
    • det var like godt at det gikk som det gikk it was just as well things turned out the way they did
  2. tett, rett, nær right, just
    • det var like før det startet it was right before it started
    • like etter dro de right afterwards they left
    • like ved grensen right by the border
    • de satt like overfor oss they sat right opposite us
  3. fullt ut, fullstendig, aldeles;
    helt (II, 6)wholly     fully, completely, entirely;
    whollyhelt (II, 6)
    • sove like til klokka tolv to sleep right up until twelve o'clock
    • dra like til byen to go right into town
    • fra 80-tallet og like fram til i dag from the 80s right up until today
  4. direkte (1)directly directlydirekte (1)
    • gå like hjem to go straight home
