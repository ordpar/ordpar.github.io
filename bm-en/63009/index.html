tuss, tusse I homograph 1

noun masculine
Inflection

Etymology

norrønt þursog þuss

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. i folketro: underjordisk vesen;
    haugbu, nisse (I, 1)in Nordic folklore: a supernatural being that lives near people, especially in the outbuildings of farms, and is pictured as a little man dressed in grey with a long beard and a red pointed cap, vette
    • tro på tusser og troll
    • en liten, grå tuss
    • være plaget av tussene
    som etterledd i ord som bergtuss, haugtuss
  2. i norrøn mytologi: troll (1)in folk belief: a (large) ugly, strong and often dangerous, but stupid and gullible, human-like being that lives in the forest, in the mountains or in the sea, jotun
    som etterledd i ord som rimtuss
  3. lite, svakt menneske;
    pusling (III), tusseladd;
    stakkar (1)helpless, pitiable person or creature
