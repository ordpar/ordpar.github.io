tåle

verb

to tolerate · to stand, to bear · to withstand

Inflection

Etymology

norrønt þolaOld Norse þola

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. klare å gjennomgå;
    greie, makte    to be able to endure;
    to manage, to cope with
    • tåle en medisinto tolerate a medicine
    • min økonomi tåler ikke store utskeielsermy finances cannot take any great extravagance
    • han har måttet tåle mye kritikkhe has had to put up with a lot of criticism
  2. kunne omgås eller holde ut med uten å kjenne motviljeto be able to be around or bear without feeling aversion
    • de tåler ikke synet av hverandrethey cannot stand the sight of each other
  3. kunne spise uten å bli sykto be able to eat without falling ill
    • hen tåler ikke glutenthey cannot tolerate gluten

Set phrases

tåle sammenligning med kunne måle seg med;
være like god somto be able to measure up to;
to be as good as Eng. phraseto bear comparison with
  • hans prestasjoner tåler ikke sammenligning med konkurrenteneshis achievements do not bear comparison with those of his competitors
