putte

verb

to put (in) · to put, to place

Inflection

Etymology

jamfør engelsk putcompare English put

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. stikke eller legge (i noe)to put or lay (in something)
    • putte noe i lommato put something in one's pocket
    • hun putter ballen i målshe puts the ball in the net
  2. plassereto place
    • bli puttet i fengselto be put in prison
    • de har puttet millioner inn i selskapetthey have put millions into the company
  3. i golf: slå puta puttin golf: to play a puttput
    • putte i hull treto putt on the third hole
