utligne, utlikne

verb

to assess tax, to levy tax · to apportion, to divide proportionally · to equalise, to draw level

Inflection

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. fastsette skattto determine the amount of tax payable
    • utligne skattto assess tax
  2. fordele forholdsmessigto apportion proportionally
    • utligne beløpet på deltakerneto divide the sum among the participants in proportion
  3. særlig i idrett: komme opp på like mange mål eller poeng som motparten;
    utjevneto even out    especially in sport: to draw level with the opposition on goals or points;
    to even oututjevne
    • Norge utlignet til 1–1 på straffesparkNorway equalised to make it 1–1 from a penalty
