hvitvaske, kvitvaske

verb
Inflection

Etymology

etter engelsk whitewash, opprinnelig ‘kalke’after English whitewash, originally ‘to limewash’

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. framstille noen som uskyldigto present someone as innocent
    • spionen ble ikke hvitvasketthe spy was not cleared
  2. plassere svarte penger slik at en dekker over økonomisk svindelto place undeclared money in such a way as to cover up financial fraud
    • svarte penger hvitvaskes gjennom investeringer i eiendomundeclared money is laundered through investments in property
