fordrive

verb

to drive away, to drive out, to expel

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtysk, jamfør norrønt fordrífa ‘tyne’; av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excessfrom Low German, compare Old Norse fordrífa 'to destroy'; from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess

Senses and Example Sentences

jage, drive bortto chase away, to drive out
  • vondt skal vondt fordrivefight fire with fire
  • fordrive triste tankerto drive away gloomy thoughts
  • troppene ble fordrevet fra stillingene sinethe troops were driven out of their positions

to keep oneself amused Eng. phraseto pass the time
  • fordrive tiden med å strikketo pass the time knitting
  • de spilte spill for å fordrive tidenthey played games to pass the time
