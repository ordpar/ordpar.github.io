slite

verbverb

to struggle · to wear out · to toil

InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

norrønt slíta Old Norse slíta

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. rive i stykker;
    dra eller tøye noe så det går sund     to tear to pieces;
    to pull or stretch something until it gives way
    • båten slet fortøyningene the boat broke its moorings
    • oksen har slitt seg the bull has broken loose
    • han har slitt over båndet til foreldrene he has severed his ties with his parents
  2. rykke (1)to tug, rive (II, 5)to tear at to tugrykke (1), to tear atrive (II, 5)
    • hunden sliter i båndet the dog is straining at the leash
    • vinden rev og slet i huset the wind tore and tugged at the house
  3. bruke (mye og lenge) så bruken setter tydelig merke;
    forringe eller tære ved mye bruk;
    jamfør slitt (II)worn, worn-out, shabby     to use (heavily and for a long time) so that the use leaves visible marks;
    to degrade or erode through heavy use;
    compare slitt (II)worn, worn-out, shabby
    • hun har slitt hull på buksa she has worn a hole in her trousers
    • malingen var slitt av the paint had worn off
    • gulvet er slitt ned the floor is worn down
    • han må slite på de samme klærne som storebroren hadde he has to wear the same clothes his big brother had
    • all uroen sliter på nervene all the turmoil wears on the nerves
  4. arbeide hardt;
    anstrenge seg;
    streveto struggle, toil · to strive (for something);
    jamfør slitentired · worn     to work hard;
    to exert oneself;
    to toilstreve;
    compare slitentired · worn
    • vi slet oss oppover bakken we struggled our way up the hill
    • hun har slitt og arbeidet hele livet she has laboured and worked all her life

to sit on the substitutes' benchinnbytterbenken Eng. phraseto warm the bench
  • spilleren slet benken hele sesongen the player spent the entire season on the bench
slite med plages med (for eksempel en skade eller sykdom) to be troubled by (for example an injury or an illness) Eng. phraseto struggle with
  • jeg har slitt mye med ryggen I have had a lot of trouble with my back
slite seg ut gå tom for krefter eller miste helsen av hardt arbeid (over lang tid) to run out of strength or lose one's health through hard work (over a long period) Eng. phraseto wear oneself out
  • de ansatte slet seg ut på grunn av underbemanning the staff wore themselves out because of understaffing
slite skolebenken gå på skolen to go to school
