formørke

verb
Inflection

Etymology

av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excessfrom for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. gjøre mørkereto make darker
    • skyer formørket himmelenclouds darkened the sky
  2. stenge utsikten til en lyskilde;
    skygge for    to block the view of a source of light;
    to eclipse
    • månen formørker solathe moon eclipses the sun
  3. i overført betydning: gjøre dystergloomy · bleak · grim · sombre og tristin a figurative sense: to make gloomydyster and sad
    • formørke tilværelsento darken one's existence
    • tiden ble formørket av krigthe times were darkened by war
    • du må ikke la dette formørke livet dittyou mustn't let this cast a shadow over your life
