formørke

verbverb
InflectionVis bøyning

EtymologyOpphav

av for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess from for- (II)for-, a verb prefix with no distinct meaning of its own · over-, to excess

Senses and Example SentencesBetydning og bruk

  1. gjøre mørkere to make darker
    • skyer formørket himmelen clouds darkened the sky
  2. stenge utsikten til en lyskilde;
    skygge for     to block the view of a source of light;
    to eclipse
    • månen formørker sola the moon eclipses the sun
  3. i overført betydning: gjøre dystergloomy · bleak · grim · sombre og trist in a figurative sense: to make gloomydyster and sad
    • formørke tilværelsen to darken one's existence
    • tiden ble formørket av krig the times were darkened by war
    • du må ikke la dette formørke livet ditt you mustn't let this cast a shadow over your life
