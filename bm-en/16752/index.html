forrykende

adjective

furious, blistering (of force or speed) · terrific, spectacular (of a party or a performance)

Inflection

Etymology

av ryketo snap · to break · to go (be lost) · to give off smoke; fra foreldet forryke ‘forsvinne som en røyk’from ryketo snap · to break · to go (be lost) · to give off smoke; from the obsolete forryke 'to vanish like smoke'
