redde

verb

to save, to rescue

Inflection

Etymology

fra lavtyskfrom Low German

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. bringe i sikkerhet;
    berge    to bring to safety;
    to save
    • redde noen fra å drukneto save someone from drowning
    • redde noe fra glemselto rescue something from oblivion
  2. i ballspill: hindre at ballen går i målin ball games: to stop the ball going into the goal
    • målvakten reddet skuddetthe goalkeeper saved the shot
