skåne

verb

to spare, to shield · to protect from damage, to be gentle with

Inflection

Etymology

av lavtysk schonen ‘behandle på en fin måte’from Low German schonen 'to treat gently'

Senses and Example Sentences

  1. spare for noe hardt, ubehagelig eller en påkjenningto spare from something harsh, unpleasant or a strain
    • skåne barna fra dårlig litteraturto shield the children from bad literature
    • jeg har vært skånt for sykdomI have been spared illness
  2. bevare mot skadeto protect from damage
    • skåne klærne sineto look after one's clothes
